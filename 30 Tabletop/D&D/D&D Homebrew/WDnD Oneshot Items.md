## Bear Chest

### Trapped

Opening the box triggers the gas trap in the room. Detecting the trap requires a Perception (DC 18) or an Investigation (DC 15) check. It can be disabled using Thieves tools (DC 16). Failure on either the Investigation or Thieves tools check triggers the trap.

### Contents

Several thousand coins of mixed denominations, mostly copper and silver but the glint of platinum, gold and precious stones can also be seen. It will take a few hours to count or sort, or you can spend 10 minutes to gather a few of the more valuable pieces.

## Strongbox

A box that is clearly of dwarven make. It has a complex locking mechanism.

### Locked

To unlock this the opener must succeed on a History (DC 15) or Thieves tools (DC 10) check.

### Trapped

A complex trap is built into the box. If the opener tries to open the box without the combination, two blades come out and stab the openers hands causing 1D4 damage.

If the box is searched for traps, it can be discovered Investigation (DC 14) and disarmed with Thieves Tools (DC 12). It can also be reset using Thieves or Tinkers tools (DC 14).

### Contents

The box contains twenty-two gold bars worth 10gp each. Each bar is stamped with a helm-and-battleaxe insignia.

## Small wooden coffer

A small wooden chest containing the entire savings of the decimated Evensong family.

### Contents

5gp, 42sp, 92cp, a jeweled hand mirror and a Potion of Healing. The gold coins are stamped with a helm-and-battle axe sigil. The mirror looks like it could be worth about 10gp.

## Bladecatcher Shield

A dwarven shield of finest quality. Although old and covered in dust, the metal gleams as if it was forged today. On the front, a helm-and-battleaxe sigil can be seen. Across the rest of the face, and around the rim, many small spikes protrude out.

### Don/Doff Bladecatcher

**Action:** Don or doff this shield in a free hand. Gain +2 AC against all attacks and an addition +1 against attacks made with slashing melee weapons.

## Ironhewer Axe

A dwarven battleaxe of finest quality. The image of a hammer striking a boulder is built into the blade and several geodes are set into the haft.

### Brute force

**Passive:** The wielding has advantage on Strength checks to break down doors and to smash inanimate objects.

### Attack with ironhewer

A**ction:** Make an attack roll to hit. This attack does D8 bludgeoning damage, or D10 if wielded in two hands. The weapon causes maximum damage when it strikes an inanimate object.

## Sunrise Hammer

A dwarven warhammer of the finest quality. The back of the head is formed to look like a 12 pointed star. It seems to give off an celestial glow.

### Attack with Sunrise

A**ction:** Make an attack roll to hit. This attack does D8 bludgeoning damage, or D10 if wielded in two hands. The damage type can be changed to piercing by using the other side of the head.

### Solar charge

**Bonus action:** Once per day, you may hold the hammer up to the sun to charge it. The next hit that you make with the hammer causes 2D6 radiant damage, in addition to its normal damage.

## Potion of Healing

A small bottle of liquid, most likely red. It contains 3 doses in a single flask.

### Drink

**Use object:** You drink this potion to regain 1D4+1 hit points immediately, up to your hit point maximum.

### Apply

**Use object:** Feed it to a stabilized, incapacitated creature. This creature regains 1 hitpoint at the end of its next turn.

## Case of explosives

A metal box containing several sticks of conventional explosives. They could be quite powerful if set by skilled hands, but will still do some damage. Probably best to keep them in this case, they seem pretty volatile.

### throw

**Action:** You throw a piece of explosives up to your Strength attribute in feet.

### light

**Bonus action:** If you have a source of fire, you prepare a piece of explosive so it will detonate at a given time. Decide how many rounds until it explodes and tell the GM.

### explode

At the start of each of your following turns, take an Arcana check (DC10). If you pass, your desired result (explode or not explode) occurs. When the Explosives go off, all creatures within 10ft must pass a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) or suffer 3D6 fire damage. Targets still take half damage if they succeed. Creatures with 5 or less intelligence automatically fail their saving throw.



# Blanket of Silence

A thick blanket 10ft on a side, made of gold and silver scales. Very heavy. It is probably worth quite a lot.

### Dampen sound

**Passive:** Sound, both magical and non-magical, cannot pass through the blanket.

# Rope and Pitons

Just 50ft of hempen rope, 10 metal pitons and a small hammer. It doesn’t do anything special but is might useful.

## Potion of Blur

A bottle of blue liquid with a lot of sediment. Probably best to shake before drinking.

### Drink

**Use Object action:** For the next 10 rounds your body becomes blurred, shifting and wavering to all who can see you. For the duration, any creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against you. An attacker is immune to this effect if it doesn’t rely on sight, as with blindsight, or can see through illusions, as with truesight.



## Torches

A bundle of torches to use in dark places. Limited supply, as they are quite heavy and unwieldy. Somebody should have brought a lantern.

### light

**Bonus action:** If you have a source of fire and a free hand, you light a single torch. It burns for 1 hours, providing bright light in a 20ft radius and dim light for an addition 20ft.

### Attack with Torch

**Action:** Make a melee attack roll. You may add your Strength to this. On a hit, the target takes 1 bludgeoning damage, or 1 fire damage if the torch is lit.

## Silkwound Blade

A drow longsword. The dark metal seems to reflect more brightly when in darkness. The pommel and hilt are almost as sharp as the blade.

### Attack with Silkwound

A**ction:** Make an attack roll to hit at +1. This attack does D8+1+Str slashing damage, or D10 if wielded in two hands. Wounds caused by this weapon cannot be healed until the start of the attackers next turn. This ability requires attunement.

## Glassvenom Dagger

A curved dagger made of glass, most likely of drow origin due to the hilt having the form of a spider.

### Throw Glassvenom

A**ction:** Make a ranged attack roll to hit. This attack does D4+Dex piercing damage. The thrown dagger shatters, and a new one appears in your hand. This ability requires attunement.
