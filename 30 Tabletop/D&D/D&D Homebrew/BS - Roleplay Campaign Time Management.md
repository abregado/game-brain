Goal

Develop a structure for a roleplay campaign that:

- Gives the players a feeling that the world goes on without them.

- Is compatible with milestone levelling.

**Idea 1: Villain Calendar**

Place villain actions in a calendar. Make some of those calendar events visible to the players.

I tried this in the Livia campaign. It created a lot of stress for the players.

**Idea 2: Development Points**

At the end of each chapter, always present the players with at least 2 choices of where to go next. Add a Development point to each location or threat that the players did NOT choose. Make sure when they finish their current chapter that some of the things the development increase did are visible to them.

Visible Examples:

- Weather changes

- Damaged structures

- People going missing

- Traders closing up shop

- Phenomena visible on the horizon

- New strange people arriving in the town.

Each chapter arc gets an increase in difficulty when it gains development points. Make sure that these increases are also clearly caused by the players waiting a long time.

Some examples:

- Captured prisoners

- New monsters that the player saw elsewhere and did not deal with.

- Items the players saw were missing being in use by enemies.

- More freshly built fortifications
