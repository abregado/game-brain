### Combat Encounters

A good encounter:

- Gives the clues for its own solution right at the start

- Challenges the players in new ways

- Plays against the weaknesses of the players

- Uses uncommon skills, features or abilities

- Show up at least twice, in increasing severity

Monsters you don’t want to encounter in a tight space

Grick,

Monsters you don’t want to encounter in a wide open space

Invisible Stalker, Flameskull

Monsters you don’t want to encounter with vertical space

Anything with Climbing speed, Roper

*The floor of this chamber is covered in a thick, oily smoke about 20cm deep. It does not impede your movement, but flows like water to fill any gaps made while you move.*

There is a Bottle of Eversmoking embedded in the stone floor. Several pits have been sunk into the floor.

Monster Ideas: Oblex Spawn, Crawling Claw, Insect Swarms, Smoke Memphit, Neogi Hatchlings

*A large stone slab is leaning at a sharp angle in this room. A huge pile of rubble lots on top of it ready to fall should the slab move. The only thing holding the slab up is a wooden quarterstaff which appears to be inserted into a hole in both the stone floor and the stone slab. The pressure on the staff must be immense but it is not even bending.*

The quarterstaff is either a Serpent Staff, an Immovable Rod or and Adamantine Rod of Collapsing.

The heroes encounter Undead warriors wielding rusty, brittle looking weapons. When a hero is hit, shards of the weapon break off in the skin. This lowers the maximum hit points of the target by half the damage taken.

A Medicine roll with DC equal to the damage reduction can be taken to remove a piece of rusty metal from a wounded creature. On a success it is removed completely. On a failure, the piece stays in. Either way it causes the target additional damage equal to the damage reduction.

If a piece is left in for 1 hour, the damage reduction increases by d6 and the target suffers necrotic damage equal to the new damage reduction. Corpses of creatures slain by this will become Zombies after 24 hours.

The pieces of rusty metal, if left on the ground, will dissolve. After 24 hours, a thorny bush will sprout at this location.
