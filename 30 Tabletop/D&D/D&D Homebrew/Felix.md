# ***Felix Firesteel***

### Statistics

| **Str** | **Dex** | **Con** | **Int** | **Wis** | **Cha** |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| +4      | +1      | +3      | +4      | +0      | +0      |

| **Armor Class** | **Hitpoints** | **Rest Dice** |

| 16              | 29            | 3d10          |

### 

### Major Skills and Languages:

2 from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Survival.

**Weapon Proficiency:** You add +2 to attack rolls using Simple and Martial weapons.

### Resource Tokens

Surge x2, Supremacy x3

### Passive Features

Great weapon fighting - When rolling damage from a two handed melee attack, you may reroll each dice which roll 1 or 2 the first time.

### Equipment

### Health Potion, One weapon which is two Handed, One weapon which is single handed, Throwing Axe

### Combat Actions

Surge of Activity - **Free Action:** Spend a Surge token. You may take a second Action immediately this turn.

Surge of Vitality - **Bonus Action:** Spend a Surge token. Heal yourself d10+3 hitpoints.

Precision Attack - **Free Action, after failing an attack roll:** Spend a Supremacy token to roll a d8, adding it to your attack roll. This can change the outcome of the attack roll.

Knockdown - **Free Action, after hitting a target with a melee attack:** Add a d8 to the damage caused. The target must pass a Strength saving throw (DC 13) or it suffers the Prone condition.

### Non-combat Actions

Size up opponent - Make an Insight check to determine how dangerous an opponent would be in 1 on 1 combat with you.

### Fallback Emotions

### Pride and Curiosity

### 

### Behaviours

When talking: Makes a statement and then turns the same statement into a question and redundantly asks it.

When Listening: Either sits quietly and listens without getting involved to size up the talker, or makes intense (but friendly) eye contact if he is involved.

Moment: Always takes time to enjoy the aroma of any food he is served.

### 4 Pillars

**Gimmick:** I’ve seen it all. Even though he looks young, Felix is actually the oldest of the group.

**Conflict:** Young adventurers need to see it for themselves, but I actually want to help them to stop them getting hurt.

**Motif:** Fire
