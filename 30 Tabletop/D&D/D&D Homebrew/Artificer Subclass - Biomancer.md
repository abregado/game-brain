# Daelkyr Biomancer Artificer subclass

## What are the fantasies I’m trying to deliver?

- Crafter.

- Collector.

- Buffing support.

- Creepy.

- Frankenstein (the scientist, not the monster)

- Jekyll and Hyde (both the scientist and the monster)

## What constraints or other goals have I set myself

- Compatibility with vanilla Artificer from the Eberron: Rising from the Last War sourcebook.

- Utilise, and build from, the base class features (Item infusions, Flash of genius, Magic item adept)

- Give an alternate method of powering up the character, aside from just levelling up in their class.

- Keep it simple. Homebrew is always too complex, especially the homebrew I make.

- If possible, allow it to mesh well with prop making for more immersive play at a table.

## Core idea and theme

Artificers can infuse non-magical items and make them magical for a time. But what if they could infuse creatures instead?

**Note**: Subclass features are coloured like this. Optional base class features and base class errata are coloured like this. Base class features are not coloured.

## Level 1

### Cantrip storing item: Magical Tinkering optional replacement

At 1st level, you learn how to store a cantrip in an object. During a short or long rest, you can touch one item that you can use as a spellcasting focus, and you store a cantrip in it, choosing a cantrip from the artificer spell list that requires 1 action to cast and does not require concentration. You do not need to know the cantrip. If the cantrip has an option for how it manifests, you must choose the manifestation when storing the cantrip.

When you do this, you can change the damage type to one of similar rarity, and/or modify any saving throws to require a different ability score.

While holding the object, a creature can take an action to produce the spell's effect from it, using your spellcasting ability modifier. The enchantment stays in the object until you dismiss it as an action or until you use this feature again to store a cantrip in a different object.

### Spellcasting - Tools required: Errata

All of your 1st level or higher spells gain the Material component requirement.

Choose which type of tools you use as a spell focus when you make your character.

When crafting magic items, the time it takes you is halved.

## Level 2

### Item Infusion

Whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch a non-magical object and imbue it with one of your artificer infusions, turning it into a magic item. An infusion works on only certain kinds of objects, as specified in the infusion's description. If the item requires attunement, you can attune yourself to it the instant you infuse the item. If you decide to attune to the item later, you must do so using the normal process for attunement (see "Attunement" in chapter 7 of the Dungeon Master's Guide).

Your infusion remains in an item indefinitely, but when you die, the infusion vanishes after a number of days have passed equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1 day). The infusion also vanishes if you give up your knowledge of the infusion for another one.

You can infuse more than one nonmagical object at the end of a long rest; the maximum number of objects appears in the Infused Items column of the Artificer table. You must touch each of the objects, and each of your infusions can be in only one object at a time. Moreover, no object can bear more than one of your infusions at a time. If you try to exceed your maximum number of infusions, the oldest infusion immediately ends, and then the new infusion applies.

If an infusion ends on an item that contains other things, like a bag of holding, its contents harmlessly appear in and around its space.

## Level 3

### Biomechanics: Artificer Specialist Feature

You can now infuse your own body as well as items. You can select infusions from the Biomechanics infusion list. You can reselect all of your known infusions at this point, and they can be a combination of regular item infusions and Biomechanics infusions.

### In Vitro study: Artificer Specialist Feature

You no longer increase Known Infusions by levelling up in the Artificer class. Instead, as a Downtime action you can spend a rare reagent and 2d6x25 gold worth of magical reagents to learn a new Infusion.

### Sample harvesting: Artificer Specialist Feature

You can spend 10 minutes harvesting components from a fallen Undead, Celestial, Fey, Fiend, Elemental, Aberration and Monstrosity creatures. Make a Surgeons tools (Intelligence) check of DC25. On a success, you gain one Rare ingredient. On a failure, gain 2 uncommon ingredients. You may add the Challenge rating of the dead creature to your roll. Once you do this, you cannot do it again until you complete a Long rest.

### Right tool for the job

At 3rd level, you learn how to produce exactly the tool you need: with thieves' tools or artisan's tools in hand, you can magically create one set of artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. This creation requires 1 hour of uninterrupted work, which can coincide with a short or long rest. Though the product of magic, the tools are nonmagical, and they vanish when you use this feature again.

## Level 5

### Human testing approval: Artificer Specialist Feature

Any Biomechanic Infusions with the requirement of “Self” can now be used on any willing creature. Infusions applied in this way last only until your next short or long rest. The number of infusions you can have active at once increases by 1.

### Soulbound Infusions: Artificer Specialist Features

When you apply a Biomechanical Infusion on yourself, you may choose to attune to it and gain the attunement bonus listed under the infusion.

## Level 6

### Tool expertise 

Starting at 6th level, your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses your proficiency with a tool.

## Level 7

### Flash of Genius

Starting at 7th level, you gain the ability to come up with solutions under pressure. When you or another creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes an ability check or a saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Level 9

### Soulbound trials approved: Artificer Specialist Feature

When you apply a Biomechanical Infusion on another creature, it may choose to attune to the infusion and gain the listed attunement bonus.

### Flash graft: Artificer Specialist Feature

Whenever you assist another creature which has one of your Biomechanical infusions, using your Flash of Genius feature, that creature chooses to either regain 2d6 + your Intelligence modifier hitpoints, or gain d6 + your Intelligence modifier temporary hit points.

## Level 10

### Magic Item Adept

When you reach 10th level, you achieve a profound understanding of how to use and make magic items:

- You can attune to up to four magic items at once.

- If you craft a magic item with a rarity of common or uncommon, it takes you a quarter of the normal time, and it costs you half as much of the usual gold.

## Level 11

### Spell Storing item

At 11th level, you learn how to store a spell in an object. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch one simple or martial weapon or one item that you can use as a spellcasting focus, and you store a spell in it, choosing a 1st- or 2nd-level spell from the artificer spell list that requires 1 action to cast (you needn't have it prepared).

While holding the object, a creature can take an action to produce the spell's effect from it, using your spellcasting ability modifier. If the spell requires concentration, the creature must concentrate. The spell stays in the object until it's been used a number of times equal to twice your Intelligence modifier (minimum of twice) or until you use this feature again to store a spell in an object.

## Level 14

### Magic Item Savant

At 14th level, your skill with magic items deepens more:

- You can attune to up to five magic items at once.

- You ignore all class, race, spell, and level requirements on attuning to or using a magic item.

## Level 15

### Biomantic Mastery: Artificer Specialist Feature

The number of infusions you can have active at once increases by 2.

### Chimeric Biochemistry: Artificer Specialist Feature

You can apply infusions to yourself as an Action. If you already have applied the maximum number of infusions, you can remove one as part of this action. You may also attune to the infusion as part of this action.

## Level 18

### Magic Item Master

Starting at 18th level, you can attune to up to six magic items at once.

## Level 20

### Soul of Artifice

At 20th level, you develop a mystical connection to your magic items, which you can draw on for protection:

- You gain a +1 bonus to all saving throws per magic item you are currently attuned to.

- If you're reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can use your reaction to end one of your artificer infusions, causing you to drop to 1 hit point instead of 0.

## Biomechanical Infusions

### Symbiont whip

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature can cast the Thornwhip cantrip as an action adding their Strength modifier to the damage caused.

### Skinfold stash

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature can store one small item in the pouch (such as a dagger). The item is undetectable to visual and physical inspection.

### Entropy inhibitor

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature can take the Dash action as a bonus action on its turn.

### Protective Wormcyst

Prerequisite: Self

Choose a saving throw type when the infusion is applied. The infused creature can add D4 to every saving throw they take of that kind.

### Adrenamental conductor

Prerequisite: Self

Choose a basic or elemental damage type when applying this infusion. One damage dice per attack that the infused creature makes can be rerolled.

Attunement bonus: Target resistances to the chosen damage type are downgraded one step.

### Steelvein augmentation

Prerequisite: Self

The infused gains resistance to one basic damage type (Slashing, Piercing, bludgeoning, poison) chosen when the infusion is applied.

Attunement bonus: Also choose one elemental type of damage (fire, lightning, cold). Gain resistance to that type.

### Subdermal codependence graft

Prerequisite: Self, while having at least one other Biomechanical Infusion active.

All Biomechanical infusions that the target has are undetectable to visual inspection.

Attunement bonus: Infusions are also undetectable by magical means.

### Stimulant gland

Prerequisite: Self

When the infused creature fails a Concentration check the check can be rerolled. The new result must be accepted.

### Weavehunger graft

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature may spend an action and a hit die to recover one 1st level spell slot.

Attunement bonus: Increase your maximum 1st level spell slots by 1.

### Toughskin graft

Prerequisite: Self

The target gains a bonus of +1 to armor class while the infusion is active.

Attunement bonus: After suffering damage, the infused can spend its reaction to roll a hit die and reduce the damage by the result.

### Fleshwarp capacitor

Prerequisite: Self

Melee attacks the infused creature make have their reach increased by 5ft.

Attunement bonus: Before an attack roll is made against them, the infused creature can spend their reaction to immediately take the Dodge action.

### Melatonin crystalizer

Prerequisite: Self

When in bright light, the infused creature can spend an action to gain 2d6 temporary hit points.

Attunement bonus: The infused creature gains resistance to one basic or elemental damage type while it has temporary hit points from this infusion.

### Moteswarm controller

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature sheds bright light out 10ft, and dim light for 20ft more.

Attunement bonus: The infused can choose an additional point within 60ft at the start of each of their turns. This point sheds bright light out 10ft, and dim light for 20ft more.

### Magefocus cyst

Prerequisite: Self

The infused gains +1 to its Spellattack and Spell DC. The cyst counts as a spell casting focus for Artificer spellcasting.

Attunement bonus: The infused creature can spend its reaction after dealing a killing blow to regain one hit die.

### Monosonic propagator

Prerequisite: Self

The infused can cast their spells without requiring verbal components.

Attunement bonus: The infused can cast their spells without requiring any of the components. Consumable material spell components with a gold cost are still required.

### Lifepulp philter

Prerequisite: Self

As an action, the infused creature can spend a hit die to suffer that much damage and gain twice the lost amount in temporary hit points.

Attunement bonus: When the infused must spend a hit die to activate an ability from another infusion and has no hit die remaining, they may instead roll a hit die and suffer that much damage to activate the ability.

### Ironstrap tendons

Min Biomancer level: 5

Prerequisite: Self

The infused creature may spend its Bonus action to make a weapon attack roll using one of its equipped weapons.

Attunement bonus: The infused creature may spend an Action to make two weapon attack rolls using its equipped weapons.

### Heartwall syphon

Min Biomancer level: 5

Prerequisite: Self

At the end of a short or long rest the infused creature may choose to discard a hit die. They gain a number of temporary hitpoints equal to twice the maximum hitpoints they could have recovered from that die (including constitution bonus).

Attunement Bonus: +2 Constitution score

### TODO: Ability score infusions

At level 5 there should be one infusion per Ability score, as with the Heartwall syphon.

### Twinmeld vesicle

Min Biomancer level: 5

Prerequisite: Self

Attunement bonus: The Biomancer can select two infusions. This infusion grants both their effects (but neither attunement bonus).
