## Borrowed Time

**Small Bracelet.**

Requires Attunement by any class.

1 Charge, recharges during a long rest.

- **Action/Bonus Action:** You may copy the action or bonus action from last round even if you are no longer able to fulfill the requirements. Expends one charge.

- **Cast as Ritual (10 minutes):** You may send a ten word telepathic message to one person you know. The message arrives in two days if the person is within 200miles. Expends one charge.

- **When you are hit with an attack or spell:** As a reaction, ignore damage until the end of your next turn. At the end of your next turn, suffer that damage and an additional D6. Expends one charge

- **Immediately after rolling for Initiative:** you may swap the Initiative scores of two friendly targets (or your own).

- **As a free action:** You may swap your weapons or change equipment as if you did it last round.

- **Passive:** You are resistant to one damage type you were not resistant to last round.

- **Passive:** The bracelet has an extra 2 charges

## Hesitant Duality

**Tiny Trinket.**

Requires Attunement by Monk, Rogue, Druid, Barbarian

- **When you make an unarmed strike:** you may make the strike from the position of any living creature you can see within 30ft (not constructs or undead). Until your next round starts, you are considered to be in that position(s).

- **When a target within 15ft of you is hit with a ranged attack:** You may use Deflect Arrow reaction to intercept the missile. You suffer any left over damage as if you were the original target

- **As an action:** You gain Truesight out to 30ft but can only detect if a target is undead, a construct or neither (living). The effect lasts 1 round.

- **When you miss with an unarmed strike:** Gain a bonus +1 to hit/damage on the next attack which hits. When you deal damage, the bonus is reset to zero.

- **When you are hit:** As a reaction, spend a number of hitpoints to increase your AC by the same amount. You may do this before or after an opponent rolls to hit.

## Reckless Innocence

**Small trinket**

Requires Attunement by fighter, barbarian, cleric or paladin

- **Passive:** Gain immunity to poison damage.

- **When you become Grappled, Restrained or Stunned:** the creature which caused this condition and you become petrified until it passes a Constitution saving throw, taken at the start of each of its turns. You can set the difficulty of the test between 7 and 15, but you both use the same DC.

- **Bonus action:** You touch a rocky surface. For D4 rounds you can see the shape of solid rock within 30 ft. This automatically reveals secret passages and stone based traps. For the duration you gain the petrified status.

- **When you become Incapacitated:** Instead you are petrified and gain immunity to all damage until you pass a DC 7 Constitution saving throw. You can choose not to roll this save. Afterwards, this item has no effect until you make a long rest.

- **When you are hit:** As a reaction, you may choose to become petrified until you pass a DC 7 Constitution saving throw, taken at the start of your turn.

## Sunwave Host

**Small cloak**

Attunable by Rogue, Ranger, Fighter, Sorcerer

- **Passive:** At the start of your turn if in strong sunlight, you may decide to stand still. If you do so then you become Invisible.

- **Passive:** You are resistant to fire damage and do not suffer exhaustion effects from hot weather.

- **As a Bonus Action:** You may teleport to any location within your walking speed you can see that is in at least partial darkness without revealing yourself or triggering AoO.

- **Passive:** You gain Truesight of all dark areas within 30ft. Illuminated areas are treated as normal.

- **Passive:** You are resistant to cold damage and do not suffer suffer exhaustion from cold weather.

## Hungergaunt

**Medium gloves**

Attunable by Cleric, Sorcerer, Druid, Wizard

- **When you expend a spell slot:** heal D6 to each target you are touching.

- **Whenever you regain hitpoints:** deal D6 necrotic damage to a target you can see.

- **When you cast a healing spell:** You may cast the spell as if it is one level higher than the spell slot you expend, once per long rest.

- **When you stabilize a target:** suffer 2D6 necrotic damage to immediately heal your patient half the damage you took.

- **Passive:** You are resistant to necrotic damage

# Relics

Some simple rules regarding relics:

- They are non-rechargable, generally having only a few charges.

- They are usable by all classes and races.

- Spell save DC’s and Spell attack values should be given for all effects as they may be used by non-magic users.

- They do not require attunement, but may need to be donned.

- They can be identified during any rest without the need of an Identify spell.

- Values shown are the sale price of the item (unused/used)

# Potions

General rules for potions. They are single use, requiring a Use Object Action in combat to gain their effects. They may be admisitered to another target only if that target is unconcious, restrained or incapacitated, which also costs an Use Object Action.

Using a Herbalism or Alchemy kit, a character can spend a day diluting a potion into 1D4+1 weaker Dilutions. Special ingredients (Essenses) can be diluted to create Potions.

Glass vials are needed for each dilution (1gp), as well as a source of clean water.

## Potion of Vitality

**Use Object Action:** The drinker is cured of all diseases, poisons and conditions. Its exhaustion level is reset to zero and any hit dice spent in the next 24 hours recover maximum possible.

**Dilution of Vitality**

**During any rest:** Hit dice used during this rest regain the maximum Hit Points possible.

## Barkskin Potion

**Use Object Action:** For the next 1 hour, the drinkers Armor Class cannot go below 18.

**Barkskin Dilution**

**Use Object Action:** For the next 10 minutes, the drinkers Armor Class cannot go below 16.

## Blur potion

**Use Object Action:** For the next minute, all attacks made against the drinker are made with disadvantage. The attacker is immune to this effect if they do not rely on sight, as with blindsight, or if they can see through illusions, as with truesight.

**Blur Dilution**

Use Object Action: Until you are hit, attacks made against you are made with disadvantage. The effect also wears off after 3 rounds.

## Trueshot Potion

**Use Object Action:** For the next minute, all attacks you make are with advantage as long as you can see the target. If a target is using the Dodge action, or otherwise has advantage to defend, you instead roll as normal.

**Distillation of True Striking**

**Use Object Action:** Until you hit with an attack, you make all your attack rolls with Advantage, as long as your target is visible to you.

## Potion of Petrification

**Use Object Action:** For the next hour, the drinker gains the Petrified condition. After this, the drinker gains immunity to the Petrified condition for 23 hours.

**Dilution of Stone warding**

**Use Object Action:** For the next minute, whenever you start your turn with the Petrified condition, you may take a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) to remove the condition.

## Potion of Flying

**Use Object Action:** For the next hour, the drinker gains a flying speed equal to their walking speed. If the effect wears off while in flight, they fall and suffer 1D6 Bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet fallen.

**Featherweight Dilution**

**Use Object Action:** For the next minute, your Jump distances are tripled and you gain advantage on all Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) tests taken for climbing.

## Master Artificer's Bracelet

Small bracelet, attunable by Artificer

- You can apply bonuses from the Phandelver Forge of Spells to projects you are involved in at least half the time.

- **As a Ritual:** The bracelet counts as your spellcasting focus for Artificer spells for the next 10 days. This includes collecting odds and ends for material spell components.

## Phandelver Forge of Spells

Lair item (unmovable)

- **Workshop:** Magic Items take half as long to enchant and their ingredient costs are halved. The maximum number of artisans working on one project is increased by 1.

- **Tannery:** Ingredients harvested from beasts, monstrosities, dragons, giants and humanoids can be prepared here. This increases the types of items that can be produced. The maximum number of artisans working on one project made mostly from leather is increased by 1.

- **Alchemical Laboratory:** Ingredients harvested from undead, ooze, fey, plants, elementals, dragons or aberrations can be prepared here. This increases the types of items that can be produced. The cost and time requirements for making potions is halved.

- **Smelter:** Ingredients harvested from constructs, elementals as well as rare metals can be prepared here. This increases the types of items that can be produced. The cost and time requirements for enchanting items from metal reduced by half.

- **Arcane Focus:** Ingredients harvested from monstrosities, aberrations, fiends, celestials, humanoids and undead can be prepared here. This increases the types of items that can be produced. The cost and time requirements for scribing scrolls, wands and spellstones is halved.
