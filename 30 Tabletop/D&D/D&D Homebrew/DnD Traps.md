### Trap Encounters

A good trap:

- is not invisible.

- presents a challenge to be overcome.

- has a twist or AHA! Moment.

- does not kill, but imposes a disadvantage

- effects everyone if one person fails

The sticky crystal lock. A Stone door bars the way. Before it lies a lectern with a hexagonal socket. In the centre of the room is an altar with a \[kraken/dragon/serpent/hand\] statue, which is holding a hexagonal gemstone.

If the statue is touched, the one who touches it is grappled \[escape 12/15/18\] and the statue begins turning. Monsters appear and attack at this point. After 1 round, the statue is spinning so fast that the grappled target loses its footing and has disadvantage on all checks and saves.

On round 2, pillars rise from the floor, which a grappled target will collide with as it is thrown around. Each round, this cases \[1d4/1d6/2d4\] bludgeoning damage.

On round 5, a set of blades begin extruding from the pillars.

On round 10, gouts of flame begin emitting from the floor grates.

A room with 4 glass bells, each covering a terrarium filled with an elaborate garden and a single \[humanoid/kobold/mind flayer\] corpse. Elsewhere in the room is an incredibly heavy chestplate with immense pauldrons. A pair of gauntlets are submerged in the stone beneath, and the chest plate is strapped down with 4 heavy chains.

A key or Important object is chained to a small altar in the centre of the 4 bells. It should be obvious that something happens when the object is freed from its restraints.

Have the players roll Initiative, and roll a fake initiative for the “enemies”. If the players hit any of the creatures in the glass bells, have them fall to ash.

Once all players have made their turns, spirits rise up from each slain creature and inhabit the armor. Creatures which are still alive move slowly towards the armor.

Each spirit which reaches the armor empowers it. A single spirit will allow the armor to break free in several hours, two will mean it breaks free in a few minutes, three spirits it will break free in a few rounds. If all four spirits reach the armor it breaks free immediately.

Dancing Crystal. A long room, with a door and receptacle at one end. At the other end is a pedestal with the Crystal shaped key. All around the pedestal are obvious signs of traps, although unbeknownst to the heroes, these are all fake. When a hero picks up the key up, they are very relieved that nothing happens, tell them they feel like dancing, but then monsters attack.

When the hero starts its turn, inform them they are cursed. Their speed is halved, and they must keep both hands on the key until they place it into the receptacle. They must succeed on a dc15 Charisma saving throw each turn or they can do nothing but dance on the spot. If they succeed then they can still take their turn, but cannot use their hands, and any movement they make includes dancing. Creative characters can invent ways to be battle effective while dancing.

All heroes in the room have their movement speed halved and cannot take the Dash action without setting off the trap.

As soon as the heroes enter the room, they can tell there is something odd with the floor. Every tile has intricate arcane symbols engraved on it. Every player must pass an Arcana DC 18 test to understand the symbols. Heroes with Arcana proficiency can roll with advantage.

There are only N safe tiles in the room. Characters who passed the check can move from one safe tile to another that is up to half their movement away.

Other characters cannot move without setting off the trap.

Characters who passed the test can use their action to give one other character instruction on where to step. After the character has moved, the insutrcting character must pass a DC 12 Charisma test otherwise the moving character ends its movement on an adjacent trapped tile, and the trap is triggered.

When the trap is triggered, all characters in the room must take DC 15 Dex saving throws or be hit with \[fire/piercing/slashing\] damage.

For course, as soon as one character is half way through the room, a group of creatures attack. Any creatures which could realistically not trigger the trap will do. Slimes, roof crawlers or ghosts would fit.

A long section of corridor with a stone grill as floor. Below about 10 feet is another corridor that is flooded about 5cm deep.

The grilled floor sections rotate, dropping anyone stepping on it down into the lower corridor. It also locks in place each time it rotates.

The party detects the trap as they approach. Anyone attempting to walk along the corridor will trigger the trap if they fail a DC 15 Acrobatics test. A hero with Thieves tools (DC 12), Tinker Tools or pitons (Strength DC 15) can attempt to jam the trap when they attempt to cross to the other side. Succeeding will give the next hero to attempt passage advantage on their check.

Anyone in the lower passage is trapped and separated from the group. However, the corridor continues along underneath and ends up in a large room which also has a stone grill floor. Here combat can break out. Each group is attacked by creatures, and cannot pass between levels. Those on the top level can only assist those below using ranged attacks, and vice versa.

After the battle is over, the heroes have time to discover a way to regroup.
