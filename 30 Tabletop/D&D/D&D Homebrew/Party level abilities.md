Party level abilities

**Heroic defence tokens**

Each character gets one method to spend heroic defence tokens.

Tokens are gained by the party and can be spent by anyone once earned.

Heroic defence abilities also have an additional requirement such as "the hero must be wielding a sword". Generally the resolution of the defence will cause this condition to be removed, such as causing the heroes sword to be broken or moved 10ft away.

These should fit the theme of "you survive but…"

Heroic parry

Heroic shield cover

Magical shield

Last effort

Equipment block

Divine deflection

Cover destruction

**Downtime actions**

These are per character. A character gets one with each background package when they are made.
