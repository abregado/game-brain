Writing every day

2.09.24

What do wizards do?

Travelling wizards offer services for board while travelling.

Wizards get a retainer to be on call for nobles.

Wizards might open a shop for other wizards.

Abjuration wizard might be a magical locksmith or give protective blessings to townsfolk or tools. Perhaps helping workers be more effective at harvest time, or protecting villagers who are attempting dangerous tasks (hunting). They would refresh wards on safes, houses, cemeteries or churches.

Main Cantrip: Resistance

Can be cast as a reaction.

Main combat spell: Shield

When using shield, you can redirect the strike

You can use shield on others within 15ft

Cast any Abjuration spell as a Reaction when you or someone within 15ft suffers damage.

Transmutation wizards can place enchantments on a forge or stove, assist with earthworks or bless crop fields. They can also assist villagers directly with magical enhancements. Weather control or protection from storms and flooding could also be offered. Basically any “bending” style jobs.

main Cantrip:

Main combat: Heat Metal

Cast Transmutation as faster action

Necromancers could deal with unwanted spirits, frighten large pests or poison small pests. They can perform resurrections or burial rights. Plant based pests like weeds could also be dealt with.

Main combat: false Life

False Life range become touch

Target can spend a hit die to gain die max additional temp hit points.

Can roll hit die as additional damage on any Necro spell

Gains a hit die if a Necro spell deals damage or heals a target

Illusionists could create entertainment like fireworks or enhance existing performances. They could assist capturing animals by enhancing traps, or cover up gaps in defences. There are also many criminal uses.

Evocation… well… explosives?

Enchantment wizards could calm animals and people or assist with interrogations for investigations. They could also be negotiators or escrow for important deals. Bartenders and law enforcement might also use them for security.

Diviners would sell prophetic services or assist with research. They could also be guides or scouts looking for lost locals in need of rescue. Valuable items that were lost, could be found.

Conjurers provide travel services using teleport, or summon guardians to protect specific locations temporarily. Moving large objects is likely also possible, as well as summoning work force for specific jobs.
