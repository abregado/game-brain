15.03.24

Tips:

Use natural language for your rules rather than numerical language.

Dice rules where the roller might need to react to the result will increase tension. EG:// deciding to spend extra resources.

Always bring an old npc back instead of making a new one if you can.

Every NPC likes one player and dislikes another player. Varied dispositions cause different players to interact!

Discrete actions always yield the same risks. D100 always for loot.

RAW = Rules As Written in official books.

1.  Ban flying races

2.  No flanking advantage (actually RAW).

3.  Long rests can only occur in safe spaces, for example, at an inn, a very well hidden tiny hut, or a prepared hideout.

4.  Warlocks automatically learn their subclass spells.

5.  All sorcerer subclasses have a spell list similar to clockwork soul/aberrant mind. (Lunar is fine as is). They learn these automatically.

6.  Your first ASI grants you both a first level feat and ASI.

7.  After seeing the initiative order, a player may lower their initiative score.

8.  Popcorn initiative "one of us goes then gm goes"

9.  Simple initiative: heroes then gm. Best initiative from players vs best initiative from monsters.

10. Reroll your max HP every long rest changing it only if you roll higher than your current max.

11. The following feats now include a +1 to an ability score: Charger (+1 STR), Defensive duelist (+1 Dex), duel wielder (+1 Dex or STR), medium armor master (+1 Dex or +1 con), shield master (+1 Dex or con), skilled (+1 to any), prodigy (+1 to any). If you want a +1 on your feat and it's not listed here, convince me.

12. If a feat gives you access to a spell and you are already a spellcaster you can also use a spell slot of appropriate level to cast it.

13. All weapons have mastery feat, not just crossbow expert, great weapon master and polearm master.

14. Potions are a Bonus Action to consume, they are an Action to feed to another.

15. Exhaustion has been replaced with One DnD variant.

16. Rolling a Natural 20 on any d20 Test (skill check, saving throw, attack roll) generates Inspiration for a player who witnesses the act (you decide which player), you're inspiring someone else. Rolling a Natural 1 on any d20 Test generates Inspiration for yourself as you push yourself to do better next time. All inspiration is lost at the end of the session unless the session ends mid combat.

17. Counterspell, Silvery Barbs and the Conjure X spells are banned, neither players nor NPCs have access to them.

18. Any class can use a spell scroll, no Arcana DC required. Each scroll had the DC and level included in its description.

19. Any spell caster can counterspell with their reaction by spending a spell slot, but must win a concentration contest with the caster. The list of the contest is the first one who loses concentration. If the caster wins, the spell is cast.

20. Casting a spell as a ritual (during a short rest) makes it last until the next short rest. Casting it after a long rest makes it last until the next long rest. Concentration as normal. Spells fail if you go unconscious.

21. Players can only take the Help Action if they also have proficiency in the same skill or weapon.

22. Players can use their passive score in a skill if they are able to take 10 minutes to do the task.

23. When players search, they say how long they want to spend doing it. GM gives a bonus based on how long they do.

24. Guidance, Resistance and True Strike all use the One DnD variant, a 10ft reaction spell that lets you add a 1d4 to your Skill Check, Saving Throw and Attack roll respectively.

25. Inspiration works as Reroll of anything. If not used in the same session, it is lost.

26. Inspiration works as an auto success. If not used in same session, it is lost.

27. Spend gold for expected travel length before leaving town. Don't track supplies but if their travel takes longer then there are penalties (exhaustion, penalties at next encounter). Spend double to avoid the first shortage, and triple for the second shortage but all supplies are lost when you arrive.

28. Each short rest per day doubles the time it takes to take another. First one is fifteen minutes, next is thirty, next is an hour, etc.

29. While in a dungeon, each short rest has a chance of being interrupted.

    1.  First short rest. No chance

    2.  Second short rest, 1 in 6 chance of discovery

    3.  Third short rest, 2 in 6 chance of discovery

    4.  Ect

30. **Damage Types/Resistances:** When you learn a spell, feat, class feature; craft or buy a weapon, equipment, magic item; turn into or summon another creature you can change its damage type/resistance by moving left \<\> right or down².

    1.  Force

    2.  Psychic \<\> Radiant \<\> Necrotic \<\> Thunder

    3.  Fire \<\> Cold \<\> Lightning \<\> Acid

    4.  Magical Bludgeoning \<\> Magical Piercing \<\> Magical Slashing

    5.  Poison

31. Monsters cannot crit, but fumbles from players generate legendary actions from nearby monsters.

32. AC bonuses give bonuses against one combination of spell/weapon melee/ranged attack roll, but at double the value.

33. Non-boss Monsters attacks always deal fixed (average) damage.

34. Lower monster AC by 3 points, but give them 20% extra HP.

35. The biggest monster in an encounter gets a legendary action that recharges on its turn on a 5+. Or add an environment action that triggers on a 5+ (on a D6) at Initiative 0, but make it obvious how the players can stop it from triggering.

36. "I know this guy" points as rewards. A player can. Spend one to have backstory with any npc the gm introduces.

37. Flashback points. Spend at any time and explain how you prepared for this situation or purchased an item. These are gained via downtime actions.

38. No fumbles on a 1, just miss (actually the RAW)

39. Players cannot discuss the state of the Death saving throws and must hide the dice result.

40. Players cannot talk while bleeding out, silenced or unconscious.

41. GM rolls all death saving throws secretly

42. You cannot long rest when travelling, only in town.

43. A skill check cannot be retaken by another player unless they have a higher modifier. If a test is retaken and failed, the test is not retakable. If the original player rolls a 1, the test is not retakable.

44. Mortal wounds and overkill damage lowers your maximum hit point score by the same amount.

45. Nonlethal strikes need to be announced before rolling. No damage dice are rolled for damage on these strikes only the modifier is taken as damage. On a 1, the damage is lethal instead.

46. Rising from Prone can trigger attacks of opportunity. If you are damaged by an attack of opportunity, you remain prone.

47. You declare standing up from Prone at any time, but the status only is removed at the end of your turn.

48. Statuses are only ever removed at the end of the affected creatures turn.

49. On a critical hit you can apply one of the following conditions for 1 round instead of dealing additional damage: blinded, deafened, prone, poisoned/weakened, restrained

50. Losing more than half your HP in one round requires you to pass a concentration check or become incapacitated for one round.

51. Critical hits against normal enemies kill them instantly.

52. Gain a level of exhaustion immediately upon reaching zero hit points.

53. When someone stabilises you, resolve your death saves with advantage.

54. Dungeon master must make all monster stat Blocks Public at the start of the combat. Gives players more chances to come up with cool shit.

55. Spend a hit die to cast a level one spell during a short rest.

56. Crits don't get extra dice, but instead do maximum damage

57. Devil's Bargain: at any time immediately after a d20 roll is made, a player may request - or the DM may offer - a Devil's Bargain. If both player and DM agree to make the bargain, this changes the roll to a Nat 1 or Nat 20, whichever is to the character's advantage. However, the DM can then change any future roll relating to that character to a Nat 1 or Nat 20.

58. Push the roll: you can reroll a failed test (not attack) but if the second roll fails it is a fumble.

59. If you can't hit the table, you can't hit the DC". Your dice only count if they land on the table.

60. PC health is visible to every one

61. If you can tell an applicable joke that makes the dm laugh, vicious mockery does double damage.

62. Initiative surprise. Players as a group always go first, but if they fail their initiative then the enemies have extra buffs or better positions.

63. Disengage gives you 5ft additional movement on top of what it already does. This is specifically to counter the opportunity attack chasedown method and lets creatures actually... y'know... *disengage* from a fight.

64. No fumbles or crit on death saving throws. Death failures reset only at long rest.

65. Flying PCs must land at the end of their movement.

66. Concentration checks are made at the start of a player turn. Max one per turn. To deal with players causing 1 damage just to force a concentration check, or small creatures breaking concentration due to forcing many tests.

67. Sorcerer's ignore non-priced material components for spells

68. Bards can ignore non-priced material components with a verbal (audio) component, or ignore them if a spell already has a V component.

69. Healers kit can use 3/10 charges and a successful Medicine check to remove a death save failure.

70. Wearing armor that gives Disadvantage on Stealth also gives Disadvantage on Dex saves, athletics, acrobatics or swimming checks

71. You may sacrifice one of your attack rolls to attempt to break a grapple, instead of having to spend your whole action.

72. If you will just make attack rolls on your turn and you pre-calculate your result before your turn, you may roll an extra dice of damage.

73. At character creation, you can decide if your Intimidation skill is Str or Cha based

74. At character creation, you can decide if your Athletics skill is Str or Dex based

75. When you Ready Action to attack on a trigger, you can ready a Multiattack assuming your character has it. This also means that monsters can do this…

76. Change Weapon Mastery -5/+10 rule to "Apply disadvantage to cause max damage"

77. Simple initiative: player group and enemy group. roll initiative for player order within player group. Group order is determined narratively.
