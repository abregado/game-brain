Level of realism

- Combat

- Damage. Permanent injury or magically sealed holes?

- Training. How long does levelling up take?

Level of Grit

- Do you expect that you can die in the story?

- Do you expect the GM to keep you alive?

- Can you respawn or are you happier making a new character?

- Can the players simply rest anywhere or do they need special locations?

Level of complexity

- Inventory management

- Sundry activities (shopping, packing, ect)

- Spell casting and reagents

Are player vs player actions ok?

Level of secrecy between players

- All knowledge is open

- Most things are open

- Few things are open

Level of secrecy between a player and the GM

Level of meta play that you expect

- What type of meta knowledge is ok to use?

- What type of meta knowledge is ok to share?

- Should the GM provide meta knowledge if it is relevant?

- How do you feel about min/maxing a character design to make it overpowered

Level of Mystery

- Do you expect to know the stats of a creature before you fight it?

- Do you expect to know the possible outcomes of every roll before you make it?

Bartering and Negotiations

- Are these game system based (roll for a discount) or based on roleplaying?

Mature Content

Gender in the game?

Would you be uncomfortable with any of these topics in the game happening to you

- Heterosexual erotica

- Other erotica

- Flirtacious roleplay

- Horror

- Moral quandaries

- Intimidation

- Manipulation

Are there any of these situations you would like to experience in the game (same list as above)

Other Mature Content?

- Sexual Assault

- Assault in general

- Racism, Prejudice

- Slavery

- Other

Level of fairness

- Are you ok with the GM changing the situation to be unfair if it is in the best interest of the story?

- Should the GM work to keep every character roughly equal in terms of involvement

- Do you expect all the encounters in the game to be solvable?

- Do the players always win?

Character Balancing

- Should the DM interfere with character power level?

- Via buffing or nerfing?

- Are you ok with a large power difference between the player characters? 2x 4x 8x?

Level of involvement

- Should the GM step in if a player is trying to hijack the story

Level of spotlighting

- Should every character be involved all the time?

- Are you only interested in what your character is doing?

- Should players respect each other’s time in the spotlight?

- Is everyone the hero of this story or just you?

Balance between social, planning and combat sessions?

- What do you expect the balance to be?

How strict should be GM be on players using races, classes or equipment that the player knows nothing about?

Do you expect to be able to design your own character?

- I want to read the rule book and design my own character

- I dont mind the GM designing it, but i want to be involved with the creation

- I want to choose the personality/class/race, but I trust the GM to come up with something fun that fits the story and other characters.

- Id be happy to choose a rough archetype and let the GM come up with something that fits the story

- As long as I can choose a name Im happy

Do you prefer ROLL play or roleplay?

Story or Mechanics first?

How heroic do you expect to be?

- A superhero

- Aragorn

- Bilbo

- A side character who does something important so the main characters can succeed

- A character somewhere in a big fight scene

How much impact on the game world do you expect to have?

- We will overthrow the Empire

- We will be mentioned in history books

- We will be the cause of something in history books but unnamed

- We will change someones/a group of peoples life

- Just trying to make a living, man

Name a movie or two that are similar to what you expect the level of seriousness to be like.

My House Rules

Do you take issue with any of these?

- You play a character with the same gender as you identify as in real life.

- Anything you say to each other OOC while in a populated area can be overheard by NPCs nearby.

- Do not dawdle during combat, try to think of your next turn before you are up.

- A dice is only valid if it lands COMPLETELY flat, otherwise reroll

- We don’t use Inspiration. Instead we use Flashbacks and Downtime buffs.

- I will often roll dice where you cannot see the result.

- When it would affect your ability to play a role, I will conceal information from you.

- Success does not mean you necessarily succeed completely.

- No Alcohol or drugs while playing

- Rolls only count after you are prompted to roll, another player or the gm needs to see the roll

- If you fail a check, only someone with higher base skill can attempt after you. Fumble means nobody can try after you.

Tips

- If you are bored, tell me. Murder Hobos are not tolerated!

- We are all making the story together

- The GM is not your enemy

- Dont use your devices during the game, you are here to play.

- When we play, we play. This is not time to be distracting or distracted

- Pay attention even when it is not your turn.

- Focus on communicating your Intention and your Approach separately if need be.

- Ask for suggestions if you are unsure what you want to do.

- As a fall back, think of what you yourself would do.

- Try to think about what your character DOESN’T know.

- We are creating the story together.

- If you are unsure, ask the DM what your character knows about a given topic.

Things for me to remember

- Don't roll if the action cannot fail, OR if there is no consequence to failure

- Explain Outcomes AND Consequences, if the Consequences are immediately visible.

- Let the BBEG get away with stuff, AND let the Players know about it.

- What is the Dramatic Question for the encounter?

- What is the conflict (where the desires of the opponent and players overlap)?
