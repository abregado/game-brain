## Potion of Healing

A small bottle of liquid, most likely red. It contains 3 doses in a single flask.

### Drink

**Use object:** You drink this potion to regain 1D4+1 hit points immediately, up to your hit point maximum.

### Apply

**Use object:** Feed it to a stabilized, incapacitated creature. This creature regains 1 hitpoint at the end of its next turn.



## Vial of Antitoxin 

A small vial of bitter tasting liquid.

### Drink

**Use Object:** A creature that drinks this vial of liquid gains advantage on saving throws against poison for 1 hour. It confers no benefit to undead or constructs.



# Rope and Pitons

## Flask of Holy Water 

A flask containing clear water which gives off a soft glow.

### Splash

**Use Object:** Make a ranged attack against a target within 20 ft, treating the holy water as an improvised weapon. If the target is a fiend or undead, it takes 2d6 radiant damage.



## Vial of Acid 

The cork on this vial looks blackened. Whatever this liquid is made of it is highly corrosive to organic matter and metal.

### Throw

**Use Object:** Make a ranged attack against a target within 20 ft, treating the vial as an improvised weapon. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 acid damage.

## Flask of Oil 

A flask of sticky flammable oil. Also could work as a lubricant, or for other mechanical purposes.

### Throw

**Use Object:** Use Object: Make a ranged attack against a target within 20 ft, treating the flask as an improvised weapon. For the next minute, whenever the target takes fire damage, it takes an additional 5 fire damage from the oil.

### Puddle

**Use object:** You can also pour a flask of oil on the ground to cover a 5-foot-square area, provided that the surface is level. If lit, the oil burns for 2 rounds and deals 5 fire damage to any creature that enters the area or ends its turn in the area.

A creature can only take damage from this item once per turn.



## Trap Bombs

A set of flasks or vials filled with a homemade concoction of explosive materials.

### Throw

**Use Object:** Make a ranged attack against a target within 20 ft, treating the bomb as an improvised weapon. The target takes 1d4 fire damage. Other creatures within 5ft must pass a Dexterity saving throw (DC 12) or take 1d4 thunder damage. All targets that take at least 1 damage gain the surprised condition if they have not acted in this combat yet.

### Craft

**Downtime:** If in a town with decent stocks of glass, oil mining supplies or a Thieves Guild, take one day to craft 1d4 bombs for a cost of 15g. Doing this may publically may attract curious glances from the locals.







## Ash Bombs

A set of flasks or vials filled with a homemade concoction of sticky black materials.

### Throw

**Use Object:** Make a ranged attack against a target within 20 ft, treating the bomb as an improvised weapon. On a hit, The target must pass a Constitution saving throw (DC 12) or become restrained and blinded.

Regardless if you hit, The target and all creatures within 5ft must pass a Constitution saving throw (DC 10) or become Blinded. Each affected creature can remove this condition if it passes a Constitution saving throw (DC 8) at the start of its turn.

### Craft

**Downtime:** If in a town with decent stocks of glass, oil, mining supplies or a Thieves Guild, take one day to craft 1d4 bombs for a cost of 15g. Doing this may publically may attract curious glances from the locals.



## Mirror Smoke Bombs

A set of flasks or vials filled with a homemade concoction of sticky black materials.

### Throw

**Use Object:** Target a square within 20 ft. A cloud of heavy smoke fills a cylinder with a 10ft radius, that is 2ft high. Creatures with size Small or Tiny which are standing in the smoke count as heavily obscured for the purposes of Hiding.

### Craft

**Downtime:** If in a town with decent stocks of glass, oil, mining supplies or a Thieves Guild, take one day to craft 1d4 bombs for a cost of 15g. Doing this may publically may attract curious glances from the locals.

# The Echo

### Deed - Property/Business in Phandalin

The current names listed on the deed are:

Halia Thornton 20%

Trisha Merryweather 80%

The deed is for a large tavern in Phandalin. It includes a modest kitchen, large bar, stage, storage cellar, a two story common room and a dorm room fit for 8 guests of Poor quality.

Currently the common room is in use by the taverns three employees: Spirit in the Sky, Artin Steelfist and Osah Naveen.

Nightly shows bring in locals, and travellers. Currently the tavern brings in around 6D6gp per tenday, after expenses. This is expected to increase as Phandalin becomes more well known, as service quality improves and if a cook was to be hired.

# Tidus 

### Contact - Fence

Location: **Neverwinter**

A human male, you guess that Tidus is his pseudonym. You have no idea who his buyers are but you doubt they are the cleanest of clientele.

**As a Downtime work week:** Select a magic item with base price up to 1000g to sell. After spending 25gp to spread rumors over 1 work week, you can roll a Charisma (Persuasion) check to see if Tidus will meet with you.

1-8: Tidus is not interested in this item.

9-20: Tidus will pay you 125% of the base price.

21+: Tidus will pay you 150% of the base price.

You may add +1 to your check for every item with base price over 300g that Tidus has purchased from you.
