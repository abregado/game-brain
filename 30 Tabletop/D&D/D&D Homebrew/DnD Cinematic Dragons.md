Ever dragon needs

- A cinematic ability to help it survive the first turn, preferably 6 rounds.

- An obvious mode transformation, in which its attack pattern changes

- Attacks which hit multiple targets unless the targets pai a cost on their turn.

- A big Cinematic attack for each attack mode.

- A threshold and methodology to run away when losing

- A series of environmental factors to help the dragon, each one stronger than the last. These are to give the dragon increasing change to survive over multiple encounters.

### Features

**Ice Walk (Passive):** The dragon can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.

**Icy Slumber (Environment):** A 60ft radius area around where the dragon sleeps is covered in very slippery ice. If a creature moves 15ft or more they must pass a DC 10 Acrobatics check or become Prone at the end of their movement. Creatures moving 30ft or more makes this test DC 20.

### Actions

**Dragons Fury**: Only when landed. Melee Weapon Attack against each target within 10ft.: +11 to hit, Hit: 12 slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage. 5 extra slashing damage to any creature which attacked the dragon’s head or tail with a melee attack.

**Tail spike:** Only when flying. Ranged weapon attack against a single melee combatant. +11 to hit, Hit: 10 piercing damage and target is Restrained if it is standing on the ground. Target tests at the start of each turn to remove status, DC 10 Strength.

**Frightful Presence:** Each creature of the dragon's choice that is within 120 feet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

### Reactions

**Ice Layer:** As a reaction when hit while sleeping. The dragon gains vulnerability to fire damage and 50 temporary hit points, both of which it loses at the start of its turn. The dragon is immediately alerted. Roll Initiative. This ability cannot be used again for 24 hours.

### Cinematic Actions

The dragon can take 1 legendary action per turn, and only at the end of another creature's turn. The dragon regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn. Each action can only be used once.

**Cold Breath:** Only when landed. The dragon exhales an icy blast in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw, taking 25(12d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Each affected target is also considered under the effect of a Slow spell until the end of their turn.

**Detect:** The dragon makes a Wisdom (Perception) check. If it succeeds in revealing a target, it may make a single attack against that target.

**Wing Attack:** Only while landed. The dragon beats its wings. Each creature within 10 feet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 13 bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. The dragon then takes off and flies up to half its flying speed.

**Ice wall:** Only while flying. The dragon creates an opaque wall of ice on a solid surface it can see within 120 feet of it. The wall can be up to 30 feet long, 30 feet high, and 1 foot thick. When the wall appears, each creature within its area is pushed 5 feet out of the wall's space; appearing on whichever side of the wall it wants. Each 10-foot section of the wall has AC 5, 30 hit points, vulnerability to fire damage, and immunity to acid, cold, necrotic, poison, and psychic damage. The dragon then lands somewhere within 10ft of the wall. The wall disappears when the dragon uses this lair action again or when the dragon dies.

**Freezing fog:** Only when outdoors. Fog fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. The fog spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. Each creature in the fog when it appears must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that ends its turn in the fog takes 10 (3d6) cold damage. A wind of at least 20 miles per hour disperses the fog. The fog otherwise lasts until the dragon uses this lair action again or until the dragon dies.

**Jagged Ice:** Only when indoors. Jagged ice shards fall from the ceiling, striking up to three creatures underneath that the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. The dragon makes one ranged attack roll (+7 to hit) against each target. On a hit, the target takes 10 (3d6) piercing damage.

**Whirling Wind**. A strong wind blows in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on the dragon (see the Dungeon Master's Guide for rules on strong wind). The dragon's flying is not affected by this wind, which lasts until the next time the dragon uses a lair action or until the dragon dies.

**Blinding Sleet.** Driving sleet falls in a 40-foot-high, 20-foot-radius cylinder centered on a point the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until initiative count 20 on the next round.
