Temperature

Roll d6 each round. On a 6 the temperature changes to freezing. When freezing starts, creatures in the wet area become restrained and must take a strength test as an action to free themselves. This lasts until a 6 is rolled again.

Roll d6 each round. On a 6 the temperature changes to boiling. All water surfaces evaporate and a scalding mist fills the room. Vision is limited to 10ft and everyone in the rool suffers D6 fire damage. This lasts until a 6 is rolled again.

Grasping vines. If a creature gains the Prone condition, they become Bound and Restrained, and must pass a Str test to escape on their turn.

Loose objects. Any strong wind will move the items around causing 2d6 slashing damage.

Loud. All creatures in the room are deafened.

Soundless room. All creatures in the room are silenced

Airless room. Con save every turn after the first or suffer a level of exhaustion.

Cold or Hot Room. Con save every turn after the first or suffer a level of exhaustion.

Horrific Effigy. All creatures in the room are frightened of one of the exits. Wis or Cha save at end of the turn to negate.

Chaos erosion. Strange shapes and eroded faces make it hard to focus. Creatures in this room have advantage on Stealth checks.

Gale force. At the start of its turn a creature must pass a save or be thrown back against one of the walls.

Wall spines. Squares next to a wall are difficult terrain. Falling prone or being moved involuntarily into these spaces causes D6 piercing damage.

Tar or Slime. Save at start of turn or be restrained. Standing from Prone costs 100% movement.

Hanging chains. Fumbling a melee weapon attack disarms you.

Power crystals. Creatures of a particular type can drain a crystal as a reaction to cast Shield or reroll a failed saving throw.

Narrow ledges. Test to move faster than half speed along edges.

Elemental Generators. Squares next to a generator are difficult terrain. Being struck or fumbling a melee weapon attack while in one of these squares causes damage.

Low ceiling. Attacks with strength based weapon attacks are made with disadvantage. Creatures that are large count this room as difficult terrain.
