Ability Card Characters

Attribute: Strength

You are strong, even for a hero. Regardless of your size, you always found it easy to move large objects. Play this card when you narrate an action that utilizes your mighty strength.

Attribute: Dexterity

You are nimble and quick, even for a Hero. Play this card when you narrate an action that utilizes your incredible agility, flexibility and accuracy.

Attribute: Constitution

You are incredibly tough, healthy, vigourous. You also find it easy to hold your concentration. Play this card when you narrate an action involving doing something that requires incredible stamina, endurance or vigor.

Attribute: Wisdom

Regardless of your age, you are wise. You intuitively make good choices and are well schooled in practical knowledge. Play this card when you narrate an action that draws on your deep wisdom and experience.

Attribute: Intelligence

Regardless of your age, you are smart. You find it easy to absorb new information and are well schooled in academic knowledge. Play this card when you narrate an action that draws on your keen mind.

Attribute: Charisma

Your personality is incredibly powerful. Regardless of what you ask, people seem more likely to do what you ask (or tell) them to do. You make friends easily, and diffuse tense situations almost passively. Play this card when you narrate an action involving social interaction.

Skill: Sneak

You are incredibly sneaky. Even heroes get spooked when you approach them from behind. Play this card when you narrate an action involving moving silently, or without being seen.

Skill: Sleight of Hand

You have nimble, and sticky, fingers. Rolling a dozen coins along your knuckles is not an issue for you. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves stealing, concealing or discretely moving an object; or when concealing the intent of your physical movements.

Skill: Nature Knowledge

Not only are you schooled in knowledge of nature, you seem to have a link to creatures and plants. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves recalling knowledge of nature, or dealing with plants and animals.

Skill: Persuasion

Whether through fancy speech or body language, you are able to convince people to do what you ask when your intentions are honest. Play this card when you narrate an action where you try to convince someone of something that is true.

Skill: Deception

Whether through vocal trickery or clever logic, your lies are incredibly hard to detect. Play this card when you narrate an action where you try to convince someone of something that is false.

Skill: Acrobatics

Sure footed and perfectly balanced, your body functions incredibly well at high speed and in the air. Play this card when you narrate an action where you need to land correctly, or make a risky gymnastic-like move.

Skill: Animal Handling

Your natural demeanor around animals causes them to be more calm around you. You also have extensive knowledge and training in handling animals. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves animals regardless of their emotional state.

Skill: Arcana Knowledge

You have studied ancient infernal texts or had experiences with magic beyond that which normal people can wield. Play this card when you recall knowledge about spells, eldritch symbols, traditions, magical creatures and items.

Skill: Athletics

You are trained in using your body to traverse terrain. Swimming, Climbing and Jumping come as easily to you as walking to others. You also know what kinds of movements will make your opponent tired. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves a great feat of endurance or physical skill.

Skill: History Knowledge

Either schooled or known from some arcane source, you have a good grasp of historical happenings. Folk lore, myth and political knowledge are included. Play this card when you recall knowledge about any of these things.

Skill: Insight

You have a keen eye for when someone is lying, and can read people's emotions. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves uncovering a deception, or when interacting socially with someone who might be lying.

Skill: Intimidation

Whether using body language or well placed insults, you know to intimidate people into doing what you say. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves convincing someone to do something you asked them to do.

Skill: Investigation

You are trained in searching for clues and making deductions based on them. Following information trails is especially easy for you. Play this card when you try to search an area for clues or research a topic of interest.

Skill: Medicine

You understand medicine and the inner workings of living creatures, both theoretically and practically. You have knowledge of diseases, ailments, conditions and therapeutics against them. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves interacting with or researching such things.

Skill: Perception

You are trained to notice small things, and are almost never ambushed. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves perceiving something that is difficult to perceive.

Skill: Performance

You are a trained actor or performer. Being on state and pretending to be someone you are not comes naturally to you. Play this card when you narrate an action that involves performing a convincing act.

Skill: Religion Knowledge

You have studied or have experiences with many religions of the world. You have a deep understanding of the common gods, and a light knowledge of rare ones. Play this card when narrating an action involving recalling facts about religion, or interacting socially with religious people.

Skill: Survival

You have been trained to survive in harsh environments, find food and track prey. Play this card when narrating an action utilizing this specialised training or knowledge.

Spell: Protection from Evil

You sprinkle a mixture of silver and holy water over a willing or helpless creature.

The creature is protected from evil aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fields and undead until the end of the scene. If the creature was already affected by evil, it may attempt to resist the effects again.

Spell: Prayer of Healing

You speak a long prayer to your god and six creatures within range are healed of their physical wounds. Non-magical healing is also made more effective with this prayer. Disease and poisons are not affected.

Ability: Knowledge of the Ages

You are touched by your god's presence, gaining some of its vast knowledge. You can use this card as a skill card of your choice.

Spell: Toll the Dead

You point at a creature and the sound of a loud bell can be heard. The creature's skin starts to be eaten away as it suffers a moderate amount of necrotic damage. The effect is very painful.

Spell: Bless

You speak a short prayer and sprinkle holy water on up to 3 creatures. For the rest of the scene, when spending a card, they can narrate a Super Risky action and still have a guaranteed outcome.

Spell: Heat Metal

Choose a manufactured metal object, such as a metal weapon or a suit of heavy or medium metal armor, that you can see within 60ft. You cause the object to glow red-hot. The effect lasts for 1 minute or until your Concentration ends.

Ability: Wildshape:

You turn into either a Swarm of Flying Beetles or a School of fish. The effect wears off at the end of the scene or when you decide to change back.

Spell: Speak with Animals

You speak a few magic words and draw a symbol in the air with your finger. Until the end of the scene, you can comprehend animal noises as if they were speaking your native language. Your words are understood by all animals that can hear you.

Spell: Moonflies

Speaking a few magic words and crushing a piece of opalstone in your hand, A cloud of silvery fireflies appears and creates pale light in a 5-foot radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within 120ft. Enemies in the beam suffer radiant damage, and any shapechangers hit with the beam revert to their original form. You can order the fireflies to follow a target when you summon them and they continue to do so until your Concentration breaks.

Spell: Healing Spirits

With a quiet plea and a touch, you place small swarms of spirit slugs on any number of targets. They can heal mild injuries and assist with medical procedures. The spirits stay and can be moved between targets until your Concentration breaks.

Spell: Pass without Trace

You spread the ashes of a spruce tree around your feet and speak a magic word. A veil of shadows and silence radiates from you, masking you and your companions from detection. Until the end of the scene, any character within 30ft of you can spend a card as if it was a Skill: Sneak card, and any Super Risky action including stealth narration is only considered Risky. Requires Concentration.

Spell: Warding Swarm

With a shout you summon a cloud of stinging glow beetles to emerge from your cloak and form a violent tornado around you. Until your concentration breaks the beetles continue circling. The following effects happen within 10 feet of you: Deafened by the sound of buzzing, All creatures have a hard time moving through the swarm, Gasses and smoke are swept away, Attacks are more risky.

Ability: Earth Aegis Teleport

You point at a friendly target you can see and an aura of glittering dust surrounds it. Until the end of the scene, the target only loses a card when having something bad happens to it 50% of the time. You can also teleport at any time to be adjacent to the target as long as you are both on the same stone surface.

Spell: Earthen Grasp

With a shout you raise and clench your fist. A large hand made of the same material as the floor rises up to envelop a target of your choice you can see within 30ft. The target is gripped by the hand, but you can also command the hand to try and crush the target. The hand stays in existence and can be commanded in later narrations as long as you maintain Concentration.

Spell: Shatter

A sudden loud ringing noise erupts from a point of your choice within 60ft. Every creature within 10ft of this point suffers thunder damage. Inanimate objects within the range suffer the damage as well.

Spell: Enlarge/Reduce

You swallow a small ball of iron and say a magic command. One effect of your choice occurs until the end of the scene, or until your Concentration breaks: \n Your size doubles in all dimensions, and your weight increases by 8. \n Your size halves in all dimensions and your weight is reduced to one eighth its normal.

Spell: Absorb Elements

In the round after being struck by an elemental damage or effect, you may play this card to narrate an effect using that type of elemental energy.

Spell: Mirror Image

Speaking a few magic words and drawing a symbol in the air, three perfect duplicates of yourself appear next to you. Until the end of the scene, the duplicates move with you, copying your actions in ways that make it impossible to tell which is which.

Spell: Disguise Self

With a flourish, you make yourself – including your clothing, armor, weapons, and other belongings on your person – look different. The effect lasts until the end of the scene or you decide to remove the effect. The changes wrought by this spell fail to hold up to physical inspection. Example, if someone touches an illusionary hat, their hand will go through.

Spell: Invisibility

You speak a word and smear some ash over your third eye, and become invisible. The effect remains until the end of the scene or your Concentration breaks. You can apply the ash to a willing creature to cause them to become invisible instead.

Spell: Nystul’s Magic Aura

You say a magic word and complete a magic trick involving a cloth of silk. One creature or object you touch with the silk cloth is changed for the next 24 hours so that divination magic does not work properly on it. Choose one effect: Mask: The object is masked from divination magic from one creature or spell source that you know about. Misinformation: The object is detectable, but you change its signature so it looks different.

Sample Scene challenges

You are deep inside the abandoned dwarven city. Well, you thought it was abandoned. You find yourself entering an enormous hall willed with 10 rows of pillars. In the centre of the hall are two statues that each hold a massive gemstone. Two Druegar are in the hall, one is patrolling between the statues and the east exit. The other one is watching his college from a balcony in front of the north exit. There is another exit to the west. You are hiding near the south exit.

In the kitchen of the dwarven city you find an Ogre sleeping on the dining table. A thick chain connects its ankle to a bolt in the wall, but you cannot see how long the chain is. The stove aflame and a large pot of something bland smelling is cooking away slowly. No doubt the Druegar chef will be back any minute. Then you see it, the body of the Halfling forward scout that the King sent ahead of you. His backpack is still attached. Perhaps it contains a map?

Of course the library is haunted. When is a library just empty? From your hiding spot on the balcony above, you see the ghost of a pain stricken dwarf (not Druegar) gliding back and forth along the corridor. Every minute, the ghost passes in front of the library. The whole time it seems to be looking for something as it mutters to itself. There are several other small rooms off this corridor, but you need to get access to the library, where the Lost Tomes of Durgeddin should be kept. That’s why we are here afterall.
