### Barthens Provisions

Barthen’s is the biggest trading post in Phandalin. Its shelves stock most ordinary goods and supplies, including backpacks, bedrolls, rope, and rations. The place is open from sunup to sundown.

**NPCs**

Elmar Barthen - Owner - [*Image*](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1BoqRpgylZgOQeZRar9FkhASv5QJttTfX)

| **Stock**                                                                         | **Cost**   |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Supplies from the PHB with value less than 25gp (50% change), no weapons or armor | Up to 25gp |

### Lionshield Coster

This building is owned by the Lionshields, a merchant company based in the city of Yartar, over a hundred miles to the east. They ship finished goods to Phandalin and other small settlements throughout the region, but this outpost has been hard hit by banditry.

**NPCs**

Linene Greywind - Owner - [*Image*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jr_dq5UTPYCzb0DkeA8w5Z-liCjk47Kl/view)

Phandalin Miner’s Exchange

The Miner’s Exchange is a trading post where local miners have their valuable finds weighed, measured, and paid out. In the absence of any local lord or authority, the exchange also serves as an unofficial records office, registering claims to various streams and excavations around the area.

**NPCs**

Halia Thornton - Owner - [*Image*](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1AJnsRtA577e_RdBDvqflvgVmShZOtE7P)

| **Service**                                                               | **Cost** |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Buys rare metals and gems                                                 | 70%      |
| Sells rare metals and gems                                                | 110%     |
| Currency conversion to Neverwinter coinage (thumbs, crowns, gears, plats) | 90%      |

### Shine of Luck

Phandalin’s only temple is a small shrine made of stones taken from the nearby ruins. It is dedicated to Tymora, goddess of luck and good fortune.

**NPCs**

Sister Pyria Garaele - Priestess of Tymora

| **Service**                           | **Cost** |
|---------------------------------------|----------|
| Healing (full hp/level of exhaustion) | 25gp     |
| Curse removal                         | 150gp    |
| Resurrection (Raise dead)             | 1500gp   |
