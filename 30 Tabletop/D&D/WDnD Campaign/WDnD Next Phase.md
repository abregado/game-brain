Divination Orb Mk1

The attuned user can choose to cast any divination spell as a ritual.

When casting a divination spell, the caster can choose to extend the casting time by 1 minute to gain one of the following effects:

- The duration of the spell is doubled

- Concentration is not necessary for maintaining this instance of the spell

- Add a charge to the orb of level equal to the spell slot expended

When the attuned user rolls for initiative, all charges in the orb are returned to them.

The Orb loses all charges when the user takes a short or long rest.

Divination Orb Mk2

When casting a divination spell, the caster can add a charge to the orb of level equal to the spell slot expended.

When the attuned user rolls for initiative, all charges in the orb are returned to them as Spell Slots.

The Orb loses all charges when the user takes a short or long rest.

Divination Orb Mk3

The attuned user can choose to cast any divination spell as a ritual.

When casting a Divination spell as a ritual, only add 3 minutes to the casting time.
