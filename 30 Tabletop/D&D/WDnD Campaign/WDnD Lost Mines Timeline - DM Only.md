Mawlock Shortsword

Finesse, Light, 1d6 damage.

After making a kill with the weapon. The weapon stores one charge of a type based on what was killed. Undead: Necrotic, Elemental: Based on Type, Celestial, Fiend or Fey: Radiant, Others: Force. You may not choose to forgo this ability.

Free action when hitting a target: Spend all the charges of one type to deal an additional 1d6 damage of that type for each charge spent. The weapon cannot use either of its abilities again until you complete a long rest.

Passive: If the weapon has 4 or more charges stored, it deals d6 damage to the wielder at the start of their round. During a short or long rest, the weapon loses all but one of its charges.

Lifeshackle Longsword

Versatile (d10), d8 slashing damage.

Passive: The weapon exists in the Prime material and Ethereal planes simultaneously. Attack and damage rolls made by an attuned wielder are made with +2. Attacks made against the wielder by ethereal undead can never be made with advantage.

Action: Impale a corpse with this weapon. While impaled, for the purposes of resurrection magic, a creature counts as having died less than a minute ago. After resurrection, the creature suffers two additional levels of exhaustion.

Mess Hall

From Overview point:

You are looking over a large room from a window approximately 60ft above the floor. The way continues down to some steps and you can see a strong wooden door where it probably exits into the room.

From Ground Level:

There are many long tables and benches in the room. Ancient stone bowls litter the floor and tables, many with dark stains of whatever food was in them 5 centuries before. There are many exits from the room, one to the north, a strong wooden door on a raised section of the room and two carved tunnel entrances leading south (explain which entrance the party arrived through). On a raised section a fancier stone table rests with several small kegs stacked on top. Here there are also signs of an ancient battle, with a few piles of bones remaining. Two chandeliers hang from sturdy chains, their candles having burnt away and creating waxy circles on the floor beneath them.

2 zombies per character are seated around one of the tables. Conveniently close to each other.

Rules: Falling from the 10ft ledges causes 1d6 bludgeoning damage. DC 12 Athletics to climb them.

Failure on any test or any combat here alerts the nearby undead and Ghouls come to inspect. 1 per character after 2 rounds.
