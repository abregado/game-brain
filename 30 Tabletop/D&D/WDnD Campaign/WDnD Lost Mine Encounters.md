## Vola Bone cube - necromancy

Passive: Your maximum Warlock spell tokens increased by 1.

Reaction, when you expend a Warlock spell token: roll a D6. On a 1, you immediately regain the spell token but suffer D6 psychic damage. On a 6 you also regain the spell token and the spell you cast is considered one level higher than usual.

Passive: After suffering psychic damage (from any source), you may not use the reaction ability of this item until you perform a long rest.

## Silixe - Cool Palm stone - transmutation

Action: Choose an open, non-magical flame within 60 feet. The flame is extinguished but explodes in a bright display before going out. Each creature within 10 feet of the target must pass a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or become blinded until the end of your next turn.

Each time the stone is used, it also heats up. When you use the stone, suffer 1D6 fire damage for each token the stone has, then add a number of tokens as directed by the GM.

During each long rest, you may remove one token from the stone, or add one by placing it in fire.

## Marat - Steel of Seeming

One Use

This item allows you to change the appearance of any number of creatures that you can see within 30ft.

You give each target you choose a new, illusory appearance. An unwilling target can make a Charisma saving throw, and if it succeeds, it is unaffected by this spell.

The spell disguises physical appearances as well as clothing, armor, weapons, and equipment. You can make each creature seem 1 foot shorter or taller and appear thin, fat, or in between. You can’t change a target’s body type, so you must choose a form that has the same basic arrangement of limbs. Otherwise, the extent of the illusion is up to you. The spell lasts for the duration, unless you use your action to dismiss it sooner.

The changes wrought by this spell fail to hold up to physical inspections. For example, if you use this spell to add a hat to a creature’s outfit, objects pass through the hat, and anyone who touches it would feel nothing or would feel the creature’s head and hair. If you use this spell to appear thinner then you are, the hand of someone who reaches out to touch you would bump into you while it was seemingly still in midair.

A creature can use its action to inspect one of the targets and make an Intelligence (Investigation) DC 15 check. If it succeeds, it becomes aware that this target is disguised. The illusion lasts for 8 hours.

## Marat - Vine with thorns - divination

Melds to a weapon of choice when attuning.

Passive: Weapon counts as magical. Targets wounded by this weapon must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be affected by the following condition for 1 hour.

Condition: The locations of the targets suffering from this condition are known to you, they cannot become hidden from you and if invisible, gain no benefit from that condition from you. If you are blinded, you can still see affected targets as if you were not. If you are Deafened, you can still hear affected targets as if you were not.

## Lumpik - Two gems in separate bags - illusion.

Action: Touch a willing creature with a gem. The gem dissolves and grants one of the following illusions to that target for 10 minutes.

Mask magic: Magical items the target is carrying no longer emit any magical aura. Magic users no longer show up as magical. Ongoing spell effects are undetectable.

New image: The targets alignment, creature type or race is changed to another of your choice (that you have seen).

Passive: When both stones are stored in the same pouch, the pouch (and everything inside) is invisible to everyone except the attuned user.

During a long rest, the gems reappear in their pouches.

## Return from Cragmaw

### Encounter 1: Peaceful camp in the Forest

**Location**: The Neverwinter wood, near Cragmaw Castle

The party is camped in the Neverwinter wood. They can hear the sound of Owlbears stalking the night, but due to their recent actions, they know they are safe for the night. An owl sits in a nearby tree and small arboreal creatures climb and play nearby.

Have the players recite a memory from the adventure thus far. Present this as talking about their favorite part and use this to tailor the coming encounters to their tastes.

Offer that players can tell a story about their characters back story as well, but make sure they keep it short.

### Encounter 2: Doppelganger Chase Skill Challenge

**Condition:** If the Doppelganger in Cragmaw did not make it out alive

**Location**: Neverwinter Wood

Check in which order the players took watch.

As the players awaken they spot a Drow spying on them and give chase. They have most of their equipment left at the camp. Only the last player to take watch has their full equipment. The chase begins just before dawn.

Successes needed: 3/3

Failures needed: 3

- Players can only use skills they have proficiency in

- Players can only use each skill once

- Based on story, give a DC of 12, 15 or 18 to each skill check

- A failure results in 2D6 damage

- After the first 3 successes, reduce difficulties by 1

- Each failure can be mitigated by another player by passing a DC15 Saving Throw of their choice. Remember each saving throw can only be used once and only if the character has proficiency. If the saving throw succeed and the story was made very quickly, the character can take the damage from the other failing player (adding an additional D6). The player should narrate the save.

If the party accumulates three successes then describe the chase moving to close range and the sun starts to come up. Track damage to the Doppelganger if any of the player stories seem like they would cause damage.

Stories:

- Arcana: Recognizing some of the magical plants of the forest, the player flanks the Doppelganger and chases it into a grove of grasping trees.

- Survival: Noticing an area of solid ground, the player gains on the target by several meters.

- Acrobatics: The player slides under a large log which had slowed the Doppelganger down.

- Animal Handling: The player imitates the call of some forest creatures which approach and disrupt the path of the Doppleganger.

- Athletics: The player jumps and throws a rock at the Doppelganger from a high vantage point.

### Encounter 3: Prison stagecoach

**Location**: The road to Phandalin (not the Tribor Trail)

**Enemies**: 3x Thug, 2x Scout, 1x Horse

A barred stagecoach approaches the characters on the road from Phandalin. There is a hooded figure shackled inside the stagecoach. A scout rides locked inside with the prisoner while the other rides shotgun. A Thug is driving the stagecoach.

When the driver sees the party, it accelerates and attempts to drive through them. Options:

- A spellcaster uses Charm Animal to stop the coach

- A party member passes an Animal Handling DC15 test while standing directly in the way of the wagon.

- The driver suffers more than 15 damage.

- The wheels or connection of the stagecoach suffer 15 damage.

Any party member who is still on the road must pass a Dexterity saving throw or suffer 3D6 damage (half on a successful save). The coach moves at 60ft per round.

After this happens, if the coach is still being controlled the players only have one more round to stop it. If it goes out of control, then it crashes on the side of the road. The guards each take D6 damage and combat begins.
