**Oghmas Blessing**

When you roll Initiative, each party member gains an Oghma token. If the blessing is still active at the start of the initiative order on following rounds, regain your Oghma token. Whenever any blessed party member takes any damage, all party members must use or discard their token, and the blessing is broken until the next Short or Long rest.

Oghma’s Foresight: As a free action, spend your Token to reroll any single die you rolled.

Oghma’s Hindsight: When you lose Oghmas Blessing, spend this token to gain 5 temporary hitpoints. You are immune to necrotic damage and the effects of any spells from the Necromantic school while you have these temporary hitpoints.

**Information Snippets**

Due to the oath, using words that are close to the topic will cause problems. The closer to the topic, the bigger the problem. For comparison, mentioning **The Chained God** will probably get you killed. Anything about who or what is a Chain, or the process of unleashing the Chained God will be severely punished. Mentioning names in a different context will probably still be quite uncomfortable now that you took the oath.

1)  The circles are Netherese (an ancient civilization that once controlled the whole world). They connect to each other in a closed network. There should be one for each element, and a fifth that is a central nexus. The smaller ones only connect to the nexus.

2)  Each circle has the passphrases for all other circles. They are written in the four dialects of Primordial. Each passphrase activates its matching circle to allow transit to the nexus, or from the nexus back to the circle of the matching element.

3)  The teleport circles do not lead into the nodes, but do transmit elemental energies between them. If elemental chaos was to leak to the material plane, it would be from the nexus circle.

4)  The more powerful an elemental, the more bound it is to the elemental plane. It is thought impossible for Archomentals to cross over to the Material plane via a direct crossing or portal.The names of the four Chains are **Olhydra**, **Ogremoch**, **Imix** and **Yan See Bin**

5)  A planar node is a small demi-plane that exists simultaneously on two planes of existence. Elemental nodes are planar nodes that are on the elemental plane and the material plane. Objects in the node are considered to be on both planes at once, and thus circumvent some of the cosmic rules.

6)  At the centre of a node is generally an object which anchors it to the material plane. This object is, paradoxically, the closest point to the other plane the node exists on. Entering from the material plane probably means entering from the point farthest to the node’s anchor.

7)  A key component to building a Planar Compass is a substance which anchors it to the material plane. This means that an area around the compass is also always slightly in the material plane, making the effects of other planes less dangerous to travellers.

8)  On the elemental plane of fire the Archomental **Imix** resides on the Plain of Burnt Dreams inside the Blazing Sea region. Its fortress is known as the Temple of Ultimate Consumption, a pyramid of black obsidian glass.

9)  **Imix** is also mentioned in an Yuan-ti bio-engineering text. It is said that a patron of that name sent emissaries from the outer planes to help with building an army. The author seemed to be under the impression Imix is some kind of ruler or god, although does not dwell on the details.

10) On the circles, written in Primordial Aurun, is a passphrase. It mentions an archomental called **Yan See Bin**. The only historical entry you can find with this name is an abyssal text which says a person with the same name was summoned to the Throne of Blood in the outer plane of Gehenna. The events that took place there were not mentioned, but the Summoners name was Amelyssan.

11) The node entrances will likely appear at the highest concentration of all elemental energies, so could be quite close together.

12) The Oghmarites tell you that there are many oath takers out in the world, and several organisations that keep an eye on events related to the **Chained God**. **The Chained God** has ways to influence the world, albeit slowly, and is always planning to break free. The last recorded attempt which was proven to be linked was over 2000 years ago.

13) The names of the four Chains are **Olhydra**, **Ogremoch**, **Imix** and **Yan See Bin**

The story

Within the vast expanse of the elemental plane, one shall find four demi planes, each devoted to a singular element. Water, Fire, Earth, and Air hold sway within their respective domains. Yet, at the conjunction of these demi planes, lies the tumultuous core known as the elemental chaos.

Upon this elemental chaos, the material plane and its reflections, the shadowfell and feywild, find their perch. However, do not mistake this arrangement as mere coincidence. It was a meticulously crafted design, intended to serve as a formidable barrier, a seal that imprisons the Chained God.

Four chains, embodiments of Water, Fire, Earth, and Air, firmly bind this deity. Each chain takes the form of an archomental, possessed by a selfish nature and an insatiable desire to subvert the material plane into their elemental dominion.

Beware, for should all four chains be unleashed upon the material realm simultaneously, it would break under the strain, and the seal that confines the Chained God would crumble. Yet, it is imperative that we exercise caution, for the annihilation of these chains would yield a similar outcome.

Take heed, for even the release of a solitary chain harbours the power to bring forth annihilation upon the material realm. While the seal that binds the Chained God would remain intact, the consequences for mortals would be dire. Such an unleashed chain, if left to roam unchecked, would unleash unparalleled devastation upon civilization, reducing cities to dust and extinguishing the flame of progress.

To safeguard against the allure of interference, the other gods, in their wisdom, chose to seal themselves away behind the ethereal plane. This ethereal barrier stands resolute, serving as a boundary that separates the inner and outer planes. It is a deliberate act of self-restraint, limiting their powers upon the material realm, ensuring that none shall succumb to the temptation to meddle with the sacred seal.

**Oblation**: Targets take any Saving throws with disadvantage.

You collapse your hands around an object, causing it to vanish from your present time. The object, which only you can be holding and can weigh no more than 5 pounds, is magically transported between 1 and 7 days into the future. You choose the precise destination time when sending the item.

When the time expires, the object reappears in the exact location it was when you cast the spell. If the space is occupied by a creature or object, the target object instead appears in the closest unoccupied space.

Every time you retrieve an item using this spell, roll a D6. On a 6, consult the DM.

## Kindle

Kindle WIP spell

Targets inside the cube:

1.  Bubbles form in the cube causing everything they touch to become weightless. Each creature in the cube must pass a Dexterity saving throw or have its Walk speed reduced to 0ft, although they gain a Fly speed of 5ft. The effect ends when the cube changes, or a creature leaves the cube. When the effect ends on a creature they gain the Prone condition as they slam into the ground.

2.  You summon a flock of friendly, animated blindfolds. Each creature in the cube must pass a Strength saving throw or gain the Blinded condition until the start of your next turn. Blinded creatures move randomly.

3.  The cube fills with a stinky blue gas. Creatures inside the cube must pass a Constitution saving throw or be Poisoned until the start of your next turn as they vomit violently. Creatures poisoned in this way also lose their Reaction.

4.  You summon hundreds of bonbons with fairy wings: Creatures inside the cube must pass a Wisdom saving throw or become Charmed by you until the start of your next turn. Creatures charmed in this way must try to stay inside the cube on their turn.

5.  A sparkling laughing gas fills the cube. Creatures inside the cube must pass a Charisma saving throw or be Incapacitated until the start of your next turn and move randomly on their next turn.

6.  Everything in the cube gets covered in a sticky substance. Unless a creature has resistance to fire damage, it gains vulnerability to fire damage. The effect wears off if they leave the cube.

7.  Tiny grey clouds cover the top of the cube, raining down on the occupants. Unless a creature has resistance to lightning damage, it gains vulnerability to lightning damage. The effect wears off if they leave the cube.

8.  The ears of every creature in the cube grow to triple size. Unless a creature has resistance to thunder damage, it gains vulnerability to thunder damage. They also gain advantage on Perception checks to hear. The effect wears off if they leave the cube.

9.  The cube shrinks 5ft and you choose if you want to roll again. Creatures inside the cube must rest again if necessary.

10. The cube grows 5ft and the effect stays the same. Creatures inside the cube must rest again if necessary.

## Revenants Promise

Gain proficiency on Survival checks to track your prey and the revenant who gave you this curse. You always know in which direction and how far away your prey is.

At the start of each short or long rest ask the dungeon master how far away your prey is. If it is further than the last time you took a rest, you must pass a Concentration check otherwise only gain one benefit of the rest. This Concentration check starts at DC 10, but increases by 5 for each day spent further away from your prey.

If you die while under this curse, you are resurrected instantly, change to the Undead type, gain the Ethereal trait and are bound to your remains, unable to move further than 50ft from them.
