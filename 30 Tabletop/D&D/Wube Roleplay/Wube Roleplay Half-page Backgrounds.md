## Mercenary

### Background 

You are trained as a solder and travelled with a mercenary company for several years. Recently you became disillusioned with your fellow soldiers who seem to enjoy their authority at the expense of the people they’re paid to protect. You then disobeyed a direct order and followed your conscience, resulting in your temporary suspension from active duty.

### Personal Goal 

News has it that there are many gangs and bandits taking advantage of commoners out on the fringe. As a person of courage and principle, you feel that stopping such villainy is a worthy goal.

### Personality trait

| I've spent so long as a soldier that I have little practical experience dealing with civilians. | I always return an insult, even if it was slight. |

## Noble

### Background

Your family is no stranger to wealth, power and privilege. In the glory days, your parents were a count and countess of a large estate. After a natural disaster 30 years ago, the estate was destroyed. You family moved to their small but comfortable townhouse in the city. As an adult, you stand to inherit a meaningless title and an empty piece of wilderness.

### Personal Goal

You have heard that villages on the fringe are in need of a civilized influence, someone to take the reigns and bring law and order. Someone like you.

### Personality trait

| I've enjoyed fine food, drink, and high society among the noble elite. Rough living grates on me. | Flattery is my preferred trick for getting what I want. |

## Scoundrel 

### Background

You grew up in a small village with your family. When a small gang of mercenaries started hanging out in your village, instead of being bullied like the rest of the citizens, you offered to help them with small jobs. Doing so bought you and your family protection, and also some decent earnings. Recently, something changed because the mercenaries tried to have you killed.

### Personal goal

The ruffians won't be expecting you to return with help so you hitchhiked to the city to look for people to help you get your revenge.

### Personality trait

| I keep multiple holy symbols on me and invoke whatever deity might come in useful at any given moment. | I never pass up a friendly wager. |

## Researcher

### Background

You have spent your life dedicated to academic pursuits. There was never much reason to lay your studies aside until recently, when ancient magic items, long dormant, began to reawaken. It is time for you to journey to the fringes to bring your knowledge to the rest of the world.

### Personal goal

Adventurers now report that out in the wilderness, ancient powers have begun to stir. Pursuit and acquisition of knowledge is for the benefit of all, and the only truly worthy goal. You will go into the world, discover lost knowledge, and use that knowledge for the good of all.

### Personality trait

| I see omens in every event and action. The gods try to speak to us, we just need to listen. | I am intolerant of other faiths and condemn the worship of other gods. |

## Aspiring hero

### Background

Your family used to live a comfortable life in a frontier town. Then disaster struck and everyone was forced to flee back to the city. You spent the early part of your life working hard as a refugee in a city filled with refugees. In ancient times, this sort of existence was impossible because of the great heroes who protected the realm. You know you are destined to be such a hero one day.

### Personal Goal

On the fringes the people need a symbol and protector, one that looks out for them and drives off dangerous forces. Once you become such a person, you hope to liberate your home village from the dark force which has taken it over.

### Personality trait

| I connect everything that happens to me to a grand cosmic plan. | I always to listen to every side of an argument before I make my own judgment. |

## Hermit

### Background

You grew up in the wilderness around some small villages on the fringe. You took everything you need to survive from the forest, so you know your way around natural places. When you needed something that the wilderness could not provide, you earnt some coin doing manual labour for farmers and peasants.

### Personal Goal

Recently, a lot of bandits, monsters and thugs have started visiting the villages in your area. Normally you would stay out of any trouble and let history run its course, but some of the innocents who were kind to you were recently attacked. Inaction and solitude is no longer an option.

### Personality trait

| I am incredibly slow to trust. Those who seem the fairest often have the most to hide. | I am utterly serene, even in the face of disaster. |
