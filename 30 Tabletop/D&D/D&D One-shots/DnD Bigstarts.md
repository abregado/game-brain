Adventures find themselves in a small room with a closed door in front of them. Behind them is the door they came through, which leads to another small room.

The players dont know it yet, but their characters are in prison. As they attempt to open the door they will discover this. If any of them try to use their equipment just say, you dont have that item with you. If any of them ask what they are wearing, say “just the rags they put on you”. If anyone tries to use spells, say, “there is an antimagic field”.

After they try something to open the door, the room starts to fill with smoke (from the top).

The door is locked with runes and a mechanical lock. If they think of something and roll really high, have the door open. Otherwise wait until the last second and have the door open by itself.

If they go back the way they came, explain that this is the room where they have been sleeping on hay for the last few days. A thorough search of the room reveals nothing.

As the heroes emerge from the prison cell, there is smoke up to their waists. Tell them that the Halfling you hired, Snowbrow Topnot was supposed to disable the cell doors but clearly didnt think about the consequences of his technique. Now that you have infiltrated the Barons castle, you need to find the macguffin and then get out. Meeting up with Topnot was also in the plan, but now you are thinking about leaving that bit out.
