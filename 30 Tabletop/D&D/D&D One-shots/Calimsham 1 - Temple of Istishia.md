What must they learn

Elemental forces are not evil, but do not care about mortal lives.

The Elemental Chaos is not pure destruction, but pure creation.

The Prime Material plane is a barrier between planes to slow the spread of Pure Destruction.

The Elder Eye corrupts Elemental forces to work them against Celestials.

Setup and Preparation

Print out all the characters sheets, cards and tokens.

Prepare dice.

Read the introduction and Scene 1 at least before the players arrive.

Prepare images of characters, locations and enemies if you wish.

Introduction and Character creation

The pasha of a guild sent the heroes to investigate a drop in taxes from a trade operation in a small outpost.

Hand out the Background cards, dealing at least 2 to each player. Also place the pile of character images in the centre of the table.

Ask each player to select one of the backgrounds and return the others to you secretly.

Once every player has chosen an image and background then hand out the Class sheets.

Have each player select one of the Class Specialities on the sheets.

Deal out the Skill proficiency cards in a Drafting style. Each player selects one and then passes the rest on to the person on their left.

Hand out equipment cards based on the Characters.

Begin!

Scene 1 Trading Post

Welcome to Wakhami Oasis, a small trading outpost in the Calim Desert. The Marketplace surrounds a large but shallow basin filled with water, itself dominated by a Fountain depicting Waukeen, the Goddess of Trade. People crowd around stalls already selling their goods while new traders are arriving regularly, their pack animals causing all sorts of chaos.

You begin looking for the Registrar but are interrupted by cries of fear ringing out from near the Fountain. The crowd moves away allowing you to see the cause of distress. A man stands in the fountain lashing at two citizens with twin whips. They seem injured but courageously stand against the attacker, keeping themselves between him and the other citizens. As you rush over, you realise that these whips are actually spike covered tentacles which disappear into the man's sleeves. Something foul is at play here. Roll Initiative!

The heroes do not have to get involved if they don’t want to. Just read out the events below so they know how it turns out.

Combat ensues roll Initiative for the Attacker. The two brave citizens act on the same Initiative count, but just take the Dodge action.

Start of Round 2: One of the defenders retreats, clutching their arm where a whip struck. You can see now that the attacker has some strange growths on their face. The other brave citizen moves into the fountain to protect the first.

Start of Round 3: Brave defender 2 takes a hit from the tentacles and falls to the ground with a splash, red liquid seeps into the water.

Start of Round 4: Another citizen leaves the crowd to try and drag the wounded defender out of the fountain. The attacker tries to grapple this new target.

Start of Round 5: The attacker uses the Dash action to get away from the scene, carrying the citizen with them if their grapple was successful. Characters nearby can take Attacks of Opportunity if they wish.

Encounter effect - Terrified Mob: Whenever a creature tries to move they must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity saving throw (DC 10) or suffer half movement until their next turn. The attacker is immune to this effect.

Resolution: The encounter is over if the attacker reaches zero hit points, or if they escape. Be sure to narrate the resolution in a dramatic way. To involve the players more, you can also have the player who dealt the killing blow narrate the coup-de-gras.

Scene 2 Post battle investigation:

The marketplace is silent for a moment, people looking at each other for someone who they can glean some security from. Their gazes eventually fall on the party. The wounded citizens have been moved to an open space, several other citizens trying to cordon off an area while others call loudly for a Healer.

If the Round 3 event occurred:

“A dark cloud of blood is slowly spreading across the fountain from where the injured citizens were. Flecks of gold shimmer in the blood”

If the attacker escaped:

“The crowd of onlookers parted as the attacker fled, but closed again quickly. One of you could track it through the crowd, but it seems unlikely you will catch it again.”

If the attacker died:

“The corpse of the attacker lies on the ground, a pool of blood with golden flecks slowly seeming onto the flagstones. The corpse might warrant a closer, but careful inspection.”

Let the adventurers decide who deals with each event. Multiple characters can choose the same event.

**Attackers corpse, if dead:**

“You inspect the corpse and find a Religious pendant on a necklace in a pocket. It is made of silver and far too expensive for a person of this status. The corpse itself has many vein like growths criss crossing its skin, each coated with gold flecked scabs, which ooze when moved.”

Take a Wisdom/Intelligence (Investigation) check. On a 10+ read:

“A closer inspection of the pendant, there are some golden flecks around the sharper edges.”

**Track the attacker, if escaped:**

Take a Wisdom (Survival) check. On a 10+ read:

“You follow the path of the attacker through the crowd. Drops of blood and marks on the flagstones lead you to the edge of the oasis. The attacker fled over the ring wall and into the desert. Something shining in the sand attracts your eye. You dig a Religious pendant out of the sand and take it with you, the attacker must have dropped it.”

Or if the roll is less than 10:

“You follow the path of the attacker through the crowd. Drops of blood and marks on the flagstones lead you to the edge of the oasis. The attacker fled over the ring wall and into the desert.”

**Red stains in the fountain:**

Intelligence (Arcana) check:

“Inspecting the blood in the water, you realise that it is spreading out in a very unnatural way. As the blood becomes thinner you can see that it is filled with golden flecks which are forming long strands in the water.”

On a 10+ also read:

“You speculate what you are looking at is the Lesser Abyssal Plague. If that is true, then it is highly contagious.”

Each character that was wounded by the attacker, or suffered the Prone condition while in the fountain must pass a Constitution saving throw (DC 13) or be infected with a Disease.

**Injured citizens:**

Wisdom/Intelligence (Medicine) Check

“Checking over the wounded citizens you can already see that the wounds are sealing, but that red vein like growths are spreading across the surrounding skin. Around the original wounds, golden scabs have encrusted the broken skin. This is beyond your skills as a healer.”

On a 10+ also read:

One of the wounded citizens has similar wounds on other parts of their body, a much more advanced case. When you ask, the citizen replies:

“Yes, I got into a fight with that guy at the bar last night. To start with that is why I thought he attacked me again today! He was from a caravan that packed up and left this morning.”

**Improvised activity:**

If a player decides to do something different, have them explain first the intent of their action to you. The intent is the intended outcome. Then ask how they propose to achieve that, and assign an Attribute/Skill combination to the test.

Have the player make the check and then narrate an outcome. If they roll really well, 15+, then have the event go basically as they planned it. As long as they roll more than 8, let them have an outcome that is a lesser version of what they were expecting. If the player suggests something outrageous and knows it, then having a high roll means they fail but get away with trying. A bad roll here should mean the character is punished, just a little bit, for trying something outlandish.

Just remember three things:

- You as Dungeon master narrate the outcome, not the player

- The Dungeon master has the final say.

- A player asking for something you are not prepared for is not a punishable offense.

Scene 3: The Registrar

On the other side of the fountain, a well dressed Registrar appears from the crowd. They are flanked by two heavily armed guards. He sees you all and approaches, making a deep bow of respect in greeting.

This is a roleplay encounter. If you have a character from a TV show, movie or book that you think would fit as the Registrar, then use that as a personality basis instead. As long the character is interested in looking good politically, and is generally friendly toward the characters, the scene will work. If this is your first roleplay as Dungeon Master, then it is suggested to change the characters gender to match your own.

Registrar Kwalid el Bhalar

Physical Description: A fit but bulky person wearing the correct robes of a Guild Registrar

Personality: Kwalid is a fair person who is good at his job. However he is a little too interested in making sure people notice when he does a nice thing. He will react in a positive manner to characters who visibly show their office or occupation, especially those from the guilds. Otherwise he will be pleasant but neutral.

If insulted or threatened, he will react as if it was a joke and continue the conversion along his own lines.

If pushed very far, he will remind the characters loudly that he is an appointed official of a high ranking guild and this sort of behavior will not be tolerated.

In the worst case, he uses his guards to block the characters while he leaves.

Goals:

- Get a recount of the event from the characters.

- Find out what the characters are doing here, and who they are working for.

- Convince the characters to continue their investigations to find the cause of the attack.

Dungeon Master Goal:

- Get the characters to go to either the Caravan or to the Galeb Duhr.

- Figure out if the players are interested more in Exploration, Combat or Roleplay.

- Figure out if the players are interested in continuing immediately, later or never.

Information available:

- The God Istishia is a Primordial water god, associated with Purification.

- There is a shine to the god in the desert somewhere, although he doesn’t know where.

- The Caravan which the attacker came from left this morning, now several hours ago, but the characters.

- There is some information in the Library about Lesser Abyssal Plague
