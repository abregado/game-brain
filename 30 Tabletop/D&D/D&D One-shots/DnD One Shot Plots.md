Why fight/encounter a \[monster\] ?:

Aboleth

Water has become polluted making people sick. Heroes track the source to a group of fish people and eventually to the Aboleth. Fish guy priest provides exposition before the boss fight.

Ankheg/Manticore/Cockatrice

The creatures have inhabited an area they normally would avoid.

Banshee

The heroes require information about something that happened or someplace that existed in a given area a long time ago. Banshees constantly relive their previous lives so can recall ancient events with great accuracy.

Nothic

The heroes require knowledge, and nothics have the ability to draw out secrets. They are interested in trading knowledge.

Chull/Spectator/Flameskull

It stands guard over a powerful magic item. Aeons ago its aboleth/mage/lich masters gave it a command to protect this place. The area should have sufficient liquid for the Chull to hide in, or height for a spectator/flameskull to fly in..

Vrock

The actual big bad is weak, but has extorted a favour from a powerful demon. That demon has sent this Vrock to fulfil its debt.

Drider

Driders rarely work with Drow. Driders are intelligent. They make good Big Bads when you want a spidery theme. They are often trying to win favour with Lolth by doing evil deeds.

Ettin, Cyclops

Living solitarily in a remote area, these creatures can be called upon to help solve a problem which might threaten their home.

Drow

A scouting party is encountered which leads the heroes to discover a slave raid is coming. But the raid is only a cover event for a larger plot move by some other powerful entity.

Galeb Dhur

The heroes require information or an item collected by a Galeb Dhur council.

Genies/Azer/Elemental planes denizens

A genie is bound to someone on the Material plane and is here to carry out the pact made. Genies hate to be enslaved, and love to enslave, so this could be a hierarchy of slavery as the genie forces those bound to it to help fulfil its pact.

Gibbering Mouther/Golem

As a pet or weapon of a mage.

Hag Coven

A spate of tragic events see the heroes hired by a ruler to investigate. After some time the heroes gather evidence that the rulers own daughters are responsible. A ruler has three daughters who shun suitors and are all interested in ruling. Turns out the three are hags who replaced the ruler’s daughters and placed him under a curse to not be able to tell. Convincing him is the real challenge.

Lycanthrope

A rich mine has been performing badly of late and the heroes have been sent to find out why. Two lycans have moved in and chased away the miners. When the miners attacked, several of them were killed. It turns out that the lycans are a couple who want the mine so they can lock themselves away during every full moon, having moved from their previous lair which was not as safe for others.

Medusa

A classic tale of making a bargain for eternal youth and beauty eventually goes wrong. The heroes might be involved before the pact is made, while the pact maker is turning or long after the medusa emerges.

Mind Flayer/Illithid

The Illithid has been harvesting humans for its experiments, accidentally drawing attention to itself.

Revenant

The party is hired to track down and kill a murderer. They find this very easy and they kill the Revenant. They think it is over until two days later when the murders continue. Why are people from this group being targeted? Who paid the heroes to stop the murders?

Rust Monster

The heroes track a group of Rust Monsters back to their lair where they discover that they have been feeding on an ancient war machine. A more powerful foe is waiting for the rust monsters to eat away all of the metals to reveal the animantine frame of the war machine.

Shambling Mound

The heroes are investigating a gnomish ruin which includes a lightning rod. Upon inspection the lightning rod is not connected to anything. A shambling mound makes its nest at the base of the rod to consume the energy it captures.
