## Overview and Synopsis

**DM Eyes only, SPOILERS**

The heroes from Faerun are transported unwittingly to Tamriel. They arrive in a Mythic Dawn temple on the border between Skyrim and Morrowind. After discovering a deactivated portal and that the temple is dedicated to a Daedric Prince, Mehrunes Dagon, they set out to find the Sigil Stone needed to reactivate the portal and get home.

Making their way to the Dunmeth pass in the Velothi Mountains, they meet a traveller who lets them know the nearest two cities are Blacklight and Wildhelm.

Upon arriving in one of these cities, the Oblivion Crisis begins. A gate opens in the city and many Daedra begin to invade from Oblivion. The heroes fight the Daedra but when a powerful enemy appears, they are taken to a safe house by an NPC. This NPC explains that they need to go through a gate, into Oblivion to retrieve a Sigil Stone.

The heroes attack or sneak up to the gate, dealing with many Daedra on their way. As they approach, the gate closes and a Tamrillian hero appears, smashing the Sigil Stone.

Travelling to the other city, our heroes plot a way to be close to the portal when it opens, diving through right as the fighting starts. In Oblivion they make their way to the Sigil Stone but it is protected by a powerful Daedra and they must fight. During the battle, Mehrunes Dagon appears and seems to assist the heroes in “making the fight a fair one”.

They escape with the Sigil Stone, return to the temple they started in, and get back to Faerun.

## Daedra in Faerun

**Style: Hotstart Combat**

The PCs find themselves in the depths of a dungeon, in a room with concentric circles on the floor.

As they investigate the circle, several creatures attack. Daedra!

As the Daedra or the PCs are defeated, blood fills the channels and the gate sends them away.

## Arrival

**Style: Freeform exploration, no combat**

PCs arrive in the Mythic Dawn temple in Velothi Mountains.

Exploring it they find a deactivated Oblivion gate, which looks similar to the one they found in Faerun.

They have with them a Planar macguffin which helps them.

They need to find a Sigil Stone to open the gate.

After exploring the temple they leave to find people.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- The gate will take them home

- The gate needs a Sigil Stone.

- Soul gems charge up when you kill Daedra

**Extra stuff to teach them here:**

- The Shrine is dedicated to Mehrunes Dagon

## NPC Merchant - Optional

**Style: NPC Roleplay**

Coming down the mountain they find their way to Dunmeth pass and Refugees Rest and meet a trader who gives them directions to both Blacklight and Wildhelm.

The macguffin points one way then the other if consulted.

They can pick which one to go to.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- Sigil Stones are not something you can buy

- Location of the two nearest cities.

**Extra stuff to teach them here:**

- When and where they are.

## First City

**Style: Descriptive Monologue, no interaction**

Party arrives, and has a descriptive montage of the city they chose.

The montage ends with them in the centre of the city.

They follow the macguffin to where the readings are the strongest.

Then the attack begins.

## Oblivion Crisis begins

**Style: Combat**

A brief encounter with some more Daedra, this time stronger ones. The fight takes place in a position where the PCs get a view (but not access) to a portal that begins opening. Many Daedra begin pouring out, forcing a retreat of the local guards. Make sure to mention how many hitpoints the guards have (foreshadowing)

When you think they have had enough, have a more powerful Daedra appear. Something they have not seen yet, and cannot defeat safely. It kills a bunch of guards in one attack (foreshadowing payoff) if the PCs don’t take other hints you have given them.

An NPC appears and takes them to a safe place.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- There is a functional portal in the city.

- Some Daedra are too powerful for them.

## Safehouse

**Style: NPC Roleplay and Short Rest**

They are accosted by an NPC who takes them to a safe place. The NPC can answer questions about where they are and what is happening.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- Sigil Stones keep the gates open, but are on the other side of the portal. IMPORTANT!

- The portal in the town leads to Oblivion, not to Faerun.

- The NPC is a messenger from another city. This is happening all over the continent.

- The NPC can help them get to the portal but then needs to go to the palace to tell the leaders what is going on.

**Extra stuff to teach them here:**

- Daedra lore

- Lore about local defence forces, based on which city they are in.

## City 1 Oblivion Gate

**Style: Moving mini Combats**

This is a power fantasy fight. The players should be able to defeat the Daedra by employing the weaknesses they learned in the two previous fights, plus they will have improved in power due to charging Soul Gems. let them kill heaps of Daedra and save lots of city guards in the process.

Each set of enemies is in a different location. Give an environmental effect to each mini combat. Combats should be 2-3 rounds max, as the players are powered up a bit now.

Each time they defeat some Daedra, they get closer to the open gate. When they are within a distance where they could run to the gate, have it collapse. A lone figure stands where the gate was and using some Magika, destroys the Sigil Stone.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- The Oblivion gates are being closed by heroes.

- How to close an Oblivion Gate

- Where to find the Sigil Stones on the Oblivion plane after going through a Gate.

- The other town they know of has not yet been attacked but will be.

## City 2

**Style: Free form planning, Montage with Flashbacks.**

Skip to arriving at the next town. Play up the differences between the two cities. One is filled with Norns and is snowy, the other full of Dunmer and is a cold coastal city.

The heroes have a good vantage point of the area where the portal will open. Get the players to explain how the PCs get to the portal and through as it opens. Throw challenges at them, and let them use Flashbacks to solve them.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- Nothing, this is where they use their knowledge

## Oblivion Arrival - Optional

**Location: Some creepy tower in the Deadlands, Oblivion**

**Style: Timed Exploration, Stealth gameplay**

Introduce and describe the plane of Oblivion. I recommend having them appear somewhere high up where they get a good view. Their destination is visible on another tower nearby (or an arena on the ground to foreshadow the boss fight), so they will need to go down and then up again (or find some other way across).

The PCs can only survive here for so long. Make sure they know it so they don’t dawdle.

Obstacles include: Door/Key, Trap, Puzzle.  
Fights should be kept cinematically difficult, or super hard but easy to avoid. Stealth gameplay could work well here.

Reaching the Sigil Stonetriggers the boss fight and removing it teleports the group back to Nirn..

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- Immediately see where the Sigil Stone is and how to get there.

## Boss Fight

**Location: Top of a tower in the Deadlands, Oblivion**

**Style: Combat**

They should have to fight to get the Sigil Stone. Drop hints here that Mehrunes Dagon actually wants them to get away with the stone, so helps them win against his own foes (also to play up Dagons ancient role as Daedric prince of Hope). I guess if the SFX budget allows, Dagon could actually be towering over the fight watching it personally.

If the PCs remove the Sigil Stone, they are teleported back to Nirn.

**Things the PCs need to learn:**

- Mehrunes Dagon is… rooting for them?

## Heading back to the first Temple -Optional

**Location: Dwemer tunnels**

**Style: Map based Dungeon crawl**

The Pass is blocked by a massive Daedra (Siege Crawler?) so the PCs need to use an alternate way back.

Length is longer if everything else went quicker.  
Maybe this part is not even needed.

## Epilogue

**Style: Descriptive scenes, no interaction**

Describe the PCs return to Faerun.

Cutscene to show the macguffin was lost in Tamriel. It is lying on the floor of the Temple, showing a low value (planar boundaries are much lower here now).

Cutscene to show the Sigil Stone in the pocket of one of the PCs. It contains something evil.
