3d level one/two page characters with complete rules.

The content is already done, but the building process needs to be completed. The choices should be like this:

1.  Select main class

3.  Decide on weapon/equipment option

4.  Select from a background

5.  Select a magic item

Example



In comparison to the rough cave tunnels you came from, it seems that the walls and floor in this room have been smoothed out. Drainage in the floor keeps the area dry and it seems very well ventilated, which is surprising given how far underground you are.

1)  Shrine

    1.  Simple: A shrine to some dwarven god it built into the wall. It includes a life sized metal statue.

    2.  Complex: Religion or Arcana check to see if you understand which god is represented. On a success you are able to use that knowledge to find a hidden compartment containing some gems. On a failure, the Statue animated and attacks assuming no other attacks were triggered.

2)  Dining Room

    1.  Simple: An abandoned dining area. There is still food on the table.

    2.  Complex: Nature or Medicine test to check if the food is still good. Success means you are able to recover some ingredients for your next rest. On a failure you think it best to not eat any of it.

3)  Work area

    1.  Simple: This room is still under construction. Tools and rubble lie scattered around.

    2.  Complex: Survival or Perception to determine why the work was stopped. On a success you find a few rough geodes. On a failure, a Yellow mold slithers out of the cracks and attacks.

4)  Sleeping hole

    1.  Simple: An area where someone was sleeping

    2.  Complex: Investigation or History. Success, you find a clue as to who was here and when, along with a few gold coins. Failure, the creature, a Druergar is still here and attacks.

Lair:

A Flameskull is lairing here, patrolling between the Shrine and the unfinished room to the south. In the main room stands a statue that has a large ruby embedded in its head. The rest of the room is filled with colorful mushrooms, some of which emit an acrid gas occasionally. It should be easy to sneak past the Flameskull when it enters the backroom.

Read out the description and then the simple POI descriptions.

4 locations to investigate. Players are asked which they will explore. No results are given until they all decide. They can investigate something together. They can also choose to keep watch, in which case when combat starts they can position themselves where they want. Or they can search for traps, which means that nobody else will encounter a trap, and they might.

Each POI needs:

1.  A simple description which does not give anything away

2.  A mini event, check or description for when it is investigated.

Traps are chunk based, so they affect everyone in the area.

Monsters are suggested for each investigation area, but also a Lair monster is described which replaces the POIs.

## One second start, landing page

- Explore

  - Is there a person in the room with you?

  - Ask them to choose a character by giving it a talent: Fast, strong or tough?

  - Read the room description to them

  - Read the secret information to yourself

  - Ask what they do.

- Fight

  - Ask what weapon the hero uses: Magic, one hand weapon and shield, massive weapon, ranged weapon

  - Choose a creature

  - Roll initiative

  - Turn 1: normal fight rolls and narration

  - Turn 2: special effect nareation, saving throw

- Socialise

  - Choose an archetype

  - Decide what the player needs to know

  - Ask your player if they are funny, persuasive or intimidating

  - Roleplay!

- Treasure

  - Present three items

  - The player chooses one.
