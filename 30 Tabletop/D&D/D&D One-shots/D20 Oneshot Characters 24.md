# Design Constraints

Two of every class

Players choose skills, species and weapons at playime

Those three things are not shown on the sheet (only the options)

Classes should be as different as possible from each other

Characters must fit on a single A4 sheet.

The assumption is that there will be no rests, or that if there are, they will be long rests restoring everything.

Where necessary, short rest recharge abilities are shown as 10 minute Rituals

Tool proficiencies are skipped. Important ones (Thieves tools) are turned into an Action.

Action, trigger is always first

Group tokens

No “At start of combat” or “when you roll initiative” abilities

Material component is just “Focus”

Costed material components are changed to tokens

# Common Actions

### Movement

Choose one of these to do each turn.

**Walk**: Move up to your base speed along any clear, flat space that you can stand upright in. You may clear a single 1.5ft obstacle during this movement

**Climb**: Move up to your climbing speed, or half your base speed either vertically or horizontally during the

Turn.

**Swim**: Move up to half your base speed across any body of water. Add or subtract speed based on the current.

**Crawl**: Move up to half your base speed through any space which would be too small to stand up in. This causes you to gain the Prone condition.

**Drag**: Move up to half your base speed. Any object you are lifting, or a target you have Grappled, is moved with you.

**Long Jump**: Move over a single 10ft gap as long as you move at least 10ft before the gap.

**Drop Prone**: At the end of your movement

you may gain the Prone condition for free.

### Combat Actions

Attack - **Action:** Make one attack roll using an equipped weapon.

Offhand Attack - **Bonus Action:** Make one attack roll using alight weapon that is in your second hand.

Opportunity attack - **Reaction, when a creature moves out of reach of you:** Make one melee attack against the provoking creature. The attack interrupts the provoking creature's movement, occurring right before the creature leaves your reach.

Creatures don't provoke an opportunity attack when they teleport or when someone or something moves them without using their movement, action, or reaction.

Dodge - **Action**: Until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with advantage.

Interact - **Free Action**: You interact with something in the environment, or take an item into your hand such as drawing a weapon. You can use this once per turn.

Use Object - **Action**: Operate an object you can touch, such as drink and potion, pull a lever or open a door.

Drop Held Item - **Free Action**: One item you are holding is dropped to the floor directly underneath you.

Dash - **Action**: Doubles your movement speed for the rest of the turn.

Hide - **Action**: When you try to hide, make a Stealth check and note the result. Until you are discovered or you stop hiding, that check's total is contested by the Perception check of any creature that actively searches for signs of your presence.

Disengage - **Action**: Any movement you make this turn does not trigger Attacks of Opportunity.

Help - **Action**: Grant an adjacent target advantage on the next check or attack roll it makes before the start of your next turn.

Ready - **Action**: First, you decide what perceivable circumstance will trigger your reaction.

Then, you choose the action you will take in response to that trigger, or you choose to move up to your speed in response to it.

When the trigger occurs, you can either take your reaction right after the trigger finishes or ignore the trigger.

Readied action - **Reaction, when your trigger happens:** Immediately take the action you stated during your Ready action.

Grapple - **Action**: The target of your grapple must be no more than one size larger than you, and it must be within 5ft.

Using at least one free hand, you try to seize the target by making a Athletics check contested by the target's Athletics or Acrobatics check (the target chooses the ability to use).

If you succeed, you subject the target to the Grappled condition (its speed is set to 0).

Shove - **Action**: The target of your shove must be no more than one size larger than you, and it must be within 5ft.

You make a Athletics check contested by the target's Athletics or Acrobatics check (the target chooses the ability to use). If you win the contest, you either knock the target prone or push it 5 feet away from you.
