On the first morning of your stay in Neverwinter you find a letter stashed amongst your belongings, where you would easily find it. You open it and see that the finely and decoratively written letter is signed Trisha Merryweather.

Dear fellow adventurers and friends,

We have been through many struggles lately, and you have all helped me more than you know. The road has been harsh and stony. Before we continue down that road again, we should have a night of indulgence. Exciting indulgence.

So, I would invite you all to spend the evening at The Vanishing Sands, a well known theatre here in Neverwinter. The tickets are on me. The show starts at dusk. So let’s dress up and have an unforgettable night.

Yours,

Trisha Merryweather

If you decide to take Trisha up on the offer, please post a short description of what Trisha sees as you approach and meet her outside the theatre. If you decide to go shopping for an outfit. expenses are 2gp for a basic outfit suitable for the theatre, 10gp for a nice tailored outfit and 45gp for a smashingly extra fancy or fashionable outfit. Either way you can find anything you need for your outfit in the city during the day.

\*\*\*

Hama decides to follow the invitation to the theatre. She went shopping before and found a calishite merchant who sold her a basic outfit in calimshan style for 2gp. (Since Neverwinter is probably colder than Calimshan it also includes a cloak. All in all Hama looks super Aladdin cool. 😎)

Kildrak had gone to a tailor investing 10gp in an outfit that was just appropriate, framing his appearance naturally. There's nothing fancy about Kildrak, whereas he has a presence like a massive rock: You can just pass him without noticing, but if somebody's gaze wanders across, they would stop and notice a neutral and strong aura that can feel impressive.

The new set of clothes comprises dark, but light-weight garment including a warming cloak (with several little pockets on the inside). Though you would not notice it as a decoration, two subtle thin lines of extra thread run diagonally over the cloak's left side in parallel, representing Kildrak's admiration for the twostring (an instrument from Mintarn that Kildrak likes to associate with cultural opportunities like this).

With this neutral, but subtly impressive appearance, Kildrak assumes to have the best position possible for his official hearing.

Somebody who dared to look really closely might notice a glowing joy behind the neutral shell in Kildrak's eyes, excited about the dramatical night ahead.

As you arrive you see Trisha is wearing a brown suede skirt, hitched up over one knee. On top she has a white long sleeve blouse with a black corset that matches her black knee high leather boots. Her whole outfit is decorated with fur trim and metal studded straps. Trisha has her hair up in the latest Neverwinterian style, clasped at the top with nair needles that look surprisingly like manticore quills. She is carrying a clipboard, with a exotic feather quill…

Entering the venue you pass some other guests, who comment on the parties varied style. Some Neverwinterian noblemen sitting in a booth all turn their heads simultaneously and stare at the lady in the elegant green dress. A pack of well dressed ladies suddenly drop their mundane conversation to look over the oriental woman, enviously muttering words such as “exotic” and “daring”. An older gentleman, covered with military awards, looks down at the dwarf and gives an appreciative nod. Nobody comments on the finely dressed half-orc, but many heads follow his progress through the crowd, all craning to look but not be seen to look...

The tables of the theatre are arranged in front of a stage area with a long catwalk dividing the room. You are seated at different tables, each with several seated strangers, one extra empty seat and a long metal pole secured in the centre. Trisha disappears into the crowd.

The warm up show is introduced as “Spirit and Steel”. A dwarven woman plays Hang Drum while a curious feline humanoid dances on the catwalk. The beat quickens and the dance becomes fast paced and sensual. The creature (one of the other patrons names it a Tabaxi) leaps between the tables, using the pole to perform highly acrobatic dance moves. The music of the drums conjures up the illusion of a jungle which fills the hall. The tabaxi’s dance a representation of hunting in the wild.

The main show is a huge troupe of exotic dancers with a large orc-hestra. They have several scenes each with a different style of dancer. It is incredibly bombastic, with dancers moving between the tables, and many illusions. Many of the people at the show seem to be rich nobles or people of influence, and the troupe seems well known.

During the first act, ten fire dancers simultaneously perform an acrobatic dance, painting flowers of flame and ember in the air over all tables. Livia's table sprouts an enormous volcano which erupts, as an illusionary dragon howls and takes flight from the caldera. It explodes in twenty pieces which turn into more dancers as they hit the ground.

The second act has the room filled with heavy fog which creeps along the floor, and dancers dressed as lizardfolk complete a ritualistic dance. Ghost like apparitions of fey and undead appear from under the tables, and the centerpiece, an elven ghost king, emerges from the centre of Thokk’s table.

The final act has illusionary piles of gold spill from the ceiling, creating piles on all the tables. The dance seems to be about a beloved queen, who becomes smitten with gold and then is murdered by her court. The tragic last moments unfold on Hamas table, as the queen is stabbed and the gold changes to red in color.

During the main show, the Tabaxi from the warm up show sits next to Kildrak. She stays in skin (or fur?) contact for the entire show. She seems to share a few words with him...

During the main show, the tabaxi introduces herself as Spirit in the Sky. Noticing your outfit, she says she also likes the twostring and hopes there will be time for her to play you something. After a while Kildrak notices that she is purring.

After the show, you are accosted by several noble men and ladies who seem incredibly interested in you. You are taken to the separee, where a private and very wealthy afterparty is taking place. Their servants bring you canapes and drinks all evening. Some of the nobles ask if you will take time during your stay to visit them in the Blacklake district at their chateaus.

You gained a new contact: Lord Owyn Valennor, a human noble who lives in the Blacklake district (where all the neverwintarian rich live). At 53 he is the youngest of 4 siblings. He was a General and served with Lord Neverember before his banishment from Waterdeep. Now Lord Owyn has been placed in charge of organizing the new town guard. Since the city of Neverwinter is phasing out the use of mercenaries from Mintarn as guards, he could prove to be a powerful ally.

New hook: Lord Owyn has a rival, Arturo Serra, who has been spreading rumors about him to dissuade the council from giving him the post of Towns Guardmaster.
