**POSTMORTEM**

**The Bad**

Quest Deadline should not have been changed.

Too many stepping stones before a big knowledge payout.

No Ambiguity free areas.

The difference between the characters' stories and campaign’s story was not clear cut.

Attempting to have 5(4) main characters continuously created a massive workload.

No clear “bad guy”

Ironfang.

War pick. Martial weapon, 1d8 piercing + 1d8 thunder damage.

+2 to attack and damage rolls.

Attunement required. Cursed item, you may not willingly unattune with the item.

3 charges. Recharge d3 per Long rest.

Earth Mastery

Passive: You can speak and understand Terran fluently (language of earth elementals, dao, and other creatures of the Plane of Earth).

Passive: You have resistance to acid damage.

Passive: You have tremorsense of 60ft.

Passive: You can sense precious metals and gemstones out to 60ft, but don't know their exact location.

Action: 3 charges. Cast Dominate Monster (DC 17) on any earth blessed creature.

Action: 1 charge. Cast Shatter.

Ritual: 3 charges. Create Devastation Orbs of Earth when inside an Earth Planar Node.

Ritual: 3 charges. Remove the Petrified condition, or one curse causing it from a target you can touch.

Ironfang’s curse:

Take three Charisma Saving throws (DC 13).

The Prince of Elemental Earth has partial power over you. You gain a new flaw based on the number of failures you had on your Charisma saving throws.

1 failure. “I must show my enjoyment to others when I see evil acts done.”

2 failures. “I must show my power to others by smashing things in front of them at least once a day.”

3 failures. “Ogremochs will must be done, I will obey its commands when it speaks”

If you lose attunement, by accident or deliberately, Ogremoch will inhabit your body instead of Ironfang, killing you.

On the upside, something cool will happen when/if you die.

You may not tell the other heroes about this curse, otherwise you will lose attunement with Ironfang. Feel free to do things that might lead them to think something is wrong. A Remove Curse spell will allow you to be free of the curse, and safely unattune to the item.

Charm of Fire Walking

Targets a creature you can touch. For 1 hour the affected creature is resistant to fire damage, does not suffer exhaustion from heat and can walk across the surface of any liquid without sinking. 1 charge. Roll a D6 during a long rest, on a 4+ the charm regains 1 charge. The ability cannot be dismissed early.

Charm of Magma skin

Targets a creature you can touch. For 1 hour the affected creature's skin turns to cooled magma. While in this state, the creature has resistance to slashing, bludgeoning and piercing damage from non-magical means and is immune to fire damage. The creature is also immune to the effects of the Petrified condition, although can still gain and lose that condition. 1 charge. Roll a D6 during a long rest, on a 4+ the charm regains 1 charge. The ability cannot be dismissed early.

Charm of Efreet binding

Attunement required:

Target a Efreet (fire genie) that you can touch. Make a contest of Charisma. If you succeed, the genie is sucked into the charm. If you fail, you suffer 6d6 psychic damage. Once a genie is stored in the charm, its effect changes.

**Balloon Pack**

**Wondrous item, uncommon**

This backpack contains the spirit of an air elemental

and a compact leather balloon. While you're wearing

the backpack, you can deploy the balloon as an action

and gain the effect of the levitate spell for 10 minutes,

targeting yourself and requiring no concentration.

Alternatively, you can use a reaction to deploy the

balloon when you're falling and gain the effect of the

feather fall spell for yourself.

When either spell ends, the balloon slowly deflates

as the elemental spirit escapes and returns to the

Elemental Plane of Air. As the balloon deflates, you

descend gently toward the ground for up to 60 feet. If

you are still in the air at the end of this distance, you fall

if you have no other means of staying aloft.

After the spirit departs, the backpack's property is

unusable unless the backpack is recharged for 1 hour

in an elemental air node, which binds another spirit to

the backpack.
