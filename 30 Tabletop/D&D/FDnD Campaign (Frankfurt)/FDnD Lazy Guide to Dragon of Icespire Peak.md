Three fronts with portents

The Dragon

As the dragon settles in, the frequency of its attacks grow stronger.

Portent 1: The dragon attacks a well armed caravan, Flying off with one of the wagons. Clearly it has found a new home.

Portent 2: The weather turns, even when it is spring, cold and harsh. Its lair effects start to take hold.

Portent 3: The dragon sends a kobold to the town to suggest an offering of gold.

The Orcs

As the dragon settles in, the orcs are displaced from their normal locations.

Portent 1:

Sign: The former inhabitants of an old abbey have moved into the Mountains Toe Gold Mine

Reason: The orcs have driven them from their home at the abbey.

Portent 2:

Sign: The orcs have come down from the hills and taken over an old abbey

Reason: The dragon has driven them from their home on Ice Spire peak.

Portent 3:

Sign: Every few nights, bright beams of light can be seen above Neverwinter woods.

Reason: The half-orcs have begun their summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder

The Ritual

The half orcs prepare to summon their god.

Portent 1:

Sign: The logging camp comes under attack from ankhegs.

Reason: The half-orcs are testing their ritual using a small animals heart.

Portent 2:

Sign: A beam of light is seen to the east near the sea

Reason: The half-orcs have made a pact with the orcs and begun a dark ritual

Reason: The half-orcs have begun their summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder.
