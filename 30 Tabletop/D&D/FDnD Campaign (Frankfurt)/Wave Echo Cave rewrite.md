### Dungeon rules

- Every change of location search and encounter takes a minimum of 10 minutes

- Track time in 10 minute blocks

- Rolls perception even time you change location

- Read the description for each area as you arrive

- Searching yields the search description

#### 1 Entrance: Collapsed garden

**Description**

The chamber holds a large orb of gnomish design suspended from the ceiling. It gives off a dim light. The floor has long troughs carved into it. Lichen and moss cover most surfaces. The next chamber was clearly a sculpture park but the north most section has collapsed into a 20 foot chasm.

**Entrance Perception Test**

- The first rockseeker camp can be found in the back of the garden area.

- A black mark from a campfire can be seen on the floor.

- Some small crates are stowed behind the camp

**Searching**

- A rope is tied around one of the statues and leads down into the chasm

- (History) The statues are all of Gnomish inventors / One of them was a famous Necromancer as well.

- (Survival) The camp fire has been unused for about a week / A body was dragged away but there are no footprints of who was dragging it.

- (Investigation) The crates contain prospecting supplies and rations for about 4 weeks / You find several flasks of oil, a lantern and a strong rope.

- (Arcane) The gnomish light orb was used to grow food plants / You could probably fix it if you found some spare parts.

- (Nature) The long troughs were used as planters. / Some seeds are salvagable

#### 3 Old Entrance

**Approach Perception test**

- The flagstones leading to this area were well used in the past. It must have been a major thoroughfare.

- Several stone doors lie along the path, they are all open.

- You hear the sound of something alive as you approach, thought it falls silent as you near the entrance.

**Description**

Many tunnels intersect this high roofed natural cavern. The walls are carved with simple reliefs showing dwarven miners and gnome tinkerers and human wizards hard at work crafting items. Scattered around the floor are at least two dozen ancient sets of armor and weapons. Although all of the weapons are of dwarf and orcish origin, there are no corpses of those types. Only a few gnomish corpses lie scattered and broken in the room.

**Searching**

- The Guardroom door has a large boulder leaning against it. Athletics (DC 15) to move it enough that someone can trigger the door.

- (Animal Handling) The gnomish bones have been chewed or nawed by something. / The bite marks are not from natural animals

- (Medicine) They did not die from whatever has been chewing on them

- (Arcane) The doors leading to the two southern rooms are counterweighted by a gnomish contraption so they open and close very easily.

**Monsters**

- There are 10 stirges hiding in the cavern which attack if the party searches the room, or spends any time there.

- Stirges ignore AC bonus from Shields and Armor when they attack.

- On a critical hit, an attacking Stirge also Blinds the target until removed. This effect is in replacement of extra damage.

#### 4 Old Guardroom

**Description**

As the door opens a light cloud of dust is ejected from the room. Piled against the inside of the door are 10 dessicated but fairly intact orc corpses. Old weapon racks, beds and a training dummy adorn the finely carved room. There are other corpses in the room as well.

**Entrance Perception test**

- The orcs left their weapons against the walls

- There are several human, dwarf and gnome corpses in the room

- There are several skeletal orc remains in the room as well

**Searching**

- Each searcher find a few gold pieces worth of trinkets and treasure.

- (Medicine) The orcs died of asphyxiation, the gnomes and humans died of orcs

- (Arcane) The skeletal orc remains have some metallic components

Monsters

- The skeletal orc remains animate and fight as Skeletons after the heroes search the room. These skeletons have the Orc ability Aggressive, and can speak both Gnomish and Orc.

- One skeleton can make a free attack if no heroes suspected these skeletons are active.

#### 5 Assayers office

**Description**

The door to this room opens easily. This smaller room has two benches and a long table against one wall. A small grill covers what looks like a scoop coming out of the wall. Many cubbyholes in the back wall contain rolled paper scrolls. In the middle of the room a gnomish corpse is pinned to the ground with a large orc spear.

**Entrance Perception test**

- The gnome has been chewed like all the rest

- Another gnome corpse looks like it was hiding under the desk. It has a large arrow protruding out of it.

- There were other corpses here but they are gone.

**Searching**

- The two gnomes were unarmed. One was dragged out from the table and killed.

- There are two copper ingots in the room

- (Investigation) The scrolls contain rows of numbers

- (Arcane) The scoop can be activated. The grill opens and a small chest on wheels rolls out containing 10 Pure Copper Ingots.

#### 6 South Barracks: Poor Barracks

**Approach Perception Test**

- A sturdy stone door designed for dwarves blocks the way

- The door has holes for air

- The door is unlocked and easily manipulated by a latch nearby

- You can hear the sound of bone splintering

Old stone bunks in orderly rows line the walls of this chamber and a corroded iron brazier full of old coals stands near the middle of the room. Three grey figures are hunched over a pile of old bones, gnawing on them.

Monsters: 3-5 Ghouls. Once one of them is destroyed, the others try to flee, demonstrating that they know how to open the gnomish doors.

Searching

- (Nature) Some of the Ghouls have fluorescent fungi spores on their skin

- (Investigation) It would seem from the equipment stored here, that the Miners used this for resting between shifts.

#### 7 Storeroom safehouse

A side passage has partially collapsed into a mass of rubble. You can squeeze through and find a ruined storeroom. The stored food has long since dried up, but the door, still strong is easily barred from the inside. There are no corpses and it seems that creatures never explore this way.

This is a safe location where they players can rest without fear of Wandering monsters. Tell the players that items can be stored here safely.

The players may take short or long rests, but make sure they tick off time if they spend it.

#### 8 Fungi cavern

**Approach Perception Test**

- The sound of the crashing waves is getting louder/softer here

- The air is more moist and there is more moss growing on the walls

- (Darkness) Some of the fungi on the walls is glowing. There is a dim glow from ahead.

The tunnel opens up into a large flat cavern. Most of the surfaces are covered with brightly colors mushrooms and mats of fungus. Attached to the ceiling are several very large bulbous plants. The whole cavern is dimly lit because some of the mushrooms are fluorescent.

Special: When moving in the area of the cavern, the fungi releases a cloud of poison. Holding your breath requires a DC 9 Constitution Saving Throw, increasing by 1 each round. If failed, the creature suffers 3D6 poison damage and gains the poisoned status for 10 minutes.

After 1 minute of the cavern being full of poison gas, the larger plants on the ceiling open up and suck up all the gas.

Searching

- (Nature) The plants on the floor will make gas. / The plants on the ceiling will suck up the gas

#### 9 Great cavern: mess hall

**Approach Perception test**

- The tunnel leads upwards and eventually turns into smooth carved corridors 10 feet across.

- Iron bowls which once contained fire line the corridor

**Description:**

This cavern is broken into three parts. High ledges on either side look down onto the lower section in the middle. Carved stone stairs lead up 10 feet embankments. In the middle section long tables with stains from spilled meals hint at a grizzly ambush. Many orc weapons litter the ground.

**Terrain:** Climbing the embankments requires a DC12 Athletics check or fall prone and suffer D6 Bludgeoning damage.

**Monsters:** 5-7 Ghouls and 2-3 Zombies

**Searching**

- (Arcane) The zombies were orcs and dwarves but also have metallic implants in various places.

- (Investigation) Under the table on the eastern ledge is a keg of Dwarven brandy (10 portions) which is remarkably preserved.

#### 11 North barracks: wealthy barracks

**Approach Perception test**

- A sturdy stone door designed for dwarves blocks the way

- The door has holes for air and light flickers from inside

- The door is locked from the inside (DC15 Arcane/Sleight of Hand, DC 20 Athletics).

- You can hear the sound of Bugbears speaking in Goblin

- (If approaching from the mess hall) A basic barricade of wooden furniture has been built in front of the door. Several gnomish corpses have been thrown over the barricade.

A well carved room lit by a burning iron brazier. This room has rows of stone bunks lining on wall. The quality of the furniture shows that this room was for the more important residents. The room has been cleared of corpses.
