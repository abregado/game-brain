Generally it is something exceptional for a baron from medieval England to own more than 20 knight’s fees. A knight fee is an amount of land to sustain a knight. It is calculated that a knight’s fee would consist of about 1 to 2 manors. One manor would have about 50 to 80 people, including the knights, their family, servants and the peasants. So perhaps a knight's fee would have about 50 to 80 people.

Since it is exceptional for a baron to own more than 20 knight's fee. So probably many barons would own about 10 knight's fee. Thus, there would be around 700 people under each baron.

Furthermore, a manor would be around 4 square kilometres. Thus a knight's fee would be around 4 to 6 square kilometres. This would make a baron to own about 40 to 60 square kilometres of land.

## Custom Items and Features given out

### Potion of Diminution

***Use Object Action:*** The drinkers size is halved in all dimensions, and its weight is reduced to one-eighth of normal. This reduction decreases its size by one category - from Medium to Small, for example. Until the spell ends, the drinker also has disadvantage on Strength Checks and Strength Saving Throws. The target's Weapons also shrink to match its new size. While these Weapons are reduced, they have damage dice that are one level smaller (d1, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12) and don’t add their users Strength ability modifier. The effect lasts for 1 minute.

### Orb of Standing Water

**Use Object Action:** You smash the orb at your feet. A wall of water rushes up from the ground. Select a type of wall from the options below.

Barrier wall: up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick

Ring wall: 20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick.

You must maintain concentration on the wall. The wall dissipates after 10 minutes, or when you lose Concentration. The wall’s space is difficult terrain. Any ranged weapon attack that enters the wall’s space has disadvantage on the attack roll, and fire damage is halved if the fire effect passes through the wall to reach its target. Spells that deal cold damage that pass through the wall cause the area of the wall they pass through to freeze solid (at least a 5-foot square section is frozen). Each 5-foot-square frozen section has AC 5 and 15 hit points. Reducing a frozen section to 0 hit points destroys it. When a section is destroyed, the wall’s water doesn’t fill it.

### Mending Song - Trisha

**1 minute:** You begin to sing and a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin repairs itself.

As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

This spell can physically repair a magic item or construct, but the spell can’t restore magic to such an object.

This ability is removed when you reach level 6.

### Arcane Understanding - Hama

You can understand how magical items work enough to know how to operate them. When you investigate a magical contraction or device, take an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 10-20). On a success you know what you need to make the device operate, or how to use it.
