Solarpunk Runners

Attempt to design a roleplay premise that fills many of the same roles as Cyberpunk, but using Solarpunk. Guns, power armor, hacking, stealth and action should still be front and center.

The end came. The rich got eaten. The world fell hard from its overconsuming, capitalist ways. Fifty years on, there are still plenty of people just trying to survive using the ways of the past. A few lucky ones were able to forge a new type of life. Green tech Sanctuaries. They defended themselves from the chaos, and refused to go back to the old ways. Your parents were such people, but it is not their responsibility to reclaim the world. It is yours.

Situations. The players are…

…arriving at a building in a large urban center to scavenge old world tech

…ambushed by scavengers on the road between sanctuaries

…on a trade mission with less fortunates

…climbing an old world skyscraper to glide back home after a successful mission

…breaking into an old world robot factory to shut it down

…investigating a rogue harvester titan

…making a connection with a new sanctuary

…trading data with a partner sanctuary

…visiting sensor stations to collect data

Sanctuary Life

We only make what we need.

If something can be reused, reuse it. If something can be repurposed, repurpose it. If a scavenged item can be used safely with modifications, then we do not build a new thing. Man already invented the wheel, we do not need to reinvent it.

Data is life and should be guarded

Our technology uses incredible amounts of data. It is sacred. Wireless transfer of data is generally disallowed. Taking large amounts of data outside the Sanctuaries is disallowed.

Self sufficiency is the goal, not Isolationism

Cultural and Information exchange is done in person

We regularly trade citizens amongst the communities to make sure everyone has the specialists they need. During these exchanges, data can also be carried between communities.

Nationalism and Democracy failed us

Each of our communities decides how they govern themselves. The methods that work best will survive and spread.

Our rules end at the walls

Those that wish to benefit from our ways must live by our laws. We do not push our laws on those outside our communities. Therefore, if you wish to benefit unconditionally, you must agree to live inside.

Overproduction and Overconsumption caused the fall

We run a tight ship. There is no place for waste and no place for gluttony. We plan for hard times, but if they don’t come then we share what we have with those less fortunate.

Seemingly Strange expressions of these tenants by certain Sanctuaries

Taking a quota of babies from scavenger families to boost population replacement.

Harvest Missionaries, which take unexpected seasonal overproduction out to raiders and scavengers.

Strict family planning, including mandatory sterilisation

Direct democracies

Sortition (leaders chosen at random)

Cold War between collectives
