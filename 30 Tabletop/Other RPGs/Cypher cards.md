8 Injury Cards (d6, d8, d8, d10, d10, d12, d12, d20)

3 At Risk cards.

3 GM Intrusion Cards

2 sided Danger level card with 0 and 1 on it

2 sided Danger level card with 2 and 3 on it

Roll summary card

- Above target: Success

- Equal target: Almost

- Double target: Success and… (Minor Effect)

- GM intrusion range: Failure and… or GM Intrusion happens.

Difficulty reference card

100 cards
