Generic Tabletop Campaign

# Design goals

Useable for 3+ players

Minimal Bookkeeping

No Global bookkeeping

No maps, movement of players, ect

Player units get better (Bragging rights, personalization)

Distributed and decentralised

Players validate each others rolls

Meta behaviours to encourage with this campaign

- Players trying to save their rostered units from complete destruction

- Players throwing their irregulars under the bus

- Players thinking hard about who they want to fight and in what order

- Players being invested in particular units

- Players using different combinations of units in each battle

# Running the Campaign

**Setup**

Have a list of 8 resources in a roll table. These can be themed based on the game played. Example: *Strategic Location, Defendable Location, Stockpile, Landing Zone. Usable Infrastructure, Logistics Network, Holy Site, Population Center.*

Roll each player 3 resources.

Each player builds a roster of units to a specific point value, approx 2x the expected size of each game.

Display each player’s name, and their resources, somewhere public.

**Goal**

Collect 1 each of 7 resources

or

Collect 3 each of 2 resources

**Phases**

- Pre-battle

  - Select scenario

  - Select irregulars

  - Declare units to opponent

- Battle

- Post-Battle

  - Resolve Wipeouts

  - Record losses and damage

  - Claim spoils

  - Refresh units in Downtime

  - Mark XP

  - Do upgrades

**Select Scenario**

If one player wants to choose the scenario, the other selects if they are the attacker or defender.

If both players want to choose the scenario, then roll off. The winner chooses the scenario. The loser chooses if they are the attacker or defender.

Players decide together what point value to use, rolling off if they cannot decide.

**Select Irregulars**

Each player rolls for or selects their Irregulars and shows them to their opponent.

**Declare Units**

Each player selects units from their roster to the given point value. Wiped out and Tired units cannot be used.

**Resolve Wipeouts**

Each unit that was Wiped out has its fate resolved according to the rules for that system.

**Record losses and damage**

All units used in the battle become Tired

Any damage caused, or losses suffered, to units used in this battle are recorded to the roster.

**Claim spoils**

Once per week

Winner chooses one card to take, loser gets a new random card

Or

Winner draws from the unused resources pile

Any number of times per week

Winner takes one random resource from their opponent and gives them one that they have.

**Refresh units in Downtime**

Any units that were not used in this battle are restored to perfect condition. Damage is repaired, weapons rearmed, health restored.

Tired Units in downtime remove the condition.

Wiped out units in downtime recover to 50% and lose the Tired condition.

**Mark XP**

Each time a unit is used and not wiped out, it gains XP

The player with the smallest amount of XP gains an additional points to distribute to units that took part in the battle.

1-3 difference: +1 point

4-7 difference: +2 points

8+ difference: +3 points

At 1, 3, 5 and 7 XP they get a Level/Upgrade

**Do Upgrades**

Units that reach a new XP threshold gain an upgrade based on what type of unit they are.

Wiped out units can be immediately refreshed by removing all their accumulated XP. They can also be modified as long as the roster value does not exceed the point limit.

# Customising for a given system

**Wipe outs**

What is the definition of a wipe out in the given system?

How likely should a wipe out be? Should there be a roll to lock in a wipe out?

**Upgrade tables**

For each unit, what upgrades make sense?

**Irregular Lists**

What can be taken as Irregulars?

Do players choose or roll randomly?

% of roster size per irregular slot?

**Rubber banding**

What events could affect the leading players to keep them from getting too far ahead?
