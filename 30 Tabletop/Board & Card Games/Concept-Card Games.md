Each card has up to 3 ways to destroy it listed. It also has up to 2 different attacks listed on it.

There could be as many different types of attack as you would like. Each one is required to cause damage to an area listed as vulnerable to that damage type.

OR

A number of villains are drawn from the deck. The other player must chose a hero to use to defeat them.

When a hero is chosen, the player gets a number of tokens to distribute. These have a color and a value. The values cannot be split.

Each enemy has a number of vulnerabilities. Each vulnerability has a color and a number.

If the player wants to switch then all villains that are still alive deal their damage to the hero player.

Multipart villains have their cards placed to the side and redrawn. When the final piece is drawn, the hero must face the boss at the same time as any creatures that are in play.

Original ten

At the start of the series, Ben only has access to ten aliens. More are gained in the following seasons.

#### **Diamondhead**

**Diamondhead** (voiced by *Jim Ward* in the TV series and by [*Daran Norris*](http://norris) in the live-action movie) is a Petrosapien ("[*Petro-*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro-)" referring to rocks, "[*sapien*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapience)" referring to an intelligent being; Petrosapien would mean "intelligent rock") from the crystal planet Petropia (a combination of the Greek "Petro-" and "topos", forming a word similar to "rock place").[^(*\[1\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiDiamondhead-0) The planet was destroyed by Vilgax with assistance from [*Tetrax*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_characters_in_Ben_10#10), who unknowingly helped him create the weapon that destroyed his home.[^(*\[2\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Secret_of_the_Omnitrix-1) Ben first transforms into Diamondhead in "And Then There Were 10" when he needed to destroy [*Vilgax's*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_villains_in_Ben_10#10) giant robot. In the planning stages, Diamondhead was set up to be one of the aliens Ben would have discovered later on, while [*Cannonbolt*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#Cannonbolt) would have been one of the original ten.

Diamondhead's body is composed of an extremely durable organic crystal, making him nearly invulnerable. Diamondhead can control his crystal physiology at will, allowing him to create crude crystal weapons from any part his body on demand or fire crystal shards from his hands. This same ability also allows him to regenerate to an extent, such as regrowing lost limbs. In addition to growing crystal from himself, Diamondhead can grow crystal over other objects. Lasers and other similar weapons are useless against Diamondhead, as his crystal body acts as a prism, refracting the beams. The one drawback to Diamondhead's crystal form is the crystal itself, which can shatter if exposed to sufficiently strong sonic vibrations. While Petrosapiens can regenerate limbs, there is a limit to how much damage they can recover from.^(*[\[1\]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiDiamondhead-0)[\[3\]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Ben10episode13-2)*)

#### **Fourarms**

Ben's preferred offensive form is **Fourarms** (voiced by [*Richard McGonagle*](http://mcgonagle)), a Tetramand (*tetra* meaning "four" in [*Greek*](http://language), *mand* being "arms" in [*Latin*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin)) from the desert planet Khoros. Khoros epitomizes a [*dystopia*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dystopia), resulting in the strength-oriented Tetramands.[^(*\[4\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiFourarms-3) Fourarms is the most used alien in the original series, since it is the form that most suits 10-year-old Ben's fighting style: blindly rushing at the enemy with little forethought. Ben first transformed into Fourarms in "Washington B.C." when he needed to fight the giant [*mammoth*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mammoth) that [*Dr. Animo*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_villains_in_Ben_10#10) had reanimated.

Fourarms is a ten-foot tall, four-armed, four-eyed powerhouse with armored skin and extremely dense musculature, making him unmatched in pure physical strength. His strength is such that he can create shockwaves simply by pounding the ground or clapping all four of his hands together, dubbed the "Big Smack", and his leg strength allows him to cross entire city blocks in a single jump. However, all the extra mass of his muscles and heavy skin makes Fourarms relatively slow, and his size likewise makes using things designed for smaller species difficult, especially when enlarging them is a bad idea.[^(*\[4\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiFourarms-3) When under the effects of an illness, Fourarms' strength and stamina are somewhat lessened and he develops pungent hives in his armpits.

#### **Ghostfreak**

**Ghostfreak** (voiced by [*Steven Blum*](http://blum)) is an Ectonurite (*ecto* playing on *ectoplasm*) from the perpetually dark planet Anur Phaetos (*phaetos* playing on phantom). Ectonurites are part of a [*hive mind*](http://mind), which is contained within the vast [*genetic memory*](http://memory) of every Ectonurite.[^(*\[5\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiGhostfreak-4) Ben first transformed into [*Ghostfreak*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_villains_in_Ben_10#10) in "Permanent Retirement" when he wanted to get away from his Aunt Vera for a while. Ben's version of Ghostfreak outwardly resembles a one-eyed ghost, the result of a second skin, while Ghostfreak's true form is that of a [*wraith*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wraith)-like [*genie*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie) with blue claws, decaying skin, and an exposed, upside-down skull with a single eye for a head.

Under Ben's control, Ghostfreak is composed of a variable-density protoplasm which allows him to become invisible and/or intangible at will. Ghostfreak can levitate freely, and he doesn't require oxygen, allowing him to survive in a vacuum. For the short time Ben has access to the form, it has no real weaknesses to speak of, aside from Ben's limited access to its true powers. The second skin protects him from Ghostfreak's otherwise fatal aversion to sunlight, and also allows Ben to frighten his opponents by pulling back the skin to reveal his true form beneath.

In his true form, revealed once Ghostfreak's personality becomes dominant, Ghostfreak demonstrates a number of powers Ben himself cannot use. In addition to the standard invisibility and intangibility, Ghostfreak can render others intangible through physical contact. His intangibility also enables him to possess humans, controlling them from within. Ghostfreak also has psychic abilities such as telepathy and telekinesis, but the extent of these abilities remains unclear.[^(*\[5\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiGhostfreak-4) His true form also has a flock of tentacles protruding from his chest, which can be used as extra appendages. In total darkness, these features become even more pronounced, and he can fire energy blasts from his chest.[^(*\[6\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Ben10episode38-5) However, having torn off the protective skin Ben made use of, Ghostfreak is extremely photosensitive, combusting when exposed to direct sunlight over a prolonged period.[^(*\[5\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiGhostfreak-4) He is also unable to possess Ben while in alien form, as the Omnitrix repels negative forces aimed at it, but can possess other true aliens. At the end of "Be Afraid of the Dark", Ben regains use of Ghostfreak's form, but as a natural Ectonurite, not the sunlight-shielded version.

#### **Grey Matter**

**Grey Matter** (voiced by [*Richard Steven Horvitz*](http://horvitz) in the TV series and by [*Carlos Alazraqui*](http://alazraqui) in the live-action movie) is a Galvan (the former a play on actual [*grey matter*](http://matter) and [*Greys*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greys), the latter a play on [*galvanization*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galvanization)) from the tiny planet Galvan Prime.[^(*\[7\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiGreyMatter-6) Ben first transformed into Grey Matter in "Washington B.C." when he saw that a gold Sumo Slammer card could be found in a box of cereal. [*Gwen*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_10#10) found him looking for the card in the cereal boxes at the supermarket. Although Ben rarely intentionally selects Grey Matter, he often finds himself in that form as a result of accidental transformations, such as in "A Small Problem".

Grey Matter is a gray-skinned, [*frog*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frog)-like, [*bipedal*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipedal) alien only five inches tall, however his size belies his intellect, which is second to none. He can create almost anything from spare parts and deduce the function of any device at a glance. This ability extends to organic organisms also, determining the position of nerve clusters under the skin of an alien creature he'd never seen before.[^(*\[8\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Ben10episode14-7). His size allows him to squeeze into small spaces, and he can climb walls thanks to tiny suction cups on his skin. He also has sharp teeth, and his slimy skin makes him difficult to grab. However, Grey Matter's size makes him unsuited for battle, and hence Ben must outsmart his enemies while using the form.[^(*\[7\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiGreyMatter-6) Showing a more light-hearted side of the Omnitrix, Ben, despite being able to understand any device, does not consciously comprehend what he is doing, leading him to remark as such.

#### **Heatblast**

**Heatblast** (voiced by [*Steven Blum*](http://blum) in the TV series and by *David Franklin* in the live-action movie) is a Pyronite (*pyro* meaning fire) from the star Pyros.[^(*\[9\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiHeatblast-8) Heatblast is Ben's first transformation, which was activated in "And Then There Were 10" when Ben was tampering with the Omnitrix upon it first attaching to him.

Heatblast is a magma-based lifeform that can control fire at will, either projecting it in a wide range of forms or absorbing it into himself. He can fire streams of flame, create fireballs, manifest tornadoes of flame, utilize said tornadoes to teleport short distances, and radiate heat in all directions for a localized explosion. He can also achieve flight by firing at the ground to propel himself like a rocket. Apart from this, Heatblast is also a highly durable alien, able to endure being thrown through buildings without being slowed.[^(*\[10\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Secrets-9)While under the influence of an illness, Heatblast's powers are reversed, giving him [*cryokinetic*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_comic_book_superpowers#superpowers) powers instead of the normal [*pyrokinesis*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrokinesis). Heatblast's dependency on heat is also his weakness, as his powers can be negated by sufficient amounts of water or similar fire-extinguishing materials. His natural body heat also prevents him from handling things without burning them, people included.[^(*\[11\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Washington_B.C.-10)

#### **Ripjaws**

The aquatic alien **Ripjaws** (voiced by [*Fred Tatasciore*](http://tatasciore)) is a Piscciss Volann (from piscēs, the Latin word for fish) of the water planet Piscciss.[^(*\[12\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiRipjaws-11) Ben first transformed into Ripjaws in "The Krakken" in order to take the [*Krakken's*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_secondary_characters_in_Ben_10#10) eggs back from Jonah Melville. Because of the extremely specific conditions required to maintain Ripjaws, he is the least used Omnitrix alien, and is not seen for the entirety of the third season.

Ripjaws shares a number of traits of Earth aquatic lifeforms—namely [*alligators*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alligator), [*eels*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eel), [*anglerfish*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglerfish), and [*sharks*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shark)—combined into a single being. Naturally, his main ability is being able to breathe underwater. Ripjaws can also withstand a remarkable amount of pressure, and his luminescent dangler allows him to see in the darkest of depths (or otherwise dark areas). He can swim at great speeds, and his sharp teeth and claws can pierce through almost anything. His lower jaw can unhinge to allow him to bite much larger objects.[^(*\[12\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiRipjaws-11) Though his tail hides a pair of legs for travel on land, Ripjaws is limited by his need to remain hydrated, dehydrating rapidly if left out of the water for even a few minutes.

#### **Stinkfly**

Another of Ben's more commonly used forms, **Stinkfly** (voiced by [*Dee Bradley Baker*](http://baker)) is a Lepidopterran from the swamp planet Lepidopterra (a play on [*lepidoptera*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lepidoptera), the scientific name for butterflies and moths).^(*[\[13\]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiStinkfly-12)[\[14\]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildStinky-13)*) Ben first transformed into Stinkfly in "Washington B.C." when he had to save Gwen after she fell from the Washington Monument.

Stinkfly is meant to be a combination of various Earth insects (dragonflies, crickets, and praying mantises specifically).[^(*\[14\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildStinky-13) His primary ability is flight facilitated by the four thin wings on his back, which grant Stinkfly high mobility and speed. Stinkfly also possesses disproportionate strength, enough to carry people and objects heavier than himself. In "Don't Drink the Water", the child form of Stinkfly (Stinkyfly) was able to unleash a powerful [*herbicide*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbicide) gas by [*farting*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatulence). Stinkfly's four eye stalks give him a wide range of vision from the sky, including the ability to look directly behind himself. Pollen ducts in his eyes and mouth allow Stinkfly to excrete high-pressure streams of liquids.[^(*\[13\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiStinkfly-12) The type of liquid can range from a flammable toxin to an immobilizing jelly. His razor-sharp tail and pincer-like legs can also be used in melee combat. Stinkfly's primary weakness is water, which can negate his flight if it gets on his wings. In addition, while his body is fairly strong, his wings are not.[^(*\[13\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiStinkfly-12) A more minor inconvenience is Stinkfly's intense body odor (hence the name), which is a result of the oils he secretes to keep his joints moving.[^(*\[15\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Kevin_11-14)

#### **Upgrade**

**Upgrade** (voiced by [*Tara Strong*](http://strong)) is a Galvanic Mechomorph (*mecho-* meaning machine and *-morph* meaning shape) of the moon Galvan B, which orbits Galvan Prime. Though originally uninhabited, Galvan B was accidentally brought to life when [*Galvan*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#Grey_Matter) scientists introduced self-replicating nanotechnology to the moon, which bonded with the moon's minerals and created sentient life.[^(*\[16\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiUpgrade-15) Ben first transformed into Upgrade in "Permanent Retirement" to stop some criminals who were robbing an ATM. Upgrade is the only alien form in which Ben's voice does not change, aside from the more computerized tone behind it. The reason for this is revealed in the enhanced version of "Game Over"; Galvanic Mechomorphs can't bond with organic things, so Ben's transformation into Upgrade isn't complete.

Upgrade is composed of nanotechnology, allowing him to reshape his body at will.[^(*\[16\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiUpgrade-15) This makes restraining or harming him difficult, as he can "melt" to avoid either and will quickly regenerate from any damage taken.[^(*\[17\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Permanent_Retirement-16) Upgrade can also merge with other technology, earning his name by upgrading it far beyond its original design as long as he is merged with it. The size of the device is inconsequential. While merged with a device, Upgrade controls it as he would his own body.[^(*\[16\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiUpgrade-15) Even without a machine at hand, Upgrade can reconfigure his form around his eye in order to fire a plasma beam from it. Upgrade is also capable of forming simple constructs from plasma, such as spikes. Upgrade's weakness is his specialization: being technology-oriented, Upgrade cannot possess biological creatures, and sentient or more powerful robots are harder for him to control. Upgrade's liquid-metal composition can also work against him, namely where electrical attacks are concerned. Furthermore, any sort of metal corrosive can upset Upgrade's delicate internal workings and wreak havoc on his systems.[^(*\[16\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiUpgrade-15)

#### **Wildmutt**

**Wildmutt** (voiced by [*Dee Bradley Baker*](http://baker)) is a Vulpimancer from the garbage planet Vulpin (a play on [*vulpine*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulpine), which means fox).[^(*\[18\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildmutt-17) Ben first transformed into Wildmutt in "And Then There Were 10" after his second experiment with the Omnitrix. This was Ben's first intentional transformation.

Wildmutt is a vaguely dog-like alien whose physical features are a cross between a lion and a gorilla.[^(*\[14\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildStinky-13) [*Ben 10,000's*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_characters_in_Ben_10#10) Wildmutt has much more pronounced feline traits, including stripes and a tail. Wildmutt's powerful arms allows him to run, jump, and climb objects at high speeds, in addition to making him fairly strong. Wildmutt has no eyes, but has gills on his neck which allow him to hear and see many times better than humans, giving him radar-like sight which is depicted as functioning somewhat like a [*thermograph sensor*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermography). According to various profiles on Wildmutt, the porcupine-like quills on his back supplement his radar sight and can also serve as weapons, either protecting Wildmutt while he's rolling or as projectiles which can be fired off at will.[^(*\[18\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildmutt-17) This ability is unique to adult Vulpimancers, and since Ben's version is only ten years old, these quills haven't developed. Wildmutt's weakness, though it is rarely an issue, is his lack of sight, which can be impeded through various means,[^(*\[18\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiWildmutt-17) and he is rendered effectively useless if is nostrils are clogged. Wildmutt is also the only Omnitrix alien who cannot speak in any understandable fashion, communicating through snarls and growls, which can be a problem when attempting to relay messages or avoid conflict.[^(*\[19\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Side_Effects-18)

#### **XLR8**

**XLR8** (voiced by *Jim Ward*) is a Kineceleran (a [*portmanteau*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of the words 'kinetic' and 'accelerate') from the hyper-accelerated planet Kinet (the first part of [*kinetic*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic), which is movement energy).[^(*\[20\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiXLR8-19) Ben first transformed into XLR8 in "And Then There Were 10" when he decided to play around a bit with his newfound power, using the form to get revenge on some bullies that he fought with earlier in the episode.

XLR8 resembles a semi-armored [*velociraptor*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velociraptor), the base creature for the form's design.[^(*\[21\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiBrainsMuscle-20) XLR8 has wheel-shaped feet and wears a helmet with a windshield, which is part of his alien biology, leaving the features of his head unknown. Ben 10,000's XLR8 also has a scanner built into the helmet, the product of nanotechnology Ben 10,000 uses to enhance his aliens.[^(*\[22\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Ben_10.2C000_-_Enhanced_Version-21) The bandai profile for XLR8 describes the actual species much differently. The profile states that Kinecelerans are vaguely elfin in appearance, with long hair, blue skin, pointed ears, large tails, and oversized feet,[^(*\[20\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiXLR8-19). The Bandai profiles also create another contrast: actual Kinecelerans are said to be incapable of running on slippery surfaces. Furthermore, because of their constant motion, Kinecelerans build up large stores of static electricity, which reacts badly with water.[^(*\[20\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiXLR8-19) XLR8 has never had either of these problems. Also, the *Ben 10: Alien Force* episode "Plumber's Helpers" directly contradicts the Bandai profile, as the female Kineceleran named Helen perfectly resembles XLR8.

XLR8 can manipulate friction to reach speeds of 500 [*mph*](http://hour) in an instant, and the same power allows him to climb up walls and run on water with relative ease. Using his speed, XLR8 can perform a number of unique feats. He can create tornadoes through [*centrifugal force*](http://force), either through running in a small circle or spinning, and can deliver speed-enhanced attacks in rapid succession.[^(*\[23\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-Merry_Christmas-22) Amazing reflexes accompany this speed, allowing XLR8 to quickly dodge attacks with relative ease. His scissor-like claws can also cut through objects. XLR8's weakness is his lack of physical strength, which is about on par with a normal human, although his speed tends to make up for it. Magnets and charged pulses are said to be a problem, apparently disorienting Kinecelerans to the point that they cannot balance properly,[^(*\[20\]*)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Omnitrix_aliens#cite_note-BandaiXLR8-19) but the series has never addressed this weakness.
