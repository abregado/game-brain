**Aim:** Catch up mechanic with does not involve: collusion, combat, rubber banding or king making

**Thought 1:** Opportunity Window to Score closed by player vote

Player ‘score’ if they collect 20 resources before any other player. Players take turns but at the end of each round a vote is called. If the vote passes then the game only continues for 2 more rounds.

Any player may spend resources to veto the vote after it is made.

If a player is clearly ahead then the other players may try to end the round as early as possible to generate a new scoring window.

The king player might spend extra resources to force play to continue, giving himself a disadvantage in order to push to a ‘score’.

Resources are reset after the 2 rounds after a vote is called.

Players will need to convince each other that they are not so close to scoring until they do, else the window to score may close.
