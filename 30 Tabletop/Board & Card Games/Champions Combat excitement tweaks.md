Equipment cards.

Equip: This happens when you play the card.

Discard = put in your discard pile. The card will be shuffled back into your deck at the start of the next round.

Remove from Game = The card can no longer be used. Place it away from the play area.

**Charger:** When you play a move effect with 1 or more distance, as long as you move in a straight line, add 1 to the distance moved. If you suffer damage, discard this card to your deck.

**Incredible mass:** When you play a move effect, spend a power token to add a Displace icon.

**Overdrive:**

Add 1 to all dice roll totals.

When you take damage, discard a card from your hand.

When you gain a Score token, discard this card.

**Overheat:** Before rolling for damage, spend a charge token and roll 2 extra dice. Then remove this card from the game.

**Plasma weapon:**

Equip: Place a Score token on this card.

Before rolling for damage, spend a charge token and roll 2 extra dice. If no hits are rolled on the attack dice, remove this card from the game and give the Score token on this card to your target. Otherwise discard this card to your deck.

**Chainsaw:** Next Attack effect against a target you get +1 dice. Place a Power token from the bank on this card. If there are ever 2 Power tokens on this card, discard it.

While this card is equipped, Reaction effects with Block you play Block one less.

**Weapon Drone:** Remove this card from the game to make a 2 dice attack against any target on the board at 3+

**Repulsor weapon:** Spend a power token to add Push to any attack. Then remove this card from the game.

**Mind Spike:** Spend a power token and play an attack effect. The Attack effect originates from a different figurine.

**Explosive Rounds:** After making an attack against a target, you may spend a Power token. Make a 1d attack, ignoring reactions against a target adjacent to the first at 3+.

**Reflection shield:** The next time you Block damage, you may spend a Power token to:

(Retaliate 1d no reaction) Then remove this card from the game.

**Blink generator:**

The next time you play a Reaction effect with Block, also Move 1.

The next time you play a Reaction effect with Move 1, also Block 1

Then discard this card to your deck.

**Body armor:**

The next time you play a Reaction effect with Block, Block one extra.

Then discard this card to your deck.

**Handheld shield:**

As long as your attacker is in your attack area, discard this card to your deck to block one damage. If you do this, you may not use a reaction effect against this attack.

**Teleport:** When you play a move effect, spend a Power token to increase the movement by 2 spaces.

**Quick reflexes:** When you play a reaction effect with Move 1, move an extra space. Then Discard this card to your deck.

**Sanguinator**

Equip: Place a Score token on this card.

If your next attack causes damage to a player with a score token, you steal a token from them instead of earning a new one.

If your next attack fails to cause damage, or is made against a player without any score, give the token on this card to that player and remove this card from play.

**Bionic Legs**

Move cards you play allow you to move 1 hex further.

When you take damage, remove this card from the game.

**Bestial Roar**

At the end of the Initiative phase, you can swap your first Initiative card with any card. Then remove this card from the game.

**Tactical Mind:** At the end of the Initiative phase, you may swap one of your Initiative cards with an adjacent card. If you take damage, discard this card.

**Power Reserve:**

Equip: Place three power tokens from the bank on this card.

When you play an effect that has one or more Power up icons, take an extra Power token from this card.

If this card has no Power tokens left, remove it from the game.

If you take damage, remove a Power token from this card.

**Stable frame:**

You are immune to Push and Displace effects. When you gain a Score token, discard this card.

**Steady Aim:**

You are immune to Fumble effects. When you gain a Score token, discard this card.
