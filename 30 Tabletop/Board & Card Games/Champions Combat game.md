## Phases of the Game

Play begins with a Setup Phase and then continues with repeated Rounds of combat.

Each Round of Combat is made up of:

1.  Draw phase

2.  Initiative phase

3.  Resolution phase

4.  Round end

The Resolution phase is made up of a number of turns. Each Turn, play continues in this order:

1.  Move or Attack effect played

2.  Reaction effect played

3.  Score damage / Turn end

## Setup

The score tokens are placed to the side of the play area.

Each player takes a deck of Action cards

Each player draws randomly a Style card from the deck.

Place a pile of power tokens to the side of the play area.

Play then begins in the Draw Phase.



*Card backs for Action and Style cards*

## Winning the Game

First player to gain 5 score tokens wins.

## Draw Phase

Choose to discard or keep each card remaining from last round.

Reshuffle discards into decks

Each player places their Fortune token into a pile.

Players draw up to 4 cards

## Initiative Phase

Add two of each player's initiative card to the deck.

Shuffle and draw all initiative cards into a line. Play then proceeds in this order.

### Special Initiative Card

This card shows all player colours. When this card needs to be resolved, if a player has the clear highest number of power tokens they can take a turn.

## Resolution Phase

For each initiative card, the shown player takes a turn.

### The player turn

The player whose Initiative is shown on the next Initiative can discard an action card, using either its Move or Attack effect. A player must use a card on their turn.



*Examples of Actions cards. Move effects are at the top, Attack effects in the middle, Reaction effects at the bottom.*

### Move effects 

These are always at the top of the Action card.

####  

The player can move the number of hexes based on the number in the Move effect. Players may not move through each other nor end their movement on top of another player.

Once moved, the player’s facing changes based on the letter.

A: the moving player chooses the facing

F: the character ends in the facing of the direction it travelled last

B: the character ends in the facing of the opposite direction it travelled last

X: the opponent with the highest charge level decides the facing

#### The Displace symbol



When included in a move effect, this symbol symbol allows you to enter the hex of another player. When you do so, you can move their character to an adjacent hex. The targeted player can then choose any facing for their character.

### Attack effects

These are always in the middle of the action card.



If your target is inside either the red or blue zone on your style card, roll the number of dice shown on the attack action. If the attack is in the blue zone, it is a melee attack. If in the red zone, it is a ranged attack. The field on the Style card shows if the dice needs a 3+ or 4+ to deal damage. Each hit causes one damage. Each attack may have one or more special rules, which are detailed below.

Calculate the number of hits before moving on, but do not apply damage yet.

#### Reaction effects

These are always at the bottom of an action card.

The attacked player can discard one action card and activate its Reaction effect, unless the attack includes a Freeze icon. Calculate the entire effect of the Reaction before moving on to the next phase.

### Turn end

#### Scoring damage

All hits from an attack are calculated together during this step.

Reduce damage by the amount of symbols played by the target’s reaction.

|   | 1 reduction of ranged or melee damage |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------|
|   | 1 reduction from a ranged attack      |
|  | 1 reduction from a melee attack       |

For each remaining damage the attacker gains a score token.

The turn is now over and the next Initiative card can be resolved.

#### Death

You cannot die from damage.

## Round End

Once the last Initiative card is resolved, return to the Draw phase.

# Design concepts

Aims:

- Speed up the game

- Positioning remains important

- Comebacks are possible

- Reduce text on cards

- Should work for any model I pull off the shelf.

- Reward players for taking risks

## Round 2 test

- Arena smaller

- No armor tokens, just 7 hit points

- No constant action card

- Critical damage is “remove a card from your discard pile from the game”

- Discarding a hit point lets you draw a card to your hand

- Discarding an armor means you need to give a power level to someone

- One extra Initiative card that could be anyones!

- Which 5 cards would you remove from your action deck?

### Test 6 changes

**Changes**

No armor

No hit points

Winner is the first first to 7? points

Reverse scoring, deal damage to get score to win

No being knocked out of the game

Play a reaction unless you are being backstabbed

No cards that ignore reactions

You must move the entire distance on the Move effect

You may not end a movement on the same hex you started on

You must always play and use an effect, no passing

No penalty for having lowest power

No extra turn for highest power (except the special initiative card)

Charge renamed to Power

No damage types

Barrage icon hits everyone in both zones

**Noteworthy things that stayed the same**

You can always take a reaction, even when no damage is suffered

Do not discard power tokens, since you spend them now
