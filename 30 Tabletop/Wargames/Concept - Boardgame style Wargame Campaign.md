Straight up wargame (head to head fight) Campaign

Aim: A simple campaign for wargames with no objective based gameplay (head to head, fight to the death). Six play sessions. Opportunity for comeback victory. Defined end point and winner. Visual aids. Pre-fight group "campaign turn". Strategic penalty for players pushing for the outright win. Power consolidation by being defensive.

Timelimit: Game over after Six campaign turns. Points awarded should be perminant. Players only ACCRUE points, never lose them or have them stolen.

Comeback victory: Players who play consistantly should have the opportunity to win over those who just get lucky a few times. Points awarded later in the campaign should be worth more than those early on. The first two sessions should be more postulating and intellegence gathering. The last session should be an all out fight for dominance, or for those who cannot have a total victory, a race for second place.

Defined Winner: Scores should be triple digits by the end to promote a clear winner. Rewarded points should be a combination of even and odd two digit numbers so that no two players are tied (or at least it is very rare). Only one instance of a particular number should be given out. Example// First round 3,4 and 7 points are awarded and in the second round 9,11 and 20 are awarded.

Visual Aids: A score table should be available for players to look at before each campaign turn. It shouldnt be just a list of names with points next to them. An interesting and interpretive approach needs to be found to display victory points.

Pre-fight Campaign turn: Before each set of matches, this campaign turn is played out with all players present. Fight matches are decided now and opportunities to fight for high scoring goals are made available. The current lowest scoring players are given the choice of who to fight and what opportunity:advantage ratio they would like.

Strategic penalty for players pushing for the outright win: Players who select the highest points reward for a possible win also gain the least advantages. If a player selects to vie for the most points this campaign turn, he must also expect to do so at a lower advantage.

Power consolidation by being defensive. Players who chose not to attack and pass when given the option (effectivly allowing another player to make their choice for them) get to fight with condiserable advantage. These players do not stand to gain much from a fight and those that attack them stand to gain more than usual, but at greater risk.

Fight match betting. Starting with the lowest scoring player

1\. The player declares which location (and the value in campaign points) he wishes to vie for, or if he wishes to pass.

2\. Any other player can declare he will contest the battle.

3\. Any other player can declare he will contest the battle with one level of disadvantage

4\. The original contestor may interject and declare he will contest with two levels of disadvantage

5\. The first player selects which disadvantage his opponent will have and the battle begins

Bidding rounds like this continue until all players have opponents, or all bonus locations have been filled.

Players who pass gain one level of advantage in their eventual battle, but do not get to select their location.

To break bidding ties all players should be dealt a hand of 3 cards. If the event of a dispute both players place one card face down, then reveal. THe player with the highest card overrides the bid.

Levels of Disadvantage: Each disadvantage should be catered for the particular game being played. Here are some generic disadvantages.

The disadvantaged player must declare his army list before deployment

The disadvantaged player must set up half of his army before the other player deploys anything.

The disadvantaged player must complete a certain in battle objective or get nothing for his victory.

The disadvantaged player only receives half of the points if he does win but suffers no ingame penalty.

The disadvantaged player has less units than his opponent.

The disadvantaged player has a set of rules for deployment

The disadvantaged player has only a partial first turn.

Some of the disadvantaged players units must be set up along the table edge

The disadvantaged players unit suffer some overall debuff, lose a vital special ability or must reroll some of their successful dice.

The disadvantaged player cannot place any of the terrain for this battle

The disadvantaged player loses some of this unit choices

Vital Locations: This is where a lot of balancing will come in. A certain number of themed location sets need to be designed. For each setting just a bunch of names for important objectives, people, equipment or locations will do. These are arranged into sets of three. Each location has a Campaign turn in which it is available for capture. Each campaign turn should give out 3-5 locations, depending on how many players there are available (roughly one third the number of players in total). Each location should have a unique amount of points awarded to it, and each ampaign turn should give an increasing number of points in total.

Nonvital locations. These are awarded to each player who vied for a vital location, but did not win. Also they are awarded to players who did not vie for a vital location and won. They are worth more points than Trivial locations.

Trivial Locations. These are worth the least points and are awarded for losing a game when not vieing for a vital location.

Location combinations. Different set of vital location give bonus points to those who control them. These could be collections of suit or a string. Example// One Factory, One Power Plant and a Space Port gives a bonus of +50% points, while simply having all the Factories also gives a +50% points bonus.

The first 2/3rds of the campaign turns should be aabout giving out locations. The last 1/3rd should change the rewards instead to the opportunity to swap one of your locations for one of your opponents.  
OR  
The players first game is based on the bidding and capture rules above and additional games only allow the winner to swap a single location
