LEGO Wargame Ideas

Drover; Lego the gathering.

1-collect a number of Lego pieces based on a resource generation capability (think lands)

2-create facilities, forces, and modifiers using pieces, perhaps with time as an additional resource type.

3-tap forces to engage the enemy, destroying, damaging, etc their stuff

benefits

\- no need to worry about movement, initiative, distance, order, etc

\- new Lego pieces add attributes, and can be mapped to already understood magic terms, eg flying, trample, banding, etc

\- no need to mix pieces, both players have play area

\- provides a cool deck building element, eg no more than 500 connectors of Lego

\- can add new rules for new pieces like magic make up shit each release with little need to evaluate against all existing rules

Resources

- Resources should be based off bricks or time, or both

- Perhaps one brick variety is used as resources

Stats and Numbers

- Use studs as a variable/stat number

- Create small piles next to a construction to denote its strength (more studs is higher value)

Minifigs

- Each minifig should be important

- goals and rules should treat minifigs differently, they are not just another brick

Movement

- Even with arbitrary placement there is still two states. Adjacent or Not adjacent

- No movement measuring

- Facing of contructions matters

- After a vehicle moves it is fixed for this round

- Turning/Aligning phase takes place after movement round

- Perhaps only parts of a vehicle mounted on turntables can move during the targeting phase

Building

- Must be involved during the game

- Players use their own collections, or share a joint collection

- Brick mixing should be avoided in all rules

- Size, shape and design of the construction should matter

- Building better should be as important as building faster

- Random bricks from a “pool” to build something with

- Some bricks have special purposes

Damage

- shouldn’t be a modifier counting system (+3-5+2\*7+2-1=damage)

- If such a system can be developed, it must be very simple (whole numbers only, small numbers, limited differing values)

Initiative

- Smaller vehicles can interrupt larger ones

- Larger vehicles must move first

Terrain

- Baseplates chould be used to build fixed structures on

- Objective terrain?

- Terrain to block line of sight

Goals

- Take and hold locations

- Destroy objects

- “Frag” count. Players fight until so many vehicles have been destroyed

- Until the death. Bricks cannot be used more than once
