Necromunda Campaign ideas

Aim: To develop a campaign system that is finished in 6 weeks, keeps MOST of the rules form original necromunda(or another wargame) intact, and keeps all players in the game until the very end. The system should be auto balancing, but without providing simple buffs. Winning players should be forced into situations where they must win against the odds, but also a clear winner must be shown after 6 play sessions. Players may play different amount of sessions, and playing more matches should be promoted. A easy to understand visual aid should be available to show who is in danger of winning.

This campaign should not be designed to push a narritive, simply to give players goals to work towards and an opportunity to be crowned the winner after 6 sessions.

Star members: At the end of each fight, each player rewards the coolest or most effective member of their opponents gang, describing why. These gang members earn an additional 5 experience points

Objectives for new scenarios:

Conserve: Primary objective complete if you failed no ammo rolls this fight, Secondary Objective complete if you only failed one ammo roll.  
Reveal this as your objective to force and opponent to reroll a passed ammo check

Kill: Pri: Kill one of the enemy gang. Sec: Take 3 enemies out of action  
Reveal this as your objective to reroll one to-wound roll

Bushwack: Pri: Take out the designated leader for your opponents gang in hth. Secondary: take out any two enemies in hth combat  
Reveal this as your objective to reroll all your attack dice

Salvage: Pri: Collect 3 objective tokens. Secondary: Collect 1 objective tokens  
Reveal this as your objective to give one of your fighters a 4+ armor save.

Hold: Pri: Retain control of 2 objective locations. Sec: Retain control of 1 objective locations  
Reveal this as your objective in any end phase to move one of your fighters 6”

Deny: Pri: Your opponent is restricted to one quarter of the board. Sec: Your opponent is restricted to half the board.  
Reveal this to force your opponent to reroll one passed to hit roll

Rout: Pri: Your opponent flees. Sec: 2 or more enemies fail leadership tests  
Reveal this as your objective to force your opponent to reroll one passed leadership test (or bottle roll)

Scorch: Pri: Inflict damage on all three objective locations. Sec: Inflict damage on one objective location  
Reveal this to reduce any attack to only one wound regardless of damage and strength stats

Deployment Options

Single blind. One gang gets tokens equal to 50% more than their number of figures to deploy on the board. Once they are all deployed the other player gets fighters equal to 25% more than their opponent has to deploy. Then the first player can deploy his fighters by replacing tokens with a fighter.

Bidding. Both players bid how many fighters they will dedicate to the battle. The player who bids the highest number must set up first. The bid is blind to both players until reveal.

One by one. Players take it in turns deploying fighters to the board. Fighters cannot be placed within 8” of an opposing fighter unless they are in base contact with a friendly fighter.

Boardsides. One player can deploy up to the centre of the table. The other can deploy anywhere on the board as long as he is outside 8” of the enemy.

Lineup. Both players place a 12” line on the board. They can deploy their fighters only onto this line.

Movement Varients

Two Boards. The table is divided down the middle. Each turn the player may only use the fighters on one board half. Recovery is still rolled across the whole board.

Lone Gun. Each turn a player can only use one fighter to move and fire. A token is then placed next to this fighter and he cannot be used until all fighters on this team have been used. Simulates more similtaneous movment.

Precincts. Gangs can ask for sponsorship to move into different precincts. If they gain outlaw status they must move to a new precinct. The "ruling" gang has control over a territory that gives them several options for taxing the other gangs in the precinct.

Efficency of the tax is lessened by the more gangs that come to stay in the precinct. Eventually the precinct leader will have to deny newcomers.

Rare trade items are given out from a deck of cards. This means the rariity of items is preserved, but also that rare trade items are limited in supply. Players add rare trade items to fighters before a game, but those items also always add their cost to the gang rating. Any items on killed fighters are removed instantly back to the deck.

Fighters who are down at the end of a fight get injured on a 4+. Fighters who are taken "Out of Action" during a fight are dead on a roll of 1, and are Injured on a 2+. Injuries are not as severe, giving penalties only after the second injury. The first roll indicates which injury the fighter has, and the second injury increases the severity of the first injury. Some injuries have less effect but will retire the ganger faster with consecutive injuries.

Retired gangers can work territory and their equipment is kept by the gang. They must still be fed, but cannot fight. Logistical skills no longer operate.

Income step is only completed once per campaign turn, probably once a week. The amount of income collected is greater, and ruling gangs get a cut of their underlings profits.

Types of ruling government/organisation- Each should give a bonus to the amount earnt by ruling gangs/underling gangs, and have a different way to keep a hold on leadership.

Stage 1: Ruling gang gets the most benifit. Underlings suffer a penalty. Easiest way to be stolen

Stage 2: Ruling gang gets a small benifit and the Underlings suffer no penalty. Moderatly defendable

Stage 3: Ruling gang and underlings gets no benifit. Moderate bonus to protection

Stage 4: Ruling gang gives up all its tax and underling gangs get a bonus. Very difficult to remove the ruling gang from power.

Kingpin: THe ruling gang rules through protection. Some of their gangers must be dedicated to protection/enforcer roles and thus cannot collect incomes.

Tyrant: The Ruling gangs leaser rules by force alone. To continue ruling he must participate in every fight the gang has. If the leader is killed then the gang is ousted from leadership

Anarchy: There is no leadership in this precinct. The gang who fights the most battles wins the tax from the other gangs. The gang that controls the Ruling Territory still can change the ruling style.

Food Embargo

Territory Swap. Instead of stealing terriory, gangs who win by a large margin must swap a territory of their choice with one of the opponents.

Options that are out:

Round Robin, Elimination, Open Ended, Gang Rating ranking, Most Terriotries Held ranking

Mechanics that might work:

Lead player must pay out to the lowest player (King pays the Fool), promoting being in second place until the end.

Special territories given each session based on differnt acheivements. Players must play differntly each session. Players can see all of the acheivements before the campaign starts.

Players bet on others game outcome. Like footy tipping. Player who bets correctly wins overall. This may mean losing some of your games when every other player tips for you to win. It may also end up with both players in one fight trying to lose.

Cumulative score. Players are matched against others with similar gang ratings. Points won by the lowest rated gangs are worth just as much as the higher gangs get. Deliberatly having a lower rating might be a valid strategy.

Suited victories. Players fight to collect sets of victories. You must have a victory against one opponent in the bottom half, one against the top half and one against the current hardest opponent.

Secret Target. Players are given a secret list of three gangs. THey must have victories against two of these, and losses against one other.

Self Balancing. Players can opt out of receiving income or experience for campaign points.

Time trial. If no players have won by the end of 6 sessions, the house wins. Combinations of the above can be used allong with alliance mechanics.

To the edge. Players who are closer to winning get conseutivly worse penalties, promoting gangs to not advance until in a position of win overall.

Objectivism. Gangs earn campaign points by acheiving goals rather than winning battles.

Bring a friend. Each time you win, you must also reward another player of your choice

Handicap. Playing a harder opponents gives you an advantage. You gain more points even when you lose. (underdog bonus).

One handed. You may chose to play with a handicap for more campaign points if you win.

Bigger guns. You may chose to play with an advantage but gain no points if you win.

Players are given a number of "advantage Points" these can be spent during the 6 weeks. The benifit you get from them increases over the 6 weeks. Unspent AP give bonus campaign points. Each week a new unknown trade is revealed.

New Scenarios need to be written THey must have clear seconary and primary goals and very interesting set up rules. Lowest gang rating always choses scenario while the larger decides if they are attacker or defender.
