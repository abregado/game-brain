Old necromunda: Start plain and develop randomly. Some fighters become renown.

Pros: Powerful fighters stand out from the crowd.

Cons: Every fighter is unique and therefore a gang is very complex. Hard to guess what each fighter has when playing against them.

New Necromunda: Build a list that determines what everyone can be right at the start.

Pros: Easier to “read” a gang. More gang flavour with new gangs.

Cons: Far less feeling of getting to know your gang. No diamonds in the rough.

Idea 1:

As gang members level up, they get ghost upgrades to their statistics. These statistics do not take effect, instead accrue as “potential”.

Potential is rolled randomly each time a fighter levels up.

Each gang house has a set of Training archetypes. Each Archetype requires a different amount of potential.

After each battle, one Training credit is generated by the gang.

After a battle, any amount of credits can be spent to send fighters away for a single battle. One credit per fighter.

When the fighter returns, roll actual increases for each potential, and choose a special skill package for them based on the Training archetype.

Fighters which already have a training package will gain new increases at a slower rate, but also get upgrades which are focused on the package.
