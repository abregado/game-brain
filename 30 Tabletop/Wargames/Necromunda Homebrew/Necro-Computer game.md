Necromunda computer game

3d viewpoint

Gang/squad based

Local persistant worlds

Two level gameplay  
Campaign  
Tactical

Random Experienced based upgrades

Simple weapon choices at start

Battle Game

Randomly generated Mission. Pre-drawn maps

Attacker chooses mission type, defender does not know until combat starts

Gangs/Squads are deployed within zones on the table (secret deployment)

Turns

Each player moves their units from a first person perspective. Once moved and a facing is chosen, the unit is fixed for the remained of the turn.  
A range of movement is placed to show where you will be able to move. Players can position their units indefinatly to try different options until fixing them in place.

Once all fighters are moved. Players choose which models will fire (targets are chosen by game mechanics, closest target, cover)

Firing attacks are calculated using code. The graphics are only used to display results.

Cinematic camera angles are used to display attack results.

Melee combat is also calculated.

Recovery is then calculated

Campaign Game

Players join a server and their gangs stats are recorded server side. Campaign lasts until one gang achieves victory.

Player gangs are persistent even after a player leaves the Lobby. After re-log they can continue with the gang unless someone has won while they are away.

When someone wins all gangs are deleted from the server. A new round starts.

Servers will have to be capable of hosting the Camapign Lobby and multiple battle sessions.

(campaign lobby could be kept server side, while battles are hosted client side, this would increase opporunitiy for hacking and cheating)
