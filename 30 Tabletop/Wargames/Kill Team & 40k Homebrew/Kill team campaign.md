Agenda Cards

### Bounty Hunter

Reveal when an enemy model is taken out of action. Place a token in contact with its base before removing the model from the battlefield. Score 1 victory point if a model from your kill team ends a turn in contact with any of these markers. Remove one of those markers the model is in contact with. You may do this up to 3 times.

Or

Reveal at the end of the game if you took one of your opponents Specialists out of action.

### Protect

Reveal at the beginning of a turn, when one of your units is within 2” of an objective. If that unit is within 2” of that objective at the end of the game, gain 2 VP.

or

Reveal at the end of the game if one of your units is controlling an objective alone. Gain 1 VP.

### Infiltrate

Reveal at the end of the game if one of your units is not visible to the enemy. Gain 1 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of the game and select one of your units. If that unit is uninjured and not visible to any of your opponents units at the end of the game, gain 3 VP.

### Breakthrough

Reveal at the end of the game if one of your units is within 6” of your opponents spawn edge. Gain 1 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of a turn when two of your units are within 6” of your opponents spawn edge. If they are both still within 6” at the end of the game, gain 3 VP.

### Area Denial

Reveal at the end of the game if two of your units are on your opponent's side of the board. Gain 1 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of a turn in which you control an objective. If you still control the objective at the end of the turn, it is destroyed.

### Search

Reveal at the end of the game if you controlled all of the objectives at some point in the game. Gain 2 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of the game. Place a token on one objective. This marker is only worth points to your team. It can still be controlled by the opponents team, but is not worth any points.

### Incite Subordination

Reveal at the start of a turn in which an opponent's unit is within 3” of an objective. Until the end of the game, or until that unit is taken out of action, you gain 1 VP at the end of each turn in which that unit is not within 3” of the objective.

Or

Reveal at the end of the game if the opponent's leader is out of action. Gain 1 VP.

### Deploy Equipment

Reveal at the start of a turn in which you control an Objective. Add a token to that objective. If you control that objective at the end of the game, gain 1 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of a turn in which you control an objective. Place a marker next to the objective, which counts as one of your models for the purposes of controlling it. Destroy the marker if your opponent gains control of the objective.

### Feint

Reveal at the end of the game if you and your opponent are tied for control of an objective. You gain control of that objective and earn 1 additional VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of a turn in which you control an objective. Choose one objective you control and add a marker. At the end of the game, you gain 2 VP if you never lost control of that objective.

### Takedown

Reveal at the start of a turn after you have taken one of your opponents models out of action. Choose another model in your opponents roster. If that model is out of action at the end of the game, gain 2 VP.

Or

Reveal at the start of the game. If your leader is not out of action at the end of the game, gain 1 VP.

### Steal Plans

Reveal at the end of a turn in which one of your models is in Melee combat with your opponents leader. Gain 2 VP if that model is still alive at the end of the game.

or

Reveal at the end of the game. If your opponents leader is out of action gain 1 VP.

### Overextend

Reveal at the start of the game. If your opponent does not have any figures within 3” of your table edge, gain 2 VP.

Or

Reveal at the end of the game. If all of your opponents remaining units have Flesh wounds, gain 1 VP.

## Campaign Goal

Each card has two of 5 resources on it. If the player scores VP from the card, then they can gain one of the two resources. To win a campaign, a kill team must collect each of the 5 resources.

Set up terrain

Goals of campaign

- Drop in play

- Players don't own figures but can affect the development of the fighters

- Visible progress

- Simple rules

- Unit enhancement

- Promote a different scenario each time

- Quick games (\<100 PTS) still count

- No backwards progression

Idea 1

Any army type not represented on the map can still fight to open up the final mission.

Each player must collect the four key victories themselves.

After each battle, all teams in the map roll a d6. The current players team can add +2. The team with the lowest has one territory destabilized and the team with the highest can destabilze one territory of thier choice, take one adjacent destabilized territory, or stabilize one of thier territories.

The strongholds can't be destabilized.

Having only stabilized territories gives access to once per battle bonuses.
