# Blackstone Fortress – summary of my ideas, experiments and rules

One consolidated overview of everything I've written about Blackstone Fortress. Each idea appears once, even where I came back to it in several notes.

**Sources:** Blackstone House rules, Blackstone challenges, Blackstone Drukari, Blackstone Campaign App, plus Blackstone-related thoughts in [[Fixing Cursed City]] and 5parsecs mods.

---

## 1. House rules

- **Maglev portal escape:** To call the maglev transport, roll a D12 action die. On a fail, that portal is locked as an escape route. The last remaining portal is an automatic success. Hostiles can still appear from locked portals.
- **Chaos Space Marine save:** A save is a 17+ on a D20.
- **Resource card limit:** Only one Resource Card per Hero or Hostile action. Passive and persistent resources are exempt.
- **AI targeting:** If an AI ranged attack has several non-adjacent valid targets, targets not in cover come first, even when a closer Explorer is behind cover.

## 2. Mission design ideas

**Conditions**
- Trip hazard. Make an agility roll after spending a dice on movement. On a fail, the hero cannot spend dice on movement until next turn.
- Debris. It costs 2 movement to enter a hex with a cover hexside
- Fog. Ranged attacks require a dice 1 higher
- Slippery. Melee attacks require a dice 1 higher
- No cover. Cover hexsides do not grant cover
- Blackout. Maximum range is 3 hexes
- Damnation. Gaining inspiration requires spending 1 additional point
- Tight spaces. Only one hero can occupy a corridor hex at a time. Heroes cannot move through each other on corridor hexes.

**Token missions**
- Search every token.
- Bring-back: search tokens, then return to the start, one token per hero. 
- Connect cables: search tokens to find out whether each is a plug or a socket. Odds to Odds, Evens to Evens. One hero at each end, one player spends a 1+ dice to roll agility. Success, both tokens are gathered.
- Whack-a-mole: tokens change every turn and must be searched in the right order.
- Damage each token in melee.
- Activate several tokens in the same turn.
- Wipe out a group to spawn a token.
- Time limit to reach a token.
- Keep enemies away from tokens, or kill enemies near them.

**Reinforcement strategies**
- Closest to heroes
- Scattered/Random
- Closest to Search tokens

**Exit types**
- Farthest point.
- Random, decided at the start.
- Random, decided when the goal is completed.
- The start zone.
- Closeable exits to stem reinforcements, return to start
- Increasing chances when searched, big wave when found. Second last fail triggers wave because Last one is automatically it.

**Enemy effect triggers**
- When a hero moves adjacent.
- When a die is spent on search, move or heal.
- After being attacked.
- When no hero is in vision.
- When killed.

## 3. Character ideas

- **Character book:** per-character story events, a chart of events to cross off, and items that come from the book.
- **Targeting token special:** placed on a 6+, with rerolls against adjacent targets.
- **Inspiration:** switches between ranged and melee but costs only 1 Inspiration token.
- **Kill markers:** place a token on a kill, with a bonus for being in visual range.

## 4. Custom challenge cards

A set of expedition challenge cards that use leader order, Agility and Defense tests, and the Archeotech, Discovery and Encounter decks:

- Discard discovery or equipment cards for a D6 roll (4+) to avoid a wound.
- An Agility or Defense test where failing everyone disables the equipment cards for the expedition.
- Discard an Archeotech card to make several Agility or Defense rolls. A success draws a clue card.
- Discard an Archeotech card to keep a card that lets you draw a second Encounter card and choose between them.
- Players place their own wounds on the card, then roll Agility or Defense, and the wounds are shared among those who failed.
- Voluntarily suffer a wound so that Search and Summon need only a 3+ in the next combat.
- Draw an Encounter card. In the next combat where that enemy appears, both heroes and enemies of that type may reroll attack dice.
- Spend Archeotech for Agility rolls to earn a card that doubles the effective wounds when rolling for inspiration.

## 5. Drukari (Dark Eldar) faction homebrew

- **Wytches:**
  - Lasher with blast pistol: pushes heroes out of position and raises movement cost.
  - Shard net and spear: remove an action die.
  - Splinter pistol and close-combat weapon.
- **Troopers:**
  - Shredder: hits every target in the space.
  - Dark lance: ignores cover.
  - Splinter cannon and splinter rifle: the rifle hits every target in the hex.
- **Pain tokens (core idea):**
  - Splinter weapons deal no wounds or grievous wounds. They add Pain tokens to activation slots without blocking the die.
  - Each Pain token raises the minimum die needed for a Move action.
  - Pain tokens are removed by making a Move action without moving.
- **Space Marine tweaks:** always counts as in cover, and other heroes can't benefit from cover in the same hex.

## 7. What Blackstone does well (analysis)

From my Cursed City rework, these are the reasons I think Blackstone works better than Cursed City:

- Pure action with no downtime.
- You can see the whole map, so you can make a plan.
- Positioning matters.
- High activation dice rolls feel special.
- Abilities that change enemy movement.
- Overwhelming enemies and constant reinforcement make you want to get out fast.
- Environmental conditions.
- Abilities and items combo with each other.
- Cover is active and positional.
- More ranged combat.
- Optional sub-quests give more loot for more risk, and raise the threat level.
- Fights are short and fast, followed by an event.

**Weaknesses I see in both games:**
- No "take a risk" gameplay.
- Items usable almost every turn.
- Unlabelled boards.
- Forgettable conditions.
- Boring initiative.

## 8. Blackstone mechanics carried into other projects

- **Cursed City redesign:** the redesign borrows Blackstone's dice-in-slots activation, wounds and grievous wounds filling slots, and fast fights. It also adds monster dice, reactions and mission types. The details are in [[Fixing Cursed City]].

## 9. Replacements for wound rules
Wounds don't remove activation dice
Wounds reduce the value of dice placed into them by 2.
Players choose where to put dice.
Grevious wounds are not healable, so carry over.
Delve ends when one hero has full grevious wounds.
If you get full wounds, you go out of action. 
Turn one wound grevious to remove another normal wound
Critical damage still cause one wound, or a special token if it is a special weapon. Criticals are already harder to reduce.
Recuperate is a 2+
Normal wounds recover between fights
Recovery Roll between fights has chance to recover one grevious wound.

## 10. Make loot great again
The feeling of finding something cool in Blackstone is lacking. You find points, and spend them in town. You never find anything that can help you right now.

When you search, you draw a random item.
You can only have one item active at a time
When you draw the item, you can make it active, deactivating any other item you have
If you are inspired, you can have two items active
If a card says Discard, then make it inactive. It cannot be made active again this combat but the player keeps it.
If a card says Exhaust, then make it inactive.
You can equip for a 2+ dice, swapping your active cards
At the end of the combat, select 1 card to take to the next area, and one to sell. The rest are discarded.
Players cannot start a combat with more than 1 equipment card
The sold card is added to a loot pile to be sold after the expedition.
The goal of each expedition is to collect as much loot as possible to sell.
At the end of the delve, you sell everything you gained
Combine with "every combat gets harder" for a endless mode
Draw from the discovery deck instead for a chance to get inspired

## Other damage token ideas
It would be simpler if all wounds lowered the die by the same amount.

Toxin: Heal all Toxin wounds on a successful recovery roll.
Plague: You cannot heal a non-plague wound while you have any Plague wounds.
Successful recovery roll removes all Plague/Toxin tokens.
Hobbled: As wound, but reduces the Move stat by 1.
Blinded: you reroll successful ranged attacks while you have this.
Weakened: you reroll successful ranged attacks while you have this.
Panic: The dice here is reduced by 2, but can only be used to make an attack.
Curse: Before spending the dice in this slot declare what you will spend it on and roll it again. If you cannot afford the action, then you discard this dice and take no action.
Damned: You cannot spend Destiny dice while you have this wound.
Fire: Lowers the dice by 2. The dice can only be spent on movement.
Webbed/Retrained: You cannot move while you have this wound.

## Everything turns to drones
After a group is wiped out, flip its encounter card.
For every flipped card, roll reinforcements to spawn Drones.
If you cant place a drone, increase the security level
This continues until the mission is complete.
