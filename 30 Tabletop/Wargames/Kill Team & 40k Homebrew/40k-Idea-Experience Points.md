Experience for Squads

Movement: (1 max) reroll Running, Fleet and Difficult Terrain tests

WS: (max = squad minimum) Single to hit reroll per point

BS: (max = squad minimum) Single to hit reroll per point

Damage: (max = squad minimum) Single to wound reroll per point

Toughness: (max = squad minimum) Single to be wounded reroll per point

Wounds: (1 max) one Save is invulnerable per turn

Initiative: (1 max) + Init to all members

Attacks: (1 max) One first-strike per assault

Leadership: (1 max) Reroll Leadship tests

Experience Gain

Per Campaign.

3 Points per battle

-2 if destroyed

-1 if 50%

Increase every 3 points up to 12, then every 4 points until 32. then every 6 points.

Prebattle.

Each side choses one mission to be the attacker in and then rolls a dice. The highest roll plays their scenario choice as the attacker.

The squad sheets have the info on one side, and the units points cost/organization chart choice on the other.

Players take it in turns bidding to see who can fight with the lowest points values. The player who ends up with the lowest gets to use the scenario special.
