Prebattle - Scenarios

5 Objectives - Each one has 2 points for you to gain, and one for your opponent to gain

    Assassinate

    You gain 3 points if you destroy one of the opponents HQ unit cards completly. When you acheive this objective, reveal this card. If revealed at the end of the game your opponent scores 2 points if he has destroyed one of your HQ choices.

    Bait

    You gain 1 point per squad over 50% your opponent has in your deployment zone at the end of the game. Your opponent gains 2 points if he has a unit in his own deployemnt zone over 50% strength

    Destroy

    Each player places a small structure in their deployment zone. If you have this mission, you gain 3 points for desotrying your opponents structure. Your opponent gains 2 points for destroying yours.

    Raid

    You gain 2 Points for each unit above half strength in your opponents deployment zone (Up to 3 points total). Your opponent gets 1 point for each of your units in his deployment UNDER 50%. (up to 2 points)

    Hold

    Place 1 hold token down. You gain 2 points for each hold token or objective structure you have a Troop Unit within 6" of. Your opponent gains 1 point per one he controls. If objectives are destroyed they do not count for hold purposes.

   

Of you have Assassinate, you need to:

    Keep 1 unit in your deployment, incase opponent has Bait

    Destroy Opponenets Building, incase he has Destroy

    Keep your Building alive, incase he has Destroy

    Keep enemy units in your deployment under 50%, incase he has Raid

    If you have units in enemy deployment, keep them above 50% incase he has Bait

    Hold the centre point in case he as Hold

5 Deployment Types

    Orbital Landing - Attacker sets up in the middle of the field

    Battle Lines - deploy on long edges

    Entrenched Defender - defender sets up his army within 12" of the centre point of one table edge.

    Agrrevated Assault- take it in turns placing one unit on the table. Two units from each team may be placed in the second quarter, and a single unit may be placed in the third quarter. All other units must be placed in the first quarter.

    Strike - defender deploys in centre. Attacker on short edge. Objectives are placed in both halves.

5 Special Abilities for the smaller army

    Traitor - Your opponent reveals his objective card

    Advance Deployment - After deploying, you may move all your units 6"

    Artillery Support - Each enemy unit must pass an Initiative test or be unable to move for its first turn

    Preparation - Friendly Reserves gain +1 to their readyness rolls

    Misinformation - After all models have been deployed you may swap the position of two objectives

Game Setup Proceedure

1\) Set up the terrain and one Hold Objective Marker

2\) Make an objective deck with cards equal to the number of players +1. Reveal all unused cards to both players.

2\) Each player draws an objective from the deck and keeps it secretly to one side.

3\) Draw the special ability card and place it face up

4\) Select Forces as below

5\) Randomly determine a deployment strategy

6\) Set up and play, remembering to deploy your armies destroy objective in your deployment zone.

Force selection

0\) Each player selects a Force Organization Chart and places it in front of him.

1\) First player selects one unit card and places it face down, filling an compulsory slot before an optional slot.

2\) Second player selects one unit card and places it face down, filling an compulsory slot before an optional slot.

3\) The player with the least points places another unit card, or declares finished. Repeat until finished.

Unit Cards may be taken in reserve for a 25% discount. Deep Strikers that are taken at full strength still start in reserve.

Deep Strike Unit Cards may forfeit their Deep Strike and deploy normally for a 25% discount, or in reserve without Deep Strike for 50% discount.

Unit Cards that were destroyed in the last battle cannot be used in the subsequent battle.

Postbattle - Experience

Each squad, vehicle or character that was involved in the battle gains 3 exp for this theatre.

If the Unit card was reduced to half strength during the fight it loses 1 exp.

If the Unit card was destroyed it loses 2 exp and cannot fight in the next battle.

Every 3 experience the Unit Card gains one increase. These are shown with numbers underneath its statline. Each number represents a particular reroll.

Planetary Control

Each planet has 5 control points at base, and an additional control point for each race who has a presence on the planet.

2 Races: 7 Points

3 Races: 8 Points

4 Races: 9 Points

A Race controls a planet when it has 60% or more of the control points. They also gain a planetary bonus which effects all other races fighting on the planet.

If a player wins a fight he steals one point from his opponents control. If a 2v2 is played then 2 points are stolen, 3v3 = 3 points.

When a large battle like this occurs, the stolen points are grouped together into a 'Bastion'. These points cannot be stolen unless another large battle is played.
