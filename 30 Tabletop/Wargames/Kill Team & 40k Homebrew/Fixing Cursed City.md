# Fixing Cursed City

## Why did Blackstone work better?

- Only the action, no downtime

- See the whole map, know what to do, can make a plan

- Positioning matters

- High activation dice rolls felt more special

- Abilities that modify enemy movement

- Overwhelming enemies and constant reinforcement means you need to get out, quick

- Environmental conditions

- Abilities and items make combos

- Cover. Active and positional ways to protect yourself

- More ranged combat

## What doesnt work with either

- No “take a risk” gameplay. You do your thing and hope it's enough

- Items are not limited enough in use. Most things can be used every turn.

- Boards are not labelled for each setup

- Conditions are complex and forgettable

- Initiative, gambits and agility are uninteresting, you just try to go first

## Nice to haves 

- Different areas of the city feel different

- Environmental conditions every time

- Players can “double or nothing”

- Always have an effect on your turn

## Concrete ideas

### Immediate enemy respawn

Basic Enemies respawn immediately on death

### Consumable immediate loot

Either one extra activation dice next turn, a reroll, an inspiration point or an armor point

### Sub or optional quests

Ways to get more loot if you take more risks. Blackstone did this. Increase the threat level if you access these.

### Damage does not reduce dice, but time does

Wounds don’t remove dice, but completing sub or optional objectives adds tiredness tokens which do remove dice.

### Higher dice do Superactions

Either more attacks, or move and attack

### Character ability to light up boards

Light should affect heroes and enemies in some way

### Players react to enemies

Enemies go first, Weakest to strongest. Players can react after each move or attack with a single dice. Once all enemies have moved, players can spend all their remaining dice.

Saving dice to react to the larger enemies means taking more attacks from smaller ones. Saving dice to complete the objective means not dealing with enemies.

### Players spend one die before enemies activate

### Environmental effects every time

### Board effects

Each board can have an effect which is either active or inactive. Maybe only two effects per game

### Much smaller and shorter fights

As in Blackstone, one fight is quite fast. Then do an event.

### Character events between fights 

All characters place thier events in a deck and one is drawn out. The event is played out. Bonuses, abilities and items can be awarded.

### Greater difference between inside and outside

Which board a model is on, and which side the board is using should have an affect.

### Character/enemy abilities based on being inside or outside

### Global effect 50%

Board wide effect for inside OR outside. Tiles which do not match the effect just have none.

### Expandable with character packs.

Character packs contain one figure, rules, equipment cards and a set of situation cards

### Stealth based cover

Cover effects which hero is targeted. Better stealth allows heroes to be completely ignored. Stealth is broken if an enemy moves into your square.

### Deployment choices

The mission setup shows a number of locations where the heroes can deploy. Each player chooses for their own hero.

### Preemptive Escape zone(s)

One or more possible escape zones are placed at mission start. When the objective is compete, the exit is revealed and tough enemies begin spawning.

### Difficulty via less sunlight

As characters get more level ups, the starting light level is decreased. This means less time to finish the objective before things get very hard.

### Bigger difference between day and night

When night falls, the players should notice a difference immediately. All enemies respawn immediately? Options become unavailable? Less destiny dice? Equipment deactivates?

### Enemies engage at different ranges

All enemies are melee fighters. This means that heroes can wait until the enemies move into range and spend all their dice on attacks. Give some enemies ranged abilities

### Unengaged abilities for enemies

Larger enemies should each have a “if unengaged at the start of their activation” ability. These should be powerful enough to cause the players to think about putting themselves in danger.

### Permakill models

Generate the max number of enemies beforehand. Some abilities allow heroes to remove killed creatures from the pool. This should be very limited, 1-2 models max between all heroes.

### Once per fight abilities

Heroes should all get an ability which only works once per fight

### Passives

Each hero should get a passive ability which effects all heroes so that different team compositions are more interesting.

### Walls, doors and windows

Rules for barriers between tiles. Cover, sight blocking, slow movement. Use existing door markers, red for window, black for door. Walls between inside and outside are always considered blocked.

### Spendable Inspiration

An ultimate ability that can only be used when inspired, which sets you back to uninspired.

### Respawn points close in

Enemies reinforce from the nearest door that no hero is adjacent to. Heros can spread out to push enemies back and spread them out.

### Emergency bug out

The heroes can always choose to leave without the prize.

### Same number of activation dice regardless of player count

6 destiny and 16 activation dice are rolled.

4p - 4 dice each

5p - 3 dice each

3p - 5 dice each - 1 destiny

2p - 8 dice each

### Red activation dice

When you take a wound you replace a white activation dice with a red dice. After rolling, remove all dice that match a red dice.

### Monsters also roll dice

Roll one D6 per monster (more for larger monsters or boss monsters). Then allocate the dice to each monster card based on an Inteligence score. Highest dice to the highest intelligence, down to the lowest. This also means that good monsters get buffed by lower monsters being on the field. Remove one dice from the monster sheet if a monster is killed (?)

### Dice are also initiative

Heroes must spend 1’s, then monsters spend 1’s. Continue up to the highest numbers. Heroes always go first, so get to interrupt the monsters.

### Roll more, keep less

Heroes roll 6 dice but only keep 4. Taking damage lowers the number of dice rolled, but they still only keep 4. This means the first few hits dont effect the number of actions that the heroes get. Being Inspired could mean rolling more dice, or keeping more dice. Different heroes could have different numbers of “damage free” dice such as:

- One dice slot that is always rolled

- One dice slot that does not give a dice, but can always be filled

- Dice slots that hold a specific number even if damaged

- Dice slots that lower the dice placed in them to a minimum of 1

- Dice slots that can accept a Destiny dice

Unkept dice could also be used to power up equipment or give rerolls, or swapped with Destiny Dice. Perhaps unkept dice of higher values could give passive bonuses for the turn such as:

- Reroll on attack one dice

- Extra move per move action

- Reroll on defend one dice

### New Behavior dice

Each monster card has a chart with up to 6 entries. Each chart also has 2 columns. The columns have a condition like “Hero adjacent”, “has been wounded”, “Hero in sight” or “Inside/Outside”. When a monster spends a dice, check the condition and complete the action shown. When spending a dice, do the model closest to the heroes first, then leave the dice next to the model to show it acted.

### Condition tokens fill damage spaces

The game includes:

8 cursed or trapped tokens

8 diseased tokens

4 fatigued tokens

4 buried tokens

**Fatigued tokens**

You can only have one. They are placed from the left. When you make a Recover roll, you can remove one Fatigue token automatically.

**Diseased tokens**

**Cursed tokens**

Add from the left. The dice that is placed here has its value reduced by 2, to a minimum of 1.

**Trapped Tokens**

Add from the left. The dice placed here cannot be used for Move (1+) or Run (3+) actions

**Wound Tokens**

Add from the right. Do not block dice being placed.

**Grievous Wounds**

Replaces one light wound, or added from right. Blocks a dice from being placed here.

# Level Generators

## Manor

Three inside tiles placed right next to each other without doors. Place three doors touching the edge. Randomly place three areas touching those doors.

### Variant: Platz

Same as manor but start with three outdoor areas.

### Variant: Street

Place the three areas in a straight line. The three areas must all be outdoor areas. The additional rooms are always indoor areas.

### Missions

**Ward**: Place a goal and lychgate in each of the outdoor areas. Start from the central area. Extract from any of the starting areas. When searching the goal in a room, succeed on a Vitality check to remove it. Then move the lychgate in the room next to the closest lychgate. If this is the final lychgate, remove it.

**Search**: Three goals in the central rooms. After each search action, Roll an Agility check, adding 1 to the quest tracker for a success and 2 for a critical success. Then move the goal to the farthest space that is not adjacent to any hero or goal marker. Goal is players \* 3 points.

**Hunt**: Draw 4 encounter cards instead of 3. The model type with the least quantity is the hunted type. Place the hunted models in the central area. Then randomize in which outer room each of the remaining models. Players place the landing zone where they like, attached to any of the outer areas. Hunted models do not have a pool, and are automatically terminated. Once the hunted models are removed, randomize where the extraction zone appears.

**Sunrise Holdout:** Only take 2 encounters for this mission but enemies start Inspired. The Nightfall marker starts on the 4th space, and counts backwards. When it reaches the start, enemies are uninspired and nightfall ends.

# Changes

## Standard Actions

| Name     | Dice | Effect                                                              |
|----------|------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Step     | 1+   | Move 1 tile                                                         |
| Run      | 3+   | Move 3 tiles                                                        |
| Recover  | 1+   | Make a Vitality roll. Success = Heal 1. Critical = Heal 2           |
| Interact | 4+   | See Scenario for effect                                             |
| Extract  | 4+   | When in an extraction zone, safely remove the model from the board. |

## Rolling Activation dice

Your pool now consists of Activation Dice (white) and Injury Dice (red). Roll them all together. Any Injury dice that rolled the same number as any other dice are discarded this round. Any remaining dice as placed on your character sheet to be spent on actions.

## Moving and Pinning

All heroes move 1 or 3 tiles, based on the dice they spend.

When a model moves into a space adjacent to a hostile that does not have a Stealth token, it ends its movement. This is called Pinning.

A model adjacent to a hostile model can move away when it starts another move action, stopping if it enters a new space adjacent to a hostile model. This includes the model it is currently adjacent to.

## Combat Changes

All hero attacks deal 1 damage on success, or 3 on critical.

Monsters have an activation order shown on their sheet. They activate from lowest to highest.

If a monster can see a hero and is not engaged at the start of its activation, it first uses its non-engaged ability.

Monsters always move and attack as their activation.

Monsters engage first the nearest character, prioritising those with a Loud token, and deprioritizing those with a Stealth token.

Their attack range and dice as shown on their card.

Heroes roll defense as normal to counter.

Success or critical success deals one wound (see below)

Critical success activates the creatures special damage, which is shown on their card.

If an enemy model is killed, it is placed back into the reinforcement pool.

Ranged actions cannot be made if your character is engaged

## Injury Changes

When you take a wound, replace one Activation dice in your pool with an injury dice.

If you have no Activation dice, remove one Injury dice from your pool. If you have no dice in your pool, your character is dead.

## Terminate

If a weapon or ability says to Terminate an enemy, then it is killed and not returned to the reinforcement pool.

## Reactions

After an enemy model has completed its move or attack, each hero can choose an unused Activation dice (not an injury or destiny dice) and say they are making a reaction. After all heroes have declared which dice they are using, resolve each action. Any attacks may only be made against the enemy that just completed its activation. All declared reactions spend their activation dice, regardless of if an action was completed.

## Doors and Windows

Doors and Windows are always placed so they touch at least three squares.

## Destiny

Any one hero cannot use more than 2 destiny dice

## Phases

Destiny Phase: Roll all dice

Monster Phase: Activate monsters (priority then distance, smallest to highest)

Hero Phase: Heroes spend remaining dice

Reinforcement Phase

End/Event Phase

## Reinforcement Phase

Roll a D6 for each model in the pool. On a 5+ it is spawned. First check if any of its special Reinforcement rules come into effect. If not it is placed at a random Portal.

# Scenario 1 - House Ritual

## Setup

Draw three Exploration cards and place their corresponding tiles together in the middle of the board, which their Indoor sides face up. Place a locked door between each room. Place D3 open doors on the edge of this Indoor area. Place a window on the edge of any room that does not have a door.

Draw 5 Exploration cards and place their Outdoor sides so that all are connected to each other and all three Indoor rooms are connected to the outdoor area. If it isnt possible, draw additional Outdoor areas.

Place 3 portals roughly equidistant around the outdoor edge of the Outdoor area.

Draw Three Encounter cards and place the models from one of each card into each indoor area.

Place one Quest Marker into each of the rooms. Be sure the 3 markers are at least 5 squares away from each other.

Phase 1: Prepare ritual

This phase ends during the End Phase when all 4 markers are flipped over

Prepare (4+): When in a square with a Quest Marker, make an Agility roll. On a success, flip it over.

End phase on Complete: Draw an additional Encounter card and add the models to the pool.

Activate Ritual

This phase ends during the End Phase when all 4 markers are flipped over

Activate (4+): When in a square with a Quest Marker, make a Vitality roll. On a success, flip it over.

End phase on Complete: Draw an additional Encounter card and add the models to the pool.

Escape

This phase ends when all heroes have extracted or died

First End phase: Randomly determine one Outdoor area. Place the extraction zone on that tile, as far from any Quest Markers as possible.
