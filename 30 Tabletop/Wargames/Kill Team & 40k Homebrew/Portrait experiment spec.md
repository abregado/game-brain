## Portrait experiment spec

### Goal

The aim is to take existing Warhammer models, zoom in on their face and use expression and detail to give them character. The portraits created in this way will be used in a character selector interface.



The portraits will be desaturated until hovered or selected. Once selected they enlarge to show the full 2:1 size.

### Delivered items

- 5 blob blockouts with background color

- 3 silhouette + outline sketch with background color

- 1 detail exploration sketch of grimdark + Tatjana style

### Requirements of the finals (not what you are making in the experiment)

- Portraits in 2:1 aspect (tall)

- Lineart + solid color

- Backgrounds simple color and shape, maybe gradient

- Low detail except on face and defining features

- When the portraits are cropped and placed next to each other in the character selector, they should be distinguishable.

### Concerns

Low detail and Grungy/Dirty/Grimdark might not mesh at all. It might just end up looking like Punk, which isn’t the target.
