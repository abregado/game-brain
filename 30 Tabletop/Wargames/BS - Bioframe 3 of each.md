# Movement

Three Ideas for movement which promotes players moving over the tabletop a lot

## Flanking/Facing

Frame have a facing and if you are outside their front arc (90 degrees) then you gain a bonus to your damage. Players will constantly want to move around to try and flank their targets.

## Player moves you when you take damage

Each time you are hit, you can react and move your Unit, but if you dont, the opponent gets to move your Unit.

## Movement makes you tough

Your Units are incredibly weak unless they move

## After a Unit moves into a Threatening position, the target would be mad to stay still

Make shooting damage so powerful that targets need to move out of the way when threatened.

## Hidden orders based movement that is very “straight liney”

Players place orders on all units before anything moves. This can create move movement but also things can move stragely and seem like they are not reacting to eachtoerh.

# Shooting

Three ideas for shooting that emulate a fast mecha genre or help with the movement problem.

## Instant destruction

Firing from the perfect location instantly kills enemy units but they also have plenty of time to react before you can fire. The Firer must be in a perfect position to fire.

## Cover 4 lyfe

Being in cover makes your Units much tougher, so players will move to attack targets where they are not benefiting from cover. Pick one of these:

- Line cover: Units behind but also touching cover lines gain cover

- Area cover: Units on a base of cover gain its effects regardless of how much of the model can be seen

- Area Number between: If there is one stand of cover between the target and the firer then the target is in cover, if there are two stands of cover then the target is obscured.

- Obstacles only: Cover blocks line of sight but there is no bonus to defense. All or nothing.
