Campaign mechanic

Take-Take mechanic

Limited resources, when you win some resources, somebody else must loose them.

Games tend to go on for a long time until someone gets a winning streak

Examples - Monopoly

No loss mechanic

Each “win” gives a player resources, resources can never be taken from a player

Games have almost fixed length

Examples – Settlers of Catan

Collectors mechanic

Each player must earn one of each resource types
