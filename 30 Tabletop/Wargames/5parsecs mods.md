Setup

Place 6 numbered tokens on the board.. Two are placed on random table edges to begin with. The others 8” away from each other.

Behaviors are too simple and static. They don't generate meaningful choices for the player.

Enemy loadouts don't generate meaningful choices for the player.

Player actions don’t change what the opponents do.

Opponents dont react to players, players dont react to opponents.

Models behind cover should be pretty safe.

5parsecs mods

No battles outside the battle phase

Sidekick characters? If your second character goes into sickbay the other one comes out?

Each planet type has a set of factions and units

Red zone and black zone are on special planets

Different xenos planets

Invasion armies

Model to stat block linking

App with setting 2 things and rolling the rest

Board segment setup using multiple divisions

Blackstone hex rules

App flow that asks you first which models you have and builds a collection of possible crew, rivals and invasion forces.

Shooting

No ranges

2+skill dice+rate of fire

Area: stun targets within 2”

Critical: 6 on hit, add one stun token

Heavy: -1 dice if moved

Impact: second stun if no damage

Piercing: no bonus from armor

Snap shot: +1 dice at close range

Terror: -1 to morale test if target killed

Skill Dice

ROF dice

Toughness Dice

Reaction dice

Cover Dice

Reaction not defeated on a target in cover then Player chooses, ignore all damage or:

Defensive. unit goes to ground

Cautious. unit moves 3” away toward cover

Beast: unit moves full movement out of sight

Tactical: unit immediately returns fire, or goes to ground if out of range

Aggressive: unit moves 2” toward firer into cover

Rampaging: unit moves full movement toward firer.

When selecting a target, Prioritize:(or at start of turn mark priority target)

Defensive: Least stun tokens

Cautious: Easiest to hit/weakest

Tactical: highest combat/reaction skill

Aggressive: Most stun tokens

Rampaging: closest target to a friendly

Beast: target closest to most friendlys

X: Target has already moved

X: Highest reaction

X: Least toughness

When moving:

Keep 12” distance.

Always end closer to an opponent.

Never stand in the open willingly

Stand still and Aim if there is a target

Stay out of sight of opponents

Stay as close to the objective as possible

Stay within 3” of already moved friendly

Minimize units that can see you

Behavior should reveal itself over the game

Behavior should change based on morale

Player can change enemy behavior.

Enemies should have their own objectives

Player should sometimes start in the center of the board

Even odds are Target within 6” in open toughness 3 damage 0

Choice of Stun the target and it goes to ground, or no damage

Choice of remove target or add stun token to nearby enemy

If you are in cover, the worst you can get is Stunned?
