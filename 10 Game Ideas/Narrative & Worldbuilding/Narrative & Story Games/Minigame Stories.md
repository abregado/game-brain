Two people in a train, or stagecoach or restaurant. They are facing each other. The player can see out the window but the characters cannot.

Player selects which character to control the dialog of.

During the conversation it can be seen by the player that someone is watching them through the window from a distance. Either a horse rider, sniper, watcher from another window, ect.

Dialog option types:

Questions to trigger exposition about the situation

Decision to set a property of a character eg:// Say what a character was doing as their hobby, or how they reacted to something.

Choice to set something up to happen at a later time eg//Waiter comes and you can order something which will arrive later

Choice to cause characters to change position

Choice that is clearly labelled to have a different outcome based on the speaker's mood

Choice that is clearly labelled to have a different outcome based on the interlocutor’s mood

The two characters seem to be friends but something has happened in the past which is currently causing friction.

They were both recently involved in an incident of their own making that saw another friend (or loved one) killed.

Guilt, frustration, Acceptance,

Twists

One of the men is trying to get the other killed

The player is a real effect in the game world eg:// guardian angel, god, ghost, ect

The player can die

Sources of Mystery

One man has a briefcase standing on the floor

The other has a large luggage on the luggage rack

There is a newspaper next to one of the men

Design constraints

The player should always understand where the branching occurs

Don’t give players a choice if it does not matter

The player cannot choose everything every time

There is no random chance

The story does not give you closure

Moments

One of the men is shot by the watcher outside

The man bends down to pick up the briefcase and narrowly escapes being shot. Security armor drops down over the window.

The man has the briefcase on the seat next to him. In anger he stands up just as the shooter fires. The briefcase is hit instead.

The door opens and a silhouette or reflection of the conductor/waiter can be seen. Player orders a drink/snack/nothing which affects when the waiter enters next.

One of the men picks up the newspaper, and the article they are reading is shown. Discussion is about the article.

The other person picks up the newspaper, so a different article is shown and the discussion is different.

“I never wanted to do this” responded to with “And I couldnt have done it without you”

A tavern with 4-6 customers. Players can initially select between two of them, but after the first run can select any of them.

Option types:

Clear saved choices from previous runs

Set a property about a character

Advance time

Start a dialog with another character

Once in a dialog, there isn’t always an option to end the dialog. You can get stuck with one person and then events unfold.

Characters

Priest. Talks to the player directly, hears your choices as the voice of god

Shady rogue type. Paranoid. Hears voices.

Big guy. Seems to do what he wants

Wizard. Has something dangerous in his bag

Bartender. Has the choice of who to serve and how long to keep them waiting.

On later runs your choices with characters you are not playing will play out as you did them last time. If the priest dies, then everything gets reset. **This seems like it will be difficult to implement.**

Moments

The player knows something will happen and wants to move seats but someone is blocking them from leaving their booth and wont shut up.

The player tries to leave the location and triggers a worse event

Three high schoolers are sitting in detention. The teacher is behind the camera. The player can choose one of two high schoolers to control. After the first run, the third becomes controllable. The teacher goes out several times, during which the characters can talk to each other. While the teacher is there, you can only examine objects and documents from the character you are controlling.

Twists.

Realizing one of the characters is imaginary. But which one?

Moments.

Taking no risks just ends the detention and therefore the story. Nothing happens.

The teacher leaves and two characters swap places. The Teacher notices when they get back.

Usage of you in a way that can be read to mean you (specific) or you (all) to create suspicion that there is only one person here.

A council meeting room in a village. There are several villagers, each acting as heads of various village facets. There are also several adventurers. An intro discussion takes place, introducing the imminent attack on the village. The player selects a hero to control the dialog of. As they make choices, the heroes are sent off to prepare the villagers. Once all heroes are distributed, a Fallout style outro plays, explaining what happens.

Twist.

The last hero can always choose to escape the battle.

One of the heroes is a traitor. In the ending cutscene when not played it tells that they are disintegrated or eaten, but that isn’t the truth.

Choices

The controlled character decides to recount a previous act, which sets the truth.

Responding to accusations or comments from other heroes sets your current character's personality.

Tech

Dialog system. Choices must get tagged if they are based on personality or disposition.

Choice tree demonstration at end.

Method of displaying personality and disposition

Cut to different view/full screen illustration
