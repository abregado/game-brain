Story Starters: situations

He sits high above. What he sees below him stretches out beyond the horizon. His attention is shifted to a light pain covering his arms. The skin between where the feathers meet is cracked and in places bleeding. For what he sees below he feels only contempt.

A new strain of virus appears on earth. Actually a whole family of viruses. Each only affects an area of earth. Having one makes you healthy but having two makes you very sick until one wins. Young and Old cannot survive the process. People don't travel or migrate knowing that they might die, or at best need to be at the mercy of the locals for a year while they adapt. Countries dissolve and turn into microstates wherever useful human borders overlap with these new viral borders
