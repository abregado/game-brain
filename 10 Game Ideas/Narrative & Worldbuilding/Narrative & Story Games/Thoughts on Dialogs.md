Dialogs

**Dos**

Very short sentences or get the point ASAP

Max 2 sentences before choice

Record important information that is said somewhere the player can see any time

Use portraits to show emotion

For incidental dialog Show when there are new lines and when there are not

**Donts**

No useless choices

No set of choices that are just variations on saying the same thing

Waiting for animations before continuing dialog

Have the player character say their opinion without letting the player choose (and make it count)
