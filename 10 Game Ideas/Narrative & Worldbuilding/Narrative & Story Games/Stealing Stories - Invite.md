**What is this?**

A roleplaying event where the players and storyteller come up with the story together.

**Where, When and What are the characters?**

A group of space explorers from the 35th century return to Earth to discover they have not only travelled through space but also through time. The 21st century earth they discover is very similar to our own with one difference: Reality is tearing itself apart.

Knots of instability are popping up across the globe, each strangely connected to a mundane object inside its perimeter. Inside, reality is damaged in strange and sometimes dangerous ways. To undo these knots, someone needs to get in, steal this object and remove it from the area.

Luckily, You are Liars.

Liars are perfect for the job. They not only can detect what is real when inside such a zone, but they wield the power to Lie to reality itself. This power allows them to make subtle changes to time, space, and even consciousness.

**Session Timeline**

| 45m | We will introduce our characters, go over a few rules and make some example Lies.         |
|-----|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1h  | Act 1. We will work together to imagine the challenges our heroes will face in the field. |
| 15m | A short break                                                                             |
| 1h  | Act 2. The Liars try to pull off their plan and something inevitably goes wrong.          |
| 15m | A short break                                                                             |
| 45m | Act 3. The Liars resolve the final problem and win the day… or do they?                   |

**What do I need to do?**

1.  Let me know you are interested and when you are free.

2.  Read the character options below and come prepared with multiple answers.

3.  Show up!

Optional: Watch a heist movie or two before the event if you have time.

Really Optional: You can read the [*player handbook*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TkYIH2pZrwKtnZm5uKF7CxW62Mx1UfAY/view?usp=sharing), but it isn’t necessary.

## Who are you?

**Choose a name.** Don’t worry about gender, your Liar will be the same gender as you identify as in real life.

**Choose a type of Liar.** Number the boxes from 1-3 based on which type of Liar you prefer.

|     | **Planners** Lie to objects and places. A Planner might get through a locked door by determining that the lock is old and easily breakable.                   |
|-----|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|     | **Plotters** Lie to the past. A Plotter gets through a locked door by changing the past so that the person with the key forgot to lock it in the first place. |
|     | **Schemers** Lie to people. A Schemer gets through a locked door by convincing the person with the key that it would be a really good idea to open it.        |

**Choose a Manifestation.**

When you lie, your physical form reacts. This can be subtle or obvious. Keep this to yourself until you tell your first lie.

*Examples: Nanotech wires extend from your hands, Veins of glowing energy extend from your temples across your skull, The air around you shimmers.*

**Choose one Adjective for your character.** Here are some examples:

*Agile, Charming, Entertaining, Intimidating, Intuitive, Smart, Strong, Willful, Careful, Eloquent, Fast, Graceful, Perceptive, Quick-Witted, Tough, Tricky.*

**Choose two Skills that your character is Good at.** Liars are skilled at everything, but these are the things you are even better than your average agent at.

Some examples:

*A science: Biology, Mathematics, Medicine, Physics, History, Metallurgy*

*Applied Knowledge: Electronics, Machine Repair, Martial Arts, Marksmanship, Piloting*

*Life Skills: Cooking, Lockpicking, Stealth, Tracking, Disguise, Sleight of Hand*

*Physical skills: Balancing, Climbing, Jumping, Contortion, Swimming, Running*

*People skills: Dancing, Deception, Persuasion, Singing, Sowing Confusion, Writing*

**Choose a Detriment for your character.**

Liars are not perfect. What is the obstacle to success that they place in their own path? *Examples: Brash, Clumsy, Slow, Distracted, Flighty, Fragile, Greedy, Gullible, Unlikeable*

**Choose a Side Goal.**

All the Liars participating in a mission have the same goal: get the key object out of the Improbability Zone. But as an individual, each character has their own Side Goal. Side Goals exist to flesh out the character as an individual as opposed to a member of a team. They indicate what the Liar is interested in beyond the main objective.

*Examples: Help people, Be convincing, Learn, Look impressive, Feel adrenaline, Do something new, Collect valuables.*

**Find a photo.** Search the internet for an image of the character you have in mind. AI generators can be useful for this as well.
