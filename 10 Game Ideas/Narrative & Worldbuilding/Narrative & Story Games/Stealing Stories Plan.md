<th>45m</th>
<th><p>Intro</p>
<p>Make sure everyone has the whiteboard and dice tray open.</p>
<p>We will introduce/create our characters, go over a few rules and make some example Lies and situations.</p>
<p>Explain that we would use our character names to distinguish if we are speaking to or about the player or character. Get everyones character and real names.</p>
<p>Player agency is sacred. Never narrate what someone else’s character does or chooses to do. Give an example.</p>
<p>Roles.</p>
<p>Storyteller is the moderator. I roll no dice but set the difficulty for the things that the characters want to attempt and also act as a timekeeper.</p>
<p>The storyteller can use anything to continue the story.</p>
<p>Player. Controls one Liar. Liars are super agents that can change reality.</p>
<p>The relationship between Player and Storyteller is not an adversarial one. We are building the story together.</p>
<p>The players can only continue the story with concepts that exist inside the scene.</p>
<p>Liars are skilled at almost every task, but each of you have two specialisations that make you stand out from your peers.</p>
<p>Point at a player. Explain an Average situation.</p>
<p>Average Hurdles are 3-4 which means you need a 5 to succeed. Have the person roll a six sided dice (aka d6). Explain the result.</p>
<p>Next player, what are they good at. Imagine a Average situation for that skill. Explain they don’t need to roll unless they are injured. Then turn it into a hard one. Hard tasks are 5-6 so you need a 7 but you have a higher dice, d8. Roll it.</p>
<p>Last player. What are they good at. This time the Difficulty is Very Hard. You need a 9 to succeed. You have a d8. How do you get a 9?</p>
<p>Boost the Dice . Increases the type of dice one step. You can get this from a card or by negotiating with the Storyteller why you should have a bigger dice (traits or equipment). You can also take a risk of Injury.</p>
<p><strong>Lies</strong></p>
<p>Canned examples</p>
<p>Go back to the first player. Ask them for something they are good at. Invent a Very Hard situation. Your character is Good, but not that good. Instead you lie. When you lie, you just say how it is. Lies always work. After you explain the lie, the Storyteller rates its difficulty. After the rating you can attempt to lower the difficulty by limiting the lie some more.</p>
<p>Stress is based on Verisimilitude and Scope</p>
<p>Limitations of lies</p>
<p>It cant affect any Liar directly</p>
<p>It cant effect the key object directly</p>
<p>The situation is resolved as you explain it. However now you need to roll for Injury.</p>
<p>Get the player to make a Lie to solve the situation.</p>
<p>Get the player to describe their manifestation.</p>
<p>Ask each player in turn what their Good At will be</p>
<p>Ask each for a Positive Adjective</p>
<p>Ask each for a Negative Adjective</p>
<p>Check if everyone wrote down a side goal.</p>
<p>Act Structure.</p>
<p>Act 1. The Heist is planned</p>
<p>Act 2. The Heist is carried out and eventually the Turn happens.</p>
<p>Act 3. The Turn is resolved.</p>
<p>Begin Act 1</p></th>

<th>1h</th>
<th><p>Act 1. Briefing</p>
<p>The setup. You are in a briefing room on the Starship Celeste, in orbit around 21st century Earth. On the table in front of you is a hologram projecting a 3km diameter area of the surface, focused on the State Library of Victoria.</p>
<p>As you read the mission details you see:</p>
<p>Zone of Improbability</p>
<p>The key item is a rare book, located in the basement storage areas.</p>
<p>The Improbable effect is that there are lizards everywhere.</p>
<p>Each one feature of the building. Each one but.</p>
<p>You are Super Agents from the 35th Century woken up by the ship’s computer to complete this mission. What challenges are at the location that are uniquely suited for your character to overcome.</p>
<p>Each of the Players comes up with one obstacle, and the storyteller adds a “But”.</p>
<p>Go around each player asking what they see.</p>
<p>Add a but to each.</p>
<p>When are you going in? Day or Night. They have in go in in the next 24 hours.</p>
<p>Summarise the plan?</p>
<p>Questions:</p>
<p>Once its clear how the Heist will go Give out cards. Explain them.</p>
