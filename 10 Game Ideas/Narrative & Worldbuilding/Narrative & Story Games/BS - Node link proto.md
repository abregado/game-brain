Make rampart into a battleship game

More sub phases.

Combat split into: firing with ammo recharge and firing without ammo

First phase: charge targetting combuters

Control lasers to shoot down enemy bombs

Second phase: missiles online

Lasers are automated, use missiles to destroy enemies

Third phase: deter enemies

No ammo recharge. Hits cause enemies to flee. Minimise damage by detering enemies early. Save ammo by only hitting them once each. Each enemy gets to fire a few times. Will you destroy damaged enemies or spread shots to take less damage. Lasers offline so missiles must be used to stop bombs.
