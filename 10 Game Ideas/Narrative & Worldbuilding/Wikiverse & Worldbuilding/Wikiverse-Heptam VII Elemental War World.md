Heptam VII Elemental War World

Elementals only know one thing. To emerge from hibernation and construct their amazing projects every thousand years. On the rare occasions that several types of elemental populate the same planet, the results are explosive.

Every millennia, the native elementals emerge to build their amazing structures. The world of Heptam VII differs in that there are many different types of elemental which dwell within the planets surface.

Iron Elementals

These immobile giants populate the caverns within the planets crust. They are indestructible stalactites and one of the few elementals that do not sleep during the 1000 years of rest. Instead they weave spells that take hundreds of years to complete. The Irons build their structures by controlling the lesser elementals and enslaving them to their will.

Mantle Demons

A form of fire elemental, Mantle Demons are the only native elemental that can resist the Irons command spells. They build their structures below the planets crust and can only continue their work by consuming upwards and outwards. No matter how many demons are banished during the war every millennium; there are always more at the next awakening.

Carvers

Commonly known as Heptamic Wood Sprites, these tiny elementals look like glowing points of light. Their powers include the ability to enhance the speed at which foliage grows and they also have incredible strength. When a swarm of Carvers get together they can lift even the heaviest objects. However whenever they do this, their hot bodies score the surface of the object. This is why they are known as Carvers. A vast majority of wild Carvers are controlled by the Irons during the Waking Years. It is not known if the Carvers are sentient.

Regulators

The local warm bodied water elementals come to life during the Waking Years, causing the frozen rivers they slumber in to thaw. Regulator towers jut from the lakes and oceans, the water around them turning into bubbling hot springs of frenzied building. Oceanic regulators do not befriend either the Irons or the Demons, however most of their freshwater brethren fall prey to control spells. While they sleep, regulators absorb heat from around them to keep their bodies warm.

The Great Slumber

Several times during the history of Heptam VII other species have settled on the planet during the slumber. None have survived an Awakening. During the slumber, plants on the surface have time to recover and after 1000 years they claim all of the land masses. The oceans continue to flow except to the ice caps that form for several kilometres of each of the regulators towers. From orbit, lines of undersea ice can be seen joining each tower. This is where regulators that were in the process of moving between towers hibernated when the slumber began.

Each of the volcanoes become dormant and eventually succumbs to the encroaching plant life. The rich soil around these places is very fertile and often huge forests spring up. Methodically the Irons weave their unseen trap and protection spells across the globe. The sleeping planet hides a dangerous secret that none will know about. Until the Awakening

The Awakening

Life. Long forgotten hatred reborn. Explosions of anger. Sorrow. Madness.

After one week the planet has completely changed. As the different inhabitants arise things change very quickly. Across the oceans the ice caps melt causing tidal waves and fierce air currents. Coastal floods devastate new grown forests. In places where Carvers group to hibernate, the plant life grows wild and uncontrolled reaching dizzying heights. The Carvers move along ancient unseen paths between their towers creating lines of overgrowth visible from space. It is here that the Irons have placed their charms to ensnare the unsuspecting critters. Lines of massive plant life converge on every Iron tower and the construction begins a new.

Mantle demons attempting to dig upwards encounter the strong protective charms placed by the Iron Elementals and take a few days to find where they fail to overlap, bursting from the ground like a fresh eruption. Ancient forests are raised. No respite is given to those who fail to avoid the tide of destruction.

War

Having 1000 years to prepare, and remembering the last 10000 wars, the Irons have a great defensive advantage. They have placed powerful defensive charms across the surface and even more powerful ones around their lairs. They have also had one thousand years to forge weapons of war to use against the demons. Iron war machines and armoured warriors line the borders where the defensive charms are placed. Once the Mantle Demons cross into the influence of Iron magic, they are set upon and the battle begins.

These battles rage constantly for the Waking Years. Neither side lacks resources or souls to sacrifice. As Iron warriors fall, new ones are raised and join the battle. Tides of demons flowing from breaches in the crust would look like a lava flow in fast motion to anyone who could get close enough to watch.

This continues for many years. While the tide is held at bay, the Irons use their slaves to work on their iron towers. Unconcerned, oceanic regulators continue to build their own great works.

During each war, a handful of Iron Elementals are destroyed, melted down and consumed into the mantle. The areas controlled by conquered Irons become part of the Mantle Demons ever growing construct. In this way the Irons could be considered doomed as they do not reproduce while the demons come in ever growing force.

After the Waking Years, everything becomes quiet and the ravaged planet is given a chance to regenerate.

Aberrant activity

During the current War, a number of Iron Elementals banded together each with a Mantle Demon allowing themselves to flee the solar system. These rouge entities call themselves the Recombinate. Fears about this small group attacking colonies of space faring races have begun cropping up across the galaxy. Their combination of indestructibility, mind control, elemental magic and sheer power cannot be ignored. Only twenty such individuals were monitored leaving the system and it is unknown if the will band together or attack separate targets.
