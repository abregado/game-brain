The only known sapient species on [*Ko'natl*](http://tamasys.vndv.com/wikiverse/index.php/Ko%27natl) are the Gorcherax, which are fish-like humanoids. Their skin has to be constantly moist, and they are excellent swimmers, and are streamlined for such. They have long, sinuous arms, with wide hands that have webbed digits. Their legs are shorter, but more powerful, and their feet are also quite wide. Their heads are reptilian, and have small, misty eyes. They are immune to many of the toxins and bacteria that fester in the swamps of their planet.

The prey on fish in the water ways, and eat their food raw. They have medium-level metalurgy, and build large structures out of trees and certain stones. Their weapons are made of an unknown metal, but it is highly resistant to corrosion.Their magic is rather poor because of their distance from the center of the universe. However, there mages are quite skilled with the magic they can use. They reproduce by laying frog-like eggs in large clusters, and then fertilizing them. These clutches are cared for by the entire clan, but only a fraction make it t adulthood. They live for 40 human years, 110 Ko'natl years.

Minotoads are a race of bizarre creatures that look like humanoid toads with fur covered legs and cloven feet. They do not have a "home planet" as such, as they are geneticaly engineered from other species. The currently control several non-habitable planets in the \[\[Androchromeus\]\] Galaxy.

== Biology ==

Minotoads are bizarre creatures. The top half of their body is that of a humanoid toad, with a flat face and eyes, thick muscular arms and green, slimy skin, The lower half has thick, fur-covered legs and cloven (hoofed) feet. The speak in a rough, guttural tongue, and have very acute hearing and sight.

They reproduce by laying tiny, shell less eggs underwater, and then fertilizing them. These hatch within days, and grow in their pool, feeding upon nutritious algae. They grow quickly, and in four-six weeks they are fully grown.

It seems they learn via ancestral memory. No formal learning process has ever been recorded amongst them, and they are vicious and cunning warriors. The little DNA that has been tested shows extremely long DNA strands, but these were heavily damaged partial fragments. Thus far, no other proof has been found.

== Technology and Magic ==

Minotoads have advanced, but crude technology. The also have mid-level magic. They fly around in large spaceships, built around asteroids. These spaceships are powered by the energy given of by elementals, thus granting a long-lasting source of energy. A combination of magic and technology is used to restrain them.

Their standard weapons are large-caliber machine guns, (75 cal) and complex coil guns. The machine guns fire self-propelled shells that explode on impact, causing tremendous damage to everything, much to their delight.

Their coil guns are quite crude. They fire bursts of superheated gas that come from a gas elemental, burning all who touch. The gun is large and unwieldy, and prone to malfunction, but the Minotoads just weather the losses.

The also possess brutal melee weapons, and their fleet boasts tremendous firepower (one ship can take over a planet). They may possess planet-killer weapons, but these rumors have yet to be confirmed.

== Sphere of influence ==

The Minotoads control a large number of Gas Giants in the Androchromeus galaxy, possibly harvesting them for fuel. It is unknown what else the control.

Rumors say Minotoads have been seen on \[\[Ko'natl\]\].
