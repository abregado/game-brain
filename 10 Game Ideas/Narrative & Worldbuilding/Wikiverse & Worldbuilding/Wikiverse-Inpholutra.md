Lighting Magic based Species - Inpholutra

Developed and Spaceworthy

Homeplanet

Due to a tight orbit of its star, the Inpholutra homeplanet of Heanphal III was a volcanic work with a weak atmosphere. Constant volcanic eruptions and very high surface temperature meant that all life on this almost barren world had to struggle to exist from day one.

Evolution

The Inpholutra evolved as many other species do. The struggle was long and hard and many times they were almost wiped out by changing conditions on their homeplanet. They were primary predators, feeding on the other less fortunate species that had developed on their harsh homeplanet.

Early sentient Inpholutra resembled a heavy set tripedal proboscidean. A main trunk protrudes from the top of the spherical body. Sensory organs are located along the top of this truck. A smaller prehensile limb can unfurl from the lower part of the body, and while much more dextrous than the main truck, it lacks strength.

Once they had begun using tools, many Inpholutra formed social groupings (pods) to protect themselves from the larger predators. Simple shelters from the dangerous acid rains followed and before long they were at the top of the food chain.

The discovery known to humans as Animal Husbandry, was the catalyst which pushed the Inpholutra into a long Feudal Age of prosperity and ultimately is the reason that they survived to become a space-faring species.

The Feudal Times

Large communities formed around the planet. More members in a pod meant that more land could be controlled, and as a result, more food animals could be raised. During this time one of the larger predators was domesticated for use as both a riding animal and as a beat of burden. Skirmishes between pods were common and during the 400 year long era, many wars were recorded.

The Unknown

The end of this era came about within the span on a single night. At exactly the same time, all Inpholutra developed the ability to perform elemental magic. Not all were blessed equally however and within a single year power had shifted from the largest, most powerful Inpholutra to those who could wield their newfound power to the most destructive degree. (See Creation Events)

Industrialization

The new magiocracy (government ruled by those who wield magic) seen developed the new magical talents into focused schools of learning. This became a regular part of the curriculum for Inpholutra of all ages.

The simplest of these was the ability to turn ones self into conductive energy (Similar to the atmospheric effect of lighting) and to return to ones own form once this energy had returned to the ground. Controlling the movement of such random energy was very difficult and only the most powerful practitioners could reappear exactly where they wanted.

The other basic ability was to generate localised EM fields in objects by making physical contact with them. More powerful Inpholutra could do so from distance, but even a toddler could power his own toys within days of being born.

The great Jumpchains were commissioned to connect the largest of the cities. These comprised of electromagnets suspended above the ground, spaced out like a metallic dotted line. These stretched between the cities and allowed even the most unpracticed Inpholutra the ability to quickly and reliably travel between important locations.

The Great War

Sometime during their Modern Era, the Inpholutra were invaded by an extraterrestrial race which commanded the powers of a different elemental force. This war resulted in the planet being destroyed and becoming what it is today, and also for the Inpholutras movement into space.

A Homeworld Transformed

After the Inpholutra fled into space, they begun the slow task of putting their homeworld back together again. Several hundred small planetoids were left after the destruction and it is these that became the new cities for the orphaned species. Advanced Jumpchains were built between each planetoid and within a decade many were heavily developed. The smaller asteroid sized chunks were harnessed and stripped of their metals.

The Inpholutra found that the new form of their once proud home was actually more efficient for them, as access to the pure metals normally in the core of the planet were laid bare to be collected. Soon, other planets from the Heanphal System have been cracked open to reveal their cores and this soon became the normal way for colonisation to proceed.

FTL Technology

Invention of the EK Jumpdrive allowed the Inpholutra to begin processing and inhabiting more worlds. Since atmosphere was no longer an issue, it did not matter what type of planets were found orbiting a star. The nearest stars were located and each of their planets broken down into planetoids to make room for new Inpholutra colonies and mining operations.
