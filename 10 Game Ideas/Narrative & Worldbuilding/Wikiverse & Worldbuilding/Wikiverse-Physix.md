The Wikiverse behaves according to immutable laws governing motion, objects, and all interaction thereof. There are several models used within the Wikiverse to describe these physics, however, the most common are the \[\[Basic Model (Physics)\|Basic Model\]\], \[\[Complex Model (Physics)\|Complex Model\]\], \[\[Quantum Model (Physics)\|Quantum Model\]\] and \[\[Thaumic Model (Physics)\|Thaumic Model\]\]. Although all of these support observations, they each have their own supporters and specialities, as they interpret in different ways. An overview of Wikiverse physics is provided below.

==Dimensions==

Everything in the Wikiverse happens in a dimension. The major dimensions involved are Time, Space, and Magic. There is only one time dimension, and it always moves forward. Usually, there are three spatial dimensions, however some models and situations call for more dimensions. The magic dimensions are the most contentious, and the number and nature of these dimensions differs greatly. It is likely that there are an undefined or fluctuating number of magic dimensions, one or more of which have been observed being effected by Time.

Because Time in the wikiverse is separated from Space, the Laws of Relativity (General and Special) do not apply. Time moves forward and a speed determined by a fixed point of reference.

==Gravity==

Gravity is the tendency for massive objects to attract other objects. The strength of the gravity depends on the mass of the object, and the distance from the object. Larger objects have stronger gravitational fields; however, objects with a strong \[\[magical mass\]\] can also create gravity.

Gravity doesn't include the force of \[\[suction\]\] or \[\[magnetism\]\]. The \[\[Law of Gravatic Propagation\]\] explains why gravity moves faster than light.

==Time==

Time is the least mutable dimension in the Wikiverse; it only moves in one direction and at a more or less fixed speed. Nevertheless it is still crucial to the functioning as it provides a changing reference point.

\[\[Law of Spatial Tethering \|The Law of Spatial Tethering\]\] is a hotly debated theory relating to time and the inability of anything tethered to the Space dimension to travel freely within it. Only entities that have cast off the shackles of physical existence may do so.

==Faster-than-light Travel==

Although believed by many to be impossible, faster-than-light (FTL) travel is certainly very achievable in the Wikiverse. How it is achieved varies greatly, but several main "techniques" are used.

===Slow and steady===

Because Time and Space are completely separate dimensions, one can accelerate slowly until they reach the speed of light. Of course the acceleration required to achieve this efficiently is quite rare.

===Reduce energy requirements===

The most obvious barrier to FTL is the exorbitant or infinite energy requirements to propel mass faster than light. To counter this, the mass of the object is negated, allowing it to accelerate well beyond the speed of light.

The \[\[Quantum Model (Physics)\|Quantum Model\]\] describes how this can be achieved.

===Take a shortcut===

This involves literally "shortcutting" a dimension by accessing and travelling in a higher dimension. Common examples of this are \[\[wormhole\|wormholes\]\], which are intersects between lower-dimensional space in higher dimensions. Also feasible is a shortcut through magical space, example through \[\[warp gate\|warp gates\]\]. Taking shortcuts is inherently risky, as there are many variables and fluctuations in the system.

==Magic==

The magic dimensions differ from the spatial dimensions in that they do not conform to strict 'laws'. This means that improbable and anamolous (read:magic) events occur. Magic as we know it is caused by this interdimensional energy transfer. These transfers occur at stellar objects known as "rifts", literally a tear in the fabric of space and magic. \[\[Duodecimus\]\] is the largest such rift in the area, so large that it is often considered the centre of magic. However there are many other rifts, smaller, but still providing a strong source of magic.

One of the most important laws is \[\[Law of Magical Resonance\|Hosenblings Law of Magical Resonance\]\] which states that all magic created wave harmonics in Space, and can be cancelled out by generating the inverse of this wave. This law is also used in the most popular theories on \[\[Stars\|Thaumatic Stars\]\].
