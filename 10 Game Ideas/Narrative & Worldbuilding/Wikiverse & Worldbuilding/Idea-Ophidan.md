Ophidan

Technology dragon idea

When pleased they help under developed races by providing highly advanced technologies. This directly contravenes the Haren-Gnos Treaties which are set in place to protect such species.

Appearance

Adults of the Ophidan species resemble large, heavy built saurians with 4 pairs of limbs. Two pairs of these are legs, the second are single jointed arms with clawed manipulators while the third are very large wings.

The head holds a long snout filled with teeth. Ophidan are carnivorous by nature and have not lost their sharp teeth.

FTL Travel

Ophidan use a process known are world-switching. The exact technology behind this technique is unknown howver it allows the user to move instantaneously between planets. Ophidan are never observed in space and always teleport to and from planets while in the air.

Mindstem Uplinks

Incredibily power processing units are linked directly into the brain, allowing complex analysis of both inputs and outputs. The result is that Ophidan can from a very early age control what they experience and can also track what they are feeling, emotionally and physically. All of their technology can interface with these processing systems to alow instantaneous control.

Contruction Techniques

Ophidan utilize nanotechnology to produce items from nearby resources. These nanobot receive their orders from the Mindstem Uplinks, allowing any user to deisgn and build advanced technology wherever they might be.

Rare minerals cannot be created and often a design will require small parts of unsynthesizable materials. These must be provided for the process to be completed. Ophidan often carry small quantities of the most useful materials on their person when they visit worlds in case they must construct something that requires them.

This nanotechnology also allows Ophidan equipment to be repaired and modified in the field. Another use is to break down unneeded equipment to build different things. This allows any Ophidan agent to be incredibly versatile.
