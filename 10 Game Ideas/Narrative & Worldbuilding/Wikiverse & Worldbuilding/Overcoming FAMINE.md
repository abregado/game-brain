Brainstorming - Overcoming Poverty/Famine

Everybody becomes poverty stricken. In effect poverty is the new normal

Mechanized food production sees the cost of living drop very low/to nothing

Self reliance increases. Food is easy to grow for ones self

Food is supplied with a dwelling.

There is no longer a requirement to eat

Food is generated inside the body

Bodys are engineered to photosynthesis

War or disaster claims most of the population. Food stores for billions mean nobody goes hungry

Introduction of edible, easy to grow alien plants from a meteor crash

Genetic Engineering/Cloning leads to replacement of Domestic Animals with Meat Factories

Domestic Animals are engineered to provide vastly more quantity

Methods of delivery change so that access becomes easier

Society focus changes to inward goals. Helping your fellow citizen is as important as yourself

Rise of the Food Cartels. Its in the companies best interest to keep everyone fed

Food Shortages starve anyone without easy access to food. The rest are only slightly effected

Change in environment sees normally non-cultivated plant species to thrive

Global Free Market and Orbital delivery means anyone can purchase anything from anywhere

Population moves to arcologies where technology provides enough food for everyone

Benefactor aliens arrive and give every person the ability to make food for themselves

Culture moves back to its agrarian roots.

Human minds are transferred into mechanical bodies. These can run on power/solar/radioactives

Radiation creates a SUPER GODZILLA PIG. Which once beaten can be eaten

Canabalism becomes rife. Nobody complains about food anymore...

The ocean depths open up to us. So many possibilities...

Algae farming for proteins
