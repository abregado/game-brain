The Allstars

In the Ancient times, before the coming of the Allstars, the world was dominated by Uniform humans. They were a primitive group although they numbered in the billions. The first to appear is known as Calsaris, his real name lost to the ages. Commanding both the power to manipulate mineral substances on an atomic level and the ability to modify living organisms on a genetic level, Calsaris came from nowhere and begun to twist the world into something more to his liking. Origninally a man of technology, Calsaris was not a warrior, but a thinker. His works of architecture surpassed that of regular man, while his designs on transferring consciousness to machines made him unmeasurable fortunes.

Alratia was the second to emerge from hiding. She was a powerful warrior and a charismatic political leader. Her apprerance also brought the first knowledge of the great crystal matrices that she had discovered, the source of her powers. These were firstly to have control over the Mind of all creatures but more controversially, the ability to manipulate, store and even create Souls. Alratia was also a masterful chemist, creating many powerful elixers including one that made the drinker young again. She became famous firstly through her global cosmetic company, and then through her fast rise to political (and then military) power.

Ancient Geomancers

At the dawn of the age, the great Geomancer {Ronaldo} was banished from the free city of {Biggurn} after several incidents involving his closest Apprentices. {Ronaldo} was very well liked amongst the aristocracy and calling on several favors managed to attempt a coup on the current Governer of the city. The battles in the city lasted just for a few days but immense damage was caused to the city and many civilian lives were lost. All Geomancers that were not enrolled in the schools of the empire had their citizenry revoked. This punishment was extended to their immediate families causing over 400 people to become instantly homeless. Many of their extended family chose exile as well and once the month was out, nearly 2000 people had fled the city in a great caravan.

These people settled just outside the current borders of the empire and utilizing their crafts and abilities they quickly contructed a new centre of trade. Eventually they created the Unison of {Ronaldo}, a small nation dedicted to the perfection of their art.
