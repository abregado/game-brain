# Things I want the server to promote

- A gameplay loop involving changing your geographical location regularly

- Give the players a driver to go out and explore from the starting spawnpoint

- Greater difficulty the further you are from spawn

- More variation in “land quality”. Currently every place in Minecraft is basically the same in terms of value.

- Higher cost for death that includes not just a reduction in player power

- Diversity of abilities, to promote teamplay

- Give the players a driver to generate infrastructure

# Ideas that require Modding

My Plan to rework Minecraft gameplay AKA gamefying Minecraft

Enemies:

1.  Enemies attack your base once a week

2.  This is done by increasing their vision radius by a VERY large amount

3.  during this time will path to any player

4.  creepers will become impatient and explode if they get stuck in one place

5.  Endermen will attempt to steal chests and items then flee

Hunger:

- Everything should use up the hunger bar

- This includes digging and placing blocks

- This is to make it harder to move farther from the spawn

- Hunger can go above 10 with the last item

- Hunger above 10 drops twice as fast

- When dying of hunger any food will restore 2 hunger

Farming

- All plants take x10 as long to grow

- Foliage regrows over time if the tree has at least one foliage per trunk section

- Foliage drops sticks when cut with an axe

- Foiliage drops only apples when smashed with fists

- wheat drops more food in the second last growth stage

- wheat drops more seeds in the final growth stage

- wheat dies if left too long in the last stage. It replants itself 50% of the time

- melons and pumpkins sometimes break their stems when you harvest them

Animals

- animals attack as zombie pigmen when there are 3 or more animals nearby

- animals run away much faster than the player when injured or alone

- the players must use ranged attacks to take down animals without injury

Dieing

- When a player dies a chest is spawned with his items inside

- When a player logs off, he remains standing in the same spot and can die

- Logged off players who are gone longer than 1 hour are considered killed, their items drop in a chest and they respawn at 0,0 when they relog

- If you are carrying gold bars, you respawn with half of them

Crafting

- there is a considerable wait until you can take the crafted item

- furnaces take much longer to smelt items

- crafting tables need iron

- furnaces need stone bricks

- The server will spawn one of each at 0,0

- ingredients stay in the crafting bench when it is closed
