Components in giant mess

- 30 cubes of each color (Green, Brown, Yellow, Blue and Red)

- Many D6 Dice

- Cards reading (Explore, Recruit, Attack, Trade, Gather)

- 15 Brown Large wooden house tokens

- 35 Orange small wooden house tokens

- 1 Blue boat wooden token

- 1 Blue meeple

- 1 Green meeple

- 1 Green obelisk token

- 1 Blue obelisk token

- 1 Green moon token

- LOTR risk tokens (Archer, Cavalry, Eagle, Shield) in Green and Yellow

- LOTR risk tokens (Swordsman, Cavalry, Troll Shield) in Black and Red

- Norse figurines (Giant, Troll, Dwarf, Hero, Dragon, Cavalry, Axeman) in 2 blues

- Egyptian figurines (Snake, Anubite, Priest, Leader, Eagleman, Pheonix, Scorpian, Mummy, Elephant, Chariot, Sphinx, Warrior)

- Greek Figurines (Minotaur, Hero, Medusa, Manticore, Centaur, Archer, Spearman, Cavalry, Hydra, Cyclops)

- Energy flat tokens (2 sided) marked with 1 or 5

- 3 large Radioactive tokens (2 sided)

- Play money (\$500, \$100, \$50, \$10, \$5, \$1) roughly 50 notes of each

- Cluedo Men (blue, red, green, white, yellow, black)

- 85 flat tokens (basket/colored cubes/biome)

3 Quick Ideas: What represents the players position?

Colored squares surround the players territory (5 player game)

A single meeple to represent the players jurisdiction (6 player game)

Army figurines to how area held by force (2-10 player game)

3 Quick Ideas: What are the “tools”?

Use the action cards to determine what a player can do.

The army figurines can perform pre-ruled actions

Abstract actions allow land area to interact each turn

Quick Ideas: What are the resources?

Resources are earned and spent immediately and require no counters

Energy Tokens when the lowest value item is still quite expensive

Money notes used if fine detail is required in transactions

Colored Cubes are used to show stockpiled resources

Abstract 1

Players take turns placing farmland tiles (use the basket tokens) or placing one of their colored cubes (villagers)

A villager can operate all farms linked in one chain (adjacency)

farms “produce” abstractly “food” equal to villagers+farm squares

When there are more farms next to a square than villagers, a settlement is placed automatically.

Each turn only one settlement is generated per farm.

settlements placed in this way must be next to a farm or settlement that is also attached to the farm.

When three settlements are next to eachother, one may be upgraded to a town

settlements “consume” 3 food per turn, villagers “consume” 1. towns consume 10 food.

villagers in a town do not produce food, instead they produce military units

military units also use 1 food.
