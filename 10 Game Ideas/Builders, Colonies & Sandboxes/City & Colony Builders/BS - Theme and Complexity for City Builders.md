Themes for cyclical city builder

- Nomadic cycle: Must pack up and move

- Agricultural cycle: Resources only available during a small part of the cycle

- Day/Night Cycle: some actions take place in only one part of the cycle

- Invasion Cycle: a destructive force comes at the same time every cycle

- Expansion Cycle: your resources (population) can only grow during part of the cycle

- Observation Cycle: the player can only interact during part of the cycle

I feel it is necessary to have tools/action that can be performed throughout the whole cycle, and some that can only be completed during certain phases. Also a player should have understanding of what he will gain from each choice/tool use (predictability).

Complexity

Player has a number of attributes that must be managed, but buildings do not modify them directly. Example:// Instead of having buildings that create Happiness, buildings create different amounts of Entertainment, Education and Health. These generate the final Happiness rating.

Example:// Instead of population increasing at a fixed rate, The system has a few different attributes that form the population growth rate. Birth, Death, Maturity Rates as well as Lifespan work together to cause the population to fluctuate. Buildings or tools in the game can effect one or more of these attributes, but never the actual Growth Rate.

Perhaps buildings and tools can have one fixed effector and one or more variable ones. A School will increase the maturity rate but could be changed to increase Birth Rate or Lifespan as an option (Health education focus).

Having too much of something should be detrimental to the entire system, not just because of maintenance costs. Too much education and you will have no labourers available. Without enough Education you will not have many Educated people to run the hi-tech industries.
