Limited Information availabllity

Primitive farming trechniques-\>high tech machinery

Items treated like liquids (when one pile is too high/unstablem it falls into neighbouring areas.)

No definates as to how long a task takes.

No direct control over creatures/workers

Players schedule tasks to be completed

Plants that become thick enough block vision

"Fog of war" in effect. Players must scout their land to renew the information they have.

Players buy "new" land. Uncleared, uncontrolled, wild land. Perhaps on a freshly terraformed planet. Fictional creatures and plants could make suspension of disbeleif easier.

Players should be forced to make do with the bare essentials at the beginning.

Lack of Domestic seed or GM crops.

Players must domesticate crops and animals through selective breeding, and later with advanced methods including GM retrovirii

Wheelbarrow skill :D kekeke

All items decay when used AND over time.

Parts based damage to items. (Shovel has blade, body and handle. Handle gets damaged on each use, body and blade get over time damage (rot and rust) and the blade gets damage per use based on the material it digs)

construction of buildings from local materials or purchased pre-fabs
