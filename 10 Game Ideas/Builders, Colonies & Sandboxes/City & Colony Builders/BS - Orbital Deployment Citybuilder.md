Brainstorming - Orbital City/Facility deployment Strategy

The player sends sounds to a number of different worlds. Each brings back a summary of what was found on the planet. THe player can then send more detailed probes back to each planet repeatedly to continue to refine this information.

At some point the player will have decided on which planet he will colonise. He must now fill a colony ship with structures (Dropfabs) and send his ship to the planet. Orbital relays, Observation satellites and other structures can also be sent with the colony ship.

Once in orbit the structures can be deployed anywhere on the globe. The strategy comes from correct placement of these structures as the actual colony development is out of the players control.

Information comes in slowly and is always out of date due to the speed of light. Ships must be dispatched to collect information reports, each one costing the player resources.

How often will information be gathered? How often will new facilities be sent to the planet? Do you act on outdated information or wait a short time for the next update?

Ideas for Tools to assist with city development

- Forbidden areas

- Development or Export focus

- Population growth inhibitors

- Detailed Intel of the planet (different views, combination views)

If a colony is allowed to develop too long without new facilties arriving many things could happen:

- social mindset becomes unhappy with change

- development is wasted on lower tech things

- easy resources become scarce, starting local conflict

- the colony might revolt declaring independance

- too many people with not enough work means lower quality of living

- unemployment

If a colony is given too much assistance it might suffer:

- lack of educated workers

- higher expectation of assistance

- slacker, carefree mindset

- too much personal wealth

- buerocratic overhead increase

- governemtn complains

Facility Types

- Civic centres (hospitals, governemnt offices, education)

- Common materials extraction (lumber, surface metals, water)

- Building material factories (increases development)

- Housing Arcos (huge population centres, minmal jobs)

- Space/Interorbit Facilities (orbital airports)

- Air/Sea ports (increase development potential and access to resources)

- Tech Factories (improve other facilities, or allow them to grow larger)

- Military Base (keeps the peace, enforces confederation)

- Agricultural Centre (provides equipment for farming around this area)

- Gamma bomb (kills all living things in an area. Good to prepare large ground areas for construction)

- Survival Station (cheap. provides the possibility of remote operations in this area)

- Weather station (improves weather based living conditions in this area)

- Power Station (provides electricity to connected areas)

One off drops (these boost local conditions but after not fixed points on the map)

- High tech flyers (for emergency services/transport)

- Military goods (improves certain industries)

- High tech meds (temporary boost to healthcare)

- Assault Forces (quells rebellion temporarily but boosts unrest generation later)

- Communications Equipment (boosts construction of local communications net)

- Utilities Equiupment (boosts construction of local water and electricity network)
