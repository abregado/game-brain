Concept – Planet-Country-State-City-District-Family-Person

Create a planetary empire simulator that takes detail from a very small level into account. A change in a single citizen has an overall effect on the entire planet (although a very small one)

Players cannot control events on a personal or planetary scale. They control cities which are developed by adding districts. These districts are designed by the player and added onto cities over time.

The countries and states are governed by AI scripts which will seek to gain power and protect themselves. The player can add cities and districts to any State, but cannot control the military actions of these groups. Victory will come from keeping all Countries even until spaceflight is invented.

The player also cannot control what each individual can do, each seeking out personal goals. Cities and States can break away over time, as can individuals.
