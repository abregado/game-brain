Concept – Real time non-simultaneous Multiplayer city builder

As the govenor it is your duty to keep this city running smoothly. The only worry is the upcoming meeting of the council. It takes place every 10 years and deals made there are binding! It couldn't be half bad except for the amount of imports the city needs to keep its occupant happy.... or if the other members could be trusted to fulfil their ends of the deals.

A game is set up between a number of players. They set the number of years between council meetings and look over several procedurally generated maps, deciding which one to use.
