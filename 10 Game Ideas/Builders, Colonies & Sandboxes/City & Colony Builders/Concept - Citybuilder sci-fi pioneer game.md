Concept - Citybuilder sci-fi pioneer game

Iterative Gameplay. Single player game. The player attempts to create a viable colony on an alien world. As each new town reaches a higher level the player must give up control of it to the computer player. Once enough settlements have been successfully made, one of them ca be selected as the shire capital and the player plays as this settlement until it has reached the next highest level. During this phase trade can occur with the smaller settlements and some of them can even revolt/rebell.

Tools include a wide variety of early earth pioneer items. Powered items can be used but cannot be replenished until later stages when light-medium industrial opportunities become available.

The cost and danger of transport should be modelled extensivly.

The options for different agricultural industries should be very broad so that the player attempts different aims with each of the smaller settlements.

Every year or two a transport ship arrives from earth to supply high technology items. Limited trade items means that the player has to decide which can be utilized best. Focusing all of his resources on tradable commodities will mean stunting his population and food growth.

Concept - City Builder Mixed Mechanics

Time Pressure, Pushing forward generates more resistance. Single Player

New colonists are arriving every day. You must expand your city to accomidate them. Space and resources are limited and people hate to live in the shantytowns building up around your pristine city. Dont build up too fast tho as those at the top have more demands than those at the bottom!

Concept - City Builder for Industry Space Game Station

Goal: Stability, Limited resources, Persistant Style, Complex logistical management

You are on a fresh frontier. The first space station in a new solar system establishing a beachhead and refuge for all those who follow. The privateers will fight, mine and build for you, if you pay them enough. Expand to provide food, equipment and new craft to the new pioneers.
