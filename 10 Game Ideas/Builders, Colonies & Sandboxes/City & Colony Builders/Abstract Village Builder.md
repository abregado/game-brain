Cooking and Food Economy

After each day cycle you can choose what gets cooked in the kitchen for your workers to eat. Recipes contain different food items in different quantities.to start with, you only need to make someting that will feed enough people. Whole numbers of food must be used, so making extra means you have wastage. Foods like Gruel or Broth are easily multiplied, while good foods like ROasts and Pies are not.

Second phase would be to have an labor efficency stat that goes down a fraction every time someone eats the same food twice in a row.

Farming exonomy.

Food should come in waves. Labor investment in farms should be ongoing and when it pops there should be a lot if inventory. This will put pressure on stockpiles and force the player to stockpile foodstuffs as well as budget/ration until the next pop.

Excess Food that is cooked goes bad immediatly. Other foods go bad over time.

Construction economy

Tools are high end items that for most of the game will be traded. Building new workshops uses up tools. Also transforming the environment uses tools during the process but refunds then at the end.

Basic Construction materials are generated by the village. Logs, Planks, Stone Bricks and Slabs are all harvested and then processed. A simple industrial process chain can be used where one item is changed into another.

Iron and Gold are the two higher level metals available. Iron mining requires better mining equipment. Building the entire metallurgy chain is difficult. Early game, the player will be selling ore, and then selling crushed ores, eventually moving up to ingots and then actual crafted items.

Stone and Wood crafts can also be made for sale, but should not be labor efficent.

Iron items are instrumental in building higher technology structures. Portcullis, lanterns and simple tools can be made from wrought iron
