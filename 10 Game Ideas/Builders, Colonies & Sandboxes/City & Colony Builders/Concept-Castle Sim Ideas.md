Castle Sim

Why build castles?

To house lords

Central point of authority over local population

Protect vital resources

Show of wealth

Why build larger castles?

Increase quantity of protected industry

Increase quantity of protected population

Store more resources

Increase the number of troops that can be supported by the castle

Resources come from:

Local Towns and Villages (imports and taxes)

Trade caravans (imports and protection money)

Local peasants (taxes)

Quarries (both local and otherwise)

Ports (never local)

Local industries

Ransoms and Brides

More Bailey area:

Pros:

More protected buildings

Cons:

More wall to surround area

More Bailies:

Pro:

Control damage when enemy breaches

Cons:

More walls to surround area

Less protected building space

More Troops:

Pros:

More “freelands” around the castle

Cons:

Need more food, support and housing

More Peasants:

Pros:

More labour

Cons:

Need more housing and food

More Road inside Bailey:

Pro:

Less crowding

Better access for carts and pedestrians

Cons:

Less building space
