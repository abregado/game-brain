Concept – Hand me on Colony Builder

Players build a colony on a distance world up from humble beginnings. Once stable the colonists can send forth new colony ships to take over the whole solar system. The catch? You can only control a single colony.

Ask your friends to play and exploit an entire solar system together, or compete against each other in a corporate battle to control resources.

### Embark Screen - Select Packages

Instead of allowing the player to select exactly what they want from a list with points costs, generate a range of packages and ask them to select a smaller number of them. Packages contain a number of fixed items, and a range of randomized items of random quantities.

These packages could be generated per embark location, so that even if you find the perfect location, the starting conditions might not be exactly what you want.

This idea ties to my concepts on Limiting Perfect Player Choices. Players become more entralled in a game, and feel more rewarded when they overcome challenges they originally thought out of their control. Allowing the player to select exactly the items he/she needs to solve a problem might lead to fun gameplay but it will ultimately give less reward as the player already knew they could achieve the goal.

### Indirect population caps

Some of the population must generate resources to keep the others alive. Most builders use food but also do not limit the amount of farmers you can keep.

Either the amount of population a single pop unit can support shoud be very low, or the number of supports should be limited.

### Tools as characters

Instead of giving the living component of a colony some personality, grey them out completely and focus on giving the tools and weapons personality. Perhaps the tools themselves have goals, dreams, likes and dislikes.

The fleshies are simply a resource to have the tools get about. The player is actually the structure of the colony itself manifesting itself as a manager.
