Turn Based Combat Defender

Colony structures with perimiter.

You set up guard routes and rosters for a limited number of soldiers.

Colony builds itself over time, increasing difficulty as more area to cover.

Fog of War and Line of Light are crucial

Enemies attack at unknown intervals.

They try to sneak in without being seen.

When one of your guards sees an enemy it runs to the nearest alarm, or starts shouting.

Once combat starts, it changes to turn based, or realtime with pause.

Manage mental and physical health of your soldiers

Some soldiers are only assigned to soldier duty at certain times.

Tired soldiers will be less active, less attentive and less perceptive

Enemies come to you but you dont know when.

Set up patrols for day and night.

Sometimes you will spot scouts, and then you know the enemy is planning something.

Reacting with full force to every sighting wears down your soldiers.

Sometimes you are asked to send a soldier or two with a caravan for protection.

You can only control soldiers once the alarm has been sounded.

Soldier which are sleeping cannot be controlled until woken up.

Adding more equipment makes soldiers tired faster.

Soldier trait ideas

- Heavy sleeper

- Light sleeper

- Smoker

- Drinker

- One-eye

- Careful

- Monophobia

- Nyctophobia

Equipment

- Static floodlights

- Night vision

- Hand torches
