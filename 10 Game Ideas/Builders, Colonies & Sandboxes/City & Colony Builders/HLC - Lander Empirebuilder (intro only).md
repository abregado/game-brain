High Level Concept

**Lander/Management Hybrid**

By Ben Buckton

**Intro:**

Players build up a colony on a remote alien world, relying on intermittent deliveries of supplies from earth. The drop ships used to ferry these resources to the planet are simulated using a Lander-esque mini game where the player must avoid environmental obstacles and battle time pressure to ensure their colony can continue.

**Features:**

- 2d Environments are generated anew each game giving ample re-playability

- Three planet types (Plains, Mountainous, Volcanic) to challenge any player

- Varied weather, environmental and external obstacles

- Several different lander types, each with its own abilities

- Simplistic colony management to focus on the lander action

- Manage quantities of resources and decide how much risk to take with shipments
