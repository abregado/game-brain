Game Idea to practice game maker.

Logistical, multileveled Lander Clone.

Basic Concept: Each level the player selects a different ship to land at a designated colony.

Difficulty: Challenges will come from the following obstacles:

Wind Speed  
Craft Thurst/Mass  
Shroud  
Combinations of the above

First Phase: Gravity, Falling Damage, Basic Obstacles  
Phase 2: Objective (landing platform), Moving Obstacles  
Phase3: Ship type selector between levels, Shrouds  
  
Advanced Phase

Once the game basics are complete, the logisitical system will be implemented. This system will generate your score and allow people to “develop” their save game.

Between each level statistics on number of births, current unrest/revolutions/crime, total population, economic output, housing/power/entertainment/transport.

These levels are adjusted by selecting and landing the different ship types. The highest (Total Population x Total Happyness) is the score that the player receives when he ends his game.

As the colony starts to get more complex, more choices will become available, and theirfore more sacrifices will need to be made. Will you land a Shuttle full of Marines to quell a rebel threat or will you bring a Cargo ship of supplies to build that new Housing Module to increase happiness.
