Concept – Agricultural Colonization game

Detailed “Working the land” mechanics

Proceedurally generated plant and animal species

Build up from nothing to a thriving sci-fi farming centre

Incorporates basic tools and high technology: Shovels until Molecular Rearangement Drills

Fight against disease, aggressive plant and animal life, supply, and labour issues.

Sell your produce on an open market

Build:

Complex fencing and gate systems to move your stock animals around

Design interiors of buildings for specific tasks

Farm equipment and vehicles as well as hand tools and protective equipment

Housing to increase how many workers you have

Hire:

A range of specialists: Botanists, Chemists, Engineers or Hunters

Varied people with hundreds of unique statistic combinations

Grow:

Local plant species or import known ones form Earth

Domesticate plants and animals through selective breeding

Tame the local species with genetic manipulation

Conquer:

Local and interstellar markets with your unique breed of crops and stock animals
