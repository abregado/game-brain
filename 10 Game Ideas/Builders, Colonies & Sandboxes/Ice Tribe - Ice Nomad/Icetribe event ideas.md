# Ideas for event content

Hunting and Exploring

steal a young animal

hunt the stragglers, or the alpha for glory

Sickness/Disease

treat the ill

send the ill away

ignore the problem for mow

event chains could be mutation?

Omens

These could modify the cycle lengths in your favor, or be criptic messages about what might happen in the future.

Dreamwalking

Events that happen to the shaman in the dream/spirit world

Visions and prophecies

Scrying

Capturing beasts of burden to increase travel capacity. But they eat something extra

Find bodies of fallen hunters

Bad omens/black magic

Loot bodies for weapons

Bury/burn the bodies

Purely negative events

the player has to choose between three things that will hurt his progress

penalties now vs penalties later?

Predictions of future events

could actually just make the predicted event likely to happen

encountering tribute aliens or evil “things” from the storm/red death

Other tribes? Friendly and Hostile

Option to send hunters home, effectivly putting them in limbo until the end of the year.

Give a goal by asking the player to collect a number of res and then not knowing when they must pay it back (through an unlocked event)

Set a minimum res level

Events that are better when you have less hunters. If you get them when you have a massive hunt, the best thing you can hope for is to break even. EG//Stealth events.

How gruesome do we want to go?

# Possible modifiers

An event likelyhood modifier that is delayed (EG// increase likelyhood of event 34 after 1 year)

carry extra freight between camps temporarily

birth/death rates

chance of surviving combat encounters (warrior training)

child mortality rates...

speed of child training? Will we make this age based like death from old age. As the children get older there is an increasing chance they will become adults.. some of them might be slow learners :D or if you dont fufil enough events to boost it, you will have a tribe of idiot children that never grow up!

# Cycles that might be cool in the game

Chapters. After a certain amount of time (5 years maybe) a set of events are added that were not available before hand. Perhaps “side quest” event chains have to be completed before the “chapter” ends. Of course chapters would be cyclical not linear.

As discussed:

Animal Population cycles. Different ones for different creatures. Could even have them effect one another.

Travel time cycles. To represent longer/shorter seasons

Length of winter shows as more food required to survive

Hormonal cycles. Birthrates and morale resilience rise and fall slowly, even if it is a negligible amount. Would reduce cookie cutter tactics.
