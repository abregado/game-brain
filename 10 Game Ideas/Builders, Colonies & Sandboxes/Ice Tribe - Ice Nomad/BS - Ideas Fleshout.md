- javelins are single use but also keep a creature at bay wile unused

- player clickd a watrior to throw a javelin

- click a wrrior wi no javelin to attack with knife (worse counter attack consequences)

- creature turns to face warriors, pain

- each creature has counter attacks and armor per facing

- if a wrrior is attacked, click to dodge or counter

- creatures give in when the pain is too great

- eah hit causes a large amount of pain and also creates an ongoing level of pain

- each time pain goes over a creatures paun threshold the creature has a chace to give up if sourrounded or flee if not

- if ambushed then the creature will be surrounded to start with

- if direct assault then your warriors will be in a pack or semicircle

- warriors spread around every time the creature attacks

- more spreading if you counter attack well

- attacking will also draw attention

- if fighting more than one creature you control a few warriors, once they win or lose you control the next group with the original group arriving to help after the same amount of time

- some creatures fight in a swarm, so they are treated like a single creature

- if you are ambushed then your wsrrirs will be in pack surrounde by crearures.

- in this case, wounded creatures will flee instead of giving up
