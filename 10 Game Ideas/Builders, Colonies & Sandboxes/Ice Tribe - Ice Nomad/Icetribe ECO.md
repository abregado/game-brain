conversion rates between tier 1 and tier 2

2 meat = 1 food

5 skins = 1 fur

10 bone = 1 tool

one labour converts 10 raw materials

max labour set by initial labour input (hut sizes)

1 builder makes a size 1 hut

2 builders makes size 3 hut, needs 1 fur

3 builders makes size 5 hut, needs 2 fur

more hunters means more resources can be brought back

more hunters increases odds of good stuff happening

more hunters decreases the chances of injury

more hunters makes it harder to pass sneaking events

more hunters makes it harder to hunt fast creatures

more hunters makes it easier to hunt tough creatures

27 events

9 hunts

3 seasons

1 year,(20 winter, 90 rest, 30 travel)food

When do hunters/campers eat?

Start of camp screen.

camp screen low risk of starving

**MEAT**

meat - plentiful

food - labor additional cost + conversion cost

food remainder - not much left overs each season.

children use half a food each?

no benefit for having a small amount of extra food

fasting only when you have a large excess of food, relative to the eaten amount

thresholds to encourage players to push further

**TOOLS**

Tools are expendable to increase chances in events

option increases

tools = available hunt slots

for travel each tribal can carry one tool for free

**FURS**

used to allow new children

huts bigger than 1 slot need 1 fur

furs returned each season or not?

**MORALE**

How to change?

During camp seasons, only through events?

Feasting during hunting?

infectious morale

overall morale decay during camp/hunts

morale to trend upwards when returned home

relative success and expectation. morale changes based on last hunt/season.

**TRAVEL AND CARGO CAP**

different amounts of food consumed to simulate travel time

amount of food per person to travel

cargo capacity for each person

how many tribals needed to carry the totem

**EVENT CHOICES**

Shouldnt be a clear best choice for each situation

combinations of benefit

(dis)advantage combos
