Big Mech

3 mechs to a side

Large hexes

Map is maybe 10 hexes to a side

Fast moves 3

Slow moves 1

Moving one extra gives a heat

Fire one or two weapons per turn?

Each mech has two weapons

Most weapons have unlimited range

First point of damage removes a weapon

Second adds a card from the damage deck. If you draw the destroyed, then reshuffle it back in.

Some mechs have a shield which can block damage

If you draw a third damage card you are destroyed

Randomly draw the order of the mechs for playing

Mat with road, river and hills

One level of hills only

Several placeable hexes with city, forest and lakes

City double sided: City and Rubble

Forest double sided: Forest and Wildfire

Lake double sided: Water and Steam

Attacks can cause these hexes to flip over

Plasma turns water to steam

Explosive turns City to Rubble

Flame turns forest to fire

If you dont move, gain a target token

If you end in water, lose a heat

Heat only has 3 levels. End with 3 heat, shutdown token

Not shooting removes all heat

Weapon cards have two sides: Primary and Secondary.

Secondary is weaker.

Fire both weapons per turn.

If one of your mechs goes down, you flip a secondary weapon on one of the remaining mechs.

One mech set is:

3 mech tokens

3 mech boards

10 weapon cards (6 get used)

One game set is:

1 board

6 Forests

6 Cities

3 Lakes

18 heat tokens

6 Target tokens

Is this a game about positioning in the most optimal positions?

Is this a game about making the most of a random set of orders?
