Mech games

Sense of scale

- human sized objects in level

- picture of human standing next to mech

- levels include regular human dwellings

Sense of weight

- difference between heavy and light mechs

- high tech mechs are lighter and more graceful

- lurching movement for heavy mechs

- footprints/smashing things that are stepped on

- high momentum

- skidding

Controls

- move, aim and firing all together?

- When stationary use a more precise control mechanism?

- Zoom aim?

- Lock on tracking aim?

- Auto aim?

- Flight?

- Movement modes?

- Speed/manueaverability tradeoff

- crouching?

- Jumping?

- Indipendant weapon firing/targetting?

- Max buttons that need to be pushed at same time

Firing skill

- delay before firing

- wind up/charge up

- heat buildup

- accuracy effected by recoil

- accuracy effected by movement

- accuracy effected by stance
