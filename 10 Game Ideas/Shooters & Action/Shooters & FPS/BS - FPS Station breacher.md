Doom system map generation

Random new polygons added on

Players interact with the vertices between rooms.

Player builds doors or machines on walls

Some machines scan what is behind the wall, what the wall is made of.

Some materials cannot be breached (to allow level design and direct the player)

Sometimes there are long sections which have no walls between them, like corridors.
