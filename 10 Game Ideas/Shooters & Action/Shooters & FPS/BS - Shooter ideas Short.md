Movement Jam

Just jam as many as many different FPS movement types as possible

No other gameplay

Simple AI

Stationary targets with multiple hit locations

Stationary targets stuck to mobile platforms

2d AI flying enemies that hover and just keep you in sight

Ai searches “cover points” that can see your current position

Shooter ideas

Mxing random dealing from card games with FPS. Team Deathmatch. Each team is "dealt" a collection of abilities or advantages. These are handed out to the team leaders and then the leaders give them to their teammates. Combinations of abilities will be more powerful of course, but then fewer team members will have abilities. Team leaders are given a isograpic map of the battle and a "game plan" is drawn up.

Commandos vs Mooks

One team is conprised of boosted mercenaries or marines while the other team controls a contunal flow of weak enemies. Each player on the Mooks team can control a number of NPC characters, these being the players "lives". As the main avatar is killed, the player jumps into the other bodies. The Mercenary team must either attack or defend (depending on the map) against these AI style enemies.

Deployable abilities should always have a "sweet spot" in their deployment time and operation. Also there should be some benifit to NOT using an ability to avoid it being used to overwhelming advantage. Perhaps some passive ability can be gained while it goes unused, or points are awarded for unused abilities at the end.
