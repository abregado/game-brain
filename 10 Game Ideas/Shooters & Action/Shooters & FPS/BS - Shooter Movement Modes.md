BS - Shooter Movement Modes

Many shooters today allow the player to continue moving around rapidly during combat. Many more allow no penalty to be applied to the player for doing so. Position is a valuable commodity in all strategy games and ability to keep moving is the primary defence in a twitch based shooter.

Strategy comes from choices. The player should be asked to choose if movement/position is his primary concern at each moment.

This is an expansion on the gameplay mechanic of crouch aiming. Some games promote the player to stand still while aiming by giving him a bonus to accuracy when the crouch button is held. In this state the players mobility is reduced as a side effect. In most cases the transition between modes (crouch/run) is instantaneous. This means that the player continues to be mobile until just before he wishes to engage an enemy, and in some games the transition is made only moments before the trigger is fired (when the bonuses are applied instantly).

Proposal:

Create an environment where the player always needs to consider which movement mode is best several moments BEFORE it would be best to be in said mode.

Apply bonuses for being in a combat ready mode over time

Penalise players for rapidly switching between modes

Have a larger utility difference between the various movement modes

Modes:

Evasive. The player can sprint in straight lines very rapidly and perform dash dodges while moving. Slides, microteleports and other rapid movement is also available. Only light weaponry can be used. Turning speed is reduced while sprinting and dashing. Strafing is also penalised.

Defensive. The player can only move very slowly but gains a defensive bonus over time. Turning speeds are reduced and only small offensive weaponry can be utilized. Active defences such as energy/riot shields, knockback effects and aura abilities can still be used. Backwards movement is uneffected.

Combat Ready. The player suffers a large penalty to offensive capabilities when changing into this mode but then gradually gains a bonus to the same (after long enough gaining a large bonus). All weapons can be utilized but turning speed and backwards movement are penalized

Observation. Turning speed is increased but movement is reduced. The player gains GUI modifications to help with observation, extra zoom levels, vision modes, hovering status bars, ect. Detection equipment can be used, perhaps to see far away or invisible opponents. Tough opponents may need to be examined before being engaged.

Mobile. The player can climb, jump, vault or use flying movement. Movement is standard in all directions. These special movement modes take time to charge up once this mode is activated and during this time very little other abilities can be used.

Other mechanics that may fit with this system

Facing. Players armor should work more effectivly when facing an opponent. Small teams of players can work together to flank a tougher opponent and hit them in the back/side/tail/exhaust port.

Lengthy Battles. Everyone has a lot of defensive ability so that battles take longer than traditional FPS shooters. Enemies should not fall to one or two hits and players should not be able to heal damage very rapidly.
