Map Idea Es_jumpgate

Concept: To reverse the flow of gameplay. The combat will begin on the ground and move into space once one team has secured control of the infantry section.

Design: The battle takes place on a UNF Mining base that is under going a “change of management”. The planet is being blockaded after joining the NGM and the only way to break this blockade is to take over the hanger which contains a plethora of UNF craft.

The NGM players must capture the hanger, open the doors and attack the orbiting cruisers, while the UNF players must capture the hanger and destroy 3 shield generators that are protecting the base and then the base itself.

Infantry Based Section: Play starts with both teams controlling one Subsystem in the main structure. They must capture the points as they go and eventually gain access to the hanger itself. Once a team owns the hanger, they can attack their selected target.

Space Based Section: The base is build onto a huge asteroid. The enemy fleet is above the base, while the shield generators are hidden in twisted mining tunnels below. There are no exits between the two without going through the base itself. The guns mounted around the entrance/exits to the base will be controlled by whichever player is currently controlling the Hanger.

Hanger Doors:

These will be slow moving doors that close over each exit of the hanger. Once pressed they will lock until completely closed and cannot be reopened for 5 seconds. These will be placed in the space section of the level, but doors made of player_clip will close over the teleport zones in the infantry section as well.
