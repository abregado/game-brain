Brainstorming - FPS Environments for different moods

Scary - Claustrophobic, Dark, Damp, Tight

- Alien space craft

- Cave system

- sewers

- Subway tunnels

- somethings burrowed (artifical caves)

- Underwater complex

- Space station

Scary - Outdoor, open, expansive, feeling of solitude

- Deepspace

- Desert

- Forest with lots of ambient noise which drops away just before something happens

- Night time in the mountains

- City streets

- Indoors but cant see the roof

- Fields of huge alien monuments

Non Threatening Exploration - Hi vis, feeling of no danger

- Daytime natural settings

- Flat expanses

- Good view points

- natural landmarks
