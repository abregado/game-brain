One Peter Month Ideas

Axe throwing target shooter

- Shooting Gallery at a circus

- Physics based projectiles

- Different objects to shoot at

- Only the sharp parts of thrown objects can stick?

- Stationary and Moving Targets

- Playable with Controller

- Two players where your thrown weapons hit each other

- Left player throws at right targets and vice versa

- Or coop against all targets.

Maze exploration game for up to 8 players

- Voronoi multi split screen, ask for explanation

- Fog of war that is revealed by all players, but fills back in over time

- Get everyone out to win

- Buttons to change the maze, for that We were here together feel

- Just movement and button activation when standing on a platform.

- One stick per player, no buttons.

- For 8 player 2 players use one controller

Tamagochi but with a scheduler

- An egg creature with branching evolutions

- 5 variables and age

- Each evolution consumes or generates these values at different rates

- Set a schedule of 3 toys per day which increase or decrease the variables

- If the creature reaches the prerequisite numbers and they stay in bounds for several days, it evolves

- After first creature, unlock weekly schedules

- After second creature, unlock fast forward

- Show all the creatures that have been found on the Main Menu + steam achievement for each.

Arcade game like asteroids

- Top down

- Enemies instead of asteroids

- Instead of one ship, you control a swarm of smaller ships

- Swarms use your fish ai from dynasty \<3

- Or more to the point, you control a central point that the ships swarm around.

- Kill enemies to collect loot

- Press a button to spend loot to add ships to your swarm

- Make enemy swarms larger over time, so its endless mode

- Zoom out as your swarm gets bigger

- Can leverage Burst and DOTS

Arcade game Missile Command clone

- Side view

- 5 cities at the bottom of the screen

- 3 Bases at the bottom of the screen

- Enemy bombs and missiles come from the top

- Shoot them down with a range of weapons.

- Endless mode, just gets harder each wave until you fail

- Make as many weapons as you can in 1 month

- One achievement, survive 1000 waves

Harvest moon mining cave

- The classic hit rocks simulator

- Find the rock that has the hole to go down

- Every 5 levels there is an elevator

- Limited stamina

- Craft items between runs

- Limited inventory space during a run

- Upgrade tools with the right items

Lander multistage action game

- Side view

- Momentum based physics

- Scene Type 1: Take Off/Landing

  - Thrust in 4 directions (or continuous with controller/touch)

  - Dodge falling asteroids

  - Reach one of 4 level exits

  - Hitting asteroid or ground reduces energy

- Scene Type 2: Tunnel shooter

  - Move on edge of tunnel

  - Dodge barriers else lose energy

  - Lose energy over time

  - Hit Boosters for more energy

- Scene Type 3: Enemies

  - Same movement as Scene 1

  - Press button to drop bomb

  - Bomb carries momentum

  - Enemy turrets shoot missiles at you

  - Land on platform to continue

- Scene Type 4: Away mission

  - Central ship landing point

  - 4 items to collect

  - Rocks to block you

  - Holes to kill you

  - Mystery Pads (helicopter or spring pad)

  - Enemies that walk in straight lines (emu, ball mouth, robot, croc)

- Bonus points for removing the shooting

- Player takes off -\> goes through tunnel -\> lands on enemy world -\> does away mission -\> takes off from enemy world -\> tunnels -\> lands on homeworld

- Things to fix?

  - If the enemy world is empty, then it is boring
