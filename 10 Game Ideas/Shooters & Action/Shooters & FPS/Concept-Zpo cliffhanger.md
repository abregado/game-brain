Zpo_cliffhanger

Concept: To make loads of exploding stuff in a level. The humans must climb up a cliff face that is covered in scaffolding and reach the safe house at the top. The zombie spawn moves up as the remaining humans reach each checkpoint (or ill use teleports). Gameplay would include loads of zombies jumping on your head.

The Cliff

This is made up of a number of “rooms” linked mainly by ramps and steps, with a few ladders for good measure. Survivors progress through them in a linear fashion, however there are “bounces” that allow some players to trigger events that will slow down the zombie menace.

Room 1 – Crash

The survivors vehicle is crashed here. Zombies spawn from a small cave located not too far away and emerge from behind a cyclone fence. Survivors must scale this part of the cliff to get away, there isn’t enough space of everyone to climb at once and if someone in the front is slow, it will slow down everyone else. Falling off is a possibility.

Room 2 –Dig

A number of tunnels are dug into the cliff face and survivors must hack down wooden planks to gain access while the others hold off the zombies. Zombies now spawn from below OR from inside some tunnels that the survivors can barricade. Some survivors can collapse the closer zombie tunnel by moving past all of the tunnels to some explosives and triggering them. An alternate route to the next room becomes available for the brave few.

Room 3 – Office

The site office is a small wooden construct suspended half way up the cliff. Once the humans have past this point they may spend a little time smashing it loose which blocks the zombies path, meaning they cannot spawn from this location. Zombie can spawn from a smaller building to the side once the humans arrive.

Room 4 – Bridges

Several swing bridges must be crossed. Once crossed they can be cut down stopping others from crossing. Anyone caught on the other side can take the long way past the zombie spawn to intercept the survivors as they climb further up.

Room 5 – Safehouse

The humans reach the top and must survive here for 2 minutes to win. There is plenty of supplies but the zombies can now spawn from just below the lip of the cliff.
