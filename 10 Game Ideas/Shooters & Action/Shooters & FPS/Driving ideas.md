Possible features for the top down shooter

- time based objectives

- time based activation of buttons

- weapons that have a firing delay (laser cannon)

- queuing up of shots to fire

- laying of traps for enemies

- defending objectives while they are counting down

- the player places bombs or markers and enemies come to disable them

- enemies respawn/reinforcements arrive

- fixed emplacements

- slow moving projectiles, grenades, mines, bombs, to show off the slow mo more

active defenses to take advantage of slow mo without dodging

4x Visualization I want to see:

- Geoscape planetary view

- Solar planets lineup

- Starlanes shown as “timeline”

- population shown by urbanised centers on a planet

- infrastructure shown as lights on the night side of a planet

Mechanics I have never seen

- Technology must be “stored” in planetary buildings (archives)

- high tech dependancy on special solar systems

- low tech dependancy on homeworld

- outwards and inwards citizen migration forces

- external migration forces (refugees, tourists and xeno-immigrants)

- level of secrecy of government

- big/small scalling of ships (colony ships would be huge!)

- colonies are a burden for a long time but give other benefits

- saving your race progression path for the next game

Mechanics that have been done badly before

- alien abilities that are abstract and not modifying discrete values (+1)

- populations not liking one another due to philosophical/ideological diffrences

Supplies and orders arrive by FTL space craft after a long wait. Players place orders with long lead times and are given a tender of items that the home system wants for trade. The player must have these items ready by the time the trade ship arrives else they forfeit the tender, dont get their required goods AND must pay a fee for wasting the ships time. Each tender is basically a short term goal or responsibility.

Players might make many small scale tender trades knowing that they cant fill all of them when the trade ship arrives. Then they can select which trades are the most important and complete those first.

These ships take a long time to arrive. So there might be more than one in transit and the player must think several orders ahead. Also there is time pressure once an order is placed as the ship will leave shortly after placing an order, and if caught before it leaves it can be cancelled or added to.

Random generated structures. Player chooses where they are placed. So each power generator has simalar parts bit they are arranged differently. The player can build a new power generator anywhere they like and it takes up some space possibly enclosing several areas.

When you want to place a new area an overlay is displayed showing three theoretical placements. The player chooses which they prefer.

Earth colony ships arriving periodically this is when new structures can be placed. Also this is when new migrants arrive.

Quality and quantity of migrants modified by policies the player agrees to for a fixed number of years.
