Permadeath shooter

How does the player develop? What does he stand to lose when dying?

> Geographical Location?

How competitive can a freshly “re-born” player be?

Player skill vs Character skill.

How long can a character last during a fight? Lots of health may make up for permadeath if options for retreat/defensive play are offered.

Why would older players help younger ones in a ruthless pvp world?

"labour" is highly prized.

Older players gain experience bonuses for assisting.

Server pays for them to help

Saftey in numbers, (meat shield)

Fame/recognition.

New players must also feel like they can make a difference immediatly without being "twinked".

The respawn state should be still effective in numbers.

Killing a new player should not yeild a large immediate gain.

Higher resources can be found further from the towns. Here older player groups can set up camps to store equpment and to log off safely.

The further from town a player group gets, the harder it is to reinforce their numbers. It will become more important to keep the members alive.

In addition,quantities of stored food and ammunition will be much larger.

Moving large quantities of goods should cost resources.

Distance from towns also will mean more frequent attacks from wilderness creatures. A portion of a groups members must be on hand to protect their assests.

Instant action can be had as when a groups members are online, its assets will be attacked by creatures. This means pve can be picked up quickly for short plays.

Arena combat is also available in towns for players wishing to practice pvp combat.

Player characters suffer from morale. If they are hit, or impacts occur nearby, or friendlys are taken out nearby, or you flee from melee, your morale drops. The only way to regain morale is to move further away from all enmies, or to return to your squads rally point.

While morale is broken the player gets a defensive bonus to compensate for a lack of offensive abilities.

Loud or explosive weapons should have a larger impact on morale, as would terror weapons like flamethrowers. These weapons should be very expensive to utilize.

Having a larger rally point or defensible position should be positive for morale. Defending your only camp while listening to reasurring voices of a command pa should make thing much easier for the defense.

As members being killed or breaking morale effects other players morale, there is a downside to bringing too many inexperienced fighters. That said, if you are fighting inside a defensiple structure you own, the amount of people you have doesnt really matter.

Morale can also cause you to drop equipment if you "go negative". Similar to overkill damage gibbing someone, this effect would only trigger if the player was already on very low morale, and then suffered a huge shock/loss of morale.

PVP attavcks on player forts are acheived by the agressor declaring an attack date. The defender then selects which time the attack can commense. During this whole day the attackers members cannot come even close to the defenders camp.

Once the hour comes, the attack can begin.

The aim for the attackers is to drive the dfenders away or allow sufficent time to raid some supplies. This can be tough as each camp will have many close rally points. These can be captured temporarily once the attackers are close. If no attackers are nearby, these slowly revert back to the defenders.

If the defenders run out of rally points, they will be forced to flee either on foot or in a vehicle, or to surrender.

Surrendering in this situation means the player cannot be harmed for 24 hours but cannot leave the camp area. They drop all their equipment and armor and cannot interact with anything.

The new camp owners must defend the area for a few hours once victory is claimed.

Trade/Barter system

Trader NPCs are set up in towns and camps. A trader NPC is given a list of items that the player wants, and is given a stack of items the player wishes to sell. Each item is given a value in whole intergers. These numbers determine the trading ratios between items.

If a player does not know how much the vendor wants for the item, they can select to “make an offer”. The vendor will display a selection of items that would be suitable for trade, but at a pre-set markup. The buying player can make 2 offers, reducing the offered items as he wishes. As long as an offer is above the hidden minimum price, the sale is made. If the player offers an amount lower than the HMP twice in a row, then the deal goes sour and the player cannot deal with this vendor again.

Environment resources: Reasons to fight

The obvious ones are petrol and ammunition. One allows you to move large amounts of equipment and people, while the other increase efficiency of your fighting forces.

Food/Water is another resource that would be severely limited in the wasteland setting. This could represent the number of players you can support in your squad/gang/empire. This must be controlled carefully as we do not want everybody forced to be farmers in a P-APOC shooting game!

Oil/petrol could be difficult to store and move. In reality you cant move a large amount of petrol around very easily, and this would be even harder in a wasteland environment where the vehicles must be suitable for offroad travel. Camps may have a large storage tank, but getting gas in and out could be very difficult.

Ammunition as well as petrol could be for sale in the main town at a fixed price. Perhaps there is a large but finite quantity of both, and the price inflates based on the remaining stocks.
