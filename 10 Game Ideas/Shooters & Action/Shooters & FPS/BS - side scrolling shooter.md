Dodging and shooting

Different weapons?

selectable paths

"3d" like paper mario

Capital ship with turrets

Cruise missile that must dodge everything then hit a target

Play several consecutive missiles that are going the same speed. Set up the level for later missiles.

Missiles might spilt up mirv style for further confusion

Active defenses need to be deployed at strategic times.

Brainwash shooter

Abilty to mind control an enemy builds up over time

Control any enemy.

Enemy falls into formation with you

Hold spacebar to have your units shield you

Each enemy fires constantly once mind controlled

Each enemy had a different attack

active defense abilites eat the mind control power.

Redirect

Shield

Slow

-------

Before playing, the player chooses which powerups he wants and during whiu segment of the level they will drop.

Levels are unwinnable without powerups

-----

Survival shooter where the levels are long and enemies hit you often but for very small amounts of damage.

Ammunition and fuel are all limited

Player makes choices to take a longer rout that has more of one resource.

Give the player some information about the levels and what obstacles to expect.

Some obstactles can only be overcome with lots of resource use.

----

Physics shooter

Shooting enemies does not clear a path, instead it makes wreckage.

Each weapon causes different kinds of wreckage.

Some enemies have reactor breaches meanin they explode after a short time.

Your shields can absorb a lot of small wreckage. Large wreckage ignores them.

Wreckage is pushed by weaponry, enemies are not

Use wreckage to destroy other enemies.

Mouse to aim

Keys to move back and forth along a line.

Aim from diffrent angles for macimum effect

Some enemies have weakspots whichcause sifferent wreckage effects.
