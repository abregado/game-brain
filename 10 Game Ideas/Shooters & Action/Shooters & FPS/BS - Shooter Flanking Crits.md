Shooter with a lock on style aiming. Players lock on than use the mouse aim to adjust their aim minutely (to aim for head, weapon, ect)

When locked on the chance for a critical hit is shown above the crosshair. This percentage is based on:

- Length of time that the same target has been locked on (targetting)

- time since the attacker fired last (recoil)

- facing of the defender (coverage)

Each weapon can have a recoil attribute. This is how much firing the weapon effects the critical hit chance. High Rate of Fire weapons will have much smaller values to compensate.

Each player has a targetting suite that determines how quickly the percentage goes up over time. Smaller armors have less power to spend on targetting suites.

Each armor has a coverage value that determines how greatly they are effected by being flanked. Tanky armors might have massive damage reduction but only a small coverage value.

Most armor (including light) has relativly high damage reduction so that in a straightout fight without criticals, the fight plays out over a long time allowing reactive strategies to be played. Two similarly matched opponents fighting head on will be engaging in a fight of attrition.

Because allowing enemies to flank you results in fast kills, teams should pursue more bottlenecked areas of the map
