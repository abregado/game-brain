Concept - Tourism FPS

Sever pays MMO. FPS. Exploration focus. Combat is cinematic only.

Instead of players paying the developer for game time, server admins pay the developer per player. Payment packages are set up so that admins pay per block of players. Admins can decide to pass this cost onto the players, work on a donation system, or provide the experience for free.

Development of the game world is provided by the developers to the admins on a per player day basis. Admins decide which developments to place into the game world, and these are increased in usefulness as more players use them.

Development options include:

- Roads (more players using them increases quality and speed)

- Vehicles (Boats:Cars:Bikes-upgrades become avialbale if more players use them)

- Service structures (More widespread services, quality of service with use)

- shelters (Safe places from certain effects: weather, creatures, exposure)

- Safe logoff points (Keeps your character safe while your away)

- aircraft (very fast transport between developed areas)

- protection against environmental hazard (hot, cold, drowning, disease, fungus, ect)

- Deployment options (parachute drop, orbital deployment, ect)

- player items: deterrants, rations, power packs

> Services can be sold for real currency, as can items. Server customization is the highest priority. Each admin can tailor his world how he likes, but ultimatly must create something popular for the players. Alternativly the admin may supply these items to players for free, or hire them out rather than sell them. Options for tourism business also should exist. Assets and services can be sold on, or hired out again.
>
> World Detail
>
> The game world does not have to be realistic but it must be detailed. Players will be coming to the world simply to look, not to interact. Some ideas include:
>
> Non-agressive Fauna hiding from the players
>
> architecture
>
> Alien/unkown locations and structures
>
> Non-natural anomalies
>
> Ways of recording and sharing adventures (screenshot sharing and built in journal system)
>
> Reasons to travel in a group (suit power limits number of items that each player can carry)
>
> Highly varying Flora
>
> Extensive cave and river systems
>
> Weather (hostile and benign)
>
> Interesting agressive creature attacks (death animations for the player)
>
> Nighttime only events, creatures, locations
>
> Orbital bodies and stars (intersting night sky)
>
> Dual suns for interesting lighting effects
>
> Wind effects
>
> Methods of attracting and repelling certain creatures
>
> Insect species
>
> Seasonal events (predicability so as to generate interest)
>
> Tracking creatures to find them (clues/footprints/other tracking tech)
