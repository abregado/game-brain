BS-Exploration multiplayer shooter

Players join the lobby

Select a saved crew

Each player selects a crew member

Equipment selection

Game owner selects planet to explore

All players review and help survey the planet from orbit

Once a majority decides on a loaction the game generates the land area.

Shooter segment of game begins.

Players are dropped off by Dropship or by Orbital insertion pods

only a rough idea of the resource location is known

resources include:

mineral deposits

alien city ruins

rare creature trapping

crashed spacecraft

Players hike, drive or fly closer to the resource location

Environment reacts to their presence

Once close, the players perform surveys while continuing to come under attack

Once the best location is found the players can call in extraction equipment

Everything causes the environment to become more hostile.
