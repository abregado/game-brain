Source Mod ideas

Urban Horror

Multiplayer game (Team Based) – Objective based

- Different Objectives on the same map

- Players don’t know what objective they will be getting until a short while into the map

- Perpitrators look like civilians most of the time

  - Manchines

  - Feral Ebons

  - Serial Killers

  - Carrien

- Security Level increases based on how sloppy the perps are

  - At high Security, Shivers and eventually power armor is dropped in

  - At Maximum Security level, Operatives are targeted as well

- Objectives include Investigation, Maintenance, Kill and Detain

- Unrest increases based on how sloppy the Operatives are

  - At low levels, civilians have a chance to fire on operatives

  - At high levels riots form or darknight operatives spawn

Setting

- Always raining

- Overcast so daylight is dimmed

- Streetlights can be knocked out to create darkzones

- Civilians walk the streets and inhabit areas

- Plenty of dark places to hide

Upper Downtown

Multilevel

Gantries

Tunnels
