Map Idea Es_jumpgate

Concept: To get all ship based combat to take place in the same area, increasing the ferocity of the battle. Currently ship based combat takes place around both capital ships, instead the combat should begin between them, and them only move to the capitals once landing is an option. Each capital ship is in its own “spacebox” and the players can move between these boxes by using a Gate positioned in the “middle”

Infantry Based Section: A small station is connected to the jump gate and consists of 3 main rooms.

Room 1 + 2: Hanger. These small hangers are open area with little cover. Only fighters spawn here. These two rooms are connected by the third room. Each hanger opens up to space on a different side of the gate.

Room 3: Reactor Room. Technically two rooms that meet across the event horizon of the gate. These two generators power the twin gates and keep them open.

Space Based Section.

The corvettes for each team are located on either side of the gate. No fire can pass through the event horizon. Unsure if you will be able to watch the action through the gate. Since both sets of corvettes are technically right next to one another, all players will be in one area fighting in both offence and defence at the same time, or on the station nearby. The battle should be intensely furious.

Once the corvettes are destroyed the capital ship can be attacked and landed on. The capital ships only have a single capture point located in an easily defended location known as the Bridge. Victory is achieved through capturing this point or by destroying the capital ship.
