More basic source mod ideas

Deathmatch/weapon mod

AIM: Making deathmatch more location, positioning based. Each “encounter” should last longer with both players trading blows. Plenty of opportunity to withdraw. Emphasis on extended gunplay and active defences/dodging. Pickups are not just offensive. Defensive and miscellaneous pickups are available as well.All pickups should have a downside to being picked up.

Modifcations to gameplay

Loose aim – so that weapons can me snap fired but very innacuratly when not locked on.

Lockon – holding the right mouse button after placing target in centre of screen allows you to track the enemy. When standing still, aim increases in accuracy.

Remove kill markers – you cant tell if you have killed someone until you see for yourself

Stun Damage – players can ragdoll but not be quite dead. After a few seconds the player will stand up again if not totally dead

Reaction time – different players will have different reation speeds at different times during combat. This effects how long it takes your weapon to track your aim (head movement)

Players never have more than 2 offensive pickups (one primary, one secondary)

Players never have more than 2 defensive pickups

Combinations of weapons create a plethora of options.

Momentum – the longer you go in one direction, the faster you go

Energy Shield

Only Protects front when deployed. If deployed correctly can richocet bullets. Comes combined with a pistol weapon. This is the basic spawn weapon.

Upgrade weapon types to be used with Energy Shield

Maser pistol – cannot be ricocheted

Blast gloves – splash damage

Wrist SMG - firerate

Plasma cannon

Takes time to charge before firing. One mode channels power into beam speed (faster projectile), the other into beam width (splash damage). Two handed. Jump halved while equipped. Attacks from rear may detonate the cannon.

Rocket Launcher

Must be stationary while loading missile. Tracks target but can be dodged.

Twin Rotary Cannon

Deployable to under forearms. Spinup/Down. Wide spread. Massive ROF

Armor Shoulder Boosters

Crouch jumping gives you a higher jump in the vertical plane. Also double jumping

Armor Boot Boosters

Allows boost skating. In combination with Shoulder boosters allows hovering

Armor Plating increase

Makes player tougher/damage resistance. Decreases reaction time.

Chaff Dispensor

Players locked on at the time of firing will have their lock broken.
