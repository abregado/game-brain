Goal: Solve the problem that there are too many individual stories going on in the early game, and not enough non-conquest goals in the late game. Reduce the very bad Signal to Noise ratio of science ships/exploration content. Fewer, more meaningful choices.

Example: The Clone Army origin.

Concept:

- Remove random anomalies and precursors.

- Base events on the Origin chosen.

- Story builds up and is viewable via Situation screen.

- Resource/Research investment in the story.

- Other minor anomalies generated based on origin “You find what you are looking for”.

Origin 1: Transmutation

- Any non-lithoid, non-gestalt empire

- Empire homeworld has a special moon.

- Chance to spawn a similar moon around any other habitable planet as long as less than 3 moons exist.

- Initial Special project.

  - 30% chance: Unlock Gas collection, add Gas to moon

  - 30% chance: Unlock Crystal collection, add Crystal to moon

  - 30% chance: Unlock Mote collection, add motes to moon.

- Each extra moon has a project

  - Adds the same deposit as the homeworld moon

- Processing event

  - Once all moons are done, start a timer for 10 years and then trigger this

  - Creates an Archaeology site on your homeworld.

  - Gives a choice for Processing, Living Space or Mutation

- Archaeology site 1 (Processing)

  - Adds jobs to planets with Moons that convert your material into different resources at each stage, in exchange for relics

  - Triggers a timer to spawn another of these on one of the other moon worlds

- Archaeology site 2 (Living Space)

  - At the end, gives a choice to convert the moons into habitats

  - Triggers a timer to spawn another of these on one of the other moon worlds

- Archaeology Site 3 (Mutation)

  - Adds the Lithoid trait to your main empire and one of Gaseous Byproducts, Scintillating Skin or Volatile Excretions.

  - Triggers a timer to spawn another of these on one of the other moon worlds

- Anomalies

  - Find similar traces of material on this planet as the moon, until X deposits found.

  - Find progress towards the next Processing tech

  - Progress toward unlocking special resource edict boosts

Origin 2: Artifact Collectors

- Any non-gestalt empire

- Old Museum blockers on homeworld

- Archeology site on homeworld

- Timed repeating event to spend minor relics to increase a trade value bonus of your homeworld.

- Special project which spawns an archaeology site in neighbouring empire

  - If they dig it up

    - they get an event to give you the relics in exchange for something

    - You get a espionage mission to steal them

    - You get a casus belli on them

  - If they don’t, after 10 years you get the options anyway

  - After the event is concluded, a timer is started to spawn another site in a neighbour.

- Anomalies are a set of options with a price. Rewards include:

  - Minor relics

  - More minor relics, but more expensive

  - Highest price can generate archeology digs

  - Give archeology specialty to this science leader

  - Add trade value to the scanned object

Origin: Hive egg/Single Destiny

- Non-gestalt

- By mid game can convert a world into a new Hiveworld empire that is a protectorate of yourself.

- Regular events to have your citizens meld with it, boosting its power.

- Eventual option to take control of the hive mind instead of your own

- Empire cost from pops and planets, but reduced from systems

Origin 4: Balloon Empire

- Any non-mechanical

- Massive pop growth at start of game

- Heavy colony production penalties

- Regular events to modify your empire

  - Decrease pop growth, increase production, increase empire size pops, decrease empire size from planets

Origin: Homeland pride

- Any non-gestalt, non-totalwar empire

- Bonuses from having your main pop be the biggest demographic in the galactic senate

- Bonuses for other empires having a leader of your species

- War type to stop an empire from breeding (puts a -50% growth effect on all planets and a decision to remove it at a cost)

Origin: Slime Mold

- Bio gestalt

- Massive increase in outpost influence cost

- Regular event which lets you make a choice

  - Harden: Starbases/Outposts get substantially tougher

  - Reach: A line of systems become very cheap to capture

  - Spread: Your owned systems get a production bonus

- Special project on homeworld to generate a target

- Targets have a special project to give a heavy reduction in influence cost and massive production increase for you.

- Outposts increase in strength over time.

Origin: Proselytiser

- Any empire

- Empire trade bonuses (for both sides) for treaties

- Border friction penalty (more friction)

- Espionage mission to effect neighbour ethics

- Neighbouring human empires get options to increase their trade value if they take an ethics push in your direction.

- Missionary Abbey starbase building. Effects other empire colonies within range and forces ethics change.

- Demand neighbours make freedom of belief a thing (+100% ethics shift chance, -50% governing ethics attraction)

Origin: Gravekeepers

- Any empire

- Regular event which searches for discovered Tomb worlds that do not have a special tag

  - Picks one and gives it the tag

  - Shows you where it it

  - Generates a special project for you on it

- Gains a Special project on every Tomb World

  - creates an archeology site for greater rewards or

  - Gives population

  - Adds special jobs/features/blockers to the world

- Gets free claims on every system with a Tomb world

- Gains a casus belli to get given a tomb world system from an opponent
