**Tech tier event rubber banding**

Each tech tier is locked until a galactic event has triggered. The event only happens once every empire has reached the end of the tech tier.

These events tend to cause the largest empires to break into smaller pieces, so that players do not want to be the strongest.

These events should have options based on ethics. Alliances between empires that have different ethics get strained or penalties are given if you choose not to break it. When that happens, the larger of the two must make the choice.

The breakthrough event happens a random amount of time after these events are handed out. Then research can continue.

All these events should give a choice to the largest empires, always be a penalty for them, and send a beneficial event to smaller empires.

Some other event ideas:

- Military. Declare war on a larger target but with a large boost to ship stats.

- Pacifist.

- Egalitarian. Allow a colony/sector to become a vassal.

- Materialist. Spend influence per month or suffer stored tech penalties

- Xenophile. Pops leave to several smaller empires

- Xenophobe. Close borders with a friend or cancel an agreement, leave the Senate?

- Totalitarian.

- Spiritual. Schism or become fanatical. If fanatical, schism or lose fanatical.

- Hive. Deviancy or schism. Purge pops (path is up to you)

- Machine. Redefined objectives.

- Mega Corp.

Generic events and effects

- Researcher upkeep increase

- Happiness rebuff

- Leader upkeep or bad trait accumulation

- Pop growth penalty

- Fauna infestation

- Star Base cap reduction

- Fleet cap reduction

Maybe these can be situations?

**Multiplayer Goals**

At year 5 an event gives you a choice of three goals

At year 50, another set is offered

Some goals are weighted to appear more often based on ethics and civics.

Goals should be achievable by the player, not based on RNG or on opponent actions.

Completing a goal gives a large bonus to income and final score

**No science ships origin**

Instead, an event pops up asking how much you want to invest in exploration. Then the next event comes in an amount of time.

Events explore and survey systems and planets. They allow you to choose direction and some properties of the planets you find.

Options can include:

- Focused scans of a planet

- Reveal more map

- Survey a particular system

There should be lots of tradeoffs and situations where you want both.

**Coop portal mod**

At year 10 each player is given the option to spawn an activated Gateway in their Home system

**Coop tech uniqueness mod**

After selecting 6 techs from a tier, you cannot get any more techs from that tier. Always spawn a reroll tech.

**Coop flattening mod**

Tech leakage to allies is MUCH stronger. This would balance tech amongst cooperative players and also discourage making friends with everyone before warring them.

Tech leakage across open borders is MUCH-1 stronger. Holding borders with potential enemies is a bad idea.

Tech leakage due to migration and trade treaties is a little stronger.

Ethics change due to Open borders is MUCH stronger.

Research treaty should be much stronger. 50-100% bonus.

The effect of tech on fleet strength is lowered considerably. Start with 10 times less effect. All ship bonuses, and fleet cap bonuses are affected.

Perhaps this means that the size of an empire has too much effect on their military power, but at least that is offset by having more territory to control.

Tiering up in a tech gives a large percentile malus to science output, probably via Empire size from Systems, but also a happiness bonus. Happiness bonus doubled for homeworld?

Increase escape chance considerably to promote always being at war (attrition is lower).

Starbase module that decreases the opponents escape chance.

Maintenance reduction from Crew Quarters is substantially higher, but maintenance for going over fleet cap is higher over all.

**War weariness ideas**

Technology Choices

Main technologies trigger an event which gives two options. You can take a new building/item/component/weapon or a bonus to your empire. Since you can only get a tech once, you only get one of these. If you skip a new building/item/weapon, you disadvantage yourself in the short term, but then you get more bonuses by the end.

Technology Integration

Finishing passive bonus technologies does nothing, but gives you a one-shot edict you can trigger which adds the empire bonus.

The Slow Fall

Empire Selection process for multiplayer games. All players make an empire. Then all players review all empire and vote which one they think are best. Each player gets one vote of 3 one vote of 2 and one vote of 1.

The empire are grouped by voted strength and an amount of starting resources is offered for the weak ones.

Players then take it in turns choosing an Empire. If the weakest is chosen then the starting resources move up like in vikings.

Time to kill increase: Just more hitpoints and disengagement chance

Military system Tiericide. Remove system upgrade items. Replace with passive bonuses.

Dumbed down Fleets: Just remove everything from ships. Only one ship type, weapon and defensive module. Tech gives passive upgrades. Fleet power is now a real measurement.

No Sprawl Empire: Much higher Sprawl from systems, much lower for pops. Influence modifier for empire sprawl (possible?). Balance building slots against system resources.

War economy Edicts: Swaps resource production from certain jobs (Priest, Artisan, ect). Change what unemployed pops make (and then disable unnecessary buildings). 10 year minimum. Secondary versions which can only be triggered

Unstoppable End Crises: You can't win if the crisis spawns. Changes the game into a “win early or everyone dies”.

Expensive Envoys: Increase Influence cost of Sending Envoys.

Embassy Homeworld structure: Species start with no Envoys. Envoys grated by a homeworld structure instead.

Diluted Envoy power: All Empires start with 3 Envoys. Regular extra Envoys are not increased.

# Stellaris MiniGoals

A bunch of ideas for goals. You would pick 5-6 during empire creation and need to achieve

3-4 of them to win solo, or a larger number to win a federation victory.

Goals should never be to complete a number of military actions, but instead be things that can be achieved through, or cause as a side effect, military action. Try to think of goals which can be achieved both through peaceful and military goals.

Goals which promote using lesser used mechanics would be considered “better”.

Genetic Goals

Have your species less than 30% of your empire, but have them all on a higher rate of living standard.

Have more than 100 pops in your empire with only your starting species

Have no planets with a single species having greater than 50% majority.

Have at least 5 species in your empire which have been enhanced.

Uplift X species and keep them inside your borders

Have your starting species in X different empires

Purge a number of non-home species pops time by game years

Ethic Goals

Pick one of your starting ethics, push it to 51% of the galaxy.

Have every member of your empire be a slave owner

Have 30% of your empire pops with the Nerve Stapled trait

Social Goals

Intimidation: Maintain two rivals for X years without going to war.

Have X claims on foreign systems for Y years without going to war

Have claims on X different species for Y years without going to war

Have traditions from all trees

Venerated Leaders: have X% of your leader pool max be of leaders over 80% max age.

Happiness average \> 95%

Material/Land Area Goals

Control at least X different strategic resources for a period of Y years.

Control a number of Enclaves

Colonize X different types of planet

Control X species Homeworlds
