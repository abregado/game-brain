Abstract

The game plays as a traditional turn based Civilization style empire builder. Players work to colonize their whole solar system, and then later, other solar systems. Information between locations is delayed based on Communications technology, and interstellar travel is "quite slow". The players viewpoint can be changed, which takes time, or other players can be instigated as local govenors. Colonies expand and make colonies of their own, bringing more players into the civilization. Coups, rebellions as well as external wars are all up to the players. The bigger a civilization gets, the more it has to rely on human trust to stay afloat.

Features:

Random system generation based on "real" physics observations

Infinite detail, unlimited scope

Planets are proceedurally generated and highly complex

Cities and colonies are tracked down to the personal level.

New colonies rely on support from their homeworld

Location tracked relative to homeworld system.

Information transfer is non-instantaneous

Scientific, Social and Cultural changes seed from the homeworld and move outwards.

All players syncronize through an online or networked ticker

Timeline for development

Phase 1

Colony -\> City management game. Turn based.

Continent Generation -\> Biome and surface generation

Multiple city control into one "state" or government (eventually all cities on one planet will be linked)

Phase 2

Planet Generation:

Each has multiple continents, oceans, temperature and an atmosphere

Biome diversity increased

Each planets orbital space is generated

Limited resources, graded by uses and ease of exploitation

Surface resources like forests and shallow minerals easiest

Crust, open cut, shaft, oceanic, drilling and other mining types all incuded

Deep core minerals ignored at this point.

Methods of travel and freight (Surface types, Air types, oceanic types and orbital types)

Atmosphere and ocean level determines habitable areas

Gravity, orbital length, seasonal variation ignored at this point

Morale, unrest and other socialogical metrics added

Phase 3

Intercontinental warfare mechanics

Limited aerospace and orbital warfare

damage to landmasses, atmosphere, cities

Multiple different player controlled states on one planet.

Simple tactical warfare implimented.

Phase 4: Economies

Wartime economy gains power faster

Peacetime economy expands faster

The first to move to wartime economy gains the most tactical advantage

The last to change gains the most of the bonuses from Peacetime economy, but loses the military advantage.

Each city/factory/facility can be regeared/retooled for peace or wartime.

Example: Automative industries build farming equipment and commercial vehciles in peacetime, and tank in wartime.

Retooling should always be expensive. Changing back and forth constantly is never worth it

Batch construction, mass production and replaceable parts all give bonuses

Rushing construction leads to quality AND value penalties

Example of population conversion

Population/(percent in family units)/Birthrate=new \[Children\] this year

Base converstion rate of \[Children\] to \[Uneducated Adult\] = 1/maturity rate per year

University zones convert \[Uneducated Adult\] into \[Educated Adult\] and \[Trained Adult\]

Tradeschools convert \[Uneducated Adult\] into \[Trained Adult\]

Pre-schools increase the Maturity Rate

The retirement rate determines how many people are converted into \[Elderly\]

The death rate determines how many \[Elderly\] are removed each year.

Hospital zones decrease the death rate AND retirement rate
