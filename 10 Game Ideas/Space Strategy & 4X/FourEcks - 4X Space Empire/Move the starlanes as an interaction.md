Starlanes require a special resource at the node location. The more of these resources in one place, the stronger the lane and therefore the faster things can move along them.

Players can relocate the starlane nodes. At the beginning of the game there are very few, very powerful lanes

As the game progresses, players can split and relocate these resources to strengthen other nodes or create new ones. Technology also increases the effect per resource, so that resources can be relocated without effecting the old location so much.

One antagonist thing a team can do is to split their lanes that lie on a front into many, growing the front that a player needs to defend.

High tech empires can employ very small amounts of resources to move effectively. Tech can modify
* Minimum strength to move
* Speed bonus
* Upper bonus cap
* Throughput bonus
* Bonus to moving away from the node in normal space

Players should have reasons to put the most of these resources everywhere. They assist with exploring the boundaries. They assist with offensive movement. They assist with logistics and planetary production. They assist with trade.

Building networks that are compatible with your allies but not with your opponents is crucial and also how a game with this mechanic represents entrenchment and home field advantage.
