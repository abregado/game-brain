Bs - 4x minimum level of customization to provide compatibility

Concept: empires who are similar should get along. What axis' are suitable to simulate compatibility traits?

Attempt 1:

Body type/size/prefered atmosphere

Attempt 2:

Portrait

Attempt 3:

Gravity/pressure/atmo

Attempt 4:

Genetic ancestry aka species heirachy

Attempt 5:

Ship silhouette/city artstyle

Attempt 6:

Instead of having one compatibility traits for all situations, have one common one, and then one per situation. Eg// ship crews are compatible if they have the same ethics and atmosphere, migrants are compatible of they have the same body size and atmosphere, diplomats are compatible if they have the same ethics and gravity preferences .
