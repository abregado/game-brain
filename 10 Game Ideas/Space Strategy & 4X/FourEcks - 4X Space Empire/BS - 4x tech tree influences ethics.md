**Concept:** The tech tree is laid out on a 2d plane. The axis of the plane are labeled Collective/Individualist and Militarist/Pacifist. Technologies are located in this field. Technologies have a research cost, but also a social weight. The “Center of mass” of your technologies determines the default ethical stance your government strives for. Your empires ethics move towards this ideal. The rate at which it moves is based on empire age. High tech options are further from the center.

Note: This requires ethics to mean something which is a mechanic you need to read about elsewhere.

**Experiences:**

- The technologies you choose to unlock affect the social landscape of your empire.

- Early technology choices affect your empire more than later ones.

- Having an unbalanced tech build order, or bee-lining for high end techs will shift your ethics irrecoverably in one direction.

- Gives another lever to balance different weapon systems without resorting to DPS.
