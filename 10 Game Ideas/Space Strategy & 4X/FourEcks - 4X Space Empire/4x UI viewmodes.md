4x UI viewmodes

Allow each client to rearrange the map nodes

Foreign Empire center control

Our empire center control

Border link control

Friendly Planet control

Foreign planet control

Link control

Side fixed summary box

**Fleet distribution mode**

Border system setting to request, beat or be lower than the nearby foreign systems.

Interactable Ui floats between border systems

Data is down only on border systems, yours and theirs.

**Diplomacy mode**

Single setting for each foreign empire. Preferred diplomatic stance.

Interactable Ui floats over empire capitals to change stance.

**Trade screen**

Ui for boycotts float over foreign capitals

Offer trade deal over foreign capital

Current deal over foreign capital

Routes between planets are shown. Thickness denotes trade volume.

**Culture screen**

Cultural effects, sinks and faucets are shown.

Ethics (pop and govt) are shown over foreign capitals.

Ethics and population Delta change per trade crossing is shown over border systems.

Immigration and emigration flow are shown in links

Each populated system shows it's ethics and ethic effect from trade.

**Intel screen**

Show exploration priorities for unknown systems

Show Intel levels for foreign systems

Over empire capital show exploration and Intel resources

Over foreign capitals show current power estimates.

No fly zones can be set for each faction on your own systems. Notifications will be shown if there is trespass. Foreign nation gets a warning before moving in.
