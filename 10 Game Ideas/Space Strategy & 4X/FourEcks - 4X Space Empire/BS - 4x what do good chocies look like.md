Examples

I'm saving up resources for a big project and a problem elsewhere in my empire occurs. There is a way to solve this smaller problem quickly and efficiently by spending that resource that I am saving up. Do I solve this problem and resolve to get my bigger project later? What if this keeps happening?

A choice is presented that must be decided within a time limit (probably a long one). Both options are beneficial and permanent.

In the previous example, By waiting to choose, I am halting progress on the next thing happening. This gives a soft cost to postponing the choice.

The choice between a permanent, smaller boost that cannot be taken away, or a larger boost that can be removed in the future (by an enemy, future choice, ect).
