Stardrive Tabletop Armor rules

Escort

Hull Points equal to Mass. When taking damage, one HP is lost for every one damage inflicted

Cruiser

Hull Points equal to ½ Mass. When taking damage, one HP is lost for every full two damage inflicted

Capital

Hull Points equal to 1/3 Mass. When taking damage, one HP is lost for every full 3 damage inflicted.

**Armor**

Armor Points are essentially armor points from higher hull classes. Armor must be allocated to a facing during the design process.

Hull Plating  
One AP is removed for every two full damage inflicted

Heavy Armor  
One AP is removed for every 3 full damage inflicted

SuperHeavy Armor  
One AP is removed for every full 4 damage inflicted.

**System Damage**

Each system has its icon distributed to a single HP. When this HP is lost, the system stops functioning. System icons must be spaced evenly across the HP track. Divide the total HP but the total number of systems to determine spacing. Weapons and some defensive systems must be placed in the first half of the HP track.

**Fire Control Systems**

Each FCS allows the ship designer to create one weapons grouping. Each weapon may be part of multiple weapons groupings, however if a group is fired, no other groups that contain weapons from that group may fire this turn. Additional FCS may be purchased as normal.

Battle groups

Up to 5 Escorts may be formed into a group. Up to 2 cruisers may be formed into a group. Battle Groups move and fire as if they were a single ship. Damage is dealt to a single ship in the group until it is destroyed or breaks off from combat (FTL disengage). Battle Groups may not be formed or broken during combat.

Victory Points

> Escorts Cruisers Capital Civilian

Destroyed 2 4 8 6

Escaped 1 2 4 3

Captured 3 5 10 8

Simplifiying points like this causes the smallest ships of each class to become targets as they are easier to destroy but yield as many points as a larger vessel.

Shields and Screens

Screens protect the hull from Energy weapon damage. This includes Batteries and Cannons. Screen icons may be placed in the lower half of the hull.

Shields come in two parts, Generators and Capacitors. Generators produce Shield points at the end of each turn, while Capacitors increase the total number of Shield points on the ship design. Generator icons must be placed in the upper half of the hull while Capacitors may be placed anywhere.

Standard Generators produce 1 SP per turn  
Standard Capacitors allow 3 SP to be distributed amongst the ships arcs during design.

Points System.

Considering removing Points value all together, balancing ships based on weight. Similar to the Victory Points values. This will cause the largest hull of each class to be the best. Perhaps fleet composition can be worked out on a tonnage basis. 100Mass vs 100Mass for instance. Historical naval battles have always been recorded in tonnage.

Race Creation

Each player gets to design 5 ship types to use during his battles. They may be designed however the player wishes. He/She then selects 1000 Mass of ships to build up their navy.

Running away

Post Battle Damage Effects

Hull and Armor Damage is not removed from ships after each battle. System damage is completly repaired and Repair Crew/Marine Squads are replenished. For each ship, decide wether you wish to place it in drydock. Drydocked ships cannot be used for the next battle, but will be completly repaired at the end of the next mission.
