Stardrive Technology

Every race begins its galactic life having just discovered intergalactic travel.

Once a race leaves the Oort Cloud in their home system, a discovery is made. A spherical chamber is found on their home planet. Its placement seems random; however the area it is found in has been discovered and monitored closely for as long as the species can remember. All tests show that it has always been there.

A single entrance that is perfectly sized for your species leads to a single room. In this room are many other “people” from all the other species who have left their solar systems. Communication barriers between these species are completely non-existent.

FTl Technologies drive the expansion of the species:

Entropic Vacuum

Reduce the energy surrounding the ship to zero so that the ship may accelerate beyond light speed

Singularity Drive

Modify the laws of relativity by generating one or more singularities

Hyper-layer Drive

Moving the ship into a different dimension that doesn’t follow the same laws

Fold Drive

Bending space/time to bring two points closer together

Rift Drive

Tears space-time allowing the ship to move without time flowing

Beam Drive

Converts the ships matter into energy and transmits it

Skitter Drive

Many teleports in succession to reach the destination
