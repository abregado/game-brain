Stardrive Ship Design

Process
Brainstorm
Select Mass
Select Engines and FTL
Select Defensive Systems
Select Offensive Systems
Allocate Shield and Armor Points
Calculate Hull Points
Name Design

Brainstorm
Before designing a new ship type spend some time thinking about what it is this design is going to be doing. Every ship class is designed to fulfil a role on the battlefield. Try to imagine what kind of tactics you will employ with this ship, and the weaknesses of the other ships you plan on taking.

Select Mass
Military Spacecraft in Stardrive may have mass ratings between 2 and 100. Civilian vessels may have any mass from 2 up to 500. Based on which mass you select the ship will be catagorized into one of three ship classes.

Military Classes
Escort Class: Mass 2-18
Cruiser Class: Mass 19-36
Capital Class: Mass 39-100

Civilian Classes
Merchant Class: Mass 2-500

Select Engines and FTL
