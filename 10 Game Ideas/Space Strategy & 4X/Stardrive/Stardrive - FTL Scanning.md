FTL Scanning

The universe is very big. New players will be placed within 100 lightyears of the outermost border system. In this way new players are not isolated from events, and older player constantly become surrounded.

Players send fleets of ships out into space to search for new systems containing valuable worlds. Factors involved in the area of space that can be covered include:

Ship Speed

Scan Range

Fuel Range

Area to be covered

Rarity of system searched for

Whenever an exploration fleet is sent out, the owning player selects the criteria of the system and planets they are searching for. An estimated chance of finding a similar match is displayed and then the search can be modified to improve the chance (by increasing the size of searched area).

This method is much more in-depth as many of the ships attributes contribute to its usefulness as an exploration ship.

Alternate method

Species may use land or space based telescopes to locate stars within a certain range of their worlds. Once located an exploration ship may be dispatched to survey the system itself.

This method makes a ships speed its most important attribute for the purposes of exploration.

The information regarding stars is procedurally generated at the point of discovery, however the detailed information of the system itself is not generated until an exploration ship spends a certain amount of time working there.
