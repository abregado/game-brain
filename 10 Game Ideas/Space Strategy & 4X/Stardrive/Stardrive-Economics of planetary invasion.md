Economics of planetary invasion

Primary Goal:

Raid resources and technology  
If defended well, minor losses for both attacker and defender. Overall less risk for attacker, but less impact on defender.

Subjugation/Enslavement  
Requires total destruction of all military units. Strong military presence required to keep population in check. Allows immediate usage of planets facilities, but needs the most resources to maintain.

Extermination  
Requires first the defeat of defending military. Then a smaller force to stay and slowly kill off the civilian population. Leaves the planet and facilities intact for research and decommission. Will give the most financial gain if successful and doesn’t damage the planet at all. Allows the bulk of attacking forces to move on to another target. Most chance of Rebel attacks and civilian uprisings.

Annihilation  
Includes the use of extremely powerful weapons to defeat the defenders at all costs. If failed, this will most likely end in the destruction of all attacking units. Orbital Bombardment and Bioweapons are the most effective ways of dealing with this type of attack.

Quandry: Orbital Weapons too powerful?

Why land ground troops when you can easily destroy your opponent from orbit?

The resources gained by taking over a planet will be immense. A successful planetary invasion will in the long run earn more than it cost. Raids almost always earn what they cost, and are very fast.

Annihilation using Bioweapons and Bombardment will not leave much for the attacking team to research. It will damage the planet perminatly, making re-population difficult. Players who unleash such devestation will never be pardoned for their crimes.

I think the best way to stop players just blowing eachother up is to make the other options much more lucrative. Raids give results quickly and are low risk, Extermination allows the warmachine to get rolling while still generating a lot of income and Subjugation takes the longest but reaps the most rewards.
