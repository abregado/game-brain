Full Thrust Battle linking system

Each player designs 5 ships types

1x Capital, 1x Cruiser, 1x Frigate and two of their choice.

Each player spends a large number of points on a Fleet of Reserves

Each battle allows players to select a number of each type of ship.

Players have a number of strategic locations that are like territories and generate resources after each battle. These are taken or destroyed by opponents during battles.

The player with the smaller reserve fleet has more chance to attack.

Each Scenario has a pre-set amount of ships that each player can use. Choosing a scenario can lead to haveing a great advantage over your opponent even if outclassed.

Each ship in the reserve needs to have a maintenance cost paid to be used in a battle

Each ship can only be used once then must miss a battle to re-tool. This is when a ships maintenance cost is paid. If damaged the ship must miss an additional battle to be completely repaired.

Ships can be used when damaged in battles.

Each ship has a number of Structural Hit Points. These cannot be repaired. Ships will eventually need to be retired.

During battle ships will receive damage until down to Structural HP, and then they will retreat. Players can attempt to keep ships in the battle, but this will require some sort of test to stop battles grinding down to 1 ship remaining.

Players must attempt to complete objectives and cause enemy ships to retreat while avoiding damage. Manuvering will be the most tactical decision. Flanking damage should be awarded. Hit Points in the rear quarter will be less than elsewhere. Damaging these locations will lead to ships retreating faster.

Players should be increasing in reserve fleet size, but slowly. Rewards for playing well should be higher than for simply playing a lot of games.

Designing a Race

Each player may chose to use a premade Alien race or to design their own. They may select a single Ultra Tech weapon for their race to use in its starship designs. Once an Ultra Tech is selected, the player must design five different starships that they will have access too during the campaign.

The player then purchases a fleet of ships to the value of 2000 points. This is the races Reserve Fleet and ships are drawn from it to create different sized Fleets for use during games.

One ship that is purchase comes with a Veteran Crew and may select a special ability from the list.

Each player also starts with an amount of Population that they must defend and a single Shipyard.

Pre Battle Setup

The player with the smallest fleet may roll 2D6 and choose the preferred result. On a 1-2 the smaller Fleet chooses the battle. On a 3-5 play the Patrol Mission. On a 6 the larger fleet gets to select the mission. Mission Types are:

Patrol: A small number of ships from each force encounter each other while out patrolling local space for resources to exploit. Both forces use the same fleet structure (3x Frig, 1x Cruiser). The battle will often take place near an anomaly of some kind (Asteroid, Nebula, ect)

Raid: The attackers attempt to damage the enemy supply lines before reinforcements can arrive. The target is a number of freighters that move slowly across the board. Attackers may use 8x Frig, while the defenders can only have 2x Frig and 1x Cruiser. Reinforcements become available as soon as the attackers open fire.

Assault: The attackers attempt to destroy an enemy Structure. They may take 2x Capital and 4x Cruiser while the defenders may select 4 ships of any class they like.
