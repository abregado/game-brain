Stardrive Propulsion Technology

Stardrive is supposed to be compatible with both soft and hard sci-fi content.

A major concern for me is that while possible, combat at FTL speeds should be avoided.

Intersolar Movement takes place at FTL speeds. Combat does not.

Does movement within a solar system take place at FTL speeds?

If ships move within a solar system at sub light speeds then range of weapons during combat needs to be considered. Also, defences would be clustered around important locations. Planets couldn't be protected completly from orbit.

If ftl/near to ftl is available within a system, then a level of interdiction must be made possible. Non-ftl Ships can be intercepted by ftl ships easily.
