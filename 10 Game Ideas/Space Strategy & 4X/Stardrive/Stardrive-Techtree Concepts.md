###  **Technology Levels**

Breakthroughs will be divided into a number of levels.

- Level 1 is pre-warp technologies and do not require specials.

- Level 2 is early warp tech. This requires refined Common Resources

- Level 3 is modern technology. This requires refined Uncommon Resources and the prerequisite Level 2 tech.

- Level 4 is high tech. This requires Rare refined resources and a combination of two Level 3 technologies.

- Level 5 is Ultra Tech. This requires refined Ultra Rare resources, and a combination of two high technologies. Only one ultra tech can ever be researched per species.

### **Enabler Technologies**

These are the trunk of the technology tree. Each enabler allows the player to create a specific technological “commodity” called a Component. These are constructed by using Special Resources. These become the components required to build all of the devices that each technology gives you.

Each level of enabler is based off one of the types of special resource. As the tech levels get higher, their needed resource comes from a rarer group (See above). A new technology level cannot be researched until you have access to a refined special resource (through colonization, exploration, conquest or trade).

**Materials**: Building Blocks, new alloys, structural component, design principles,

**Computation**: Computers, Intelligence Enhancers, Biotech thinkers, Jupiter brains, storage techniques, communication techniques

**Detection**: Sensors, Cognitive upgrades, detectors, microscopes, scientific modelling, meters

**Manipulation**: Building techniques, control processes, high level reactions, growth methods, tools

### **Refinery Techs**

Once you have a rare resource planet, and a colony on the planet, you can begin mining the resource. The Raw resource is completely useless at this point except to research a new Refined Resource type.

These Refined resources are used to research new Enabler technologies.

The value of Raw resources, will see them being stolen and traded for high amounts of cash. This is mainly because they will be the key to unlocking the next level of tech without becoming dependant on another race. For this reason, Raw Resources will be much larger in size, costing more to freight.

### **Maintenance Fees**

After making a breakthrough to a new level/category, an empire must pay a maintenance fee for each technology researched.

This will mean that empires must overcome a hurdle but then the next hurdle will be harder to achieve.

### **Archives**

Technologies may be flagged as “Archived”. This means that the maintenance fee is divided by 10. This technology can be re-activated by paying the original maintenance fee.

Empires will only be able to progress if they archive older unused technologies. This also means they will become dependant on the high level effects

### **Research Fields**

A Field is a collection of technologies that all relate to one particular device or effect. Technologies within a field can be researched and upgraded but may never surpass the abilities of the “higher level” fields.

*Example: An empire researches Plasma Warheads as a Level 2 field. Within this field are technologies relating to payload, targeting, efficiency, manufacturing and design of the missiles themselves. No matter how much this field is researched it may never exceed the possibilities of the Level 3 field of Anti-matter warheads.*

### **Tech names and prerequisites**

When selecting to research a new topic, the player will be asked to select the appropriate commodities to fill the pre-requisites for the new technology.

Whenever a new technology is discovered, the player selects the Name for the tech and for each of the new items which have become available.

### **Reaction types**

Each type of “reaction” has a type of factory which can perform it. These are:

Chemical: (refining, chemical reactions, metallurgy)

Physical: (manufacturing, nuclear reactions, assembly, machined parts)

Biological: (growing things, genetic reactions)

Special: (everything else)

Option: perhaps I will just change this idea completely due to its seeming lame.

### **Technology Sub-fields**

Each technology advance has ~5 sub fields that are minor enhancements to the technology. Each field increases areas like Efficiency to build, range, damage, cost, time to build, effect by a small amount. Each field can be gained up to level 5.

*Example: The Early tech Torpedo technology has the sub fields; Range, Power, Size, Materials, Time. Each level in these sub-fields gives +1% to that area.*

### **Subfields and new Technology**

Each technology has a Base Cost. Each sub-field has a cost per level. Then unknown the new technology is displayed to the player with a Cost Range. Once the base cost is reached, the player can view the stats for the new technology and either select to invent the technology immediately, or to spend further research on the project. At any time the player may invent the technology and the surplus research is allocated RANDOMLY to the sub-field levels.

This means that players can just quickly invent technologies to use as stepping stones to higher techs, or they can spend a bit longer developing a more masterful version.

The maximum amount of research is equal to the cost of HALF the total sub-field levels. This means that players cannot ever “max” out a technology.

### **Re-research**

An empire can opt to research the same technology again to attempt to gain a better combination of sub-field levels. Both technologies may be kept, or either may be scrapped. Remember that each technology requires maintenance.

### **Enhancement**

Enhanced versions of technologies occur when the two Enabler commodities were created from different refined commodities. This generates a regular version of the technology except that the maximum number of sub-field levels is equal 75% of the total.

This gives empires reasons to colonise planets with different Special Resources when they already have an enabler for that tech level. Also remember that the empire must PAY for the extra sub-field levels and that they are still random. Thus this might be utilized by empires with stronger infrastructure or those looking to gain a boost against enemies in the same tech age.

**Conceptual Techs**

Policies – Pre-warp

Each policy negates one negative effect of city problem and generally costs income or uses resources. Drastic policies effect the entire empire with their downsides.

Welfare: Negates the effects of unemployment on morale. Costs income per unemployed citizen. Increases unrest on all other planets by 0.001%

Curfew: Increases the effectiveness of peacekeeping troops by 50%. Increases all unrest modifiers by 20%. Increases unrest on all other planets by 0.01%

Martial Law: Increases the effectiveness of peacekeeping troops by 150%. Increases all unrest modifiers by 50%. Increases unrest on all other planets by 0.1%

Birth Control laws: reduces birth rate by 30%, never going below deathrate. Unrest is increased by 0.01%

Public Festivals: Reduces effects of overpopulation by 50%. Costs income per population.

Enforced Labour: 30% of citizens in unrest still work. Negative morale effects are doubled.

Wartime propaganda: Decreases the public opinion of a species you are at war with. Cost income. Effects whole empire

Double rations: Reduces the effect of war weariness by 50%. Costs food production per population.

Luxuries distribution. Reduces the effect of famine by 50%. Uses luxury commodities at location.

Colonial Shift workers: Reduces distance from homeworld effects to zero. Costs a lot of income per citizen outside of homeworld

Environment Drills: reduces the effect of unfamiliar territory to zero. Reduces the productivity of all colonists due to reduced work hours.

Bombardment Drills: reduces the morale effects of bombardment and citizen casualties by 80%. Lowers productivity across the planet and costs income.

Navy Sponsored Immigration: Your empire can offer incentives and training for people to emigrate from a planet. This allows you to select the rate of emigration on a planet but costs income per citizen.

Corporate/Local Sponsored Emigration: Companies and governments are allowed to operate civilian spacecraft and can fund emigration activities. A certain amount of emigration is free but you can also fund it further.

Personal Emigration choice: Each individual can chose to emigrate at anytime. You have no control over emigration rates. A majority of emigration is free.

Civilian FTL Ownership: Civilians can operate FTL craft for their own purposes.

Corporate/Government FTL Ownership: Bodies of power can operate FTL craft for their own use.

Civilian Sublight Ownership: Civilians may own starships equipped with sublight drives.

Corporate/Government Sublight Ownership: Same as above except individuals are not allowed to own sublight vehicles.

Navy Controlled Sublight/FTL: Only the navy may operate spacecraft
