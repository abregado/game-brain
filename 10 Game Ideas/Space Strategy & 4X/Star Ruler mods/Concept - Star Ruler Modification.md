A Star Ruler Modification Concept

#  Goal

- Simple game mechanic additions

- Minimal addition of subsystems

- More choice required for colonization

- Reasons for early game military conquests

- Work with what is there as much as possible

- Start easy, finish hard

- Make it more “gamey”...

- but also promote storytelling and community content generation

# Stage 1

- Changes to planetary conditions

- Every planet has a penalty to building cost between 0% and 200% (Bell curve with 100% as median), as well as a population growth penalty (Habitability)

- One in 5 planets has a bonus to Luxuries/Goods, Metal, Electronics, Adv Parts, Food or Research of +50%

- Add in Lux/Goods/Research modable rates for conditions

- Add in population growth rate penalty

- All planets have 13-15 slots, Homeworlds have 40 slots

- Added a hotkey to change camera focus through each item on the pinbar.

- Add the Domed Colony structure

- Colony Ships build Domed Colonies

- Governor replace Domed Colonies with other structures

- Reduce Labour cost of Planet Capital

By the end of stage 1 the game should already have a different playflow. Players need to explore all the systems in the game and choose which ones to colonize early. The closest, biggest planet is no longer the best choice every time. Your homeworld is a stronghold for your empire.

# Stage 2

- Changes to system generation

- Gas Giants are uncolonizable

- System size is increased by at least 5 times

- Number of planets in a system increased by double (max 15 planets in a system)

- Space between planets increased

- Exploration View: A view to show a Star, and planets lined up next to it with basic intel on each, detailed on hover, and complete on select. Planets with positive conditions are highlighted Yellow, while ones with a positive condition and no negative condition are highlighted green. Others are highlighted red.

Stage two sees solar systems take on a very unique feel. Searching all the systems in the game for the right planets will be a major element of gameflow. The new system view should show all elements in the solar system next to one another so you can view each newly explored system quickly.

# Stage 3

- Placement of planet types based on star color and orbit distance

- Each type of planet has a different set of bonus conditions

- H3 harvesting from Gas Giants (stations or fuel ships) – More efficient than Fuel Depots on planets

Stage three sees that planets of different types are represented graphically. Terran/Ocean worlds will have bonuses to food, population, luxuries, ect. Rocky and Lava worlds will have bonuses to metals, electronics. Ice worlds will have bonus to research and advanced parts. Gas Giants will only be for collecting Fuel.

# Stage 4

- Distance between star systems increased

- Jumpdrive changes to range, fuel use, charge speed, downtime after jump

- Jumpdrives only usable from outside system

- Jumpdrive emergence points are outside target system

- Likely emergence points are shown on system hover/select (point closest to enemy systems)

- Jumpdrives and high fuel conventional drives both suitable for interstellar travel

- Shields and capacitors set to zero after jump

- Better information when zooming a long way out. Stars shouldnt shrink as fast as everything else, and hover information should change to include enemy presense, number of drydocks, civilian ships, ect

Since solar systems are being moved further apart, interstellar travel needs to be worked on. Jumpdrives should have much longer range, while fuel use should be based on the mass moved. Scale should determine maximum travel distance. When using a jumpdrive, some way of causing the ships to jump repeatedly until they arrive needs to be created. A drive system that only works outside of systems needs be created a well. Perhaps this can modify the mass of the craft (divide by a number) to increase the acceleration. This would only work outside the system boundaries.

# Stage 5

- Custom starting ship designs for each saved Species

- Several more Racial Abilities

- Automatically import new ship designs as tech becomes available

- Record each ship design you made and link it to the current Species

- Record which order you researched technoloies and save this to the Species file

- Option to load “research queues” from the last game you played with this Species

- Ability to easily share Species

- Species files available for the AI to use (fight against your own successful Species)

Once the other stages are complete, the more indepth coding begins. Players can now develop their Species over a number of games, trade them with other players, or compete against them as AI. Each player will build up his own slice of Star Ruler fiction as he plays.
