#  Description

A new set of economic structures could be added to Star Ruler, linked to the Computer and Nanotechnology trees. These could be used to replace existing structures. The benefit of these new structures are that they require no workers, meaning population is only used for the production multiplier. As a downside they will produce a little less per slot than regular factories

# Structures

## Orbital Nano Forge

Massive Labour production without workers and their associated needs. Replaces Shipyard and Drydocks.

High Nanotech, Computers and Engineering

## Automated Deepcore Mining

Produces Metals from a planet without workers and at reduced ore consumption. Replaces regular Metal mines.

High Computers, Engineering and Metallurgy

## Hypernet Retailer

Produces large amounts of Goods and Luxuries but consumes Electronics

Medium Computers, Economics and Sociology

## Zero-G Manufactory

Produces Electronics and Advanced Parts, as well as enough Trade Capacity to move the produce to the galactic bank.

Ultratech Computers, Nanotech and Ship Construction

## AI Research Council

Produces more research than a regular laboratory. Consumes Electronics.

Ultratech Computers and General Sciences.

## Sentient Metropolis

Houses and Feeds larger populations. Generates no labour or resources. Consumes Electronics but has enough trade capacity to cover its needs

High Computers, Biology and Sociology

## Transcendence Mainframe

Houses and Feeds insanley large populations. Has high resistance and health. Consumes Electronics and Advanced Parts.

Ultratech Computers, Sociology, Biology and Mega Construction

## Planetary Defensenet

Worker free Laser defense structure. Increases damage resistance and health of structures on the planet. Powerful short ranged planetary weapons. Consumes Electronics.

High Computers, Beam Weapons, Shields

# Gameplay Changes

If a player focuses on Computer technologies they will not gain a pure economic advantage. Instead their dependency on population will be removed completely and replaced with a slight dependency on Electronics production.

Population can still be used to gain Production Multipliers on planets. Later on, Sentient Metropoli and Trascendence Mainframes will give large mutipliers for just a few slots. These could also be used with regular facilities to provide workers and food.

Computer structures will have Electronics as their highest cost. Metals and Advanced parts will be required in much lower quantities. A player focusing his early economy on Electronics production and midgame on Computer buildings should experience a economy explosion in the mid to late game.

# Other Changes

The AI Paranoia racial trait should be increased to 2 points as the Computers technology tree is much more useful now.

Computer opponents should all take the AI paranoia trait so as not to damage their current workings (Do AI players use Computerized ships?)
