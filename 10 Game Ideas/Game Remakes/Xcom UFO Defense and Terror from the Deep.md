## How multiplayer works
Multiplayer 2-4 players.
### Geoscape
Each player gets their own XCOM instance.
They can build new bases within range of their own existing bases
You cannot see where other players bases are.
Every player gets their own research
Every player who completes a research causes every other player to get a small research boost to that research, and unlocks it.
### Ground Missions
When a player lands at a mission, each player can decide to control aliens or supply 2-3 soldiers as an exchange. if it is an alien base mission, then the attacking troop quantity is divided amongst all players and nobody can play as aliens.
When players control the aliens, the aliens get far less equipment and units.
Alien controlling player gets funding bonus for their XCOM instance for every kill they get, and another bonus for wiping the enemy squad.
Both teams start with the whole map uncovered but with fog of war.
Exchange teams get funding bonus for making kills, but lost funding bonus for every XCOM agent lost by any XCOM player.
Each unit has a new state, if it has acted this turn. Activation is alternating. Spending any TP with a unit will cause it to become activated.
Reaction fire is used from the left over TP from last turn. Activating a unit refreshes its TP (points from last turn are wasted if not used in reaction fire)
Aliens deploy their units on the field, but must spread them out.
XCom deploy their units in groups along one edge

## Peters Alien mission idea but twisted to my liking
Launching an alien mission costs a number of resources. UFO Navigation, Power Source, Elerium, Alien Alloys. Whatever XCOM does not steal, you get back. Elerium is always lost.

When you launch a mission, you select how much of which aliens to send, and which weapons/equipment to send. Each mission type and size has a max crew (same as the UFO sizes in the original)

The mission launches from your base, then flies to the target location. Supply missions go to Cydonia.

**Alien Research:** This adds a new research topic to the list that is currently researchable. Picks a random location on earth to go to.

**Alien Harvest:** This lets the player collect biomass as a resource. Picks three random locations on earth to go to, landing at each. Collects less than Abduction but does not raise XCOM funding.

**Alien Abduction:** This lets the player collect biomass as a resource. Picks three random locations on earth to go to, landing at each. This collects more resources but raises XCOM funding in the countries effected. 

**Alien Infiltration:** If this mission succeeds, XCOM’s funding from the country in question is reduced, and possibly cut to 0 entirely.

**Alien Base:** The alien player uses this to build a new base. Alien player can select the position. 

**Alien Terror:** This lets the alien player reduce XCOM’s score significantly. Picks a random location to attack.

**Alien Retaliation:** This mission type from the original game should be renamed Alien Base Attack. It is used to attack discovered XCOM bases. Target can be a base or a fixed point. If a fixed point, the UFO goes there and waits, attacking any nearby interceptors/aircraft.

**Alien Supply:** Launch a ship to Cydonia. Fly to a random place on earth, then go to Cydonia. After a few days, return to a new random place on earth and then fly to the base. Place an order for Elerium, Alien Alloys, UFO components and base equipment. Engineers and Researchers are also ordered from Cydonia.
## Alien base
Same modules as XCOM bases, but everything costs Power Supplies and Alien Alloys to build. Each hanger allows one simultaneous mission.

## Alien economy
Aliens do not have cash or upkeep.

Economy gameplay is more centered around manufacturing equipment AND troops from materials. Manufacturing jobs have good speed with low number of engineers. The player should be pressured to launch missions with the minimum quality and quantity of aliens+equipment.

## Alien passive effects
Bases constantly lower funding in the country they are in.
Every kill the Aliens get reduces funding.
