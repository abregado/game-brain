## Smoo2th combat (flocking)
Goal: Change tactical combat from turn based into real time. Player choices are before combat begins and then the player watches the battle, pausing to assess the situation to make better judgements later.

### Pre-fight UX
A view of all the ships in the fleet.
There are 6 squadron slots available. Ships can be dragged around and dropped into these squadron slots.

Each squadron has settings:
Primary Target type: Biggest/Smallest/Fastest/Slowest/Hull/Shields/Isolation/Firepower
Primary Target weight: 0-1
Range: Close/Mid/Far (as a modifier of shortest non-pd weapon range)
Flee at: 75%/Half/25%/10%  | Shields/Armor/Structure
Distance from SquadronLeader: Close/Medium/Far
Distance from zone center: Close/Medium/Far
Distance from enemy fleet mass center: Close/Medium/Far
Distance from friendly fleet mass center: Close/Medium/Far

These could also be 01- sliders

A ship in a squadron can be clicked to mark it as the squadron leader.

Also on this screen can be seen a breakdown of enemy ship count and size. Exact models are not shown. 

Once the player is done configuring, they click the Begin Battle button.
