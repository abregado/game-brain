**Steam account linking determines monsters**

Every player needs a steam account.

Monsters are generated based on the games they own in steam.

Trading cards determine their items

Every monster gets a series of attacks to unlock.

The next attack in a monsters unlock the gains points when it is involved in a battle.

When a move is unlocked, it is unlocked for all monsters you have.

After so many battles, you can unlock another monster from your library.

When a game is unlocked for the first time, your name and the game used is sent to the server. You get "first unlock" trophy and everyone sees your name.

Make deals with game studios so they can design the monster for their own game.

**Pokemon Ideas from Work**

Per “Type” Expereince

- 

- Each monster gets XP in each of its Types

Gain TypeXP by fighting in an associated environment or against same type enemies

Increasing a type in level makes its resistances and Vulnerabilities greater.

Moves used also increase TypeXP

Evolutions are branching and based on TypeXP

Behavior Stats

- 

- Happiness based on how often you use the monster

Obedience is how often the monster will do as you say

Initiative is how often the monster will do something if you dont give it an order, or if it disobeys

Aggression is how often the monster will pick an attack move over a defensive move, and how much happiness is gained from taking down enemies.

Move System

- 

- Monsters get a selection of moves

each move has a Type and gives Type XP for using it

Moves evolve as well as they are used.

Some moved develop multi-step combos which are activated by selecting the move several times in a row. The end moves can have different Types and therefore shape monster evolution.

Defensive moves increase stats for the rest of the battle, and also a large amount for the next turn.

Move type selected modifies resistances for this turn as well

Trainer experience

- 

- As you fight more battles your trainer gains skill

Trainer has a Awareness stat which gives a chance to see what move an opponent will select before you choose.

To gain this advantage you must have a large difference in Awareness compared to your opponent.

Wild monsters have a low Awareness so you will often gain this advantage.

Monster catching

- 

- You will fight multiple monsters in the wild at once

Your monsters will mostly be fighting at the same time, only one or two in reserve.

Area specific teams will be important

You can sometimes encounter a nest and can capture eggs of monsters unhatched but must face off against higher level “mother” monsters.

You can also catch wild monsters that are fully grown but they will have high levels of disobedience, low initiative and high aggression or pacifism (less useful traits overall).

Effort values are used to determine stat increases but stats are only awarded on each TypeXP increase

Max levels is 20 in a single type. A well trained level 10 monster should be able to beat a badly trained or wild level 20.

Stats do not bloat with level. Instead most stats come from Effort Point values.

Development potions will increase the amount of stat earnt in the next level, This makes them useful to feed to monsters during periods of intense effort value training.

Monsters hatched from eggs are better in the long term

Higher evolutions will gain levels slower so that keeping monsters unevolved is good for rapid growth

Moves are gained at evolutions, and better moved must be developed through use.
