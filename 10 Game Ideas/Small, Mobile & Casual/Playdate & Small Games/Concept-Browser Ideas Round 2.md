Current BB games have (Good mechanics in bold)

Tick Based

Multiple Resources

Levelling to unlock new/more content

Instanced multi-player interaction

Special Resources purchased with real money

Advertisements

A reason to log back in every ~1 hour

Rewards people for playing constantly

A Single way to play efficiently (linear gameplay)

Fixed odds

Minimal Player skill requirements

Almost unlimited unlockable content

Future content hidden until accessible

Predictable gameplay

NO consequence for death or playing badly

NO punishments or setbacks, only lessened progression.

Non complex resource relationships (Gold = Sheep = Food = People = Gold)

Current BB games need

Multiple paths to victory

Player inputs more important (reflex/puzzle elements,time based inputs for text combat)

Non-repeatative gameplay (Minor differences each time)

less guarantees

Since Consequence for death, set backs and punishment are right out, how about limited time to achieve a goal

Extra rewards for playing through a whole gameplay set

More output as to WHY players are not progressing as fast (hints, tooltips, guides, ect)

Three Rough Ideas

Techrush

Players control a research facility on a remote planet. They progress through the technology tree, discovering new technology options and obsoleting old tech. Old tech may be traded to other players to enable more options.

Players develop products using their technology, which are sold for cash based on their rarity (compared to all other players). Their aim is to keep their facility funded over time and eventually research the ellusive Ultra-techs.

Players are limited by the number of technologies and also the total research points they can maintain, and thus must be careful not to become too powerful too early. Eventually a player will stagnate and be forced to reset their technology tree. This gives them an advantage on the next play through.

Competition is in creating the rarest products without overspending on different research options.

Olympian Rage

Players take on the role of gods. Over time they charge up their godly powers which allow them to meddle in the affairs of mortals for a short time.

The first part of the game revolves around players developing a god over time that maximises their playtime. Both power regen rate and maximum stored power can be increased, but finitly. Once the first segment of the characters development is over, the player moves on to the next phase.

Second Phase is generating resources in the form of followers, wealth and respect with other gods. The intention is to impress some Goddess and create a godly heir to continue your line. If you cannot create an heir you must mate with the mortals, beginning a new game as a Demigod. Your spouses wealth and followers are added to yours and a portion of this is inherited by the heir (you start again as the Heir).

Once you have a god that reaches the third phase, the height of power for gods, you must do battle (political and military) to secure the worship of particular temples/diocses. You move up and down a heirachy building a party of lesser gods to help you take over these temples.

Temples are what give your god his purpose. These are unique (Such as God of: Sun, Moon, Earth, War, Peace, Love, Wine, ect) Each one gives you a special bonus ability that is uncomparable to other gameplay elements. Gods in this phase cannot marry/create heirs so they must be used until they become old OR until you give in, starting with a Demigod.

This cycle means there will always be players who are in all phases of the game. Players who recycle their toons will have a larger advantage if they choose to compete in the third phase. A single player can “win” if they get all of the godly temples and be claimed the one true god.
