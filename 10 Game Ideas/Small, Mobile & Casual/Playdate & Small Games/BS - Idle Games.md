Idle Exploration game

spend res to find more things to develop to get more res

map expands outwards on a node based system

proc gen map will generate forever

develop nodes to get res

visiting a node sends its res back to the homeworld

set up waypoints to move around while idle

making resources AND moving them to where they need to go is important

moving people from the homeworld as a resource

people make your production upgrades actually tick

people effect tick rate of a node - too few/too many is not good

each planet in a system is a slot

click a slot to build something

click a thing to upgrade it

everything has a maintenance cost - only build what you need

maintenence cost is fixed per upgrade, not based on how efficent the upgrade is (due to consumtion or population)

homeworld has MUCH population

growth rate based on over population, but lags both ways

growth should swing back and forth and never be constant

fixed initial starting conditions - earth and our solar system

most nodes should be not worth developing heavily

build freighters to move commodities back to the homeworld

these use resources to build and consume some resources to maintain

research/development comes in pairs. choose one and lose the other.

build option become available in systems with resource stockpiles

players will recognise good systems after a while.
