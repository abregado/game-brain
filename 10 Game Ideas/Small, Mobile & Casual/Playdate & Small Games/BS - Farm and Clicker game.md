Box folding or city game

Grid based gameplay

Each structure has a cool down

Tap to activate

Tap and hold to speed up a cool down

Limited speed up power which recharges while offline

Structures get exp each time they are activated

Structures:

- Simple 1x1. Super Fast cool down. Copies self to adjacent tile. If no adjacent tile it levels self quickly.

- Drill 1x2. Fast cool down. Places simples in a line 3 tiles long away from self. If no tiles free then levels up self. Higher levels can spread further

- Tapper. 2x2. Long cool down. Taps all structures within 10 tiles. Additional exp for all tapped tiles

- Heat sink. 2x2. Very long cooldown. Lowers all cooldowns within 10 tiles by 1 minute.

- Central. 3x3. Very long cool down. All cooldowns based on distance to Central. Further means longer cooldowns.

- Conduit. 1x1. Slow cool down. Levels self. Can only be built next to central or conduit of higher level.

- Waller. 1x1. Medium cool down. Places mountains adjacent. Higher exp places higher mountains.

- Disabler. 2x2. Slow cool down. Slows enemy tiles cooldowns within range
