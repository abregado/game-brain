Building to get the teleport tool

Building to get the item sending tool

Building to get the player town portal tool

Tool system

Requests Office

- Opens a UI to order new structures with the next Supply Ship

Shuttle pad

- Adds more shuttles for moving items

Power plant

- Creates new building areas

Power silo

- Increases number of structures connectable to a power plant

Docking Bay

- Opens a UI list of ships waiting

- Ship is basically a queue of items in and out

- Each item in the queue is marked as necessary or not

- Ships stay docked until all necessary items are given or taken

Supplies Drop

- When you complete a task, new equipment is dropped here

  - New structures

  - Stacks of resources

Structure kit

- Arrives on Supplies Drop

- Select it to place it somewhere

- Appears immediately (for now)

- Must be placed next to a power plant or another structure.

Fuel Refinery

- Add space crystals and it will create 10 items of space fuel over time.

Space Fuel tank

- Holds one unit of space fuel.

- Fuel can be collected from here.

- Fuel is put in by connected Refineries.

Space Bar

- One sad crew can be dropped here and it turns into a happy crew over time

- After 6 crews have been converted, it asks for a Space Beer item.

- Stores happy crews in Space Hotels when complete, if connected.

Space Hotel

- Stores Happy crew.

- Can only take items out of these.

- Happy crew that stay here too long turn back into sad crew.

Processing Bay

- Takes Rocks and turns them into Metal or Crystals

- Stored created items in connected Warehouses.

Warehouse Kit

- Can only be placed next to Processing bays, Logistics Centres, Auction House

- Items get stored here from those buildings.

Logistics Center

- Stores multiple items per Warehouse

- Items can be taken from and put into the Logistics Center for easy use

- Specific Items can be taken from Warehouses.

Galactic Auction House

- Items stored here, or in connected Warehouses are sold whenever the Supply shuttle arrives.

Ship Types

- Mining Ship

  - Essential Give

    - 1x Rocks

    - 1x Sad Crew

  - Optional Give

    - 5x Rocks

  - Essential Take

    - 1x Parts

    - 1x Fuel

    - 1x Happy Crew

- Police Ship

  - Essential Give

    - 1x Sad Crew

  - Essential Take

    - 1x Happy Crew

    - 1x Part

    - 1x Fuel

    - 1x Space Beer

  - Optional Take

    - 5x Sad Crew

- Supply Ship

  - Essential Give

    - 3x Sad Crew

  - Essential Take

    - 3x happy Crew

    - 1x Part

  - Optional Take

    - 2x Space Beer

    - 3x Metal
