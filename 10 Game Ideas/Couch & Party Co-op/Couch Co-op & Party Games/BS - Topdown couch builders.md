**Triangles**

Position points and join them together into groups of three. The area between the three points is filled with machinery. The larger the area, the better the function.

**Continuous pushing game**

- **Flat plane**

- **Cliff edge detection**

- **Put down anywhere you can get an object**

- **Linking mechanic**

  - **Press button on start**

  - **Walk to other**

  - **Press button to connect**

- **Adjacentcy detection**

- **Empty space detection**

- **Everything is a device**

- **Smooth rotate**

- **Placing matching items next to each other creates a deco item in between.**

- **Devices can only be turned on, then they are on for 15 minutes.**

**Idea 5 CaveDwellers again**

Every round the player spawn from a cave exit into a forest. The basic loop is that you need to clear land to build on. Each time you start a round, the forest encroaches on your area. Spending more time clearing land just means you cant utilize it as much.

Basic stuff can be set up near the entrance, but mid game stuff needs special locations on the map. Paths need to be cleared and maintained between these locations.

**Idea 4 (Not in space plox)**

Something to work against the player: Animals

Animals move randomly

Sometimes they escape and you have to collect them

Each day you can put each animal on a machine to collect its resources

Machines make stacks of resources

When a stack is full, you can carry it to a truck

NOW IT’S A FARMING GAME

**Idea 3**

Build a space base

Before each play session, players are offered three trades to choose from.

Player has 15 minutes to deliver the trade, and unload the trader shuttle

Most Trades give more resources than they take to make, so the player can progress

Some trades give less resources than they take to make but give “progress points” which can be used to unlock new things to build.

Between trades, the player can play as long as they want.

Size of the construction is limited by power producing objects, which are hard/impossible to build.

You place the docks so you know where the traders will appear.

Certain transformations only take place in fixed locations.

Player builds these locations, and the paths between them.

No bad events. But you have to do "work" to progress.

**Idea 2**

Focus less on what the player builds and just that they place SOMETHING, and it is persistent between rounds.

Add in a mechanic which promotes redesigning areas into more cpu efficient designs.

- Idea 1

  - Power generators have a max output

  - Only one generator can be online at a time

  - So the player can build a huge thing, but only a small part of it can be active at any time

- Idea 2

  - Player can always develop more efficient things which do more per update cycle

  - Promote player replacing old things with the new things

- Idea 3

  - Simply limit the building area

**Idea 1**

Make it a damn space game already

Hexes

Whole screen on level

New building blocks come from centre.

Place machines next to their pre-reqs to turn them on

Some tiles are the “first prereqs” called sources

These sources are on the edges of the level.

As machines run out, they request new resources. If they need a base resource, Pallets of these resources are added to a queue to be brought up on the elevator.

Whenever there is a free elevator, it brings the next pallet or item in the queue.

Once it reaches the top, it waits until you unload it before going down again.

Items can also be queued to be sent down.

Producing and delivering a large amount of something at once unlocks it for ordering.

This way supply chains do not become overwhelmingly labor intensive.

Place items on the floor to start a stack. These stacks can be much higher than pallet stacks.

Machines take items from behind them, so you can determine how much work will be done by stacking to your required height.

Oxygen round timer

Sanity round timer (hard cap)
