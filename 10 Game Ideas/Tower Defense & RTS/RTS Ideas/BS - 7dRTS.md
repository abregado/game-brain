Abstract RTS idea

- areas of operation

- assign units to AOs

- Combat is automated

- player manually activates special attacks and abilities

- Current units displayed next to AO

- Current special abilities selected next to AO

- AO links

- Resources over time and from capturing sets of AOs

- Each AO can be part of multiple sets

- procgen maps

- units can be moved to linked AOs for free or shipped to unlinked ones for a fee (airdrop)

- slow combat, units make many hits to die and can retreat to be repaired

- units move at different speeds between AOs

- cannot move units when enemy units are coming down a link

- 

Rts breakdown

- Allocating resources to immutable unit variations

- Allocating units to high value locations

- Location value based on economic/combat bonuses or victory conditions

- Wealth and resouces start low and increase over a game.

Things that could be done

- Design to create dynamically important locations on the level.

- Resource gatherin rates drop (or fluctuate) over the course ofthe game to create different economic pressures

- Units can be recycled or transmuted at locations of lowstrategic value (travel to a distant part of the map)

Mapping ideas.

- Node based maps (oil rush)

- Obvious points of stratigic interest (starcaft)

- Expansive area to battle (landair battle)

- Flow lines with ambush points (dark reign)

- Two height levels (sunage/starcraft)

- Safe zone/construction area/ battle area (dune2)

- Bases are "off map" (earth 2030)

- Single point bases (rise of legends/nations)

- Important lines instead of important points (Age of empires)

- Territories or zones of control (company of heroes/z real soldiers)

Elements of real time not in turn based

- Reaction/reflex

- Precision control under pressure

- Micromanagement is about pushing the button faster rather than pushing more buttons

- Information availablity in the hud is more important

- Harder to intercept enemy strategies in real time

- Resource income per second is less predictable

Specific idea

Survival competative rts

Ship comes with surplies three times in the first 9 minutes.

Players build up resources to trade

Limited easy resources on map, deeper reaources are harder to get.

Once ships stop coming players must survive as long as possible

Trading/fighting available

Slow grind down after ahips stop coming

Two phase of build up and frimd down
