Frankfurt Game Jam ideas

Using existing assets

5 horizontal lanes

touch lane to move to it

character moves between lanes

blocks fall into lanes

touch a lane you are in, that has a block to pick it up

tap a lane you are in to throw

sobekites come from other end

thrown blocks squash the sobeks but them remain where they are

you cant throw blocks at sobeks behind an existing block

sobeks can climb over blocks

survive for a fixed time

dont let sobeks past

original game idea

build walls to funnel sobeks off the screen

sobeks turn left or right when striking a block

flame bowls determine which way they turn

turn away from the nearest bowl

a few sobeks come early during the build phase

then a hoard of them arrive

terrain includes unmovable blocks and places where you cannot place

countdown shown as three lights

when all lights are off the hoard will arrive

three phases really build, hoard, cleanup

4 player local
