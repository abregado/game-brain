BS - Tower Defence cyclical waves

Each TD has its own way of generating waves of creeps. Most simply have pre-determined waves designed for their difficulty by the developers. Some have Waves that use a formula to increase in difficulty over time, while others use completly different proceedurally generated waves.

Some ways to determine creep waves

**Non-sync'd cycle timers**

Break creep wave makeup into several categories and have these values fluctuate over time. Desyn the fluctations and introduce a random element so that each wave is different and the player experiences periods of heated action, and periods of rest.

Example: Creep Stats: Speed, Toughness, Damage Reduction, Regen, Size. Each fluctuates between -50% and +100% over a different period of time.

Introduce harmonic and distortions. "Wave fronts" could be generated by each wave adding a part of its unique makeup to the next wave. These will continue to add up until they are counteracted by the natural cycles. Sudden spikes could also be introduced.

**Player chosen difficulty**

The player himself chooses how difficult the waves will be. Fending off a larger wave would obviously bring more risk, so rewards must be balanced carefully. Perhaps there is a bonus reward for fending off an attack without any creeps "getting through" that increases exponentially with the higher difficulties.

**Creep Bank**

All creeps must be sent every 10 waves but the player can determine how many to send each wave. The player must send as many as possible while building up for the final wave. If things go badly a few earier waves can be dealt with but then the final wave will be much larger.

Whether or not the player can decide on the type as well as quantitiy of creeps, or how much information the player gets about the creep types is up to the individual game design.

**Push away**

The player can select a few creeps to be held back until the next wave but cannot see the makeup of each wave until it arrives. Doing so might make the next wave very difficult. Creeps pushed back cannot be pushed back again.

**Attraction Towers**

One variety of tower causes more creeps to arrive, or the wave timer to accellerate. This might be best tied with a wealth bonus from this tower. This variety of tower should be completly optional, as new players will most likely not require the game to be more difficult.

**Creep market**

The player is given a set quantity of several different resources that must be spent on Creep Waves. The strategy will come from matching the creep waves with the towers that have been constructed. Each wave has more resources to spend, and the player will eventually not be able to simply choose creeps that he knows can be beaten.

This might also work as a TD where the player cannot choose which towers to build. After seeing what towers are built, creeps must be matched to ensure that the towers win. Reverse TD but the player still wants the Tower team to win.

**Smart Creeps**

Some TD use this already. Creep varieties that make it through a tower maze feature more heavily in later waves. This may lead to players creating very balanced mazes.

**Strategic run throughs**

Difficulty of waves might be based on how badly the creeps are doing. If none make it through for many waves, the Creeplord could start to stockpile some creeps from each wave to form a "bunker-buster" mission. The player, knowing this should let the minimum number of creeps through that is possible to stop the buildup.

**Difficulty based on savings**

The less a player spends, the more creeps arrives. This could work well with any TD that has an Interest mechanic. The player will always have pressure to spend, but towers are not as good as cash in a lot of cases.

**Difficulty Based on towers**

The number of towers determines the wave size. Selling up unused towers will lower the number of creeps that attack.
