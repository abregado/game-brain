Checked 2018 July

- Click to realign a set of tiles which over time, unalign by themselves.

- Limit critical choices about character advancement, slow players will lose the points or perhaps they are assigned randomly

- players are offered three sub optimal sets of advancement each character level and must pick which to take

- “Three sub-Optimal” could be applied to a number of genres: city builders, strategy, rougelikes

- FPS game in which you rotate cubes to destroy enemies

- the same game but in 2d: rotate squares to destroy enemies and protect friendlies

- platformer in which you bring prize items back to the spawn/teleport/level select room to improve your character's progression

tower defence city builder

- build a small outpost in the centre of the map

- enemies attack occasionally from the edges

- construct a maze wall and set up a rotation/roster for your defenders to keep watch

- each citizen can be assigned a job based on the available tools

- some tools allow a choice on job: guns allow defense or scavenging

- if you want to grow food sustainably, it uses MOST of your labour and tools

- a single worker should be able to make x1.1 food, while consuming x1 food in the same time

- the player sets up his base near water towers. These can be refilled manually but it takes a really long time so most often a player will move on to a new water tower

- Thematically might be better to be a space colony or other non real world scenario

- Link in with facebook to get towns people nmae list

- Using vehicles you can send workers on various quests for resources.

- Maps have proc gen houses which you can reinforce or booby trap to stop enemies.

- The players block off areas between buildings to create thier "town"
