Cthulu Calling

or

Buildfags vs Griefers

A high level concept document by:

Glenn Fisher, Steven Crossley and Ben Buckton

Introduction

A creative world building game where one team must fight to keep their creations alive from the nigh unstoppable onslaught of Cthulu and his minions. Players can fight for Creation or Destruction in Semi-Persistant worlds, sculpting the earth and legions of hellish creatures to do their bidding.

Background

Creative games are becoming more and more common in the market today. This concept hopes to bring competative gameplay to a currently conflict free genre. Some players compete to create a more persistant environment in which to build, while other direct the creatures and environmental effects that challenge the builders.

Description of Gameplay story

Key Features

- Semi-Persistant gameplay environments

- Build enormous structures to defend your land

- Build your own way

- Command the minions of destruction to overrun the builders

- Tower defence style creeps attack with or without the forces of Destruction to guide them

- The shape of the land affects the battle's outcome

- Herd, direct, evolve and attack with creatures formed out of evil itself

Genre

Thinks god game, mixed with tower defence and action rpg. The forces of destruction will have a less direct impact on the game, commanding creatures to attack vital targets.
