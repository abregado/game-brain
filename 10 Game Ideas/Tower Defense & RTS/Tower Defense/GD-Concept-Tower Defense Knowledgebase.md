Browser based TD

Three TD Ideas

Browser Based TD game.

Instead of a path, the creeps must move from node to node on a map.

Players design the defenses and then send waves of creeps against their friends

Resource model:

Players get resources per tick from their facilities

Players get resources per creep killed in their facility

Players get resources per creep that makes it through a facility

Players get creeps per tick

Players can focus on either attacking or defending, or a combination.

City TD

Takes place in a city

Players demolish buildings to create the paths (puzzle element to path creation)

No lives or bases

Robots patrol the city looking for humans to kill

The player loses when he has no humans left

Resource Model:

Humans are the most important resource, but are limited

More humans are given for completing challenges (rescues, broadcasting)

Metal/Tech/Weapons is the other resource, collected from destroyed creeps

Players move their towers through the city protecting from searchings during day and night

Different tactics are needed for day and for night

Browser based game ideas

Not designed to be a viral marketting scam. But microtransactions have to be in to support the game costs. To start with, regardless of the game, all bonuses should be available to players over time for free. Slowly, key abilities become “pay to unlock”

Most games ive seen dont allow you to interface with your friends who also play the game. This should be paramount. You should have the option to fight against or ally with your friends but also with others not on your friends list.

Since players play browser based games at different times of the day, and different players play for a different amount of sessions, the game would have to reward people for having a playing pattern.

Perhaps players can set when their ticks recharge, so as to correspond with their login times.

Players might also get a bonus for picking non-popular times to play in

Also, like other browser based games the gameplay needs to seem like realtime but actually be disjointed “instances” with time running in the background.

Players also should have their assests stolen while they are offline. Perhaps it could be a WoW like system where players dont lose anything for being killed/conquered.

Players should constantly need to upgrade their stuff to stay ahead of the curve, but shoulnt be limited by “leveling” everyone should be able to interact with everyone else.

Economic Model 1:

Players gain resources per tick

Resources spent on attacks or invested into defence

Defences are never damaged

Players can never lose levels or stats, even if they do very badly.

The worlds total worth inflates every day quite quickly.

New players wont be able to catch up very quickly

Persistant World or Server Reset?

Economic Model 2:

Atrophy model. Things degrade over time

Everything has an upkeep cost

Limits the older characters to doing things more efficently rather than just MORE

Perminant death. Players can be destroyed and have to restart

Players get bonuses to their starting stats for restarting

New players catch up to old players much faster

Older players will learn to do things differently the second time

Persitant World, non persistant players

Cant destroy buildings/assests but can steal income

Stealing enough income means the player cant support all his assests

Economic Model 3:

Free market model

Resources are generated over time per player.

Finite resources generated over the whole server

Players battle to gain control of a larger portion of resources.

Resources spent on attacking are lost, however the gain for winning can be 120% of this.

Resources spent on defence are non-refundable but income is given for killing invading units.

Resources are traded to other players on a basic market system

Prices fluctuate for different minerals, creating more demand and conflict over certain parts of the world

A player who gets lucky and gains a good resource might find himself attacked repeatably or even destroyed.

Perhaps resources can be desotryed or mined dry to promote decreased supply and monopolies.
