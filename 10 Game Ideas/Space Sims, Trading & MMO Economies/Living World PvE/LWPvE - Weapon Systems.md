Weapon systems should be designed so the players want to know how many resources they consume per damage dealt.

Weapons have a spectrum of construction costs. As construction costs go down, the cost of ammunition goes up.

This means each weapon category has a different payback period.

To promote players spending more, weapons with higher ammunition costs also have higher Maximum damage payload carried per loadout.

Lasers - Highest construction costs, no ammunition costs, effects Operational condition of mounted unit directly,

Particle Beams - High construction costs, low ammunition costs, low magazine payload

Machine guns - Medium Construction costs, medium ammunition costs, medium magazine payload

Cannons - Low Construction costs, high ammunition costs, large magazine payload

Missiles - Almost zero construction costs, highest ammunition costs, largest magazine payload

Within each category, there can be variations which effect DPS, magazine payload, unit speed, optimal range, alpha damage
