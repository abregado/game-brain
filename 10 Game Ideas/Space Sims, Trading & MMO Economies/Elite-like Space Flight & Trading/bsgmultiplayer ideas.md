Game starts with a multiplayer pve battle . Each player is in control of a random ship in orbit around a random planet.

There is a battle raging. Players with military ships must find and protect players in civilian ships, herding them into fleets.

The first few waves of enemies can be defeated but eventually the players will be forced to flee.

After jumping the players are lost, without points of reference they travel randomly and must explore a massive slice of space searching for supplies.

Pve attacks are common and force the fleet to run away again randomly.

Civilian ship pilots can fly small military craft launched from larger capital ships.

Food,fuel and supplies are stored on each ship but need to be shared over the whole fleet. Also each ship has a population that needs supporting.

Large military craft have very large needs and this is where civilian craft have the bartering power.
