BS Space Flight sim

Mode to Auto match velocity with target unless you are adding thrust. Use whatever engines you can based on current direction.

Button to set aim direction to the opposite of your current one.

Friction pads: generate thrust in the opposite direction of travel. Torque to match. Work better either closer or father from a gravity well.

Subspace waves. Thrust is modified by local perlin noise field. A system can smooth out the area in front of your ship. Many ships travelling in the same direction will smooth it more. Costs a lot of energy so it isn't used in combat.

Gravity multiplier. Increase the effect of gravity forces on your ship. Uses energy. Of course to use this for long distance travel you need to be clear of local objects.
