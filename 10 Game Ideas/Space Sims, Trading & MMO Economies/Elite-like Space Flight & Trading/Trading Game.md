Concept Goal

Create a multiplayer game that is based on trading with other players and involves some metagaming

Included ideas

zero sum game world

both limited and renewable resources

local currency value changes

simple trading (mark resources as outgoing and a price)

resource “weight”: difficulty to trade

phased based or realtime?

hidden information or open?

Mechanics

Each player has a number of “reactions” which will change resources into other resources

players attempt to “react” a final material to win

limited number of currency units available for trade. These just change hands

players can put their inventory up for sale at a fixed price.

any other player can buy these at any time for that price (assuming they have enough money) by clicking on the resource

the quality of a players reactions are shown on the buttons

resources slowly degrade over time to promote trading them away

higher “tier” resources degrade much slower

each player gets a package of the lowest level resources every minute. all players get the same amount.

reactions can be set to autorun when resources become available

GUI and game state

The players screen will be comprised of a main area displaying all the available reactions

the reactions screen is comprised of layers showing the reactions and inventory of that tiers items

at the top of the screen, each other players trade offerings are displayed

the local players trade offerings are also displayed here

Other options

The second last tier might require an item available ONLY through trade

cycle timers on reactions: quality through speed instead of effeciency

proc-gen the reactions so players cant win unless they work together

have a time limit on which all players fail

players can pay pirates to increase the rate at whihc resources are lost
