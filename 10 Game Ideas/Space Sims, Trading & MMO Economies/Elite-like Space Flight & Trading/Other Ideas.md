Space Combat wargame:

ship vs ship

small fleet warfare

planetary invasions (Large fleets!)

Fleet Game with Ground Combat post-battle

Space Combat battle linking (campaign)

Planetary Conquest (different species)

Political Power (different groups of the same species)

personal Wealth (Mercenaries)

Ground Combat tactical game (DONE TO DEATH NO THANKS)

Wargames with strong RTS elements are very rare

Complexity is an issue

Perhaps move most of the complexity to pre-post battle

Army Creation can be very complicated, if it makes the gameplay much faster

Objective Based gameplay integral to the rules AKA never to the death

Completing objectives actual a vital part of the rules (effects recharging/unit interactions)

Chained events/Combos/Emergent strategy

Perhaps a wargame where you must fight but also prepare an exit strategy for the late game.

Application of JUST the right amount of force. Overkill is bad for the attacker! Taking risks and “just skimming through” is the most efficent way.

Perhaps more powerful abilities and special rules cost Victory Points

Perminant VPs, Over-time VPs or a set amount from the start.

Board control VERY important. Locational strategy of maximum importance.
