Elite Backwards Concept

Many new games attempt to recreate the Elite franchise but so far very few have come close. Each new attempt adds something deemed lacking in the original, or something that they feel will make it more viable to current market audiences.

The X-series of star flight simulators has attempted to increasing the depth of the space flight and combat.

A lesser known Elite clone, Prospector, has assimilated many elements of *Rougelike* games.

Descent is often though of as an Elite descendant, but only focuses on combat and a linear level system.

EVE-Online is an MMO version of Elite. Its development was heavily influenced by Elite and although it is a persistent, multi player world, the complexity of the “buy low, sell high” philosophy is very similar.

Vegastrike is an open source space flight engine attempting to emulate *Wing Commander: Privateer,* another Elite ancestor. The flight mechanics now include Newtonian movement, but the trading is incredibly shallow.
