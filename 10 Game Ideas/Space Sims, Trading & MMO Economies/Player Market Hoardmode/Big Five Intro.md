The Big Five are a collection of game concepts. They are heavily work in progress and represent games that I would like to play in my lifetime. The scale and scope of these games makes it impossible for a one man team to develop.

The one liners are as follows:

**Player Market Hoardmode:** A multiplayer game where everything in the game world is built and run by players without intervention from the game developers or game masters. Features asymmetric cooperative gameplay, and only market based PvP.

**Living World PvE:** A cooperative multiplayer survival game where the main enemy is an ever changing set of Hive Mind AI npcs, which mutates when they are destroyed.

**Survival Space Station:** Players work to unlock and explore a persistent proc-gen station environment where there are no rules and no safezones.Death is made meaningful through loss of geographical progress.

**The Factory:** A social story and exploration game set inside a huge Rube-Goldberg machine-like structure, where player knowledge is the only valuable commodity, permadeath is real and appearance does not equal truth.

~~**FourEcks:** In a universe that is truly persistent across play sessions, control the rise and fall of many individual civilizations. Discover the ruins of your civilizations on later paythroughs.~~

**(2018) Xenosurge Remake:** A remake of my first game, Xenosurge 2 would keep the defensive gamepay and randomized building of the first game, while adding a market puzzle to the mix, and bring in some of features for the factory genre.

Each concept has many individual ideas and is forever in a state of flux. Some of these ideas even share mechanics
