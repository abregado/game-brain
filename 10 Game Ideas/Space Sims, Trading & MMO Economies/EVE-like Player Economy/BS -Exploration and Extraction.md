Another take on my industrial/exploitation game idea using FPS.

Two types of player. Strategy and Action. Strategy players get resources for "free" from the game world for risking their assests and at the same time create opportunities for the action players to earn their own resources.

Teams of action players explore a hostile environment from FPS view. As they move through the game world they are on the lookout for enemies and resources. Resources cannot be extracted by the action players, and enemies earn no (or very little) resources on their own.

Once they discover an area rich in resources they "make a claim" and survey the immediate area looking for the best places to exploit the resources. They can then sell the location to a strategy player.

The strategy player calls in extraction equipment to be dropped at the location.Defense equipment and supplies also can be requested. Once extraction begins the game world/environment begins to fight back.

The longer the strategy player keeps his equipment operational, the more income he will earn. He places bounties on enemies to encourange the action players to kill them. Action players will fight to protect the extraction equipment becasue of the money they are paid, but also because they risk losing personal wealth if they fail.
