Industry Space Game to RTS Idea

Coop RTS defence game

One player controls the extraction process and pays players for defending. The game owner can decide who is extraction player (or select random, opting in or out of this choice).

Each non-extraction, or Mercenary player can only control a small amount of units

No leveling up. Players simply spend income earnt to upgrade units and get a partial refund on the alive units value at the end of the match.

All upgrades and units are available to any player with enough resources.

Left over funds are saved between missions. Each player has two saved resources, Industry and Mercenary funds.

Before the match begins players can spend on units and upgrades

Extraction player spends money on buildings and static defences, as well as limited replenishments (healing, ammo and energy) for the mercenaries.

Every structure, unit and weapon generates threat over time.

Most units will have a primary attack and at least one energy based ability.

All weapons will run out of ammo

All units and upgrades have a time to arrive on the battlefield. Once ordered the upgrades arrive on a dropship at your LZ and units must be moved back there to receive their upgrades.

Many unit upgrades can be purchased. Each unit should be unique.

Threat onlygoes up. After a time the players must decide to leave. Deciding when to leave before things get bad is essential as it takes time to recover the structures and units. Also coordinating to evac at the same moment will mean less unit loss.

Industry money cannot be used by mercenary forces

Mercenary money CAN be used as Industry money

Largest amount of extraction is used as a high score

Longest extraction time is used as a high score

Least equipment lost is used as a high score

Extra money is payed to each player who participates in a game where a high scoreis broken

Extraction is linear based on time and extraction equipment available

Extracted resources must be lifted from the area before payment is made

When resources are lifted off, the Extraction player is given his money and the Mercenaries get paid whatever they have earnt so far.

Mercenaries are paid from the Extraction players payment

Extraction player decides what each enemy is worth as payment

Extraction player decides how much to charge for healing, ammunition and recharge services

Mercenaries can order their own supplies but then cannot sell them to other mercenary players (less efficent)

Each Upgrade has the following stats:

Cost in Merc money

Time to arrive

Energy Cost per second

Each Unit has the following stats

Cost in Merc money

Time to arrive

HP, Energy, Ammunition, Upgrade slots (like hero inv)

Merc Player resources are:

Banked Extraction or Mercenary Money

Earnt money from this mission

Money yet to be paid
