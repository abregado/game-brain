Isues of having a complex indutrial system in a player driven economy

- new players cannot be involvd so esily

- purchaser making uninformed choices

- lack of process transparency

- requirement of wikis and spreadsheets

- if designs are items then what happens when a valuable player quits?

- dependancy on players that want to play industry

all but the last of these can be addressed with good design. industry players will be those in charge so that might make them stick around anyway.

the choices that purchsers make can be limited by moving those choices into the hands of industry players. this means that product design also takes place in the industry chain.

the accessability for new players need to be addressed. perhaps there are some items that are effected less my bad desgn, so that new players can be competative. consumable items come to mind.

Design of components

players input resources and then wait. They are presented with a design that is completely random. Each attribute of the design is randomized and fresh designs will probably not be very focused.

Players can then build a prototype or send the design back for refinement.

Each attribute an be locked in place for the next design

Designs are automatically scrapped if they are not as good as the last design

Each locked attribute makes the cycle take longer

Resources music be paid periodically to keep the design going.

When a design is prototyped, the cost is based on how high each attribute is.

Some attributes are not better or worse, but instead subjective (slot shape, prototyping resources, aesthetic choices, ammunition variety)

Locking too many attributes or trying to refine for the best numbers will take a very long time.

To begin with players just hit "design" and end up with a very random item plan for a random type of equipment. Players can consume a prototype item to lock SOE stats in to start, or a mass produced item to set only the type of item.

The same process is used to engineer a prototype into a factory plan. Here the attributes of the finished product are fixed but the final cost, efficiency and build time are variable. Also the types of components needed can be modified.

Ship hulls that are designed have differing slot layouts. Each design will have differing amounts of each slot shape and size.s

Weapons and systems fit into these slots, so some items will not be suitable for some hulls.

Ammunition has a set of characteristics. Only weapons that allow all these can use that ammunition.

Designing new slot types allows you to add modifiers to the maximums of your designs. Slots use a point system so they are never rolled 100% max stats, but they do get more points for each reroll. eventually a new slot will be so much better that players will reengineer their existing designs for this slot (tech levels)

Players should be able to name their designs from a word dictionary. Words can be added to this dictionary but will need to be checked each time for profanities.

Manufacturer stamps should also allow players to mark designs as their own.

Parts and modules are packaged as spares or used to construct finished vessels. Once packaged as spares, these parts cannot be used in manufacturing.

Manufacturing Simulation

Requirements

Difference between „just in time“ and „kanban“ logistics techniques

Adjustable timers on processes

Trade off between (Speed per batch:batch size:waste efficency)

Processes must be configurable

Retooling costs!

„positional“ strategy of building Process combinations

Inputs and outputs can come from internal cargo bays or external „trade bays“

Cargo bay customization. More specialised means holding more.

Interlinked effects (everything effects everything else)

Player skill add from:

Timing of different processes

Designing processes that are compatible with one another

Organising other ships to supply sufficent materials

Using cheaper process's together successfully

Minimizing intermeadiate cargo transfers

Process of Play

Player deploys a „research ship“ and is presented with an interface for designing a new process module.

Two dropdown boxes are available: Output and Input

Three sliders are available: Frequency, Quantity and Efficency

Several tick boxes are available: (modifers on the process, to be added later)

Each may be locked, and increasing one decreases the others.

Output determines what is being constructed

Once Output is selected, Input shows the available combinations to create the Output

Frequency effects how often a batch finishes.

Quantity effects how many units are created per batch

Efficency effects how many input units are needed per batch (multiple of the output)

Tick boxes add modifiers to the overall
