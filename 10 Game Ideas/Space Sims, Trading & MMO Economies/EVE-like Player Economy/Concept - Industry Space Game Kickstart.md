Gameplay

Combat players defend the ss contruction with fighter craft brought from earth or by using transport craft on the freightships. Freightships have output docks that are filled with contruction materials and reloading bays filled with ammunition. Cargoplanes are hired to move goods from output docks to input docks. As more cargoplanes are in space, the number of enemies will increase as well as their aggressivness. Navy fighters can be hired to defend them. When a ship is returned, a fee is taken to pay for fuel and ammo used, and also a repair bill. If the ship is lost then an total loss fee is payable. The maximum bandwidth for each player should be 3 units, while a cargo plane takes 2 and a fighter takes 1. Threat from a cargoplane should be 5 times as much as a fighter.

Gameplay:

Players can fufil defence contracts in multiple places. As the SS has limited ship docks for hire, ships can be purchased. Tenders are available to be filled by the new Utility Ship class for SS drydock. SS also converts materials-\>commodities-\>ammunition for the time being. Cloudscoops first must have materials delivered to them from the SS (input/output docks), then at regular intervals the cloudscoops will produce Fuel which can be ferried to the SS in the same way. Players who arrive early to score the fuel run will have to pay extra for defensive contracts.

Utility Ships can be purchased and taken to nearby asteroids. Smaller chunks of rock can be processed into ores and then refined into metals. These can get hauled back to the SS to fill tenders. Contracts must be made to protect the utility ships while they are working
