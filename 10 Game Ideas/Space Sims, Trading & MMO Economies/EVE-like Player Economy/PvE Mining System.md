pve mining system

goals

- teamwork

- allow solo and teamplay

- teamplay always more rewarding

- no boring bits

- enough attention needed that botting is less common

- 

different resources require different equipment set ups

industry player commits to a set period, incentivising longer play sessions (~1 hour minimum)

deployment of equipment that collects resources, how when and what equipment all matters. Example you can double your units gathered per hour, but it only also reduces the yield of those units by 45%. This means you have to spend more time refining, or transport a larger amount of resource back to the base.

some resouces can be collected by combat players, but most is by non-comms

resources in one location get continuously shower to extract. This should promote exploration to find the “low hanging fruit”. Exploration pioneers will always be getting the best yields (and therefore the best profits).

Option for explorers to sell claims complete with ore scanning results.

any “waiting style” of collection gameplay generates gameplay for others

choices to make at all stages

resources are collected AND refined on site

Resources can be refined to maximize a number of variables (value per unit, size per unit, weight per unit). More processing means less to transport

player selects whento begin a refining cycle, locked to location until it finishes

transferring goods and refining takes time

collecting at maximum efficency means contstantly tweaking your setup

there should always br danger when you are generating resurces

matching symbols to shapes that move constaty, or adjusting sliders (controls placed in the environment would be better I think)

involvement in combat for miners an option, but it lowers the yield, Example hybrid mining/combat vehicles or mining vehicles with turret enplacements)

consumables when collecting resourcs to create scondary markets

resources must be placed into hoppers and skips otherwise they can be collected by anyone

waste products from all processess, these still contain trace elements (slightly less than zero value) This is where new players can get involved with cheaper equipment and less knowledge.

equipment should costs something to run (consumables, either fuel or “mining ammo”) and the equipment that can harvest very quickly should cost more to run. This means that wastage won’t get hoarded since its “not worth it” to mine the small stuff. Also areas that are heavily utilized will eventually be not profitable for the large excavators, but will still be good for the smaller vehicles.

mining should damage the harvesting vehicles slowly to add in a secondary market

### Terrain Composition

The system for determining mineral abundance should take into consideration that valuable ores are not immediately available. A system that requires the players to first mine an area at low yield before having an opportunity to mine at high yield would mean that as the large harvesters break through the overburden, many smaller harvesters will arrive to take a bit of the action.

So the sweet spot for productivity is sometime after the area has begun to be mined. This creates an extra layer of investment for the players, since developing one area for mining will be important over multiple play sessions.

These systems can be complicated and taught to the player before they use it through the exploration system

### Exploration as a gateway Profession

Identifying areas where mining will be prevalent in the future should be a variety of profession that new industry players seek to be part of. I would definatly design the game so that everybody must do some action based combat before getting into industry.

Claiming areas should be temporary for both the discoverer and also the person buying the claim. It should also not be free. Repeatedly claiming the same area should involve going out there to reclaim it. Information gathered from ore scanning should be kept indefinitely since it will not change over time.

Experienced industrialsits will be able to recognise a decent area apon seeing a area scan. New players will most likely learn which sites are of high quality based on which claims get purchased from them.

I am not convinced that a system where players claim areas they have scanned will work. Perhaps the scans themselves can be sold, but only have a limited lifespan. If this is to be the case the scans needs to have a wide range of qualities.

### The shapes of resources

Each resource type should have distinctive patterns in the way they generate. One might have long spiralling arms, while another could be pinpricks of high concentration. I would like to avoid the “large diffuse clouds” of resources that most games have. This only promotes players moving a few feet left or right to continue harvesting.
