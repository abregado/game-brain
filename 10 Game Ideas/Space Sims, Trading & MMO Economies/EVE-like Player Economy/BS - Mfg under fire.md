Concept – Feature – Manufacturing under fire

Players cannot build or purchase items quickly in a safe location. They must activly participate in the game to spend resources on equipment.

Genre specific examples:

Tower Defense. While playing, converting wealth into towers or abilities:

- spawns creeps

- disables towers and requires the player to fire them manually

- can only be done when there are creeps nearby

- requires a Quicktime event or minigame to be completed

City Builder. While playing, construction of new buildings;

- spawns enemies which must be countered by an activly contolled army

- suffers from frequent setbacks which the player must fix

- requires the player to complete a complex puzzle/management minigame

Space colony game. Buying goods from a passing trader convoy;

- requires the player to play a “lander clone” vs the planets weather elements

- the player must fight off waves of pirates in an FPS or topdown shooter

- must load and unload the goods in a time management minigame

- protect his colony from land invasion at the same time

PVP Shooter. Whenever the player/team wants to upgrade his weaponry;

- He must order the equipment and then defend the LZ from enemies

- he becomes visible to all other players for a set time

- he must go unarmed for a period of time (suit powerdown)

- he must place a beacon and stay close to it for a time (beacon visible to enemies)

- he must enter a well known, neutral and undefenseable location

PVP RPG/Hack and Slash. When the player wants to spend experience points;

- he must do so in a PVP zone

- he must be near a fresh enemy corpse

- visit a trainer and participate in a duel with another player

When the player wants to craft gear;

- his team must set up a series of “capture points” and defend them while one player crafts

- visit a rare “power location”, which the enemy also wants to use

- have other player or parties harvest/fetch ingredient items

When the player wants to sell/buy items;

- he can only open direct trade windows in PVP areas

- must wait while the trade is completed

- must defend a messenger, courier or convoy while the items are transferred

- place his items into a vendor NPC, which must be defended (and can be stolen from)

Space Industry game. To build items;

- players must doso in PVE or PVP combat areas

- Set up complex systems of contruction craft to complete the whole production chain

- fight off waves of NPC enemies or players

- cooperate with other players to complete the whole production chain

- protect incoming transport ships with ingredient items

- harvest resources from local space
