World generation should be procgen to avoid development time overspent on level creation.

Level design can be limited to set peice locations

Players must leave devices set up for extended periods, returning late to collect the spoils.

Enemy difficulty in an area is based on how many players drop devices there.

Higher tech items need res from areas that are increasingly farther away. This should promote higher level characters to travel further.

The longest trips require teamwork to survive.

More res can be collected by using devices that take longer to extract it.

No rewards are given for kills, only collecting

Collecting in an area can only be done once enemies are killed.

Wait for others to kill the enemies or do it all yourself.

High traffic areas will become safer as enemies can never build up.
