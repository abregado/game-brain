The energy crisis of the early 2000's was not solved until 2194. Of course Earth struggled right up until the grand discovery, and most likely its development was stunted by several hundred years. As many people had speculated, space travel played a large part in saving humanity.

Our earliest Lunar colonies were deployed in 2031 but were largely research stations. No cheap method of synthesising breathable atmosphere limited the number of people active on the surface at any one time. Moon mining was so prohibitvly expensive that spacecraft were still being contructed on Earth.With Earths billions screaming for living space and food shortages occuring globally, something had to be done.

Solar power generation was the only thing that made the Lunar surface viable. Cheap energy but nowhere to live. The porous rock not retaining imported atmopshere, all habitats had to be manufactured Earthside and shipped over.

Spacecraft manufacture. Someone once said „Orbit is halfway to everywhere“ and they were right. A few enterprising companies began zero-g manufacturing in Earth orbit. Corporate efficency quickly saw a range of interorbit craft developed. Finally construction materials were available to build larger hi-resolution probes and survey craft. 2045, Missions were sent out into the solar system to discover if there was anything to being hope back out home planet.

2047, research in the microgee labs came across an amazing breakthrough. Instantaneous communication technology was to be out technological messaiah. Bandwidth limitations were extremely high. Firstly this technology was intergrated into a new breed of probes to be sent out into the solar system. The first were not yet at the closet destinations and suffered from normal interplanetary latency issues. These new probes, able to be piloted remotely in realtime, survey results available instantly, were designed and built within two years using extremely experimental transmitters. They were sent towards to asteroid belt with the aim of finding an ore rich rock to bring back to earth orbit.

It took more than 70 years for the first and last asteroid to be delivered to Earth orbit. During a eco-terrorist attack, the asteroid was crashed into the Lunar surface. Since it had landed on corporate owned lunar land, a „gold rush“ began between the orbital corporations.
