Tutorial ideas

- No attributes

- Show me all the skills that affect a given fit and my current progress to maximizing them.

Desirable mission content

- Small groups or solo

- Industrialists want missioners in thier station

- Missioners want good idustrialists to own thier agents

- There is a reason my someone else would kick down your sandcastle

- Don't remove the cool lore content

- Make reading lore not mandatory

- Mission content for more playstyles

Mining missions

- Take place at normal mining locations

- Require non tradable ores which decay after 1 hour

- Incentivized mining and returning to this station with haul

- Incentivizing setting up buy/sell orders

- Incentivizing taking a mission every time you return to station

- Increasing standings increases refining quality

- Levels require more throughout to complete in the time frame, rewarding investing in better equipment.

- Small teams can complete larger missions by working together.

Manufacturing missions

- Given a blueprint copy

- Quest is completed by giving the same blueprint copy back with zero runs remaining

- Reward is a named item of the same type

- Promotes market interaction and player mfg slots being used.

- Focus on initial investment to compete task as analogue to buying a ship.

Sec missions

- Pay more in lower sec

- Increased system sec over time

- Groups of rats spawn when player arrives

- Get first mission in person, but subsequent missions can be chained remotely

- Level 1 always in the same system

- Level 2 always in an adjacent system of the same constellation

- Level 3 in adjacent system in the whole region

- Level 4 always in adjacent constellation if same region

- Level 5 always in lowsec and same as level 4.

- Declining a mission means that agent doesn't talk to you for 4 hours

- Competing 10 missions offers you a storyline mission (faction rep)

Freight mission

- Same as sec missions

- Pay a cargo deposit

- Leave the player some cargo space

- Select next destination based on trade hubs activity

- Show buy sell differential for one product

- Level 1 constellation high sec wide, up to 200m3

- Level 2 is region wide up to 5000m3

- Level 3 is inter region wide also through lowsec up to 5000m3

- Level 4 is inter region up to 100000m3

- Player chooses to go through lowsec or the long way.

- Aim for market gameplay

- Players make missions by placing buy orders!

Max number of missions per 24 hours

Add fleet member sec status together for giving missions.

All fleet members need to be on grid to activate acceleration gates,

Don't need to dock to accept missions, only to start a play session.

Next mission is offered in space once the current mission is complete

- Agents have superiors.

- Superiors gain some of the standing boost from completing missions for subordinates.

- Superiors have 2-3 subordinates.

- No superior has two subordinates in the same area as themselves.

  - Level 1: station

  - Level 2: system

  - Level 3: constellation

  - Level 4: region

- This means there is one level 4 agent per company per 2-3 regions.

- Every agent has a home station, to which they will return of the player structure is destroyed.

- Players bid to move agents to thier structures

- Public can donate to keep agents at their noc stations

Players can bid to place Isk Faucets such as agents, asteroid belts and sites.

The "owner" can set tax on the faucet

The owner is incentivized to get as many players to use their faucet

The price to own a faucet is purely demand driven

When there are disruptions to service, it is best to pay to solve them.

Owners can also pay extra for using the faucet, and run it as a loss leader.

Remove many agents

Agents only go inside they own empire

Higher level agents only go to lower sec systems

Goals:

- provide simple, lasting and effective solutions for common issues

- generate more content through these systems interacting

- make the pie smaller in general

Problem 1: Nullsec is unrewarding for industry

- 4-6 hour site respawn to promote multi timezone use

- boost profitability by increasing sites and mining yeilds

- increase costs of Highsec slot use

- increase costs of highsec pos maintenance

- increase slots in Nullsec outposts

- Nullsec pos can be controlled while in a friendly Outpost in the same system

&nbsp;

- Building a system up gives a bonus to one thing and a penalty to another. Systems shoudl become specialized rather than just better.

- System penalties should spill over to neighbouring systems, friendly and otherwise.

- A friendly corp setting up a ratting system next to your mining system should penalize both to promote internal friction or alliance level planning of housing.

- Surrounding a specialised system with other systems of a similar specialization should unlock being able to develop it further.

- Player activity should also be used for system development

- Systems should be inherently better at one specialization or another

Problem 2: No conflict drivers in Nullsec

- Building up a system places penalties on neighbouring systems that are controlled by a different alliance

- Building up constellations places penalties on neighbouring constellations that are controlled by a different alliance

- This should promote “no mans land” between each alliance which is less profitable and the location of some fights.

- As an alliance grows it effects its neighbours negativly, promoting battle or intergration

- Alliances will become the largest player group entity again

- There will be more reason for players to form larger formal insitutions, which creates more internal pressure.

Stellar design

- NPC nullsec systems should create benefits to ratting in nearby systems.

- other null sec systems should be inherently better at certain specializations

Empire control

- removal of local, constellation and region chat for nullsec

- Beacons must be built in each system to enable local chat

- Beacons can be built anywhere in system but show a perminant overview beacon

- A corporate capital must be buildt in a constellation to enable constellation chat

- Corporate capitals can be build around any planet (one per system)

- An alliance capital must be built to enable region chat

- Alliance capitals can only be built around terran worlds

- bonuses for being closer to each tier of capital

- This should create inward migration pressure as all players will want to operate as close to the centre as possible

- beacons are very tough but can be pushed into reinforced very quickly to promote raids and harrassment.

- A system with a broken chain of beacons to the capitals loses its passive bonuses.

- Backup Capitals can be built, and the level of a capital can be transferred. This takes 24-48 hours to complete and requires an unbroken chain of beacons to the new captial

- Also, upon initiating the transfer, a large number of packages are spawned in the capital and must be shipped to the new location.

Problem 4: Highsec profitability

- increase cost of NPC station slots based on sec status

&nbsp;

- New pos fuel trade good: Administrative Consumables

- Corporate bonus to pos Admin Consumables costs based on number of anchored pos.

- This should remove alot of one man pos corps and promote high sec deccing

- Empire POS Charters available through LP stores

- POS charters are a larger part of POS fuel costs and useage increases with Sec status

- Lowsec requires a very small amount of charters, almost none at 0.1.

- POS have a Despatch module automatically outside the shield

- this is tough but easy to reinforce with a few medium ships

- Reinforcing this structure makes the pos run inefficent for the next 4-6 hours (either increased strontium use, or just a modifier to productivilty).

Problem 5: Missioning Island

- Courier missions interact with players

- Players can flag packages as “in need of courier” and specify a destination

- Players also specify what level of mission they wish to use. Level 1 is the cheapest per m3 but also will be moved in smaller sections, Lvl 4 the opposite.

- Courier missions are generated to move these items.
