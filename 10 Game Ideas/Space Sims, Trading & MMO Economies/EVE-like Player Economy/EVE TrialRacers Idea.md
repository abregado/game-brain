TRIAL RACERS - An EVE Online Business Model

**Mission Statement**

We at TrialRacers Pty Ltd want to bring the excitement of EVE Racing back to the people of New Eden. Our goal is to deliver NON-LIVE racing events in an exciting format without heavy investments of time and effort.

**Conceptualisation**

Events shall be shown on Youtube and other popular casting websites. To keep the competition tight, all entrant Capsuleers must be younger than 14 days. Each pilot can be sponsored by an older Capsuleer, Corporation or Alliance but must join the TrialRacers Corporation before each event. The TR corporation will supply equipment, ships and modules for practice as well as courses and obstacles to attempt.

**Format**

Several differnt stages are placed together to create a single race. Racers can practice for each stage individually but wont know the details until the event. The stage types are:

**Sub-warp**

A series of tight turns, drag strips and complex 3d moves without using warp drives. This tests the pilots manual piloting skills

**Warp**

A series of warp checkpoints to test align time and warp speed. The next destination is shown on the Container waypoint

**Encounter**

The pilots must navigate through a hostile mission area or cosmic signature.This tests the tank of each chosen craft, or the ability to shadow someone equally fast as you.

**Dropoff**

The pilots may drop items collected during the race at strategic locations to earn bonus points

**Filming**

Trial Accounts will also be used to film events. FRAPS or other recording software will be used from several vantage points.

**Scoring**

Overall speed through the course should provide most of the points. Those that show finesse by collecting items from the waypoint containers will gain valuable bonus points which may secure them the win in a close race.

**Publicity**

Because so many new trial accounts will be made, Many new “faces” will pass through the racing league. Those that race many times will be known to those watching. Those that win multiple times during their 14 day stint will become the legends of the racing circuit! Those that sponsor consistently competitive racers will also become known. It is my hope that these trained victors can have their trial accounts sold for a small sum of ISK to fund the league.

Advertising should not be done through normal channels. Instead a number of publicity videos should be made as a “proof of concept”. These will include the organisers name and eventually pilots will start to become interested.

Once popular other avenues can be explored for advertising.
