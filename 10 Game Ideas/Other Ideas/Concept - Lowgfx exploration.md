Concept - Pure exploration, low gfx game

The player should:

- Be rewarded for exploration.. with more exploration

- Not have any risk of "losing"

- explore the content indefinatly

- have access to every tool after a time

- be able to get detailed information out of the game without high tech gfx

- be able to view explored areas again at any time

What is being explored (definates)

- 2d world

- world without limitations (generated during game)

What is being explored (options)

- surface of a world

- fantasy world

- underground caverns (diving, climbing, abseiling)

- space

- inside a computer

- an information network

- a spaceship/station

- human mind

- robotic automaton

- human build maze (tech or magic)

- a large building

- village

- pyramid/tomb

- dungeon

Graphical Options (emphasis on low dev time)

- Rougelike ascii

- icons

- simple vector graphics

- text

Movement divisions (measurements for)

- Squares

- Hexes

- Node and Path

- Cartesian

Sound options (almost zero skillpool available!, remember bad sound is worse than no sound!)

- stock effects

- learning the basics myself

- abstracted sound effects

- no sound?

Gui Options

- Minimalistic with hotkey emphasis

- Minimalistic with options on hover

- Keyboard control only (better for reactive gameplay)

- Mouse Only

Savegame options (when can the play session finish)

- At any time (save on exit)

- Only at safe locations (return to town/lobby/ect)

- Autosave constantly (could cause problems with random generated intel)

Considerations

- Should the player be forced to explore all of the curently generted content before being given new content?

- Depth Levels of exploration/generation?

- Tutorial/Early/Late game mechanic differences?

- Should the focus change midgame from outward exploration to inward exploration? Example:// you can explore solar systems for the first half of the game, then exploring planets in more detail becomes available suggesting the player come back to pre-explored solar systems.

- How to combat bordem from repetition? Points of interest? Anomalous generation?

- Slow paced game, how slow? Player determines pace, or perhaps there is something enforcing a constant pace to keep the player going?

- Ability to show off what you have found? High Score? Screenshots? Report generation?

- Content sharing with other players? Offline multiplayer?

- Level of abstraction?
