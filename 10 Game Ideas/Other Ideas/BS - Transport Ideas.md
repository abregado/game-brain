BS - New transport ideas

Modern Ideas

- Cars/Trucks/Motorbikes/Road Transport

- Rail/Rolling stock

- Aircraft/Prop/Jets

- Low Orbit craft

- Monorail/Trams

- Bus/Tramcar

- Human Powered Vehicles

- Hovercraft

- Submarine

- Ship/Naval travel

- VTOL/Helicopter

Expansion on Modern Ideas

- AI Controlled private cars (efficent, traffic negligible)

- Super cavitation (Super speed subs)

- Rigged Hovercraft (no road infrastructure)

- Private VTOL or Helicopter (Cheap, efficent for personal travel)

- Solar vehicles (infrequent trips, zero trip energy cost)

- Gauss/Magnetic trains/Monorail (even higher infrasrtcuture cost, fast/efficent)

- Failproof/Crashproof Aircraft

- Cheap Orbital Spaceflight

- Walking vehicles (slow but can tackle any terrain)

- Wagon Trains (LOOOONG road trains, all terrain, replace trains and trucks)

- Tunnelling becomes cheap, underground train/road networks

- Skyroads/Raised infrastructure

- Tellyportahrz

- Anti-gravity (advanced hovercraft, height limited)

- Anti-gravity aircraft (cheaper/cleaner/better air travel)

- Boson skullbuggery (Cargos are cheaper to tranport)
