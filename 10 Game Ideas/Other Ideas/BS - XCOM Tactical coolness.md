Alien Units

Scout Caste

Floater -\> Blob/Headcrab/Climber  
A small creature which has night stealth abilities. Moves very quietly, or very fast when nessesary. Can climb very well, emits very low infraread and when dormant very little psi/mental activity. Can capture civilians by attaching onto them. Often goes undetected until XCOM agents are very close. Often a small group of these accompany even the smallest alien mission. Communicate with Sectoids via Psilink.

Regular Caste

Sectoid -\> Grey Alien  
The brains of most alien missions. These aliens are taler than a human with a large head. They are not very physically strong but can be trained to be incredibly mentally strong. Even low level ones can lower their metabolic rates so as to be almost undetechable when still, allowing ambush tactics to be used. If they get into close proximity with hostile humans they can will engage in melee using high tech melee weapons or pistol weaponry.

Most missions include some of this caste. They act as the aliens engineers and scientists, and often are involved in capturing live civilians.

Tactics include hiding until XCOM agents are detected by Floaters. Ambushes are then formed to take out key personell so as to destabilize morale. They will rarely engage in a pitched firefight and if this happens they will fall back towards the UFO or map boundary.

Often single Sectoids will be found harrassing and attempting to capture civlians from remote locations.

When Sectoids are used as soldiers they should be mainly technology boosted or very well psi-trained.

Soldier Caste

Muton-\>Green Alien

The Aliens cheif soldier caste. They are regularly seen in battle as biologically boosted troops. Variants include musculature, dexterity, regeneration and speed boosting. All have toughened hides, reinforced bones and multiple redunancies in organs. Rarely a bio and tech boosted Muton will be seen, these are extremly dangerous.

Most often Mutons will assault the XCOM agents directly, buying time for the other more subtle races to acheive their goals. Single well armed Mutons should be able to keep several XCOM agents pinned down. When directed by Sectoids, they will attack buildings with suppressive fire once they know XCOM agents are inside. Scortched earth tactics are also an option.

Mutons rarely have any Psi powers but Commander ranked specimins can do. This power is mostly used in a defensive and supprot role, blocking the attack of XCOM psiagents and communicating with lower rank mutons.

Mutons are rarely paired with Floaters, only when there is a Sectoid controller present. Sometimes the controller will have been killed in the crash and the Mutons will take over control of the remaining Floaters.

Artillery may also be utilized by Mutons and when cornered they often Frenzy rather than panic.

Snakemen-\>Techno Soldiers

Snakemen are used in situations where one caste must fufill all duties on a craft. Snakemen are more expensive to produce than Mutons but may see more action once the numbers of Sectoids have been reduced. They are almost always tech boosted, sometimes outragiously so. Some may even be able to climb, float or fly.

Snakemen will use more indirect tactics, ambushing, camping, hiding and even kamakazing. They will often attempt to outnumber their opponents, or when in a pitched firefight attempt to win by attrition.

Snakemen have very good detecting abilities due to their enhanced bodies. XCOM agents cannot hide as effectivly from them and as such ambushes are much more likely to succed

Snakemen are also most likely to weild the more complex alien weaponry such as homing/tracking bombs, mines, traps, launchers and bio weapons.

This caste also make fantastic Engineer and support castes as their bodies can be customized for specialist roles.

Leader Caste

Ethereal -\> Mastermind

This caste commands all others through overwhelming Psipower. Ethereals can detect XCOM minds at long range, even when XCOM psiagents are blocking their abilities.

Loner Ethereals rarely leave the UFO and its even less often that they participate in Terror missions unless in a group. When groups of Ethereals are present, one will always stay behind to co-ordinate lesser castes while the others move out to provide hires intellegence, often picking out loner XCOM agents for attacks.

If a pitched shootout with mid-large numbers of etherals does occur they will first use their psipower to boost the lesser caste soldiers while attempting to possess or panic XCOM agents. In large numbers they can even animate corpses of agents or aliens to continue fighting. When all of their soldiers are dead or beyond animation, Etherals groups will hunker down focusing their power on defence (inaccuracy) while joining together to take over a single XCOM agent at a time. This is the highest threat of the game.

Terror Castes

Cyberdisc -\> Weapons Platform

Cyberdiscs are used by Sectoids and Snakemen alike. Their role is either as heavy weapons platforms, kamakazie distractions or to paralyse and transport civilian captives. They employ antigravity abilities to move freely around, curring their way into builds to achieve their goals.

Tactically the Cyberdisc will stay back and use its weaponry to support the other soldiers. They can utilize tech links to target XCOM agents detected by other alien soldiers. When in terror missions they will often stay back and only move forward to collect civilians that other castes have incapacitated.

Cyberdisc weaponry should be impressivly powerful when used in a fire support role. Sniper, area effect, suppression and cover ignoring weaponry should all be available. Cyberdics may also be used as minefield deployers

Crysalid -\> Genocyde

Crysalids will be deployed when the aliens have either given up, or have achieved their goals on earth. They multiply rapidly in areas of high population density due to their unique method of reproduction.

These are melee only soldiers, or more like pets. They seek out human civilians to incapacitate and impregnate with their eggs. Once impregnated the humans becomes a zombie slave to the crysalid helping to incapacitate more human hosts. If a crysalid dies its human zombie slaves flee and hide waiting to become full grown crysalids.

In combat Crysalids will use their speed to seek out and zombify civilians. When XCOM agents are detected they will attack only if undetected, preferring to take more zombie hosts first. The longer XCOM takes to respond to a Crysalid terror mission the more zombie hosts will be present in the battle.

Crysalids are normally fielded by Sectoids or Ethereals. Mutons are too stupid to control them properly. They also die after a certain amount of time and are effectivly the alien version of a biological weapon, wiping out the humans to leave the planet empty. Each crysalid can onloy infect a number of hosts before running out of eggs. Perhaps XCOM can research ways to lower the chance of successful impregnation.

Zombie

Reaper -\> Bruteforce

Reapers are the answer to firesupport for Mutons. They are extremely dumb and easy to control with Psipower. Even Muton captains can control them effectivly. Biped hulks, Reapers are used to carry the largest weapons, or when heavy weapons are in short supply, as front line cannon fodder. EXTREMELY hard to kill but easy to avoid, They rely on Mutons and other soldiers to detect prey for them. Often XCOM agents can lead them away and surround them.

Tactics include smashing through the wall of a building to get to their prey, Firing as much as possible to disorient their prey. They may also stop to consume bodies of dead aliens and humans.
