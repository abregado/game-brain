Top view

One ability per button slot

Abilities are in the level

Go to an ability to equip it

When equipping an ability, your old ability falls to the ground so it can be re-equipped. Old abilities are temporary and dissolve after 60 seconds.

Multiple players

Gated level sections when you need certain abilities

Demo level where the abilities need to be collected in a particular order, focusing on level backtracking.

The level includes two places where two players can skip a section as long as they have the correct ability combination.

Abilities

- Dash

- Jump

- Invisible

- Freezeray

- Open

- Smash

- Shield

Obstacles:

- Chasm - can't walk across

  - Dash ability

  - Activatable bridge

  - Jump ability

- Camera sentry - locks room if it sees you

  - Deactivate button

  - Invisible ability

  - Freezeray ability (disables obstacle temporarily)

- Door

  - Keycard ability

  - Open button

  - Smash ability (disables obstacle temporarily)

- Force field

  - Shield ability

  - Smash (disables obstacle temporarily if you have access to the powercell)

Each level segment has a number of ways through, each requiring a set of abilities
