Multiplayer Notepad Genres

RTS - Implies that you control a whole army

Objectives:

Capture and Hold

Land Grab

Annihilation

Avatar Hack and Slash - Implies you are a one man army

Objectives:

Exploration

Survival

Travel DIstance

Protect point/town

Building "Engineering game" - Implies low importance of combat

Objectives:

Travel Distance

Repair and break objects and paths to help youself and to deter followers

Exploration Game - Implies no combat at all, or PvE at its most basic level

Objectives:

Discover things players have built

Discover what the RNG has built

Mechanics:

Proceedural Worldgen

Player constructions break down over time if not visited

Ability to get lost in the game world

Navigation by Landmarks

Progressive build allowances (build a small cairn, then you can build a larger one, then a small hut)

Coop mechanics for a competative Goal

Survival game mode where players must get as far away from the starting point as possible. Working in groups is the best way to get distance, but as your journey becomes harder, you will be tempted to take what your friends have to give yourself the best chance to reaching the high score. The benefits for killing a party member are not given if that party member simply dies from NPC attack.

Other parties could also attack you to boost themselves. This would be less and less likely to happen the further you get away from the centre (less people in a greater area).

Option: You cannot control where your avatar goes, only what he does. You can influence things happeneing around him, sculpt items for him to pick up, knock objects away that you dont want him to have.

Meaning

Wrap it in a visual story

Connect to personal passions and goals

Connect to a meaningful community, reputation

Beware of social context meanings

Mastery

Fun arises from mastery

Interesting challenges

Learning is fun under optimal conditions

Structured Goals

Varied Pacing

GIves you harder challenges so that when you finish them you feel more

successful

"juicy" feedback

Autonomy

Play is voluntary

Anything that makes it seem like the player has to do it, curbs this autonomy

Know your target audience

Prototype, Playtest, Interate
