Aims of design

- a game in which you must play once every 2 hours but only for 3 times (4 hour game time)

- the player must complete the minigame component as close to every 2 hours as possible

- the minigame should include some advantage if the player remembers what he did last time

- player skills should include timing and memory

Score Concepts

- players compete to be on a high score board

- players put their name in FIRST

- their score is uploaded regardless of victory

Players interact with the game only with the mouse

objects on the screen can be rotated/interacted with/flipped by clicking on them

minigame probably involves lining up something, or having something move into position at the right time.

clicking objects begins them moving/aligning but they move slowly

players should interact with the game once every 2 hours for about 5 minutes
