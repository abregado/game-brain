Ideas for a survival game

Survival against only the environment?

Survivial against:

Wild Animals

Temperature

Flood

Sunlight exposure

Hunger

Energy Depletion

Exhaustion

Drug Addiction (no doses)

Injuries without medicine or operation

Blood loss

Poisoning

Disease

Being killed in your sleep

Nutrition

Old Age

Not reaching a goal fast enough

Time based Objective

Advancement through:

Discovering useful items

Manufacturing crude items

Repairing old items

Constructing shelters

Boarding up ruins and derelict buildings

Preparing the land

Resource Management

Difficulty Scaling

Measuring Success (score board, report of how you did)

Save games?
