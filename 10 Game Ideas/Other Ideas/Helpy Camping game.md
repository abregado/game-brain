Aims:

- Game that is mostly focused on cooperative gameplay

- 2-5 players

- Dont need to play at the same times

Interaction

- request items you need, they go on a public request board

- trade locations found

Map

- There are multiple maps, each has a campground

- Only one player can be at each campground

- Each campground has different resources available

- players can change campgrounds

- new campgrounds can be discovered

Gameplay Goals

- earn badges

- survive two weeks of real time

- collect photos of wild animals

- trade with other players

Activities

- Picking fruit, mushrooms, herbs, nuts and berries

- Exploring old mines and structures

- Fishing

- Exploring down the river

- Collecting firewood

- Building shelters

- pitching tents

- cooking/baking

- collecting water

- Crafts (making rope)

- Photographing animals

Players start with a decent amount of supplies and campers. As time goes on some of the campers will need to leave. The aim is to push as far as possible into the forest.

At each campground the player will have to set up the camp, then begin collecting food,water and firewood. They can survive on their rations but will need them for later on.
