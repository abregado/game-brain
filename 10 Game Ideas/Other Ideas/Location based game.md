Go to location to rally a team

Objectives appear and the team leader can send them to each player

Players go out and place feeds on objectives

Players play a short mini game every two minutes 5 times

- Could be Tower defense, defend five waves until the FEED/base is set up

- Send units to help other players

- View over players feed/base

- 

Being next to other players helps with the mini games

help can be sent to linked nearby players
