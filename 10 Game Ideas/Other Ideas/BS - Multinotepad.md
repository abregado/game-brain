Multiplayer notepad

The viewport is the players avatar

The cursor is the players “hands”

Players create the content

Players interact with the content by changing, adding to or deleting it.

It looks like a notepad document: lines, letters, numbers

“resources” could be empty space. Near the centre of the document there is very little space available

The closer a space is to the centre, the more valuable it is.
