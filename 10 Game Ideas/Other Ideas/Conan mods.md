Exploration is nice but static

Once I know a safe route, it is always safe

Attacking a camp does not bring reinforcements, or increase the difficulty.

Surprises drop to zero after exploring the map a lot.

Faster to fight than to harvest for materials

**Conan mod ideas**

Progression gameplay moved to capturing thralls and building.

Start with 40 feat points

No crafting feats

Stone weapons feats cost 20

Each level of weapon feat costs 20

Hand craftable items

- Stone tools

- Campfire

- Bedroll

- Artisans bench

- Gruel

- Steak

- Shredded roast

- Upgrade to large campfire

- Warming soup

- Ect

- Bindings
