BS - Transport building game

Things to consider when building a road or rail network

- Maximum speed of vehicles on road/rail

- Modifier of max speed based on angle of a turn

- Percentage of modifier applied based on vehicle suspension and tires

- angle of incline on road/rail

- torque of vehicle

- cargo mass

- wind sheltering

- water erosion

- maintenace of surface/land under/lane marking/signage

- prebuilding earthworks cost

- time to complete earthworks

- gauge of rail

- width of rail (number of lanes that can fit)

- shoulder width

- saftey equipment (armcor barriers, steel cable, reflectors,ripples)

- Speed of traffic:turn warning ratio

- signage

- rest areas

- signal controls/sub stations/track changers for rail

- crossings and required buildings/equipment/signage

- overtaking lanes/ speed limits

- access for emergencys, repair and maintenace

- effect on traffic during upgrades

- expectation of drivers on trip times

- offroad capability of vehicles

- rain/water/weather damage

- Parking for cars/depots for trains
