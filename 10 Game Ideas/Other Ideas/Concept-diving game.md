Explore

Unwater wreck, narrative goal, time limit, multistage, tutorial above water

Unwater wreck: conflict with underwater creatures is not fatal the first few times. The player must use trial and error with discovered items before he encounters the enemies too many times. Items to resolve conflict are found through exploration.

Narritive Goal: Goal of the game is to progress the narrative. Each stage introduces a new plot piint and the final challenge resolves all but one of these.

Time Limit: Character has limited Air Supply. Different tutorial choices effect this limit. Player can discover more airtanks in the wreck from lost divers however the amount of nitrogen in their blood give a fixed overall time limit regardless of how much oxygen is found. At the end tthe wreck surfaces, which will kill the charvater if he has breathed in too much nitrogen (decompression sickness).

Underwater enemies return to attack the player after a certain amount of time has passed. Using the correct item to chase them away increases this time, and eventually the coret item can kill these enemies.

Performing actions, especially stressful ones use up more oxygen (and therefore limit the players time to complete the quest).

multistage: The game is broken into 4 stages. The first is the tutotial stage that does not take place underwater however player choices made now effect the rest of the game session.

Wreck approach: you have your diving partner. Player learns about the time limits, and encounters some of the challenges breifly (whale swims past). Top down view, player chooses when to go a little deeper. Perspecitive graphics. Deeper you go, darker it is.  
Finding a wreck entrance: Your partner explores a differnt part of the wreck. Big Hole, Jammed Door, Through the rock caves below the wreck (reef). Side entrance.  
Bringing the wreck up: you parnter is dead or alive based on your choices.

Tutorial stage: the player must select a number of variables to change the chances he gets during hte game. Options include: Equipmnet Choices, Boat choice (sailing, motor,kayak), Day of dive (weather and conditions), Oxygen levels, Other divers.

The character has limited resources to spend on these choices. Boats have different advantageds and disadvantages, chosing a good day to sail may require waiting longer and therefore cost more in food and accomidation, with a better boat you could bring more divers with you. More advantaced equipment costs more and may require waiting for it to be shipped to you.
