- up to 4p, same screen coop shooter

- 2d

- start in centre of level

- cant shoot while moving

- stay still and dont shoot to deploy big guns (deploy and packup times)

- stay still to charge up a jump boost to get over obstacles (or to dig under them)

- stand in defensive spots to powerup weapons

- move forward as a team, 1-2 staying still to cover others

- reach a distance from the start in a time to win

Gameplay Tricks

- constantly one or two enemies approaching so you cant keep running, but they are not worth stopping for to shoot.

- players need to work as a team to keep moving

- stationary enemies set up in bottlenecks

- 

Exploration component

- Buildings to explore for powerups but exits always face inwards

- world is large and generated the same every time, finding a better way is possible
