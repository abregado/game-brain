BS- How do we get over the personal motorist problem

Radical Changes

- Fuel Prices

- Roads become ecologically concerning

- Anti-car government raises tax/registration costs

- Insurance companies stop supporting car owners

- Militaries take control of oil production/bouycott

- Auto industry changes direction

- Auto industry controlled by military

- Price of cars goes up

- Outward pressure to adopt change

- Ecological disaster

- Surface travel no longer viable

- Natural disaster or external danger forces popultion to live close together

- New cities are designed without roads, population immigrates to these new places

- Arcologies become standard habitation

- Private motor vehicles are simply outlawed

- Automotive industry is controlled by the enemy. Patriotism stops national demand.

- Cars become for the rich again, class controls ownership

- Infrastructureless travel

- Aliens condem private motoring and threaten invasion

- Safety of drivers cannot be guaranteed. Driving is a high risk occupation

- Flooding means land area is diminished

- Every city has a port due to water shortages, private sailing becomes popular

- Poverty. Regular folks cant afford food, let alone a private motor vehicle

Calm Changes

- Technological change

- Cities adopt pro-public transport policy

- Radical new method of private transport

- Public opion of shared transport changes over time

- Automation of agriculture sees automotive production shift to agri-gear

- Distances are closed (population moves to high density centres)

- Personal aviation becomes cheap

- Introduction of AI sees public transport become efficient

- Work from Home ethos becasues popular worldwide

- Sensory recording and playback sees the death of the road trip/holiday

- All manufactorying moves to orbit or underground, freeing roads of industrial traffic

- Archetectural developments remove the need for private transport
