Vegastrike Modification Ideas

Goals:

Show off Vegastrikes Movement model

Demonstrate the combat depth that can be had

Fleet combat

Larger Ships

Dogfights

Fights near larger objects

Anti-missile combat

Instantaneous strike and relativistic projectiles

Demonstrate the engines use as a multiplayer platform

Create an ongoing exciting engagement system (not mission based)

Create a coherent visual style with the art

Discussion

The neutonian physics model in vegastrike is fantastic. It overcomes the learning curve of this genre by allowing ships to compensate for momentum completely when below a certain speed. While the “Combat Speed” of each craft is an arbitrary number set by the coder, and not calculated from the other thrust based fields, it still gives new players the edge they need.

To be a truly ace pilot in VS a player must learn how to fly efficently while exceeding the combat speed of his ship. This is very easy in deep space where there are no obstacles, but closer to stations and capital ships it is hairraising!

So in our test the player must be fighting nearby to a large object. Some ideas for this are:

Huge Industrial Ship

Several medium sized industial ships in a fleet, perhaps miners

An Asteroid field

A Space station

Orbital Defense Ring

Ancient space platform

Wreckage from a fleet battle

Many of these give the player something to defend or destroy as well.

The engine demo should be an “instant action” style game. The new player should only have to click twice to get in a ship and start flying. Failure should be based on failing mission objectives. Dying should not end the mission.

Perhaps the player, once selecting to begin the mission is presented with 3 distinctly different spacecraft (all the same visual style). Some examples of these include:

Ultrafast Interceptor

Medium Fighter with missiles AND guns

Gunship with tracking forward firing weapons and a turret

Corvette, a light capital ship with a main cannon and several turrets

Heavy Escort bomber with Combat Drones

Salvage craft with drones

Control of a turret on one of the industrial ships

Enemies should attack in waves and be initially weaker than a single player ship. Most enemies will be concentrating on the industrial ships. The aim of the mission is to survive as long as possible.

The mission should get harder as time wears on. The player should be penalized for dying and rewarded for making kills. If the player is too effective then more industrial ships should enter the mission, causing more enemies to spawn more often. The mission should remain unwinnable, and difficulty should slowly increase until it becomes impossible to win. Perhaps after 1 hour the odds of staying alive should dwindle to nothing.

Perhaps the quantity and quality of enemies attacking should be based on the “strength” of the defending fleet. If the players team is doing well more industrial ships enter the battle to make it harder. If the player is doing poorly then the enemies will be able to destroy some of the fleet, meaning difficulty increases less slowly.

The player should have a sum of “currency” and a few ship choices available at the beginning of the mission. One may be selected and its value is only removed from his balance if it is destroyed. Kills made while in the ship add bounties to his balance. At certain points during the mission, the player should be informed that new ship choices are available. This should be based on both the mission progression and the players balance.

Several points on the industrial ship should be available for docking. Some are simply to refuel or reload ammunition (both costing the player currency). Others are for switching ships.

Should all the civilian ships and the industrial ship be destroyed, the player must restart. The longest playing times are recording for comparison.

When possible, multiplayer should be made available so that multiple players can work together to get a higher score.

The enemies should be both numerous and strategically different. The visual style of the enemies should be different from the players team. Ideas for enemies include:

Rouge AI constructs (matrix drones)

Crazy Space Pirates (reavers)

Another empire,

Organic (draken)

Nanotech (drifters/replicators)

Warlike (klingon)

Racist (empire)

Jealous (corporation)

Types of enemies:

Swarmers: Fly in tight packs, very small, attack the same target

Arty: Long range enemies with lasers or cannons

Bomber: Fires large torpedoes and missiles at the civilians while avoiding players

Interceptor: Agressivly attacks players who fire at bombers

Gunship: Tough, Gets in close to the civilian ships and lets rip

Interdictor: A mini-cap that sits back and fires at any players approaching

Sentry: stays back and protects enemies and mini-caps

Sneaker: stays in cap-ships blindspots

Targetter: fires on turrets to disable them

Stealth: Had to see until its very close to the civilian ships

Repairer: fixes up drifting enemies

Carrier: Spawns new enemies from itself instead of in the distance

Hulk: very tough slow, moves inwards constantly

Kamakase: suicides into civilian ships

Wingman: flys next to a rnadom enemy until either is destroyed

Booster: Increases the speed of nearby enemies

Shielder: Blocks missiles from hitting nearby enemies

Ward: Blocks guns from hitting nearby enemies

Ping: Increases the number of enemies that spawn, stays a long way away

Sludge: slows down the player

Babble: makes targetting difficult

Fake: makes many fake copies of itself

Flicker: Moves in and out of subspace randomly

Chaff: Missiles always track this enemy

Boss: mini-cap that fires on the civilian ships from distance

Protegie: mini-cap that has flak guns. Moves into combat range

Dukka: Multimount chaingun mini-cap. Loads of turrets. Gets in really close.

Enemies could have just a single one of these abilities. They must also have AI to match. Harder enemies could have multiple abilities, or just work together with other enemies.
