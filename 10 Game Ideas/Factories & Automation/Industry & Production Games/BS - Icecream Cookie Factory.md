A more challenging rethink of the terrible cookie making games that have flooded mobile devices.

Concepts:

- game seems easy until about 5 minutes in

- game is constantly in motion

- problems require the player to tap/click on the problem

- some machines are automatic, some need you to activate them manually

- new parts for your factory can be placed anywhere you like

- raw materials come in at increasingly fast pace

- to score the player must fill an entire order in a time period or get nothing

- chaos must occur

- swipe to throw commodities on the floor

Level Components

- grid level area

- fixed points where materials come in

- fixed points where products must leave

- machines with random input/output sides

- multiple inputs possible

- conveyor machines that just move items (random in and output)

- conveyors have all sides with in and output, mostly 3 in, one out

- machines get activated for a number of ticks, then must be reactivated with touch

- conveyor that splits the input 50/50

UI and Layout

- two game states, manufacture and construction

- during manufacture, ticks go past every 0.5 seconds

- machines take items in, hold them for one tick, then spit them out

- conveyor belts only move and display items

- items can be touched to throw them off the conveyors

- machines can be touched to activate them for a number of ticks

- ticks of activation are shown in a ticker timer above each machine

- clean, minimal UI

- player can move machines between phases

- machines have a set of items they can convert, and a waste product they produce if anything else is made

Gameplay Ideas

- waste product that reaches the score area counts negative!

- sometimes bad ingredients get unloaded

- adjustable or ever increasing, tick speed

Machine Ideas

- Oven

- Mixer

- Grinder

- Pourer

- Bottler
