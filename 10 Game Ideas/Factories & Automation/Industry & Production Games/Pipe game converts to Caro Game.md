NB: just make the research system from this as is own game

Pipe game converts to Caro Game

Loop

- Produce

- Sell

- Invest

Every machine and system advances one step at night

Plants take varying lengths of time to grow

Each machine takes one day to convert produce but takes different amounts of input

Each machine has a list of recipes that they will accept

Set a machines recipe by adding the primary ingredient

Then the options for the secondary ingredient will be available

Machines have one or two input slots

Center of territory is a landing pad

It's where you spawn and leave

It can be upgraded with vending machines to sell things while you are away

Special traders also land here in certain days, or visitors for dialog events.

Machines

Vending Machine Shop. Fill with produce. Items sell over the week.

Market Stall. Add produce holograms. Once per week at the market the items are taken from storage.

Water Tank

Pumps (need daily powercell)

Water Collectors. Low throughput but no upkeep. Needs lots of space

Sprinklers. Creates area for a plant to grow. Cant place them too close to each other. Connected to a hub that takes a powercell.

Chopper

Food processor

Dehydrator

Oven

Recycler. Turns empty containers into Obtanium

Lightning Charger will charge 25 cells when it is storming

Wind Generators will charge 3 cells if it is windy or higher, 5 if it is Stormy

Solar charger will charge 5 cells if it is Sunny, and 2 if it is Cloudy or Windy

If you plug in a machine it will run it if it isnt raining

Weather is a spectrum: Sunny, Cloudy, Windy, Rainy, Stormy

Powercells are maintenance

Powercells expire after a few days of sitting around

Each powercell powers something for 1 day

Empty powercels can be sold for a small amount or recycled into metal

Every few days there is a market.

Rent a stall during the week and fill it with produce

During the market day, you only work in the market

Special items are available for sale at the market

Level Design

Outdoor area

Plateaued terrain

Very clear which areas are flat

Collectable Materials respawn in fixed locations every few days during a specific season

Certain resources and endless but require pipe setups

Resources always spawn near a landmark. The player should be able to tell what will spawn in an area based on these landmarks

Landmarks change what they look like in every season

08.25

Caro and I are playing Old Market Simulator

What about ye olde capitalist sim?

Buy a machine. Fill it up. Sleep. Next morning it has produced something valuable. Sell it. Buy a machine.

NPCs around town will sell goods, but not buy (premised they all buy and sell from one another already). Unlock recipes each day randomly? Maybe by adding ingredients to a research machine you get partial recipe hits? Upgrade your lab to run multiple experiments.

Salvage basic materials from the environment to sell or research.

Sell duplicate blueprints to the town for cash. Villagers pay less for blueprints that have been sold often. Sold blueprint items are available for sale in local businesses. The margin goes down the more you sell meaning they are more competitive. It's not worth selling items that you don't have the exclusive recipes for. It can also be a strategy to sell many of a recipe you don't intend to make that you still need as an ingredient.

Research.

Add up to three items to the bench. You will get a recipe that includes at least one of these. Collect all recipes which include these items. Recipes with more of these items get a double or triple chance. Then halve all chances for blueprints you dont have. You can use a research item as one of the ingredients to divide the chance for each recipe you already have by 25. Hopefully this means that if you have a resource but no use for it, you will have a recipe soon.

Each item has a number of tags. Customers come in looking for tags. Customers are recorded as well as the items they possess. Each item has a date in which they will wear out, refreshing the customers needs. This means that you will see the same customers with the same habits.
