Brainstorming - Production simulator

Aim: To design a stand alone production simulator as a proof-of-concept for an xcom remake. Prototyping, Mass production and process refinement should all be included.

Concepts used:

Workshop stats

Floor area

Workstations for engineers

Robotic fabricators

Product stats

Floor area of finished product

Processes for completion

Materials consumed for each process

workstation overload

Process stats

Re-tooling costs

Penalties for early cancellation

Mass production efficency

Wasted componets

Option to include component manufacture or import

Man hours per item

Cost per item at end

Overall production time

Batch size

Number of batches

Cramming (finishing batches faster for some penalty)

Prototype production

Addon/Upgrade designs

Real time with pause

Constant time pressure

"Experience" on schematics

Narrative:

Commander Dictator has put you in charge of a top secret manufacturing operation. His research goons are always coming up with new concepts for vehicles and weapons and its your job to manufacture these items from prototype phase to mass production for his evil armies. Choose wisely as new technology is always becoming available and will need to be intergrated into your production schedules, and cancelling production jobs is very wasteful!

Schematic Experience:

The player receives a new weapon design. The schematic is at level 1 (Conceptual). To increase the schematics level the player must construct a single unit and send it back to the lab for testing upgrading it to level 2 (Prototype). Its maximum batch size is increased and the player must manufacture more and more to raise its level. Further levels make this product faster, cheaper and easier to produce.
