**Tap-Tap Factory**

**A Concept by Ben Buckton**

**Farmville and Factorio had a love child!**

**Player Story**

After loading Tap-Tap Factory on his tablet, Johnny sees that he has an urgent order for 7 crates of Wildberry Cheese. Damn! The factory was set up to produce Snigglebutter. No matter. The Milkers are quickly diverted to the Curd Sieves, the coolrooms are converted to handle to larger blocks and the Diary is reconfigured to stack instead of slice.

Tapping on the products to move them through starts slowly, but built builds in speed. Soon the rhythm stabilizes and Johnny is in the zone. Only 4 minutes remain to finish off the Wildberry Cheese order. A piece of ca…. wait! Another order for Wildberry Tarts comes in. The Pie Ovens are on standby, but the Wildberries are needed to finish off the cheese. The rythym changes slightly, perhaps Johnny can complete both orders.

**Features**

**Plan your Rhythm:** Build up your factory and then operate it by tapping on the machines to move ingredients through the system. Keeping the beat will help you achieve the best results in the shortest time. When you synchronize your amazing tapping skills with your strategic forethought you will become a master.

***Positional Strategy:*** Where machines are placed is just as important as the types you place. The factory is shaped by the player before each play session.

***Random Goals:*** New orders for product come in when you are least expecting it, requiring you to modify your rhythm to complete them. Combo bonuses await those who complete multiple orders at a time.

***Building Complexity:*** As you play, new machines will be unlocked. The production chain can get quite long but space is limited. Build only what you need to succeed.

**Cute Art:** To help you relax, Tap-Tap Factory features all the cutesy building and character art you need.

**Genre**

Tap-Tap Factory is a Sandbox Rythym Action game for Tablet and mobile devices with Strategy elements.

**Target Market**

The player who finds Tap-Tap Factory ideal are those who are looking for a casual game which still suits their more complex gaming needs.

Cute art, no lose condition, and interaction similar to Facebook Farm games means that Tap-Tap should attract players of other casual games as well.

**Technology**

Tap-Tap Factory will be designed to run on Tablet devices, and due to its smooth rhythmic gameplay, performance is of utmost importance.

Tap-Tap could also utilize in-app purchases in order to be offered as a free game.

The core gameplay of Tap-Tap is designed for single player.
