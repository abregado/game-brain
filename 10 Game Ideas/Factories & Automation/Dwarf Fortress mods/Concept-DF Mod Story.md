DF Industry Survival Backstory

It was a time of great immovation. Dwarven constructs and modified creatures stalked the land, doing the dwarves bidding. Finally the dwarvenholmes were not limited to only Mountains, as huge hive cities were contructed accros the land, mountains each themselves.

The dwarves reached the height of their glory. Relying on the powerful automatons, the common dwarf was free to enjoy a life of relaxation. The old trends were handed down to the few dwarves needed to keep society running. Disease and strife were annihilated, war something unheard of.

Advancements in magical and technological implants could give willing dwarves the power to live forever. Artifical organs, size and even brains could be manufactured and surgically implanted. Dwarven lifespans were becoming more and more similar to the elves of old.

Everything was fine for several long, dwarven generations.

Then the Affliction began. Nobody noticed until it was too late. The first to be treated with these amazing advancements were of course the weathliest, most noble of dwarves. The Afflicted, as they came to be known, covered up their growing madness and after only 2 generations the common dwarfolk also had access to the treatments, even tho they spelt doom for the entire dwarven race.

It was too late when the madness finally took over. The rulers of the Mountainholmes went completly made, becoming as animals. Their last crazy decrees tearing appart dwarven society. Others who had been implanted recently tried to have the treatments reversed, but the knownledge was already lost. The first dwarves to take up this new blessing were of course those who championed its use, the inventers and doctors themselves.

Your settlement is one of the last settlements free of Afflicted. Even then you must rely on the services of enhanced dwarves as they alone hold the keys to many lost trades. Most dwarves who survived were those too poor to take up treatment, or those who lived such decadant lives that they have lost their skills over many generations. The lucky few with real talents must keep the survivors alive. At some point you must begin to turn back the migrants arriving from the old mountainholmes.

Castes:

Dispossesed. The majority of dwarves in your settlement are useless, weak, shattered souls. They have very little potential and make terrible warriors. They are prone to depression and have many, many wishes that need to be fufilled. Try your best to keep the quantity of these dwarves down.

- Low skill potential

- No mining or idustrial skill potential at all

- All attributes half of a regular dwarf (strength, Toughness, agility)

- High Depression stat

Sentinal Dwarves. These dwarves have undergone some treatment. Normally enhanced dwarves would not be tolerated but they alone retain the physical attributes to become great Miners and Warriors. Choose wisely how you use your Sentinals. They are prone to violence and fits of rage, sometimes directed towards your dwarves. Perhaps a separate burrow is the best place for them to stay.

- High strength

- Steel organs

- No Pain

- No skill potential except combat, wood cutting and mining

- High frenzy stat

- Low Depression stat

- Neutered (Single Sex, no reproduction)

- Heal faster

Greybeard. A learned old dwarf. This caste was spared the disaster as it did not benifit so readily from augmentation. The bookeepers and taxation agents of the Mountainholmes. They are fit for many jobs but not able to gain high physical stats.

- Legendary potential in Social skills and Architecture

- Poor physical stats

- Reduced reproduction rates

Stonetooler. A family line who stayed pure and kept the secrets of dwarven stonework alive. They can become Master Engravers, Masons, Craftsman and Engineers. Architecture and Carpentry can be learned to a competant level. They also make decent opponents in combat if you wish to risk their lives.

- Regular dwarf attributes

- Reduced maturity rates

- Talents as above

Forgeborn. Those who ran the dwarven industry before the fall. Most took on some sort of augmentation to make themselves more productive. Already the madness is setting in, but they can be put to good use if keeped away from weapons. Problem is that they are your only source of quality weapons and equipment.

- Higher toughness

- Skin layers made of fire-proof material

- Vision enhancements make them talented Marksmen

- Easily effected by tragedy and prone to frenzy

Wildsmith. Those dwarves tasked with controlling automatons and taming the land. They make excellent Farmers and can tame the wild automatons in the wild. These dwarves can learn decent skills in most Farming related activities as well as Carpentry, Leatherworking and Blacksmithing. They are your best hunters but also your only source of quality farm produce. Will you risk them outside the fortress?

- High Ambushing, Hunting and Marksmenship potenial

- Farming and related skills also high

- Good Agility

- Low toughness and defence skills

Servitor. Created by the mad noble classes toward the end of dwarven society. Servitors are almost mindless but can learn a wide range of skills to a decent level. They require no drink, cannot reproduce and are incredibly fragile in combat. While tragedy does not effect them, they cannot recover from any psycological damage and like all Afflicted eventually go insane.

- Pitiful Attributes but broad knowledge over a range of fields

- Socially inept they almot never have a happy thought. Only a matter of time until they break

- No Drink requirement so they can work all year long

- Cannot reproduce

Afflicted. Afflicted dwarves come in many sizes and shapes. Some have gone feral, occasionally venturing near your fortress. Some still attempt to have some sort of society, living together in some mock version of the mountainholmes of old.

- Sentinel Afflicted. Mad, berzerker dwarves with steel organs who often fly into a frenzy. Even unarmed they are a match for your regular milita.

- Hulking Afflicted. These dwarves have opted to enhance their bodies in physical size as well as power. They tower over most creatures and have enhanced bone structure, thicker tissue layers and immense strength.

- Demented Afflicted. Lightning fast and often weilding whips, Demented Afflicted are deadly opponents. Often also seen riding Rutherers or Trollkin Automatons.

- Hiver Afflicted. Enhanced with the ability to control multiple bodies, Hivers attack in swarms. They have venom and steel hard teeth to latch onto your hapless defenders.

- Feral Afflicted. More Yeti than dwarf. These crazed creatures have enchanced attributes all round and also long claws. When organised they weild projectile weapons.

- Dogbody Afflicted. Animalism became popular with augmentaion technology. Many dwarves took on the aspect of cats or dogs. Both castes of these type of Afflicted are dangerous and easily more powerful than your Dispossesed milita.

- Drakewing Afflicted. Dwarves were never ment to fly, but some managed this transformation before they were sent mad. This variety of Afflicted has the power of flight and often wields projectile weaponry.

- Clockwork Afflicted. Clockwork and Steam technology were the height of dwaven times. These Afflicted have transferred their brains into clockwork Suits. They are perhaps the most dangerous of all Allficted. However they are incredibly slow. Dont let them enter your fort else their billowing smoke may choke your citizens.

New technologies.

Composite Bar. By combining Porcelain and Steel your dwarves can create Composite armor materials that are far stronger than regular Steel plate. They also are resistant to fire based weapons.

Clockwork Workshops. More efficent on resources, but requiring many advanced tools to contruct, these workshops are the pinnicle of dwarven Industry. Leather, Cloth, Stone and Bone Industries have new, second teir workshops and materials but require a new reagent.

Lubricant. Made from oils (plant or animal) these are consumed with the new Clockwork Workshops during every reaction. If course the Distilery requires magma power to work.

Firestones. The secret of creating firestones are not completly lost. Forgeborn are the only dwarves that can create and handle these rocks. They can be used to start fires or for use in creating. Can only be created at a Magma smelter.

Redsteel. Steel tempered and alloyed with Firestones. These weapons glow hotter than regular dwarves can handle but are very useful for fighting augmented Afflicted.

Clockwork Armor. Using Clockwork parts and Composite Bars, a full suit can be crafted allowing a single dwarf to hold off many aggressors.

Blackpowder. Can be mixed by by the Greybeards and used with stone bullets

Clockwork rifles. Use Blackpowder and stone bullets to make short work of Afflicted. Only your very best marksmen should be equipped such as the bullets are very expensive to produce.

Distilation Plant. A magma powered structure that provides unlimited pure water. This is used to brew alchohol enmass, or used on its own as a cheap drink.

Giant Mechanised Brewery. This series of workshops is used to brew much more beer than is possible with the traditional still. This will remove some strain from your farmers but requires a lot of materials to construct and many labourers to keep it well stocked.

Billeting Mill. Steel and Iron bars can be turned into Billet Steel here. This helps with storage and can later be fed into the hot roller and turned into Sheets.

Hot Rolling Mill. Steel and Iron bars can be hot rolled into Sheets which are much more efficenct for craftsmen to use.

Machine Shop. With the addition of advanced tools, a single skilled dwarf can produce steel and iron items much more cheaply than before. Furniture, Weapons, Armor and Mechanism can be constructed here.

Gem Warfare. Sharper weapons can be combined with Gem Powders to create specialty weapons of much greater power. Useful when dealing with Iron and steel clad automatons.

Environmental Changes

Poisoned Atmosphere. Plants only grow outside for one season (Autumn). Local Animals are only active during this time and many types have been wiped out completly.

GM Crops. Many plant types give no seed and forcefully take over whole areas.

Aggressive Grass. The local grass has been wiped out and replaced with a fast growing and destructive grass type. Local burning will be nessesary to keep it at bay.

Forage for survival. Farms are not productive and hunting is very dangerous. Forage for local food items as often as possible to feed your aging, dying, weak masses of dwarves.

Enhanced Creatures. Regular animals are almost extinct, replaced by enhanced versions. Now that such creatures have returned to the wild, hunting is much more dangerous. Domestic Animals have increased yeild of harvestable materials, but wild variants are simply more dangerous and often have little in the way of valuable materials to butcher.

Economy Changes

Alone. There is noone else. Civilization has collapsed. You will not get dwarven caravans arriving to help you. Migrants are the displaced survivors from around the region, hearing about your desparate attempt to rebuild they will come in droves. If they lose hope and stop arriving, your cause will be doomed!

Hunting inneficent. Reduced Leather and Meat supply from wild animals mean your hunters will struggle to bring in enough to feed you easily. The trade off is that wild creatures have much tougher hides, which can also work to your advantage.

Food scarcity. Multiple food sources and proper processing before cooking/brewing is essential as the number of raw items is vastly reduced.

Stone Heavy. Stone items are much heavier now. This added to your regular dwarf being much weaker means that moving items around is not so easy. Stone Pots are easy to get but may not be the most efficent method.

Porcelain. This and Clayware are important to create composite armor peices and sharper weaponry. It is also a little easier to access now.

Lubricants. Above ground oil plants are nessesary to perform reactions in Mechanical Workshops. Your economy will benifit if you can get these industries running. Of course these items can also be made into other items. What will you choose?

Guard Humans. Animal Trapping and training will be very important as you wont have many effective soldiers. Feral Humans can be trained and will equip spare weaponry if you craft it.

Domestication. Domestic Animals you bring with you will make a big difference as they are not availalke from the wild. Grazing is a little easier to compensate for you haveing to defend them. Milk, Wool and Extracts are all nessesary for high end industries.

Dwarven Arco. Above ground constructions will be vital as your dwarves have lost the knowledge to tunnel deep into the rock. Underground caverns are even more dangerous but can yeild amazing amounts of resources.

Weak Metals. The simple metals all have uses now. Since stone items are so heavy, Non-military metals make good substitutes. They can also be spun into threads to enhance leather and cloth products.

- Trollip (Wool, War, Milk, Slow repo)

Burrowkin Automaton. These Cave Crawlers have been enlarged to dig the underground highways between dwarven cities. EXTREMLY DANGEROUS.

Macaque II. Monkeys with increased intellegence and speed. They move around in smaller packs than before but can be much more dangerous. Still untamable.

Badgerman Nomads. Actually the most advanced creatures aboveground (next to your futile attempt at survival).Badgermen weild metallic weaponry and are prone to attacking your hunters. Often enraged, be careful when dealing with them.

Humans. After being conquered by the Dwarves, Human society turned feral. Humans were caught and bred for servitude. Many underwent augmentation for various reasons. Some were transformed into grotesque creations by their mad owners. Now they are a risk to dwarven kind, although if caught can be trained to help secure the fortress.

Fellworker Automaton. These hulking beasts were used to clear forests until the Afflicted took over. They have a body made of brass and weild huge iron tools on their arms. They can sometimes be seen near your fort in groups of 1 or 2. Approach with caution as they are more than a match for several Dispossesed militadwarves.

Hellstalker Automaton. The obvious continuation of dwarven cat obsession. These creatures have had their organs replaced with stronger varieties and designed to reproduce even faster than regular cats. They can spit projectiles at their prey to disable it and walk on two legs.

Pseudodragon. Cave Dragons, driven from their underground homes have learnt to survive above ground again. Some can even fly. Mostly they keep to themselves. Untamable but yeilding very tough leather and bone.

Trollkin Automaton. Trolls were always useful, but became even more so when they could be hardened for battle. Subdermal iron plates and a steel skull were added, as well as enlarged natural weapons and a prehensile slashing tail. These creatures are entirely too dangerous for all but enhanced dwarves. If you are lucky enough to trap and tame one, they can be trained as war animals again.

Steeltusk Automaton. The dwarven advancement in the feild of Elephantology. These creatures have lost their pack insinct but have been upgraded with steel fibre bones and tusks. A source of much meat if you can bring them down. Also prone to frenzy and destroying your aboveground buildings. Even the Wildhorns cannot tame these creatures now.
