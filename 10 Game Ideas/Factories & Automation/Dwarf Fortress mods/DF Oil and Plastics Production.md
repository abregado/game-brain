oil production

Many kilns collect Peat as clay.

Loading station combined peat globs into a Wheelbarrow item

Oil separator takes combined wheelbarrow items to run. Water and Fuel is needed. If a isotropic powered version is used then no fuel is needed.

Oil Separator.(iso and normal versions) Turned production fluids into Oil, Gas and Waste Water.

Gas can escape causing damage to nearby workers over a long time

Waste Water must be contained in something and stored, or dumped on the ground. This also will cause problems over time from walking in it.

Advanced Three stage separator can capture all of these items for use in different industries. Most of the efficiency comes from a single stage separator, but yeilds the most pollution.

Oil is sent to the Refinery

Furnace used to create: Asphalt, Petroleum Coke, Parafin Wax, Lubricating Oil,Fuel Oil, Kerosine, Propylene Gas, Ethane Gas, Sulfur Gas

Basic Refinery makes: Petroleum Coke, Lubricating Oil, Ethane

Gas escapes as pollutant

Secondary Refiner (plastics) workshop takes Ethane and makes it into polymer

Advanced Refiner also takes Waste Water from Advanced Separator to create Sulfur powder

Advanced refiner creates Pitch, Paraffin Wax/Mineral Oil/Kerosine, but one less Lubricating Oil and Coke. Over all efficency is better (more output units) and no pollution gas

**Pitch (Asphalt)** is used as a sealing/glazing item when combined with beeswax, and can be combined with crushed rock to create blocks

**Paraffin** **Wax** is used for cooling, electrical insulators and can be turned into a stronger wax for making plastic/metal molds (investment molding). Also can be used for wax crafts like beeswax, as a polish for metal, and in fertilizer production.

**Kerosine (Mineral Oil)** is used to make Lamps, and to catalyze any reaction that relies on heat (Charcol making, ect). It can also be used to store objects that oxidize or react with air and for pyrotechnics and paints. Kerosine can also be used to wash oily or waxy containers.

**Lubricating Oil (Grease)** is used in machine shops and other mechanical workshops as a reagent. The most advanced reactions will require Lubricating Oil and Water during a reaction. with the addition of other reagents it can be stretched to Grease, another form of lubrication.

**Petroleum Coke (petcoke)** is used in the construction of batteries, to smelt advanced alloys and as a fuel source.

**Sulfur** is used in the production of bullets and fertilizer
