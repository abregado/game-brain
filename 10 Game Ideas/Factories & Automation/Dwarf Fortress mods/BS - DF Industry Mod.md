BS - Dwarf Fortress Industry mod ideas

Aims:

- create another level of industry and gameplay for "late-game"

- have the normal now basic industry intrinsically linked with the new

- ability to expand to yet another level (for a sequel mod)

- tie in new content with Toadys world

- promote burrow use

Things to consider

- Inability to remove any existing content

- Move dwarves required for a single industry

- New workshops more efficent on materials and produce better products

- use existing labours (fish dissection, strand removal, architecture)

- must be Adamantine compatible (more uses for the rare ore)

- new content should not be available until the old industries are all set up and running

Bloat features

- New weapon types

- New armor types

- New harder civilization to war with

- Armor for war trainable creatures

- War trainable creature farming (trolls, rutherers, ect)

Farming/Fishing/Hunting Industry

- more uses of milk, eggs and honey

- recipes for expensive meals

- preserving fruits and meats

- seed control methods

- domestication of plant species (changing the plants into fewer seeds of a better variety)

- fertilizer production from compost (scrap excess food/plants to make fertilizer)

- non-edible plant species for composting

Bone Industry

- Powered Bone

- Shell/Bone/Ceramic/Ivory casting

- Imitation products

- fertilizer production

Ammunition Industry

- Drop forge/smelter (bullets)

- Casting bulk bolts/ballista ammo

Cooking/Brewing

- Mecahnical Brewery

- Different still sizes

- Different stages of brewing

- heat!

Smelting Industry

- Batch forge/smelter

- fertilizer production

- new alloys

Wood Industry

- Sawmill

- Proper carpentry tools means finely crafted items with more value

- Wood becomes an integral component of most industries (isnt just used for beds)

Alchemical Industry

- Gunpowder

- Grease/Lubricants (graphite)

- Fertilizers

- New beverages

Craft Industry

- Bulk crafts

- Intricate Crafts

- Larger crafts

- Multimaterial crafts

Tool Industry

- Fine toolmakers

- Specific tools (Sawblade, Forge Hammer, Bellows)

- Each industrys tools needs items from other industries

- Smelter/Forge requires: Building Plans x3, Blacksmiths Tools (steel, leather x2, stone), Bellows (leather, 2x wood), anvil, stone pot, 6x Block, 1x Grate

- Toolsmith requires: Building Plans, Toolsmith Tools (cut Diamond, steel, mechanism, wood, cloth, leather, thread, wool, claypot, glass vial, 2x Table, 4x Block, 2x Log

- Greenhouse requires: Building Plans, Gardeners Tools (Steel, Wood, clay pot x2, leather x3, Fertilizer), Glass x4, Block x3, 4x Table, 2x Pipe

- Distillery requires: Building Plans, Brewers Tools (Glass, leather, Wood, cloth bags x2), Giant Steel Barrel, Magma source, Bucket, Block x1

- Yeast Doubler: Building Plans, Brewers Tools, 2x Giant Steel Barrel, Magma source, 3x Metal Pipes, 2x mechanism.

- Gilders Press: Engravers Tools (cut Diamond, steel, fired clay blocks x4), 3x Mechanism, 2x Metal Block, 5x Stone Block

Mechanical Industry

- Different types of mechanism

- Clockwork parts

- Chains used more

Weapon/Armor Industry

- Ornate weaponry

- Gilding

- Lightweight armor and weapons from more intricate construction

- engraving

Cloth/Leather Industry

- Automated Loom

- Pattern forming/cutting

- Batches for clothing/item mfg is more efficent (20 mittens or 10 bags from 8 cloth)

Glass/Clay Industry

- Glass blower

- Mold claywork

Arcitecture Industry

- Building Schematics/Draftsdwarf

- requires architects tools and stone slabs as well as canvas/cloth

- every high end workshop requires "Building Plans"

Furniture Industry

- Mechanical doors

- Multi-material furniture

Gem Industry

- Gemstone jewlery making

- Alchemical properties for special weapons

- hard gems used for fine toolmaking

Traps

- trap only weapons mass producable

- mechanisms for traps mass producable
