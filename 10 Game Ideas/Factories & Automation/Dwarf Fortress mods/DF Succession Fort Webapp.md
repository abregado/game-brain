DF Succession Forum Plugin

Fort OP Info

Fort Name, Year of start, Rules/Challenges, DF base version, modded raws, Fort controller(Monarch), How many jobs per manager (mandates).

Options:

- Open Dorfing: Any player can send an application to have a dwarf renamed to suit them

- Reserve every 5th year for Monarch: Every 5th year, the fort is locked until the Monarch makes a post

Journal Post Info

Textwall, Image, save file, Ending wealth stats, Ending Dwarf Number, Ending Date, New Mandates, finished mandates
