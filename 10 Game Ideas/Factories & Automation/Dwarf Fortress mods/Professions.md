Names for professions should be local slang words for people who do these important jobs. Maybe apostrophies and appreviations could work as well. Maybe referring to how these workers look/smell/sound/speak/act.

Only canned food (synthcake) is vermin proof. Workshop processes other food to cake at a poor exchange rate. Maybe needs metal reagents as well. Protien synth machine.

Fishing creatures include. Algae, Pongscum, water based insects, sludge spider eggs/other eggs/spawn.

Look at lowering all attributes and changing to human. Buffs all creatures.
