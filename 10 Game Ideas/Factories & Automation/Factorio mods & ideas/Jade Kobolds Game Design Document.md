1)  Project Overview

    1.  Executive Summary (Elevator Pitch)

        1.  Player(s) play as a nature spirit with the intent of destroying and reclaiming the natural lands. As the player reclaims certain aspects of the wilds, they unlock new powers, and the civilizations become stronger and more aggressive in trying to industrialize them. AntiFactorio.

    2.  High Concept

        1.  Nature spirits are real, and are being hurt by expanding factories. The player has claws, plant and vine growth, minions/animals, as well as various mobility options (wings), As various areas or structures are reclaimed by the wilds, you unlock new powers appropriate to those regions.

    3.  Core Gameplay

        1.  Decide from a list of available regions, attack that region and destroy the opposition and infrastructure. Return to the wilds to unlock new abilities. Choose a new region to conquer.

    4.  Genre

        1.  3rd person Action Adventure.

    5.  Target Audience

        1.  20-35, Environmentally minded, Mainstream players of God of War/Dark Souls

    6.  Team Members / Jobs / Contact Info
