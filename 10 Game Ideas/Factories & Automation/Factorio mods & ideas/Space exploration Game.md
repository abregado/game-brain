Space exploration Game

Deploy objects on Asteroids and connect them for automation

Ore itself cannot be stored. Only put into machines to be converted.

Digger: Generates ore based on asteroid

Refinery: Turns ore into materials, puts them in containers. Ejects excess.

Tank: Holds fuel

Container Rack: Holds containers

FactoryLine: accepts one recipe.

Ship hulls fit chunks

- Fuel Chunk

- Deployment Chunk

- Container dock
