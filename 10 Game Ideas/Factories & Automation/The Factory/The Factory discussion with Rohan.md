The Factory discussion with Rohan

Initial questionnaire or survey to determine player type. Offer to play as “Explorer, Builder or Story Hunter’ Player can choose for themselves before the survey or after the survey.

Initial fragments of information from ‘before’ for each new character. Story Hunters will want to collect these together.

Crewsuits with a number on them that increments with every player. Once a number has been used, it is never used again. This is the only identifier.

Players can introduce themselves in chat to everyone nearby. Others must participate to get benefits (active players only). Introducing player can choose what his name will be, but can of course lie. Every character gets a ‘real name’ on generation but there is no way for other players to access this information.

Daily minigame to learn about your character, playable on mobile device. The concept of puzzle pieces of mechanical parts hinting at hidden character personality/skill set.

Players can test one another by forming a committee of experts to assess skill levels. If a new player can see the same thing as a panel of experts, they are most likely also an expert.

Everyone roleplays, just some people roleplay as themselves in the fantasy situation.

Prototypes:

- **Aim: Test player engagement when giving a random character from unrelated survey.** Initial Questionnaire/Bartle Test which ends up asking you if its selection is correct, giving you a short teaser intro and then asking for an anonymous comment. Generate a random character bio and display it.

- **Aim: Test random minigame combinations to see if players will learn the individual systems to predict and gain skill in the overall game.** simple minigame made up of several interactions with random timing and combination. Levels generate forever from seeds and an increasing difficulty number. Will players detect and learn individual component games or just eventually grok the entire system?
