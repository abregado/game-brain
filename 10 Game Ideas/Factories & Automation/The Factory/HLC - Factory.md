Game Concept Document

*“The Factory” (Working Title)*

The machine keeps us all alive, so keep it running… even if it costs you your life

Ben Buckton

### **Introduction**

“The Factory” is a Multi-User Dimension that immerses players in an oppressive world where paranoia and certain doom are parts of everyday life. It is set in an expansive location filled with machinery of all types. Players struggle to keep this machinery running, while attempting to keep all of their limbs attached.

### **Player Story**

On your way past the Medical Office you see two men carrying Larry, the foremost expert on Dynometric Deregulation, on a stretcher. Nearby the Foreman yells out over the sound of hissing steam “Bob we need you to get down to Sector 8a and deregulate the Metricdynometers. Larry got his hand caught in there!” That machine has been chewing up workers for weeks, but if you don’t get down there and fix it, everyone will be in danger! You pick up your wrench and run off towards your fate.

Sitting in the Tea room with some of the other off-duty workers, you enquire about one of the Hydroturbines down in Level 3. “Sure don’t worry, it was acting up so we sent Jones down to check it out” one of the men replies. A faint click draws the entire group’s attention to a sign reading “Hours since a fatal accident”. The number underneath has just been reset to zero. Sighing you collect your tools and leave to check it out.

### Background

Multi-User Dimensions (MUD’s) have been around for over twenty years. While their hay-day has passed, we feel that modern computing has removed certain past limitations of this gaming structure. With a MUD Server a deep and immersive game can be created without the need for expensive and time consuming Art Content.

### **Key Features**

- **Environmental Modeling:** Effects such as Ambient Temperature, Air Pressure, Liquid Levels, Radiation, Noise and Lighting Levels are all dynamic and tracked by the game.

- **Downward Spiral:** Together all the machines create a complex system that must be constantly regulated to achieve maximum efficiency. Player decisions affect the system as a whole, sometimes with disastrous consequences.

- **Perception skills:** Descriptions of characters surroundings are modified by his skill levels. Players will never be sure if their information is correct.

- **Hidden Skill Levels:** Player cannot see which skills their character is good at. Over time they will have to learn when to trust their instincts.

- **Defy Death:** Characters are often horribly scarred or suffer limb loss in the race to keep themselves alive.

### **Genre and Theme**

“The Factory” is a puzzle game with a paranoid horror theme. While non-violent the setting is intended to be mildly gruesome. Ultimately it is designed to support a social community of players who work together to succeed.

### Technology

Multi-User Dimensions (MUD’s) which are text based online worlds.

### **Platform**

A Multi-User Dimension base has been selected for this project mainly because it does not require any art development. All content can be generated by Developers who are not working on coding. There is also a plethora of documentation and tutorials on designing and programming MUDs and this should help to keep the project on track. (2018. Writers are needed for content anyway, developers cant do content!)
