All heroes

- 6 hearts

Warrior

- Good against melee enemies

- Smash obstacles to progress

- Melee attack

  - Costs mana

  - Aoe damage in close range

  - Gives more mana than costs if you hit

- Bullrush

  - Charge straight forward

  - Can catch ranged guys

  - Costs mana but gives none

- God mode

  - When rage bar gets full it auto activates

  - Invincibility

  - Quad Damage

- Mana starts at zero and builds up during fight

  - 1 second after gaining any mana, it starts to decay

  - Charges by default to 25%

  - Taking damage reduces mana instead

Wizard

- Good against ranged enemies

- Charge and shoot

- Gravity/Levitate

  - Blink to target with raycast

  - If wall or ceiling, stick and float for X seconds

  - If floor then return to normal movement

- Mana starts at max and runs out during fight

  - Mana regens over time.

  - Or you have to “reload”

4 rooms

Throne room

Castle courtyard

Doors

Levers

Pressure Pads

Character selection

Objects which can be activated.

Ranged Enemy

- 1 hit

Melee Enemy

- 3 hits

Fake it!

- Asymptotic XP gain

- Fake skill tree

- Fake inventory

- Weapons in level, you click them to swap your weapon model to swap it out.
