Descent Final Boss Types

When fighting the final boss discard all the Conquest Tokens the heroes have accumulated. For each one the Overlord must discard one Threat token. Each Overlord turn, he may spend remaining Threat tokens to make special attacks. Once the final battle has begun, no Heroes may respawn or use Glyphs of Transport. The Overlord player does not draw Event Cards. All current event cards are sold for Threat.

Type 1: Metallic Dragon Final Boss  
Movement: 3  
Health: 18  
Armour: 6  
Special: Fear (2), Sorcery (3), Immune to Knockback, Poison, Grapple and Stun

“The creature before you resembles a dragon wearing a thick armour plate. No, its skin is MADE of metal. As it moves the overlapping armour on its joints produce a shrill grinding noise. Luckily it seems too heavy to fly”

Attack 1: Chomp (Melee)  
Cost: 2 Threat  
Damage: 2 Red, 2 Green, 5 Black  
Surges: \[1\] – Increase Fear rating by 1  
\[1\] - +3 Damage  
Special: Armour is 3 for this round, Stun, If target is killed, heal wounds equal to overkill

Attack 2: Swipe (Melee)  
Cost: 1 Threat  
Damage: 2 Red, 1 Green, 3 Black  
Surges: \[1\] - +1 Damage  
\[1\] – Sweep or Reach

Attack 3: Tail Impale (Ranged)  
Cost: 1 Threat  
Damage: 1 Red, 1 Green, 1 Blue, 2 Black  
Surges: \[1\] – Peirce (2)  
\[1\] – Poison

Attack 4: Rend (Melee)  
Cost: 0 Threat  
Damage: 1 Red, 2 Green, 1 Black  
Surges: \[2\] - +1 Threat  
Special: Knockback
