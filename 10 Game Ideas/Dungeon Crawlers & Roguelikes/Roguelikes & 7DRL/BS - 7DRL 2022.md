### **Straight Line mover RL**

2d level

**Movement**

You aim movement with the mouse and travel in a straight line until you hit a wall

Then you are stuck there

Your abilities recharge timers countdown once per move

**Abilities**

After aiming your move, you can queue abilities to be fired along your path, choosing a target for each.

Process for abilities:

- Click the ability from the hotbar

- Select where it starts from along your movement line

- Then click to choose a direction (if the ability has a direction)

At any time you “end your turn” which begins your character moving.

Once you land, the game continues

The basic ability is that you can stun one target every time you move.

**Turn 1:** Player targets their jump into the room. They will land at the green point. The game tells them that an enemy can see that point and must be stunned, else the player will get hit and die.



**Turn 1, Ability setting:** The player chooses to use their Stun gun, which can be used on one target per turn. They select where along the line they will fire from and at which target.



**Turn 1 Over:** The player reaches their target point. The enemy does not fire because it is stunned for this turn.



**Turn 2 Choices:** The player selects the next jump point, which cannot be seen by the shooting enemy. Then they select to stun the trigger enemy.



**Turn 3:** The door is open while the trigger enemy is stunned, so the player chooses to target the exit with their jump. Victory!



**Ability ideas**

- Simple gun, available every time but only stuns enemies

- Big gun, takes multiple moves to recharge

  - Radius damage

- Blocker placement that lasts for 2-3 turns

- Activates and makes you move really fast so you cant be hit this round

- Shield which makes you invulnerable for 1 turn. Does not recharge, limited use. Get more at level end?

- Activates at the end, hitting all enemies on the wall you just landed on

- Smoke bomb, places a smoke cloud along your path for later movements

- Delayed missile. Is deployed on one move, then hits stunned enemies on later turns.

**Enemies**

Player has 1 HP.

Enemies aim at your target location, and fire when you land, always hit.

Killing enemies that can see your target location is part of the strategy

Locked doors that have a “key” enemy you need to kill to open them

Shields protect some enemies which are killed by destroying the Generator.

After a player selects a target, show which enemies can hit that spot.
