Definite Features

- Players dont start out with nothing

- early game is exciting

- use what you have at the start to get enough resources to start from the bottom of the tech tree

- hunting creatures for food

- detailed intellegence: sound, smell, sight, heat

- no skills

- player selected implants at start of game give bonus gameplay styles

- crashed ship acts as starting shelter and a place to sleep

- procedureal terrain

- stamina tracking

- non-HP health system

- non-limited use/charge tool durability

- difficulty is low at beginning but ramps up quickly after about 10 minutes gameplay

- after 10 minutes the player should be moving along one of many paths

- limited carrying capacity

- BPM meter

- three stage running. Shift key pushes your avatar quicker into faster modes.

- Slow backwards and strafing movement

- fast, long action system using food for fuel (fast travel)
