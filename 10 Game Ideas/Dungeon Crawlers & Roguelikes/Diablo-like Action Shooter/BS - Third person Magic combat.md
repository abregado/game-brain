After playing Two Worlds 2

**Replacements for Casting time, Mana costs and mana maximum.**

1.  Spells still have a manacost, and characters have a Mana rate. However there is no max mana on a character. Instead when you cast a spell, it fills with mana at your Mana rate until it is complete.

    1.  When not charging a spell, all spells recharge an equal percentage of your Mana rate, but at half speed. This means that you go into combat with everything charged, but so do the enemy.

2.  Different spells have different recharge methods. Ideas for recharge methods include:

    1.  Get a certain distance away from where you last cast the spell

    2.  Stand next to certain terrain features (big rock, river, tree) to recharge

    3.  Kill a certain type of enemy before it recharges

    4.  Spell linking: One spell to charge another spell.

**Replacements for constant ROF, cheap spells. Standing still and firing at targets is very uninteresting.**

1.  Much more expensive spell, that you need to stand still to charge. Once it is charged you gain a number of shots with a quick projectile spell. You can move while throwing these projectiles. This is a gun/reload analog.

    1.  Each shot has 2 effects they can be spent on. One defensive and one offensive?

2.  Spells which change your position but recharge other abilities. Forward dashes, long range inaccurate teleports, ect which force the players position to change in a not very accurate way.

3.  Melee combat charges up magical spells, so you are always swapping between magic and melee.

**Other ideas**

1.  While charging a spell you gain an aura. The aura can be modified per spell. Examples:

    1.  A Fireball spell. While charging it up, you heal allies nearby.

    2.  A teleport spell. Select the target destination. After the charge up period is complete you are teleported. While it is charging you have a damaging aura around you.
