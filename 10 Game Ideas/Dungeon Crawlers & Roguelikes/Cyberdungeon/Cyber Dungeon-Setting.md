Cyber Dungeon Setting

The main characters are all crew members aboard a Salvage Barge that has been working along the Human/Scarbrae Border. While the entire crew is Human, the ship itself is not part of the military and thus avoids direct conflict with both sides.

The salvage crew are divided up into teams, each equipped with a Shuttle. These teams are sent out to examine sensor readings that the barge detects, salvaging where possible and helping the crews of damaged craft get back to port.

Of course, with the war going on, these simple guidelines are often overlooked. The parts and cargo found in a single craft can often make a man’s fortune.
