10 Human Crew Cards

Civilian Crew

Trained Crew (Security Personnel)

Elite Crew (Marine Troopers)

Specialist Crew (Security Drones)

Lockdown  
Any Rooms with Encounter Cards still in them are “locked down” All doors leading to these rooms are closed and locked. Place this card face up inside the nearest one to show the condition.

Defence Turrets  
Two defence turrets are operational in the room.

Lights Out!  
The lighting system is disabled by the remaining crew. Al enemies encountered will be nearing Low Light gear and will not be affected by bad lighting Conditions.

SOS  
The remaining Crew have triggered the automated SOS message broadcast. Enemy Reinforcements will arrive in 20 turns. This time limit will reduce by 2 turns each end phase until the signal is stopped.

Self Destruct  
If there is only one crew member left, and at least one Escape pod, the Self Destruct Countdown is initiated. The Crew only have 5 turns to turn it off or vacate the ship.

Double Back  
randomly select an unused encounter card from the same Race as the current ship. Place this card in the first room the Heroes entered. If the Heroes wish to exit the vessel they will have to remove these defenders first.
