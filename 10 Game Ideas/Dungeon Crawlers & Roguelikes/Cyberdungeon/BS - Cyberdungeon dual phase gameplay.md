Brainstorming

Cyberdungeon dual phase gameplay

This concept is designed for a computer game system, however it may also work in a boardgame system.

Players vote to complete either a Combat or Non-Combat action. Both of these require interaction with different game mechanics.

A Combat action is any situation where the party attacks an enemy, goes on a mission, or completes a mercenary job.

A NonCombat mission is any situation where players explore an area that does not have a direct goal or conflict to resolve. This could be Exploring a planet/area/derelict, visiting a town, spaceport or station, or simply hanging out in the parties ship.

During a non-combat action, the party members are free to split up and do as they please. These would also include spending earnt resources rather than accrueing new ones.

Perhaps new mission/plot hooks could be discovered in a Non-combat area, or even portals to Combat areas could be located.

Content in non-combat areas could be proceedurally generated to encourage exploration.
