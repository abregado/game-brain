Brainstorming - Cyberdungeon Zero G Movement

When boarding a craft without gravity movement is restricted to straight lines. Figures that are not adjacent to either a wall, fixed obstacle or grab point cannot move until assisted by a friendly.

Moving down corridors and access tunnels requires moving in front of the tunnel first. When moving off from the fixed point a speed is decided on. This speed is maintained until a wall or fixed point is encountered.

Characters can move along walls and fixed objects at half their normal combat speed.

If gravity resumes suddenly then half a charcaters actions are used reorienting to the new movement style in the first round.

All full actions in zero-g take an additional action to complete (searching rooms/containers, modifying equipment)

Being hit by weapons discharge pushes the character away from the hit at a speed of one tile per turn. Unless the character was attached to a fixed point or obstacle

Being fixed to a fixed obstacle requires a free hand, or a successful dexterity test.

Firing Different Weapons

One Handed Weapons - Single action to fire, two to fire accuratly

Two Handed Weapons - two actions to fire, three to fire accuratly

Two Handed Heavy Weapons - three to fire, four to fire acuratly

Each weapon has two firemodes. The first is a standard shot, costing 1,2 or 3 actions to perform. The other is either the accurate, burst or special firemode costing 2,3 or 4 action to perform.

NPC actions and you

At the start of the turn all players are dealt 3 cards plus one extra for each NPC on the field. The player with the most cards in hand must play a card representing an NPC. After playing a card for an NPC the player draws a new card.

Elite NPCs always draw an extra card and use whichever is better, the card chosen by the player, or the drawn card.

Becasue each NPC only gets one card, they can only Fire at the players if they are not shot at. If an NPC fires then it gets no chance to defend itself if fired upon.

Elite NPCs always get to draw a card to defend even if they already fired.

Fate/Heroic/Narritive/Plot Points

The Hero characters have a number of tokens which represent their unlikelyness to die even in the face of overwhelming odds. They are the "goods guys" who cinematically must win. Each time a Hero is hit he/she may gamble one or more of these on ignoring the damage. As long as one of the dice rolls 4+ then the hit is ignored completly. If all the rolled dice score 4+ then the player gets one token back.

Goals and Reward

Players should not be rewarded for making kills, but for completing the mission objectives.

Rewards should be in liquid currency, but bonuses should be fixed items that give an opportunity for payout later on (rare commodities, cargos,)

Setting

Players are space combat mercs, pirates, military or ex-cons who perform boarding actions or planetside missions for cash.
