Commodity Types

Tools – carriable and useable by a single character. A deck of generic items makes up the majority, while a smaller deck of racial specific cards is shuffled in at the start of a boarding action.

Small Containers – Carriable one at a time by heroes. These can be loaded into the ship without checking what is in them. Opening containers takes a whole turn.

Large Containers – Not carriable by heroes. If the boarded craft is secured, then these may be moved to the boarding ships cargohold in one turn by two heroes. Checking contents takes a single turn.

VOLATILE CARGOS

Some types of cargo are dangerous or illegal. Carrying such goods often can cause serious problems for the crew. Some cargos also have conditions that are triggered if the cargo was moved without being inspected first.

Fighting using a Condition based abstract system

Heroes and Enemies do not move in single squares. During a combat all heroes and enemies are considered inside “a room” and combat is modified by conditions that can be modified during said combat.

Conditions:  
Long Range  
Cover  
Hidden  
Melee  
Stunned  
Unconscious

Each condition has a set of rules that effect the model/unit that it is on. These rule are further modified by special equipment bonuses.
