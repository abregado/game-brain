Encounters.

Certain actions will create Noise or notice. When this happens the lead heros drawns an encounter card. If it is a new encounter card, then just add the reinforcements from the previous card that is in play.

Only one encounter card can be in play

Any number of persistance effect cards can be in play alongside one Encounter

Types include:

Helpers

Events

Environmental Effects

New enemies can spawn in any unseen square of a room or corridor. Once the heros have cleared a room it is considered "Secure". No enemies can spawn there. However if new enemies manage to enter the room while no heroes are present then it becomes "unsecured"

The only way multiple Encounters can be on the board at once is if the heroes enter or activate multiple rooms at once.

Gameplay Elements!

The Core Features of the game design are:

The ability to eliminate a lot of the random element to the game through good desisions

Teamplay in combat due to overwhelming ods.

"Character deveoplemt" is acheived as a team. The Ship and crew are created over time, and no player claims ownership of a single character.

Fast paced combat. 1-2 hour play time per adventure

Lengthy post battle. Optional. 30mins - 1hr between games.

Actions/Special Equipment/Countering equipment

Plasma Welder

a heroes can seal up metal doors and bulkheads. each turn roll a single dice and add the successes to a total. Once it meets the total required to seal the door, it cannot be opened except if it is cut through or blown up.

Shaped Charges

A hero can use these to blow open a door. Each door type has a number Damage Points. For eachone, a single success has to be made to place one explosive on the door. Once the number of explosives on the door reaches the total required, a player may use his whole action to blow the door.

Decompression charges

These may be placed on the floor or outside wall of a craft. The number of charges required is equal to the remaining Integrity of the craft. Once enough charges have been placed a player may use all his action to detonate them. The room is then classified as Decompressing.

Stimulants

These may be administered to any ajacent character. They heal a single point of Brusing, Fatigue or Shock damage. Unlimited Uses.

RegenX Hyperderms

These drugs regenerate damaged tissue. They heal a single 1-2 point Cut, Burn or Bullet wound. Alternativly they can heal up to 3 points of Brusing or Fatigue damage. 5 Uses per adventure

Dermal Conditioner

This device cleans and renews skins, removing any harmful substances while it works. Heals a single 2 point Chemical, Burn, Freeze or Radiation wound. It can also remove one 1point cut instead of its normal use. 3 Uses per adventure.

Medical Kit

Complex field practices can be acheived with a Medical Kit. progressive Skill checks are made for all wounds being treated. Patient and Medic must remain stationary during the whole treatment. Maximum wound size is 4. Wounds healable: Burn, Cut, Bullet, Chemcial, Electrical, Freeze, Radiation. Most types except Pressure/Psychic

MagBoots

While in a room with a hull breach, a hero must use one hand to negate the effects of being dragged toward the hole. With magboots, a player does not have to. He/She is immune to the effects of breach movment.

HyperDerm wrist injector

A hero may inject a single dose of drugs without using an action. This may not be used to inject an adjacent friend or foe. Unlimited Uses

Skills

Most skills will simply add a number of dice to certain skill checks. These rest will allow heros to spend fatigue points to perform special actions.

Spinning Strike - HTH - 2 Fatigue

Allows the hero to attack with one HTH weapon with both hands, hitting all figures adjacent to him/her. If the weapon has a longer reach further targets may be hit on 4+.

Hipshot - 2H Gun - 3 Fatigue

Allows a hero to attack using a 2handed gun as if it was only a one handed weapon.

Medical Knowledge - Passive

Whenevr this hero makes a skill roll for any Medical Equipment, he/she may add an extra dice to the roll

Parry - 1 Fatigue

This hero may forfiet a single action to count as being in cover against a single HTH combat attack.

Dodge - 1 Fatigue

When being attacked, this hero may be moved 1 square at the cost of 1 fatigue. Only a single square can be moved.

Charging Swing - HTH - 3 Fatigue

A hero with this skill can immediatly make a single attack against a single adjacent target after taking a RUN action. The fatigue is paid regardless of the outcome of the attack.

How will it play....???

Well technically in any boarding action where the enemy knows u will attack, there will be an Encounter waiting at the airlock. Once combat starts, it will rarely end until all combatants form one side are dead. Each turn that any shooting occurs, reinforcements/events are drawn. So either points in Crew/Security will be spent or earnt. Unless the heros take out the last enemy, and then do not draw a new Encounter, combat will continue.

The cases when this will not happen are when the heroes board a supposably "uninhabited" craft. The heroes will more than likely enter a craft and move about for a while before the "inhabitabnts" show themselves.

Also there will be special skills that will allow the heroes to gain entrance to the target ship in different ways, such as infiltration, breaching entrance to a particular room or initiating a false trade.

Sometimes the crew will have to defend their craft against boarders. This will be a totally new expansion for the game, since it will need a different event generation mechanic.

Play Order

Equipment Changing

Heroes must declare whihc equipment they will be using this phase. Any or all equipment can be changed from the pool of cards the hero brought with him into the combat. Enemies do not change equipment, they fight with the equipment that is listed on the cards and none others.

Actions Phase

Starting with the hero or enemy with the lowest initiative, take it in turns performing actions.

but may be interupted by "faster" players.

Each hero may perform one action with each hand

or

a single action with both hands

or

move 2 squares and a single handed action

or

Run equal to his Speed in a straight line

Any other player/enemy can interup between individual actions if their initiative is higher.

Movement

If a hero or enemy decides to perform only a single one handed action, he/she may move 2 squares, splitting the movement any way they want. Following Combinations:

Move 2, Act

Act, Move 2

Move1, Act, Move 1

If a hero or enemy elects to perform no actions and RUN, it may move equal to its movment in a straight line or diagonal line.

Attacking.

One action that can be taken with a weapon is attacking. Declare whihc weapon card you want to attack with and your intended target. Depending on Cover and Range you may or may not hit.

To Hit System

All weapons have an optimal range. If the target is within this range and not in cover it is hit automatically.

if it is in range but in cover it is hit on 4+

if it is outside range but in the open it is hit on 4+

if it is in cover and outside range then it cannot be hit.

Damage System

Weapons have a damage type and a damage number.

Armor has a base protection value and special protections vs differnt damage types.

Armor has a number of HP

Each hit removes a single point of HP. If all HP on armor is lost it protects less

Damage - Protection = Wound Size.

If target is a hero, replace HP equal to Wound size with a Wound marker of the appropriate type.

If target is a m00k then just place HP next to him.

If a target is out of HP then he is unconscious.

Unconscious targets can be "terminated" by using an attack on them from an adaject square. These attacks autohit and auto ignore all armor. It is assumed that u remove their helmet and shoot them in the face or somesuch surefire method.

Other Actions

Equipment other than weapons can be used to perform actions. It is stated on their card whether the activation time if instant or set to a number of turns based on special skill rolls.

Instant Effects

These happen as soon as the action to activate them is taken. They may interrupt another figures action. Sometimes these need skill rolls made for them to be successful.

Timed Effects

These are resolved at the end of the turn on whihc the effect is listed to occur. Timed(0) means that the effect happens at the end of the turn the action is taken on. Skill rolls are made at the time the effect is calculated.

Progressive Rolls

Each turn an action is taken that will add to the progressive total is made, roll the total skill dice and for each 4+ rolled add one to the total number of successes. When the total required is reached, the effects of the action are applied and calculated.

Remove Persistant Effects/ Calc Timed Effects

During this phase, work out the effects of any Timed Effects that have zero counters on them and remove one counter from all Timed Effects that are still waiting to happen. Any effects that are listed to last until the end of the turn end now.

New Encounters

If there was any action performed that are considered "Noisy", draw an Encounter Card on 4+. The placement of any new enemies is done immediatly. Effect cards are placed in play, or if instant, calculated immediatly.
