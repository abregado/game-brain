Developing the Playing Field

AIMS:

Using the same set of cards, many different Boards can be created

Each Board has different tactics needed to complete it

Every element is variable

Elements that can vary:

Board Layout

Number of Rooms

Types of Rooms

Ship Systems/Operational

Crew Type/ Ship Racial Design

Crew Numbers

Security Rating

Ship Damage

Deal Damage Cards

These cards show a number of different damage types that could be affecting the ship. The number of these dealt is determined by the “reasons for Salvage”

Airlock Destroyed  
One of the airlocks on the craft has been destroyed making access impossible. The Heroes will have to find some other way in.

Impact Breach (2x Size 1)  
Something has caused several small breaches in the hull, venting atmosphere into space. These are placed randomly but can be used to gain access to the ship.

Impact Breach (3x Size 1)  
Something has caused several small breaches in the hull, venting atmosphere into space. These are placed randomly but can be used to gain access to the ship.

Blast Breach (Size 3)  
Something has caused a large breach in the hull, venting atmosphere and cargo into space. These are placed randomly but can be used to gain access to the ship.

Life Support Down  
The Life support systems have been damaged and will need to be repaired before they can be brought back online. Follow the rules for “No Life support” until it is repaired.

Gravity Down  
The Artificial Gravity systems have been damaged and will need to be repaired before they can be brought back online. Follow the rules for “Zero G Movement and Operations” until it is repaired.

Power Generators  
The ships main power is damaged and will need to be repaired before any of the systems can be brought online. All systems operate at Emergency Status if they are operational without power. See the rules for “Emergency Systems”.

Doors non-operational  
Door Controls and Emergency Bulkheads are offline. The Heroes will have to cut through doors if they want to make progress. Randomly determine which doors are open/closed as the Heroes encounter them.

Spaced Crew (-D6 Crew)  
A number of the crew can be seen floating in space near the ship. No life signs detected.

Escape Pods Jettisoned  
All of the ships Escape Pods have been Jettisoned. The Heroes cannot use them to make a quick getaway.

Lighting Broken  
The Lighting and Emergency Lighting systems have been disabled and will require repairs before they are operational. Heroes will be subject to either the “Pitch Black” or “Emergency Lighting” Effects listed in the Environmental Conditions section of the book.

Hull Integrity Compromised (-D6 Integrity)  
Due to internal and external damages, the ship has become very unstable. Weapons fire and use of Explosives is strongly discouraged.
