### Emerge

Underground civilization discover old elevators leading to the surface. Some more crazy pioneers head topside to explore and colonize. These pioneers are the players. They have the weakest agoraphobia from the entire population. Even though, it is enough to hamper their mission.

Bases do not build up because the culture has never needed to build multi-story buildings. Normally you just dig and create negative space to fill.

The surface is a desert wasteland, with ancient structures peaking through.

Structures cannot be built on the sand. Machines build foundations which can be built upon.

Characters can’t go far because of agoraphobia.

Round ends are sandstorms, midday, midnight. Random events are generally weather related.

Characters wear masks and protective equipment, but to start with it is not very good. The civilization does not know what they need yet. Technology comes from below as the players explore.

Characters recover when they go below. Things are just fine down in the depths, civilization runs along and people are happy and healthy. There just is not much space, and the population expands very slowly.

The elevator is a fixed size and is quite slow. This limits the amount of resources that the characters can bring with them, and gives an excuse why they need to get back on at a certain time.

Not being able to move the elevator could create some interesting logistical decisions.

Mysteries and special events:

- A green electrical storm that passes over. Items left out afterwards have a chance to be stolen, but by whom?

- Ancient data banks can be repowered and downloaded. This requires precise timing and planning over many game sessions.
