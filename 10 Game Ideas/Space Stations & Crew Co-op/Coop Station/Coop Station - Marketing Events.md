Short pitches

Explorers from a high tech underground society build a surface outpost to help unravel a mystery. Just why is the surface a desert wasteland?.

Non-violent, cooperative multiplayer game where the players plan, build and coordinate construction of an outpost over several rounds while uncovering why the world is as it is.

Keywords

- High tech

- Underground

- Explore

- Mystery

- Non-violent

- Relaxing

- Multiplayer

How do the players interact?

Planning a strategy before each round

Allocating tasks between each other.

Bringing items to each other.
