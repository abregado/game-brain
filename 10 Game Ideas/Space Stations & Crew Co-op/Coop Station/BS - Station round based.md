**Idea 1:** Start in middle. Random junk station with mildly functioning parts.

Players waiting for next round can work as AI if they have played enough rounds before. Or wait to be downloaded into a clone, monkey, robot.

Time until round end based on consumption.

Can never make it go positive, only that it goes down very slowly
