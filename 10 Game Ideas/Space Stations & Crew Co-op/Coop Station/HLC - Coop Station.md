# Station Co-op

Cooperative round based colony builder for small groups

By Ben Buckton

> A relaxing, roleplay building game where the players control different characters during numerous work shifts aboard a budding space colony.

###  

### Player Story

You disembark from your ship onto the station concourse. At the end of the last shift you and Lenny had to load the shuttle during a meteor shower. Pretty hectic but manageable. Now you just need to return all the equipment you rented.

The rental assistant checks over the cargo. “Sir! The manifest says you rented 4 Beam Extruders, but there are only 3 in the cargo hold. I will have to send a pickup shuttle to get it. The fee is fourteen hundred credits.”.

Swearing under your breath you double check the count. Damn! It must have been left behind in the confusion. “Let me just check with my coworker” you declare.

Now, where is that lazy bum Lenny? I didn't see him the entire flight back...

### Background

Zero player storytelling games (such as Rimworld) are very popular at the moment but I feel that these games lack the ability for the player to roleplay as one of the characters.

Colony builder games rely too heavily on surprise events to generate conflict. This project aims to create strategic challenges through forecasted events, instead of surprise, similar to how it is done in Stardew Valley.

I also see a market for games which small groups of friends (2-5) would enjoy playing together over an entire playthrough.

### Key Features

| **Plan for your future:** There are no sudden random challenges, instead you always have several rounds notice of impending problems such as meteor showers, radiation events or swarms of “guests”.                                           |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Build a crew, both in game and out:** The game is designed for small group cooperative play. Each round you select one character to play as. Invite your friends to play as the other characters for a round or two.                         |
| **Uncover a mystery:** Each asteroid is tied to one of several mysteries. You and your crew will need to explore and expand to find out what is behind them all.                                                                               |
| **Relaxing and Non-violent:** Play focuses on building and developing your creation. This is not a game about fending off hordes of aliens.                                                                                                    |
| **Create a server community:** The gameplay is designed for player groups from different time zones to be able to interact. As the owner of a dedicated server, you can control when and how often other players can play rounds on a station. |

###  

### Genre and Theme

The game is real time but played in rounds of 15-60 minutes. The minute to minute gameplay involves deploying machines to expand the station, tending to those machines and then packing everything up before the round ends.

The game should be relaxing, and light hearted. The style should reflect the feeling of a gritty and dangerous work environment, but with a light humorous touch. I would look to games like Factorio and Space Station 13 for inspiration.

### Technology

Currently I would be pursuing access to the Factorio engine, since it already has many of the systems for a 2d building game in place. However the project could be completed with a conventional engine (Unity, Unreal or even Godot) as long as it has robust multiplayer functionality.

### Platform

Initially the product would be made available only on Windows PC. Early player engagement is essential for any new project and PC players tend to have higher hours per game title. The gameplay interactions themselves should be simple enough that mouse and keyboard are not required, so other platforms would be considered later in the development cycle, assuming crossplay is an option. Linux and Mac are both options as well, but require more support, which we will not have the resources for.

### Market

Our target audience is the subsection of builders who also like to share their creations, either through screenshots, or by inviting friends to participate. Builder players like when they can invest in a project long term and we target those that want to do so with friends.

Our future players are currently playing games such as Stardew Valley, Rimworld, Space Engineers, Stationeers, Astroneer and Space Station 13.

### Development

Development will consist of a 6 month proofing phase, followed by 12 month to get the project to a crowd fundable level. Early on the bulk of the non-programming roles can be filled by myself. This includes: Project management, community management, campaign moderation, marketing, human resources, content scripting, user interface and initial placeholder art. An artist would only be hired after the initial 6 month phase. A programmer partner is required for the first two phases.

### Design Pillars

The sacred axioms. Design choices will not be considered if they violate these.

| **Small groups.** Sure, you can play single player. 100+ players? Sure, do so at your own risk. The game is however designed for 2-5 players with fluctuating player count in each round. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **No sudden danger.** The player is never surprised by a randomly generated challenge. They always see challenges coming and have several rounds to prepare.                              |
| **Shareability:** Solutions should accommodate the social gamer archetype. This includes players who share on social media, or by inviting friends to play.                               |
