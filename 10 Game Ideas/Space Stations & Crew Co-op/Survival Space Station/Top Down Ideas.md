Factory room idea

- each “day” you choose what work to do to repair machines

- each day more machine rooms becoming unlocked

- dev created room content

- proceedural placement and relationships based on seed

- share seed to try high score comps

- live as long as possible

- invite players to play with you when your factory becomes too large

Permadeath Survival Ideas

- very few instant deaths. Deaths tend to happen due to players becomin wittled down.

- Incentive to stay alive comes from respawning/getting a new character far from the good areas

- limited safe logout spots at key locations

Survival Mapping Ideas

- no minimap

- players navigate with temporary beacons which run out of power eventually

- beacons can only be detected at set range so a “breadcrumb trail” must be left

- current sector or hints to location can be linked into terrain

- maps contain landmarks occasionally but mostly terrain looks very similar

- infrastructure upgrades might include longer range or long lasting beacons to create “paths” or roads

- each player has a limited number of beacons that can be operational at the same time. To make longer paths players must work together

- beacons can be placed on any frequency

- players tune their beacon detectors to a frequency

- perhaps players can hack beacons to find their frequency or deactivate them. Then these players could hunt down otherplayers by following their beacons.

space station environement for survival

- town/shop/safe areas can be set up by moving heavy air proccessing equipment to a new location

- the amount of equipment a character can carry is very low while still being combat capabile

- players start at an airlock at the edge of the station.

- respawns all take place here

- players must set up heavy “safe logoff” equipment in new locations by moving it from the airlock

- other infrastructure gear must also be moved manually from the airlock

- possible revenue from RMTs for this equipment

- as players venture deeper into the station, more and more of the alien machinery is active and probably dangerous

- tilesets could include almost anything: Alien arcitecture, jungles from overgrown biodomes, clean crisp alien laboratories, grungy factory floors, immense storage zones, organic sub-systems internals.

- when a player uses a consumable item,fires weapons or reloads, garbage builds up in the area over time. Well used areas will be visibly so.

Character progression

- NO EXP GAIN/GRINDING/ECT!!!!

- player skill is the most important thing

- learning how enemies and terrain affect your gameplay

- learning who to trust

- learning to pack enough survival gear, but also to save as much space as possible for loot.

- learning your environment

- choosing the right equipment for a situation, or if your future situation is unknown, packing for a range of eventualities.

- Since players cannot take equipment off the server when they log off, players are actually progressing the server state rather than their player state.

- perminant item/effects/socket that once filled cannot be removed without destrying the item. players can see that you have these equipped and cant kill you to get them.

- if a different item is needed for each situation, only give the player enough to carry 1/3rd of the solutions\\

The “crafting” chain

- all survival games have items that can be “transmuted” into other usable items, or have a variety of uses

- items being pushed through this chain often cannot be pushed backwards (aka recycling).

- generally players transmute items by using skills, shops or infrastructure

- in a space survival game, players could trade loot items for shipments of equipment which arrive at the airlock after a certain time.

- or players could set up infrastructure to convert items into other items over time

- due to the survival element being time critical, I feel that transmutation should take decent amounts of time to reward planning ahead (or penalizing failing to plan ahead).

- perhaps players can find fixed alien infrastructure that cannot be moved which can perform unique transmutations. This could promote occasional events where a group of players have a common interest to travel to a location which is normally of low strategic value.

- Since all wealth is stuck on the server, the power to transmute is given to the player who possesses the item NOW. Possesion in any one instance doesnt mean that you will control the item indefinatly, but it does mean you can mildly control how it will be used in the future.

- Gaining wealth/loot items just means you get to purchase items for the server that YOU want, effectivly your power to change a community driven project.

Generating a need for players to move through the game world

- there should always be more things to do available than the players can do at one time

- it should be up the the players to determine the priority of things

- this can create schisms in the players when there are disagreements

- this can create interesting leadership events when all players want to do the same thing but it is not optimal for ALL players to do it at the same time.

- key locations could generate potential wealth on regular intervals, but also reach a maximum wealth storage after a set period to promote regular visits.

- players may compete for these resources so as to have choice over how they are used, or work together and then give the items to a central leadership player

- server events that will have concequences that effect ALL players. this gives strangers a reason to work together temporarily.

Focus use idea

- having active equipment costs your character focus

- having full focus means you have a large FOV arc

- keeping more equipment (scanners, weapons,computers,sensors,ect) online means your FOV will drop down very low

- One player might use a full thermal suite which uses his entire focus but also gives him a 360 degree thermal imaging view. He is then vulnerable to attack by thermally invisble creatures

- pistols might cost less focus than rifles and heavy guns

- couple this with encounberance and player should be encouranged to keep their equipment offline except when they need it.

- This should make cautious players who travel with thier weapons active not just go slower, but also cause them to need more time to check corners and create opportunitties for cockups.

More Survival Ideas

- Players can immobilize others by becoming immobilized next to them (holding them down/in place)

- players can search another players inventory if that player lets him

- Immobilized players cannot search

- Stronger players must be immobilized by multiple weaker players

- Items can be hidden inside of clothing items. Better tools allow them to be hidden more thougfhly

- Destroying an item yields hidden items but also there is a small chance that the hidden items will be destroyed.

- Items do not get destroyed, only damaged until useless.

- The same items and clothing will be in circulation during a single game.

- Tools will be the single most important item in the game

- Weapons should function similar to tools

- Even low quality tools will be valuable when the player count starts to go up

- tools represent what kinds of work, and how many players can do kinds of work

- Players can log off but their character stays where they were and will be effectd by hunger/temperature/ect. They may log back in to find themselves dead!

- Loggin off “safely” means finding a warm place with friendly people around. Sleeping bags, blankets, campfires, caves, ect.

- Even when logged off safely a player can be murdered in their sleep!

- Hunger needs must be satisfied when a player logs in. Sleep near a food supply!

- clothing that gets wet will lose its insulation value and not protect against cold any longer

- bedding will be the same, sleep under some shelter!

Exposure mechanics

- Encounberance effects hunger

- hunger effects body temperature

- body temperature effects health

- nutrition effects health

- health effect speed/strength/carry weight/ect

- Wounds heal over time based on health and have an effect on speed/strength/ect

- wounds that are not treated increase their penalty over time, and take longer to heal

- when a wound is healed it gives a perminant penalty which is a small percentage of its old penalty.

- wounds should rarely kill a player, just make it harder to survive.

- insulation effects body heat decay

- rain/water/ect effects insulation levels

Possible features for the top down shooter

- time based objectives

- time based activation of buttons

- weapons that have a firing delay (laser cannon)

- queuing up of shots to fire

- laying of traps for enemies

- defending objectives while they are counting down

- the player places bombs or markers and enemies come to disable them

- enemies respawn/reinforcements arrive

- fixed emplacements

- slow moving projectiles, grenades, mines, bombs, to show off the slow mo more

- active defenses to take advantage of slow mo without dodging

- time based buffs, aoe effects (atmospheric things, gas, flashbangs)

- Time effected powerups: items explode after one second so you need to collect them immediatly but picking them up leaves you exposed for half a second. This means you take no risk with the last enemy killed

- Consumables "ripen": consumables give a benefit if eaten instatly but also this benefit is increased if you holdit for a while. Holding too long makes the item go bad.

- Consumables give a passive bonus: as well as being consumable, powerups give bonusrs over time.

- Soft cooldown: Abilities can be used as often as you like but dont get full effect unless left to cooldown.

- Hold wait to activate or collect. Means time flows but you are not moving.
