Idea 1: Persistent Permadeath Action based Shooter with permanent PvP and Server based goals to unlock new areas. Limited release supplies player market. Focus using weapons and equipment combinations, as well as dwindling consumable resources. Procedural station that unlocks as players complete server objectives. Position based tactical action, choosing where to fight an enemy is more important than having bigger guns or more squad members. Detection and information warfare also important. Quiet stealthy gameplay until players choose to “pop” an encounter (or fuck up and trigger early), and then hectic combat for ~10-15 minutes.

Idea 2: Round Based mechanically enforced roleplay game with player skill based advancement and reputation system. Mystery and Intrigue with a “someone is the bad buy” one vs many covert gameplay. Complex Item interaction. Persistent Meta bonuses (access to more roles/classes/starting equipment) for good play. Characters not persistent between rounds but maybe the station is. Pseudo random levels, designed in chunks which are put together at level start, random item placement to encourage exploration of each level. ~1 hr round times. Heuristics to create objectives for each player which overlap with other players, causing interaction (AI director).

Idea 3: Community/Server Cooperative Survival against overwhelming NPC opponents. No PvP except through market competition and leaderboards. Each server has an “uptime” highscore that goes onto a global leaderboard. Players do not “own” anything, but just use/rent and upgrade server equipment. Server reset on fail. Players vote to change settings/spend money on the station (increase spending on fuel, ships, ammunition) or can vote in a council who make decisions for 1 week. ~6 week server cycle time.

Concepts common throughout these:

- Player investment in the server they play on to gain “player agency”

- PvP is either always on or always off, no zones of changing PvP status (focused gameplay on either PvP or non PvP)

- Gameplay design is focused on making the most of the unique points, not on covering as many gameplay scenarios as is possible (depth over breadth)

- Focus on log in/log out casual style of gameplay rather than needing to play 24/7. Play loop design aiming for 1 hour play sessions.

- Space theme because space is cool. Can be CuteGore, Grim Dark or Post Apoc style I dont really care as long as its in space, on a space station or spaceship.

- Multiplayer game, although the targeted player counts are different.

- I would want Minimum Viable product within 12 months, with a further 12 months of feature implementation planned and ordered before commencing work (its good for PR and to keep in scope). Obviously this means needing more than 1 person working on it.
