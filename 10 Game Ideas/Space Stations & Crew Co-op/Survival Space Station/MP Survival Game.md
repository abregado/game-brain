Aims:

- reason to play multiplayer

- surviving on your own is almost impossible

- building up infrastructure is a key survival mechanic

- travelling far away is the end game goal

- not perma-death, but geological penalty for death (respawn at 0,0)

- zero sum server inventory: objects cannot be taken away from the game

- everything decays

- sense of adventure when you leave the safe areas

- Server "embark points"

- Player embark points

- Choose an outfit which gives afvantages from the first moment

- Dont use durability!

- every choice to build or craft should be a difficult one

- thinking ahead should be rewarded

- clear out/destroy monster nests/spawners to make safe areas

- boss monsters for larger spawners

- 

Concepts:

- Alarm additions to warn players of mob ingress

- backpack item that must be removed to access

- player must remain stationary when accessing backpack

- clothing determines active non-backpack slots (pockets/holsters/ect)

- different slot size (small item/large item/long/rifle specific/pistol specific/liquid)

- hands are omni-use, one hand for most items, two for large items

- larger crates that can be carried by two people or loaded onto vehicles

- zoom levels and focus on mouse to create forward awareness but remove local awareness

- Players should feel safe when in areas of their creating

- empty areas should be visibly so

- Players should always get warning of bad events/monsters so they can react

- no “unfair” deaths

- defending constructed areas regularly against attack (but not constantly)

- interlinked systems

- players modifying the game area affects other systems

- “jobs/class” dictated by tool use

- tool quantities are abstract “job slot maximums”

- regional markets: some resources are only available in far away areas

- hunger bars which drop passively and on every action

- crafting requires ingredients, correct location and time expenditure (waiting)

- common “reactions” that each item can be subjected to to limit the need of wikis

- limited carry space to promote building logistics infrastructure or remote bases

- group bonuses for being near other players

- no experience gains or class bonuses. All players are equal

- bread crumbs for players to follow during exploration

- procgen maps and challenges

- players are given personal goals that must be achieved. Ask for help or keep them secret.

- A clean victory only happen when all players fulfil their personal goals and the team achieves the team goal.

- team goals are different each game

- the combinations of team goal and player goals makes for a different experience every time

Game length and persistence

- Games should go for about 2-4 hours

- levels can be saved for playing again later

- Infrastructure might rot each time the players “hibernate”

Crafting

- resources can be changed into more useful items but not changed back

- no “recycling”

- construction takes time, so players can do something else while they wait

- limited number of concurrent crafting “slots”

- competition for crafting slots should make the choices much more interesting, as having a huge wealth of raw materials does not mean you can have everything now.

- types of reaction for crafting: Burn, Cut, Join, Crush

- these should make it easy to intuit crafting recipes

- some reactions yield two products

- some reactions can have two inputs

- most resources are colleted from crash sites or ruins.

- Items are not constructed from basic resources like wood, stone, ect

- Buildigs are created on foundation blocks, which are pregenerated on the map

- Map is non deformable

- Structures share power for appliances and lights

- Each structure has a atomgenerator that is working, broken, and could be missing a fuel cell

- Appliances and lights that are added to the structure tun if they are powered.

- Solar panels can be added

- Batteries can be added but are very heavy (prrhaps scavenge from other houses)

- Structures could also have heating and cooling

- To retreive atomic fuel cells ghe gen must be disabled for three days, else the cells are hot

- Powered doors and lights mean entering a strucutre, disabling the power, then getting out in the dark. Only to return later for the prize.

- Startig weapons use much more valuable resources as ammo, so using low tech weapons is forced as low tech ammo is widely available.

Resources

- ammunition and food are the two primary resources

- water could be used as well

- other objects are used as ingredients

- clothing and heat sources are important

- cooling for hot areas

- batteries for tools

- biomass for respawns? feeding corpses into a tank could be grim
