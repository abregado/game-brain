**Concept:** Round goals must be reached for the game to be saved, otherwise the next round just loads the same game.

**Bens Matrix points hit:**

Two masters: Players must “produce” something but also expand the station. Just fulfilling the goals will not make the station larger, and it will get stuck eventually.

Server goals: Not only coop in the round, but also in the meta. Shared stamina/food clock and saving system means nobody can “get rich” on their own. Tool/Class lockers mean nobody owns anything themselves.

Succession: Stations expand and evolve not based on a single player's choices, or the choices of separate groups. Building choices made early on will have a big impact on the late game design of the station.

Baby Leaving the nest: You start a station on a public server with 1-2 players and when you come back it has grown from other players doing shifts in the meantime.

**Points:**

Round goals settable by the server owner. Admins can override the goals to force a save at round end, or to force a save to not happen.

Unlocking max round players requires building the station larger.

Unlock max job counts by installing job/class/tool lockers.

Capture assets/asteroids/wreckage in one round and exploit it in another. This spreads the responsibility of success over multiple shifts and possibly different players. Also gives rise to different “styles” of shift. In a shift where there are no resources to exploit, the gameplay will focus on scanning and harnessing new resources for the next shift.

Should the team running out of stamina automatically trigger a no-save round? What is the threshold? Griefing WILL be a thing.

**How to determine goal difficulty?**

**Idea 1:** Number of crew stations determines goal difficulty. Self regulating, nice. Players can discuss and regulate future round difficulty based on the size of their organisational unit.

**Idea 2:** Set by server owner. Server owner is then like the dungeon master, or content provider, setting the feel of the server. Brings a third party into the meta. Might fracture the community and undermine the idea of putting your creation up for others, or it might strengthen it.

**Idea 3:** By progress. Tough to do because progress might be made without infrastructure, causing stagnation. Is stagnation ok? In the worst case the station needs to be restarted or an admin must come in.

**Idea 4:** By progress but random round events. Random events at round start, which would make it easier or harder to achieve the goals and get a save. These would be different every time, even after loading the same savegame. This gives players a chance to try a remixed version of a hard shift.
