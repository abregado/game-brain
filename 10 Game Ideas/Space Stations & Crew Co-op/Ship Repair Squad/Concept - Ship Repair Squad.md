Ship Repair Squad

**Multiplayer tile based, pseudo turn based roguelike.**

Player teams are randomly generated, then players select a character from the crew. There are always several more crew than players.

The game is round based. Player spawn in a small rescue ship and have a time limit to explore and repair a much larger ship.

The larger ship is procedurally generated from a seed. Then random components are damaged or destroyed, until the system determines that the engines are non-functional. Then some additional damage is done and time is passed to make sure the ship is in a highly non-functional state.

The systems aboard the larger ships are highly interconnected, so players will need to diagnose the problems very quickly.

This game is (to begin with) completely cooperative. All players are Engineers and have the same stats, skills and equipment.

Aboard the smaller ship is a replicator, which can create any items that are required but these cost a large amount of “currency points”. Winning a round will save the crew and their currency for future games. Each larger ship has a value, as do components of it. Players aim to either completely repair the ship to sell it whole, or they salvage what they can without blowing themselves up. Diagnosing components can only be done accurately by turning them on for a period of time.

The best outcome is to have the Engines onlined so you can fly the ship to a chop shop. This requires the crew to risk turning everything on, and also a lot of repair work.

Thematically the crew of this salvage ship have a limited time until the actual owners, pirates or the police show up to claim or destroy the ship.

**Addons**

- One or more players are flagged as traitors and must work to cause the crew to fail, or need to steal particular items of importance from the target ship.

- There are aliens or other hostiles aboard the derelict that need to be destroyed. Would need an entire combat system.

- More classes (starting equipment) and hierarchy to support larger groups of players.

- Mattermost integration for external communication. Unique player accounts created when the game starts.

- Longer job based shifts ala Space Station 13

- Actions per second are increased when not wearing a spacesuit, to promote players getting the Atmo back online.

- Random events such as

  - Meteor Showers

  - Pirates Docking

  - Random explosions

  - Rogue AI aboard

  - Your replicator malfunctions and cannot make some items

  - Power surge

Player Stories

Jury rigging machinery by uncoiling wire from a spool onto the ground. You run out a few meters short, so you steal some from another machine nearby, only to discover later that your crewmate was working on repairing that machine.

You reactivate the Central Cortex but there is a virus that seals all the doors and overloads the Information Hubs, generating a lot of heat. Several Crew are slowly cooked alive unless you find a way to solve it.

A Hull breach has caused the aft cargobay to de-pressurize. A connected section still has atmosphere but the door is jammed. Do you blow the door and risk damaging more of the valuable cargo, or do you spend time finding another way?

Your team has restored power to the bridge and information systems but the info panels cannot read anything from the rear engineering bay. There is however a high reading on the thermal scanners in that area. Maybe turning the reactor back on was not such a good idea...
