## My Player introduction

The aliens arrived without warning, immediately broadcasting their plans to every communication device on Earth. They were travellers, passing through our solar system on a long journey and they had a gift for us. One of their repair ships was damaged and they did not have time to repair or disassemble it, and so, they planned to leave it behind.

They landed shuttles on Earth, and deployed near undetectable drones which began scanning people. They were looking for humans compatible with their neural interface technology. While the earth governments squabbled about who had the right to take control of this alien technology, you and several others were silently invited by the aliens to receive the gift on behalf of your planet.

Of course you accepted.

Your trip up to the repair craft revealed the true scope of your responsibilities. The craft was huge, hundreds of kilometers long, and clearly only because it is folded for transit. It is a deployable dry dock, capable of repairing, refitting and constructing everything that the aliens need on their long voyage.

After a brief tutorial on board, in which you interfaced with the craft for the first time, the aliens left the solar system.

## My Superpower introduction

The aliens arrived without warning, immediately broadcasting their plans to every communication device on Earth. They were travellers, passing through our solar system on a long journey and they had a gift for us. One of their repair ships was damaged and they did not have time to repair or disassemble it, and so, they planned to leave it behind.

They landed shuttles on Earth, and deployed near undetectable drones which began scanning people. They were looking for humans compatible with their neural interface technology. All other attempts at communication with the aliens were ignored.

After several days, the shuttles and drones left the atmosphere, and then shortly after the solar system. Only the repair craft, now visible in the daytime sky as it heads for earth orbit, was left behind. Your advisors tell you it must be several thousand kilometers long.

You assume that suitable candidates have been found, but who are they?

## ChatGPT Player version

The message came without warning, a sudden broadcast interrupting every screen, radio, and device across the world. The voice was calm, almost melodic, and it spoke in every known language simultaneously. "People of Earth," it began, "we are travelers, passing through your solar system. Our journey is long, and our resources are finite. We have a gift for you: a repair craft of immense capability. It is damaged, and we lack the time to repair or dismantle it. We leave it in your care."

Panic rippled across the globe. Governments scrambled to interpret the message, leaders convened emergency meetings, and speculation spread like wildfire. Who were these aliens? Why leave such advanced technology behind? What would it mean for humanity?

Then the shuttles appeared—sleek, black vessels descending silently over major cities. They released swarms of drones, barely visible, that began scanning people at random. The world’s fear intensified. Some hid, some protested, and others waited, wondering what the drones were searching for.

For days, the scanning continued, and then it stopped. The shuttles lifted off, leaving behind only confusion and unanswered questions. That evening, as the world tried to return to normal, you received a message. It didn’t come through any traditional means—it simply appeared, as though spoken directly into your mind.

“You have been selected.”

The message was clear and personal. It told you that you were compatible with their technology and offered you a choice: accept their gift and the responsibility that came with it, or decline and remain on Earth. The voice was patient, friendly, and respectful of your decision. But how could you refuse? Despite your fear and uncertainty, you agreed.

That night, a black vessel descended directly to your location. You were alone when it arrived, its silent presence both terrifying and awe-inspiring. A hatch opened, and you stepped inside, guided by instinct and curiosity.

The shuttle whisked you away, ascending through Earth’s atmosphere. Along the way, you glimpsed others in similar shuttles, collected from their own homes or wherever they had been found. As the shuttles converged on the alien craft, the true scale of the ship became clear. The repair craft loomed ahead, a massive structure hundreds of kilometers long. Even folded for transit, it was an awe-inspiring sight, its dark surface gleaming with alien markings and countless points of light.

Inside, the aliens were waiting. They did not linger to explain themselves. Instead, they led you and the others to an auxiliary command center deep within the craft. Here, they began a brief tutorial—an introduction to the neural interface technology they had determined you were compatible with. The experience was overwhelming, like plugging your mind directly into a vast and unfamiliar network. But somehow, you understood. The ship, a deployable dry dock capable of constructing, repairing, and refitting alien technology, was now partially under your control.

The aliens left as suddenly as they had arrived. Their final words echoed in your mind: “This is our gift. Use it wisely.”

Now, you stand in the auxiliary command center, its walls pulsating faintly with alien energy. The systems hum with latent power, waiting for your command. Somewhere below, Earth spins in the void, its people unaware of what lies ahead. The ship is yours, and with it, the responsibility to shape humanity’s future.

## ChatGPT Superpower version

The broadcast shook the world—a single message delivered to every device, every screen, every station. "People of Earth," the calm, melodic voice began, "we are travelers, passing through your solar system. Our journey is long, and our resources are finite. We have a gift for you: a repair craft of immense capability. It is damaged, and we lack the time to repair or dismantle it. We leave it in your care."

Chaos erupted in the halls of power. Presidents, prime ministers, and premiers convened emergency sessions, their advisors scrambling to parse the implications of this alien transmission. Intelligence agencies sought meaning in the tone, the timing, and the alien’s words. Military leaders debated whether this was a Trojan horse or an unprecedented opportunity. And amidst the growing clamor, black shuttles descended over major cities, releasing near-invisible drones to scan the populace.

For days, the drones scoured the Earth, searching for individuals suited to their alien technology. The shuttles left as suddenly as they arrived, and while some celebrated their departure, others feared what they’d left behind. Then came the reports: select individuals—civilians—had been taken to the alien craft. They were not chosen by governments, nor by any earthly authority. They were chosen by the aliens.

This was unacceptable.
