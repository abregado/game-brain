# The story thus far

Our intrepid crew once again finds themselves in the black. This time they are aboard the NSS Noobship heading for a Nanotrasen shipyard. There they will deliver supplies to a different crew aboard the half constructed SS Atlas.

Nanotrasen has confirmed reports that the Syndicate has sent operatives to interfere with the construction, both aboard the NSS Noobship and the SS Atlas. Suspect everyone. Your best weapon against agents is to keep your fellow crewmates busy and in sight.

<th colspan="2"><strong>Step by Step Tutorial</strong></th>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<th><p><strong>Picking up items</strong></p>
<p>Left click on them in the world or in your inventory. This will move the item to your hand.</p>
<p><strong>Dropping items</strong></p>
<p>Press Q to drop the item in your hand.</p>
<p>Ctrl-Q will throw the item at your mouse cursor.</p>
<p><strong>Primary Interactions</strong></p>
<p>Many items have a primary interaction.</p>
<p>Press Z (or Y on a german keyboard) to activate the object you have in your hand</p>
<p>Press E to activate the object you are hovering with your mouse.</p>
<p>Left clicking with an empty hand also has this effect on many items which cannot be picked up.</p>
<p><strong>Secondary interactions</strong></p>
<p>Some items have a secondary interaction.</p>
<p>Alt left click on something in the world or in your inventory to activate its secondary interaction.</p>
<p><strong>“Use on” interactions</strong></p>
<p>When holding an item and left clicking on another item, a special interaction is made if one is available.</p>
<p><strong>Interactions menu</strong></p>
<p>Right click on an object to see a list of what you can do with it.</p>
<p>If you right click on a pile of items, instead a list of all items will appear. Right click on one of them to see the interactions for it, or left click to pick it up.</p></th>
<th><p><strong>Going into space</strong></p>
<p>Equip an Emergency suit, EVA suit or Hardsuit</p>
<p>Equip a Breath Mask to your face slot</p>
<p>Equip Mag Boots to your feet slot if you have some</p>
<p>Equip an oxygen tank to your back slot</p>
<p>Go to the Airlock</p>
<p>Equip an EVA helmet if using an EVA suit</p>
<p>or</p>
<p>Activate your helmet using the quick actions</p>
<p>Activate your oxygen supply from the quick action bar.</p>
<p>Activate your magboots from the quick action bar.</p>
<p>Wait until the inner airlock closes.</p>
<p>Pray</p>
<p>Go outside</p>
<p><strong>Moving in Zero-G</strong></p>
<p>If you can’t move, equip any item to your hand</p>
<p>Throw the item in the opposite direction you want to go.</p>
<p>Don’t let go of the wall and you can move freely.</p>
<p><strong>Toggle flashlight</strong></p>
<p>Find the icon on your quick action bar that has a small blue torch on it.</p>
<p>Click it to toggle light.</p>
<p><strong>A bunch of other stuff</strong></p>
<p><a href="https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Jumpstart"><u>Here</u></a> is a list of other small interactions you can try.</p></th>

## Security

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 50%" />
<col style="width: 50%" />
</colgroup>
<thead>
<tr class="header">
<th colspan="2"><strong>Step by Step Tutorial</strong></th>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<th><p><strong>Subduing someone</strong></p>
<p>Open your security belt by left clicking on the small icon in the bottom right corner of the belt slot.</p>
<p>Equip your stun baton in your hand.</p>
<p>Equip a set of manacles to your other hand.</p>
<p>Press Z (or Y on a German keyboard) to activate the baton.</p>
<p>DO NOT enter harm mode. The baton works without it.</p>
<p>Left click on a target until they go down. One hit should be enough. Two at most.</p>
<p>Press X to swap hands.</p>
<p>Left click on the target to apply the manacles.</p>
<p>Ctrl-Left click to start dragging the target.</p>
<p>Take them to a chair.</p>
<p>Left-click drag them onto the chair.</p>
<p><strong>Searching someone</strong></p>
<p>Restrain the target</p>
<p>Right click on the target and select Strip</p>
<p>A UI will appear.</p>
<p>Click on an item with an empty hand to remove it.</p>
<p>Have patience, it takes time.</p>
<p>Press Q to drop the item, then keep removing items.</p>
<p>Lock the items away for a later thorough search.</p>
<p><strong>Jailing someone</strong></p>
<p>Give the prisoner a Prison jumpsuit.</p>
<p>Open the brig door.</p>
<p>Ask them nicely to enter the brig cell.</p>
<p>If they resist, knock them out then drag them in.</p>
<p>Let them out after 1-2 minutes.</p>
<p>They get their stuff back except for contraband.</p></th>
<th><p><strong>Securing equipment</strong></p>
<p>There are lockable lockers in the Security area.</p>
<p>Left click to unlock, Left click to open.</p>
<p>Add any dangerous items you want out of sight.</p>
<p>Left click the locker to close</p>
<p>Alt-Left click to lock the locker again.</p></th>

<p>Cut dough into dough pieces with knife</p>
<p>Cook each dough piece for 5 seconds in the Microwave to make buns.</p>
<p><strong>Cheese</strong></p>
<p>Right click your container and select Spill contents or find a second container.</p>
<p>Right click beaker, set Transfer amount to 5u</p>

## Botanist

[<u>Growing plants</u>](https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Botanist)

[<u>Plants and their uses</u>](https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Hydroponics)

-\> Start by finding seeds - crates, vending machines , asking colleagues...

-\> Plant them into the planting trays  (You can also hide other objects in there)

The trays will show you how your plant is doing with the small light on the bottom. You can examine them for detailed information.

-\> Use a harvested plant with the seed extractor to get more seeds for it. Other than that you may try to eat it or process it, or just hand it over to others to process (banana peels are fun!)

### Tray Status Indicators:

|  | Red             | Dead, needs re-planting. Use spade to remove.            |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
|    | Flashing Orange | Weeds. Use hoe or weed spray.                            |
|   | Blue            | Needs water. Use bucket on the water tank and fill tray. |
|  | Green           | Ripe. Click or use hatchet.                              |
|    | Yellow          | Unknown.                                                 |

### Your Tools:

|       | Mini Hoe       | Removes weeds                     |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------|-----------------------------------|
|  | Plant Clippers | Takes samples (seeds) from plants |
|         | Scythe         | Sharp, helps harvest              |
|         | Hatchet        | For cutting wood and tower caps   |
|          | Spade          | Removes plants from tray          |
|         | Bucket         | Used to transfer water            |
|       | Plant Bag      | For transporting plant materials  |
