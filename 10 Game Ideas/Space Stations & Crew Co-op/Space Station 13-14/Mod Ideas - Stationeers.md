Much bigger viens spread out much farther

Ore detector that gives rough (within 1km) ranges and directions

Simple door with state output

Indicator light that does not need power but only signal

Sandbox tutorial missions

Respawn safely

Solve water

Solve food (basic)

Increase power storage to X (2 station batteries)

Spend an entire day without a spacesuit

Drive a rover to a place

Breathe like at home (earth atmosphere mix, need steel walls)

Moon Scenario.

No Ices found on the surface. Every few days, a random drop of Water (enough for players x need) is dropped. Other gases are dropped randomly as well. Distances increase over time, so that Rover gameplay is necessary.

Space suit has a decent liquid storage, and you drink automatically while it is not empty. This cannot be refueled except by putting the suit in a suit rack.
