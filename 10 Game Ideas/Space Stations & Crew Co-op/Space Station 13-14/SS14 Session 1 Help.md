# Introduction

### The story thus far

The intrepid crew of the SS Knightship head out into the black to search for treasure and glory.

Since SS14 is still under development there is far less content than the original game (SS13). However the most fleshed out system, the Salvage Magnet allows the players to summon space junk to explore and exploit. All roles will be doing this task, or supporting the others in doing this task. It should cause enough chaos even without traitors.

### Crew Goals

As a crew member of SS14 and an employee of Nanotrasen, your job is to:

- Survive the shift.

- Keep the station or ship running by completing a range of not-so-mundane tasks.

- Obey the Nanotrasen representative, normally the Captain.

If/When the Emergency Shuttle is called, it is your job to be on it. Nanotrasen values its employees so leaving any crew behind is considered wasteful of company assets and so should be avoided.

### Traitor Goals

***For the first few rounds I’ll disable traitors.*** You will have access to a range of special equipment to assist with both overt and covert operations. An important thing to remember is that the Traitor wins if they complete their objective and get away on the shuttle, regardless of if there are others on the shuttle as well. Remaining undercover until safely away from the station is advised. Winning without murdering a single person is the highest achievement one can strive for. If you MUST murder another crewmember, try to be creative!

### I’m new, please help

Install the latest build, run the launcher and select “Direct Connect to Server”. I will post the IP on Discord before the session.

Civilian Jobs are the best place to start, but feel free to select any job you want. If you select Assistant (you will spawn with a gray jumpsuit) then find another player and ask for a task.

Once in the game just remember, SS14 is not designed to be easy to play. It will take a while to learn the controls. First up, [<u>read this wiki link</u>](https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Interactions), and then just experiment in your area. Everyone will be a newb when we play, so don’t feel pressured to perform.

Once you know how to move around and interact a little, try to complete some of the tasks associated with your role (See below).

Shift left click on something to look at what it is.

Most gameplay is done by holding an item in your hand and left clicking on another item.

Q drops an item, Ctrl-Q throws an item.

You can’t harm someone directly unless you activate the Harm mode (by pressing 1). Negligence however, puts everyone at risk.

# What if I die?

Don’t worry! You went and got a medical scan before you died, right? If so then the medical staff can clone you.

If not, then you can opt to respawn as one of the various options based on the map. A maintenance drone, animal or personal AI assistant are generally available.

When none of these are available, the admin can create new ones for people to use.

# What if I get caught breaking the law?

Space law protects you! A bit. Generally the worst punishments should be 1-2 minutes in the brig, or a demotion to a menial task for a longer period. Equipment may be confiscated, meaning that you need to source new tools once released. If you feel you were mistreated by the security staff, report them to the captain after you are released.

[<u>https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Space_Law</u>](https://wiki.spacestation14.io/wiki/Space_Law)

# Roleplay Tips

Read the server rules when you join. You can ignore the rules about Out of Character chatter since we will be using Discord voice.

Harming other players, even as the antagonist, should be avoided if possible. In the case of self defense, Space Law tends to protect you a little.

Interact with other players, roleplay, and don’t immediately try to murder someone who doesn't do as you ask.

Interaction with others, being visibly helpful and roleplaying is your primary weapon against the traitor, who will have to play along with your roleplay else draw attention to themself. Hiding in your department alone will get you murdered silently.

Groups of three are the safest, as there is only one traitor.

As for roleplay, remember that Nanotrasen Corp Human Resources screens all employees thoroughly for signs of Space Madness. Do not attempt to roleplay as an insane or mentally ill crew member, or attempt to excuse your actions with “my character is just random sometimes”. If you are high on space drugs, well that gets you off the hook... see the Chef or Hydroponics expert. Respect Space Law and it will protect you!

# Game Tips

You can close doors behind you instead of waiting for them to auto close with Left Click.

Shift-Left Click on things often, especially on other players. You might not be able to use the info right away but you can look at the logs later to find any suspicious details to report to the captain.

Many held items, especially tools, have multiple modes. Hold them in your hand and press Z to cycle modes.
