## Short Descriptions

### Captain

**Pros:** Heaps of Access and Equipment. Almost completely in charge. Gets to decide what is researched. Total power during Red Alert and Martial Law.

**Cons:** Still answers to the Lieutenant, has too much to do, Target of much suspicion. Until things go bad nobody really has to obey.

### Lieutenant

**Pros:** Heaps of Access and Equipment. Almost completely in charge. Has control over job assignments. Pet Dog.

**Cons:** Still answers to the Lieutenant, has too much to do, Target of much suspicion. Until things go bad nobody really has to obey.

### Cargo Tech

**Pros:** Can order whatever they want as long as they don’t get caught. Access to Mining Hardsuit. Can go into Space without asking.

**Cons:** Everyone wants something from you, tricky to get away with anything illegal.

### Bartender

**Pros:** Lots to do, if you want. Not very affected by the chain of command. Lethal weapon at spawn. Pet Monkey. Meat Grinder in Kitchen.

**Cons:** Basically no access and no power to tell people what to do.

### Janitor

**Pros:** Essentially unlimited access to station. Immunity to slipping over. Solitary Job.

**Cons:** Basically no real access. Nobody cares what you say. Solitary Job.

### Station Engineer

**Pros:** Really interesting to play. Lots to do. Gets a lot of tools and equipment. Engineering Hardsuit.

**Cons:** None.

### Security Officer

**Pros:** Gets to think they are in charge. Everyone fears them. Lots of weapons. Get to arrest and search people. Donuts.

**Cons:** Difficult for new players. Need a lot of skill with the Interface.

### Detective

**Pros:** More interesting than the Security Officer. Lethal Weapon to start with

**Cons:** Even harder for new players.

### Scientist

**Pros:** Lots of fun stuff to build and do. Private section of the station where you won’t be interrupted. Lots of interaction with other players.

**Cons:** Expectation that you report what you are doing to the Lieutenant. Responsibility when things go wrong. Lots of Blame, even if it wasn't you.

### Medical Doctor

**Pros:** Interesting gameplay. Lots of interaction with other players. Lethal equipment everywhere!

**Cons:** Can be boring early on. Players need to drive their own gameplay early on. Can be too hectic once things go bad. Lots of pressure.

### Chemist

**Pros:** More stuff to do than the Medical Staff. More access to even more dangerous stuff.

**Cons:** Mostly has nothing to do. Best when mixed with Science or Medical staff job. Is confusing for new players.

### Clown

**Pros:** Nobody cares. You will probably be reassigned. Cooler hairstyles and clothes.

**Cons:** Nobody cares. You will probably be reassigned. Self Loathing.

### Assistant

**Pros:** You will definitely be re-assigned to a job AND be given another player to help you

**Cons:** Round start tends to be boring until the Lieutenant reassigns you.

# Captain

## Where is my Department?

You have special access to Command and the E.V.A room. Command is secure from everyone except the Lieutenant and from here you can access a lot of station information and security cameras. E.V.A is used to store important items and the stations space suits.

You have access to almost every location on the station. and will spawn in the Command.

## What powers do I wield on the station?

The Captain can access almost any equipment on the station. Your most valuable item is your ID Card. If it is stolen it can be used by the thief to access the station freely. Although you can access any equipment on the station, there is rarely a reason why you should be using it. Order a subordinate to do the work for you.

You have the power of authority. Your word is law, but must be handed down through the chain of command. You still answer to Centcom (The Military) and Nanotrasen (The Company). Refusing a written order, stamped by yourself and the Lieutenant is punishable by demotion or brig time. In a Red Alert scenario, it is punishable by execution.

## What should I do?

Firstly always greet the crew in Arrivals. If any staff have already gone to their stations, make a point of going to greet them personally.

The second most important thing is to call the Shuttle once the time limit is up.

Messages will arrive from Centcom and Nanotrasen and it is your job to inform the staff of any situation you think is “need to know”. Also the productivity of the station is your responsibility, so check regularly to see if everyone is doing their job and assign tasks as needed.

Patrol the halls regularly and interact with the crew.

Ask for written reports.The lieutenant should be generating these and filing them in his office. He will ask you to stamp them regularly.

Choose specifically what gets Exported through Supply, and what gets developed in Science.

Make sure Engineering gets the Power turned on.

## Who will I need to interact with?

Everyone on the station needs to obey your orders, but only through the Lieutenant and their Department head (if one is appointed). Make written demands and have your Lieutenant stamp them. You cannot punish someone for disobedience unless you have a written and stamped work order. If you are polite, the staff may do as they are asked without such permissions.

Remember to delegate tasks to the staff! You dont need to do the dirty work, nor will you have time!

## Other Jobs you might have to do

If there is no Lieutenant, Security or Engineering staff you will need to complete these roles, or assign other staff to those areas. Any other jobs are below your pay grade and should be assigned other staff. If there is a Lieutenant, then filling these roles is not your responsibility.

## How can I cause trouble?

If you are a Traitor then you are very lucky. As a Captain is it much easier to be covert in your mission. Try to avoid outright murder to achieve your goal.

Framing others will keep Security busy and also move suspicion away from you.

You can invent an Emergency and call the Emergency shuttle to end the round early, once you have achieved your goals.

You have access to the space suits, but remember you have no reason to be in space.

Framing the Lieutenant means you gain complete authority over the station.

You have access to all of the weapons on the station but remember you cannot openly display them unless Code Red has been initiated.

## Who do I answer to?

You cannot refuse an order from Centcom (The Military) or Nanotrasen (the Company). If the Lieutenant finds you disobeyed a direct order from them, you can be removed from duty.

# Lieutenant a.k.a The Military Bureaucrat

## Where is my Department?

Your office is located next to Arrivals.

## What powers do I wield on the station?

Your office contains everything you need to give others access and to reassign them to different jobs. Your word is law, assuming you have a written order stamped by the Captain. You are the direct superior to everyone except the captain. Just remember that most of the staff are not Military and may refuse a direct verbal order.

You also count as Security and may openly wield weapons during Code Blue and Red situations.

## What should I do?

Fill staffing holes, generate reports, give orders.

As the Company representative it is your job to ensure the station is productive. When the staff arrives, check if there are any important roles unfilled and reassign as needed. The station needs at least one Engineer, one Scientist and one Doctor.

Assigning a new job is as easy as modifying the staffers ID card to give them more access. Also be sure to update their Job Title.

Next you need to assign work orders and keep everyone working. If verbal orders are refused, then create a written request and have the Captain stamp it.

After that you need to have each department fill out work reports. Get these stamped by the captain and file them in your office. The Company demands paperwork at the end of the shift.

Enforce the staff having their Medical trackers (in the PDA) active, and during Red Alert have them turn on their trackers (or force them to receive tracking implants).

Dont let your Stamp get stolen. If the Captain gets it, he has total control.

Keep an eye on the Captain and Security. If they are traitors then it is probably up to you to stop them.

Most of all, Keep your Dog, Ian alive!

## Who will I need to interact with?

You need to keep contact with all departments while taking care of the Captains orders. Security and Efficiency are the most important factors. Keep an eye on everyone and interact often with each staffer. Ask a lot of questions.

Report in with the Captain and Security regularly, both so he knows you are safe, and to keep an eye on him.

## What others jobs may I have to do?

If there is no Captain then you assume control of the station. You may need to staff Medical or Security if there are no available staff, but other roles are beneath your pay grade. If there is a crime committed, and there is no Detective you will need to take care of it (or assign Security to it).

## How can I cause trouble?

Be friendly with the Captain. With his help you can order every other crew member to do as you wish.

Try to get the Captains ID or his stamp. Framing him will make your life a lot easier.

If you accuse the Captain then you will need the help of Security.

Sabotage the station and blame Engineering.

Frame the Cargo techs by planting items in their area while they are out mining.

Give out work orders which implicate other departments, or that are conflicting.

Demote, or give Janitor access to a mid-tier crew member and then order them to clean the airlocks (which you remotely control). Flush.

## Who do I answer to?

The Captain, Centcom (the Military) and Nanotrasen (the Company). The Captain could order you to stamp something, but it would be dangerous.

# Station Engineer

## Where is my Department?

Your department is Engineering and Telecommunications, as well as all of the Maintenance areas on the station. You have a good excuse to be in any part of the station, and if you have a written and stamped work order, other staff have no reason to deny you access.

## What special powers do I have?

Wow. Where to start? You have control over Power and Life support in the entire station. You have access to detailed information regarding these things. Aside from the Captain, you have access to the most hiding spots in the whole station.

Your equipment includes a Hardsuit which is the second best armor in the game and allows you to go into space whenever you want. You dont have a reason to be wearing it inside the station however.

Your other tools allow you to construct almost anything with the help of the Cargo Technicians.

Everyone will want to be your friend.

## What should I do?

Firstly you need to start the Thermal Generator in Engineering so the station does not run out of power.

Equip some Optical Meson Scanners so you can detect damaged walls and components.

Next you will need to check for broken equipment in each department (a random selection of stuff will be broken at round start).

Respond to maintenance requests from other departments. Unless the order comes from the Lieutenant or the Captain you can prioritize (or ignore) as you see fit.

Make sure nobody gets access to your tools or Hardsuit. If someone finds a bloody screwdriver they are going to blame you! Lock things in the secure lockers in Engineering.

Get yourself Scanned at medbay before you go EVA. This means you can be cloned if you die and your body is not recoverable.

Put out any fires that start.

## Who will I need to interact with?

Mostly engineers stick with each other. They don’t need to interact with the Captain very often. Rare components may need to be requested from Science, and Chemicals might be needed from the Chemistry Lab in Medical. Other supplies need to be requested from the Cargo Techs.

**What others jobs may I have to do?**

None. Your job is very important and the Lieutenant and Captain should not distract you from it.

## How can I cause trouble?

Your options are quite limitless.

You can disable Life support to each section manually.

Cut off power to any area (or the whole station) and blame it on temporary maintenance.

Pump deadly gas into the Life Support.

Turn the Thermal Generator into a huge bomb.

Hack into communications, reroute secure Captains comms to your own headset.

Hack the comms and send the Captain and Lieutenant conflicting orders from Nanotrasen/Centcom. Watch them try to demote each other.

Disable the station Gravity Generator.

You have a reason to go into the Lieutenants Office, Security and the Command area.

Only you and the Janitor have access to the Garbage Grinder. Its a good way to get rid of evidence.

You have access to every airlock and have a reason to be outside. Evidence in space is as good as destroyed.

You can freely carry the tools needed to open any door on the station.

Sabotage doors so they crush people, or so modify them so you can remotely open them.

Order a Syndicate Stamp. Write your own orders and use them to gain access.

Order a Voice Changer. Yours is one of the few jobs where you will need to wear a gas mask regularly.

Order Thermal Imaging Glasses. See through walls. These look like your Optical Meson Scanners which you should be using anyway.

## Who do I answer to?

If one of the other Engineers has been promoted to Chief Engineer then you answer only to the Chief Engineer. Otherwise the Lieutenant or Captain.

If one of the other Engineers has been promoted and the Lieutenant or Captain tried to give you a verbal order, you can tell them to go see your supervisor. You cannot be punished for refusing a non-direct order.

# Security Officer

## Where is my Department?

You have access to the Brig and Security areas. It is in the north part of the station.

## What special powers do I have?

Weapons and fear! You have access to Flashbangs, Handcuffs, armor, but no Lethal Weapons. What more do you need. Your goggles make you immune to flashbangs.

## What should I do?

You should patrol the station, keeping a sharp eye out for traitors. Use shift-left click to examine a target.

or…

Stay safely in the Security room and use the Cameras to search for criminal activity while ordering more Donuts from the Bartender.

No matter what you do, you need to obey the Lieutenant and Captain, and use non-lethal force as non-lethally as you can.

Once Code Blue has been called, you can perform random searches of staff. They should comply. If not you are allowed to use handcuffs or stun batons to incapacitate them while you search them.

Break up fights. If two parties are being violent then stun both of them, cuff them and then call for a Medic.

Inform the Lieutenant of any oddities, unless you suspect him.

If the lieutenant orders someone brigged, then you are in charge of doing that. Place the criminal in a brig chamber and then set the time lock on the door. You should only brig a staffer for a few minutes at a time. Brigging someone who is naked is considered bad form.

After someone has been brigged, you need to take their items back to their department or store them in a Security locker.

## Who will I need to interact with?

As many of the staff as possible. If you have not seen one of the crew for a while, they are probably trying to avoid you.

Report in with your superiors often so they will know if you go missing or stop responding.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

If the Lieutenant doesn’t like you, he might ask you to mop the floors while on patrol. If there is no detective and the Lieutenant is busy you may be assigned to check over a crime scene.

## How can I cause trouble?

As a traitor you do not have much covert power but you do have WEAPONS!

Civilian staffers will be afraid of you because you can instantly stun them and pretty much do whatever you like.

You can steal important items while performing random searches.

If you meet a witness in an area alone, you can stun them and then stuff them in a locker.

Try to complete your objectives without being noticed, then cause an emergency and coordinate the evacuation efforts. “Accidentally” leave someone behind.

Syndicate Soap is your friend!

## Who do I answer to?

The Captain and the Lieutenant as well as the Chief of Security if one someone has been promoted to that role.

# Medical Officer

## Where is my Department?

Your department is Medbay which is located on the East side of the station.

## What special powers do I have?

You have access to lots of useful drugs and many sharp things. You also decide who lives and dies on YOUR station.

The Medical profession requires a lot of knowledge but it can be fun to learn how to operate on a patient “in-situ”.

You also have access to a Cloning machine...

## What should I do?

Collect and equip a Medical Hud and Health Analyzer.

You have a reason to stay safely in Medbay. Attend to patients who arrive or are brought to Medbay.

Respond to calls for emergency aid. Be sure to take some First Aid packs with you.

Frequently check the Medical Monitor, report staff who have it turned off, and clone those that are showing Dead.

There are many space diseases and genetic conditions you can cure. Nanotrasen (the Company) prefers staff to receive check ups and treatment while on duty.

Request other staff attend the Medbay for a checkup. If they refuse, get the Lieutenant to order it. He cannot refuse Company Policy.

You can also take genetic scans of living staff so they can be cloned and revived if they die and the body is not recoverable. This is of extra importance to Engineers and Cargo Techs. Ask them to come for a scan ASAP. Better yet, get the Captain to make it a standing order that nobody goes EVA without this.

## Who will I need to interact with?

Pretty much everyone. Anyone can request paramedical assistance, but be careful if they request it on a non-public frequency.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

If there is no Chemist you may be asked to take on this role. Also if the station is quite peaceful you could request Botany access and take up Gardening.

## How can I cause trouble?

Deliberately clone a brain damaged body for someone.

Lie about a perfectly healthy persons condition and prescribe limb amputation. There are a lot of conditions in this game that can be solved with surgery!

Once someone has been diagnosed it is your sworn duty to help them, and you are company bound to take care of it during the shift! Sleeping drugs will help your patient agree.

There is a syringe gun in the Medbay storage. Learn how to use it.

Poison pretty much anyone you like. Donuts are a popular target.

Use the sleepy pen to fake someones condition. Prescribe surgery or immediate cloning either of which go horribly wrong.

## Who do I answer to?

The Nanotrasen Health department and the Lieutenant. You are not military so obeying a verbal command from the Captain is your choice, unless a Red Alert is called.

# Science Officer

## Where is my Department?

Most of your time will be spent in Science. You will also need to know where Supply and Chemistry are.

## What special powers do I have?

You are the whole reason everyone else is here. The Captain and Lieutenant are under strict orders to make your research as painless as possible.

The station has a bomb making lab which is your responsibility.

Other research will result in you having access to many high tech items.

## What should I do?

If its your first time being a Scientist then try to make a bomb and destroy the test target by firing the bomb out of the Launch tube.

Write reports for the Lieutenant otherwise he will want to come in and see what you are really doing.

Its your job to make bombs and dangerous stuff. Its also your job to get them tested. The Medical officers may be interested in helping. The Janitor and Clown might be suitable candidates.

Look up a guide for research. There are many paths to follow. Build an AI or create a Cyborg (it will need the brain of one of the crew). Build Mech suits or new weapons. Test them.

Request needed items from the Cargo Techs. You might need the Lieutenant to sign off on those requests.

Ask Medbay or the Chemist for all sorts of dangerous chemicals. You have a good reason to obtain hull eating acids!

Get yourself scanned by Medbay incase your body is not recoverable… no seriously.

## Who will I need to interact with?

Most of the time you will be fighting with the Cargo Techs who don’t want to get blamed for any of the stuff you order. Make friends with the Lieutenant. Watch the Janitor every time he comes in the lab.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

None. Yours is the most important job on the whole station. The Captain might receive orders from Centcom or Nanotrasen to change your research topic, but thats about it.

## How can I cause trouble?

Umm.. did I mention you have access to a Bomb Lab and the most private area of the station.

The options are too numerous to mention, so just figure something out.

**Who do I answer to?**

The Head Scientist if one has been chosen, otherwise just the Lieutenant and SCIENCE itself.

# Cargo Technician

## Where is my Department?

Both the Supply area and the nearby Asteroid Belt will be your home.

## What special powers do I have?

Access to a mining hardsuit and a reason to go into space. Also complete control over what gets delivered to the station. Guns, drugs, bombs, you name it.

## What should I do?

Familiarize yourself with what can be ordered.

Once someone orders something, you can call the supply shuttle. Remember you can order whatever you like but the Lieutenant will probably check the logs.

The Captain and Lieutenant can see when a shuttle has been called. They might be suspicious if they did not sign off on that.

Get yourself a genetic scan at Medbay incase you die while Asteroid Mining.

Suit up and go Asteroid Mining. The materials you collect can be used by the crew for Science, or they can be sold to buy more guns and drugs.. I mean Important Supplies.

Set yourself to “Walk” when outside or you might slip and disappear into the void.

## Who will I need to interact with?

You will want to interact with nobody. Everyone will want to interact with you. Make some friends and call in favors. Even the Captain and Lieutenants requests for items can be refused unless they both agree.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

Pretty much anything. Your job is pretty low in Importance. Don’t piss the Lieutenant off unless you want to be a Janitor.

## How can I cause trouble?

You can order whatever you want and forge the logs using an Emag.

Sometimes items are delivered by accident or items do not arrive. On your watch this might happen more often.

Request a second Space Suit for your Cargo Tech friend. Be sad when you tell the Captain he didn’t make it back from your Mining Expedition.

The Cargo bay is quite private…

You have access to tools just like the Engineer. Actually you can do pretty much anything he can.

Poison the food before you deliver it to the Bartender.

If someone is in the docking area when the Supply ship arrives they might be crushed instantly with no evidence.

Other staff need to frequently write requests with a pen. Offer them to use yours.

You can put corpses or stunned staff in crates, close the lid, then ctrl-click to start dragging the crate. Makes it easy to get to the Grinder.

**Who do I answer to?**

The Lieutenant and only him. If one of your colleges steps up and is promoted to Supply Boss then stop listening to the Lieutenant.

# Bartender

## Where is my Department?

You have been assigned to run the Company owned Bar and Restaurant on this station. It is located at the centre junction in the middle of the station.

## What special powers do I have?

A shotgun, a meat grinder and all the cooking prowess on the station. Your kitchen and freezer are also the most private areas on the station.

Oh and a very nice Suit.

## What should I do?

Prepare meals for the Staff. Either make them to order, or have them ready on the counter.

Control the consumption of alcohol on the station.

Deliver food to the Scientists when they request it.

Clean your bar

Ask the Cargo Techs about a source of good meat

Sabotage the vending machines, which are providing very poor quality food.

Keep your pet monkey Alive.

Grow things in the Garden if you are bored.

## Who will I need to interact with?

The Captain and Lieutenant might ask if you have seen one of the staff. Everyone needs to come to your bar regularly to eat so keep your eyes open.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

Theoretically you might be forced to double as the Janitor but only until everyone starts to get hungry.

## How can I cause trouble?

You have a shotgun. Its hard to conceal but its one of only three deadly weapons on the station.

Poison the food supply.

Make burgers out of someone then feed them to everyone else!

Rig the bar doors to close and lock at the same time as the room fills with flammable gas. Invite everyone to a party. Know the detective is a smoker.

**Who do I answer to?**

You’re about as civilian as it gets. There isn’t much reason to obey any order unless it is written and stamped by both the Captain and the Lieutenant. Obey the monkey.

# Janitor

## Where is my Department?

Your department is located in a small closet across from the Lieutenants Office.

## What special powers do I have?

SPACE JANITOR POWERS! Your job is to keep the station clean, and seeing as nobody every even notices the dirt, grease and dried blood, you need to patrol all sections of the station looking for mess.

You have Galoshes(Gumboots) which make you immune to slipping over. Any area you have mopped recently will be slippery and anybody running nearby will slip over.

You also have access to the Grinder for disposing of… garbage.

## What should I do?

The Janitor is quite complex and you will need to read a guide on using his Janitor Cart.

Empty bins, mop floors, clean up spills.

Nobody has any reason to refuse you access to their department. Report anyone who refuses to the Lieutenant or the Captain immediately.

Nobody expects you to report in unless you have a problem.

Ask Chemistry for new Space Cleaner on a regular basis.

Pick up anything that isn’t where it should be (or where you feel it should be)

## Who will I need to interact with?

Preferably nobody, but unfortunately everyone.

## What other Jobs might be asked to do?

You’re already doing the job nobody else wants to do. The fact you volunteered should protect you from being reassigned.

## How can I cause trouble?

Clean floors that were not messy. Forget to put up “Slippery when wet signs”

Your cart has quite a large inventory for stolen items

Clean the floor in front of every airlock. Accidently cycle airlocks at seemingly inappropriate times.

Plant items anywhere you like.

Wear gloves so there are no Fingerprints

Get an Agent Card, so you can go anywhere.

Dispose of evidence in the Grinder.

The Thermal Generator and Armory need cleaning too!

**Who do I answer to?**

The Lieutenant and Captain but they are probably too busy to bother with you anyway
