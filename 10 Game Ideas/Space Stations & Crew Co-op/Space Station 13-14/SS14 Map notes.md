## Role specific Orders

Everyone

- Contraband should be reported and delivered to a Department head as soon as you find it.

- Non-contraband items should be brought to the Quartermaster for stocktake.

- Use the ATVs when possible to increase efficiency. Ask the HoP for keys.

- Always shift-click people when you see them

- Suspect everyone.

HoP

- Appoint someone as Security Intern

- Constantly check up on people to make sure they are not traitoring

Chief Medical Officer

- Do the Chemists job if there is no chemist

- Keep an eye on the HoP in case they are the traitor

Bartender/Chef

- Keep a steady pile of food available for the employees

- Complain about customers to keep the HoP busy

Janitor

- Clean up spills and trash

- Sort items that have been flushed into the disposals system

- Check Disposal units for unflushed items

- Report bodies contraband

Tasks

- Setting up the Antimatter Engine

- Keep the engine fuelled

- Plug in the north solar array

- Expand the solar network

- Seal off construction areas and fill with atmo.

Syndicate agent

- Use the syndicate uplink you received to read about syndicate items

- Order in antagonist items.

- Antagonise.

- Don’t get caught with those items.
