## Zombie game
A level based zombie survival game. Each level is one location. After a number of days the story pushes the player to pack up and leave. The next level begins as the character arrives at their next safe house. 

Each location has a narrative that is completely optional to discover and some modifiers to the set of options the player has so that the same strategy does not work every time.

## Quest system
Quest system where every time you complete a quest, a point of progress is added to all other quests you have. These progress points make the quest harder, and eventually cause it to be unfinishable. If a player visits a quest location after many progress points have accrued, then they find only bones. Going to a quest location before the quest begins means only finding what was there before. Some Quests may only be findable after enough others have been failed.

## **Kobold Earth Program**

A “Real Physics” ™ game. 2d game where you design craft which fly using rocket boosters. The catch is that these are dropped into underground caverns, must land and offload their workers which will collect resources and then you need to fly the craft back up to return to the surface.

Players need to decide how much fuel, cargo and passengers to take down, and how many they can afford to return with!

Phase 1 would just be a playable level or two, with a tool for level design. Players compete for online highscores.

Phase 2 would be adding in some sort of economy gameplay where resources need to be collected.

## **Generative Genetic Algorithms for NPC generation**

Measure fitness as a function between resources used and Human player presence.

Outputs include.

- Designing a new unit type

- Sending an attack

- Withdrawing all units

Inputs include:

- Number of Player Units

- Distance of Player Units

- Number of Friendly Units

- Number of own Units

- Units of resources removed by Players in Radius

Place Enemy Bases and their detection radius so that they overlap. Moving close to one witll set off multiple others.

Each base has a diffeerent Neural Network. If a Base has been declared “failed”, either through death or through No being Fit, then it breeds with a neighbouring Base, producing a new Neural Net with similar properties to both.

Random mutation is only added in when a Base is destroyed.

OR

If a base is destroyed, then its programming overrides nearby bases with lower fitness. To truely destroy an evolutionary strain you need to isolate it first.

Bases start on a medium fitness and can go up and down. If fitness gets too low then the AI suicides and a mutation takes its place. This way human players can breed and farm stupid AI, as long as they do not cause too much damage or have a large impact on the area around the base.

## **Cryopods - A spawncapping/permadeath system**

There are limited cryopods in the game environment. Each represents a player space on the server. This is effectively a Player Cap. However, every player can see who is active on the server and how long those people have been inactive for.

If a player has not logged in since 3 weeks then everyone can see that their cryopod status is RED. other players can make a choice about RED cryopods.

Choice 1: Renew the cryofluid, which extends their storage for one week. The pods status is changed to GREEN and nobody can effect that player anymore (for another week). This takes ~5 minutes of idle gameplay. Each time this is done, the effect of replacing cryofluid is reduced by half until it is less than 5 minutes bonus time (less than it takes to renew the fluid).

Choice 2: Dispose of the body. This takes much longer (~2 hours). The player in cryo has their character and records deleted and will wake up in a new body when they log in next, if there is available spaces. The gameplay should be several sets of 5 minute gameplay spread over the 2 hours. At each stage the player must wait a set time to begin the next session, and if they come back too late the process is cancelled.

When a new player logs in and there are cryopods with red status, the new player will replace player in the pod which has been idle the longest.

When a new player logs in and there are no red pods, then they are told to come back later.

Obvious microtransaction addition is cryo fluid that keeps your pod green for a longer time without intervention.

Ship Repair Squad

**Multiplayer tile based, pseudo turn based roguelike.**

Player teams are randomly generated, then players select a character from the crew. There are always several more crew than players.

The game is round based. Player spawn in a small rescue ship and have a time limit to explore and repair a much larger ship.

The larger ship is procedurally generated from a seed. Then random components are damaged or destroyed, until the system determines that the engines are non-functional. Then some additional damage is done and time is passed to make sure the ship is in a highly non-functional state.

The systems aboard the larger ships are highly interconnected, so players will need to diagnose the problems very quickly.

This game is (to begin with) completely cooperative. All players are Engineers and have the same stats, skills and equipment.

Aboard the smaller ship is a replicator, which can create any items that are required but these cost a large amount of “currency points”. Winning a round will save the crew and their currency for future games. Each larger ship has a value, as do components of it. Players aim to either completely repair the ship to sell it whole, or they salvage what they can without blowing themselves up. Diagnosing components can only be done accurately by turning them on for a period of time.

The best outcome is to have the Engines onlined so you can fly the ship to a chop shop. This requires the crew to risk turning everything on, and also a lot of repair work.

Thematically the crew of this salvage ship have a limited time until the actual owners, pirates or the police show up to claim or destroy the ship.

**Resource Collection Game**

Player just need to explore and find resources that spawn in the world. When they find these resources they can claim the resources and call in a friend to collect them. The scanning equipment and collection equipment are mutually exclusive.

Players must be near the resources to get their payment for the claim.

Once claimed all players can see the location and monsters start to attack.

Some zones are hard enough to need two claimants.

When the collection player arrives it still takes some time to extract the resources and then all players are flown back to base.

Players get three slots to fill. Collectors get to choose: movement speed, extraction speed and Max cargo size. Scouts get movement speed, terrain countermeasures, defender bonuses. Military get damage bonus, ammunition magazine size, movement speed.

Everyone pays for modules on a rental basis. They are kept until the player returns. The number of modules available is shared between players. Players can spend resources to increase the public stocks of modules or spend lots to buy one for themselves, which costs per use.

Real world evolution triggers: Evolution paths are based on real world data. Time of Day, day of week, stock market values, ect.

Automatic Maintenance: Once the tick storage is full, monsters will use ticks on their worst stat.

Some affectors are long term affectors which have timeouts of several hours or days. Affectors also have statistic requirements for acivation.

Difficulty to evolve decreases over time. As a monster ages and has more ticks spent on it, it becomes easier to complete the evolution challenges.
