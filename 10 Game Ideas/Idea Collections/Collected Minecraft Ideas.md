# Eternal Night

Using Coros Zombie Awareness mod, set up a scenario where the players must defend a castle eterally from waves of zombies.

**Adventure!**

This gamemode would be played in Adventure mode, Normal difficulty.

**Nightime Eternal**

use a daylight sensor to automatically switch time back to night if the players manage to somehow break the system. doDaylightCycle is set to False.

**Server safety**

doMobDrops and blockDrops will be set to off to stop the server getting inundated with object updates and to help FPS.

**Food issues**

There will be unlimited food available from buttons inside the castle. Some potions and other consumables will also be available.

**Equipped for life**

keepInventory will be on, iron weapons and armor will be provided at game start. Ingots for repair will be made available throughout the game to replace broken equipment and to repair items at an anvil.

**Weapon mayhem**

Several weapon mods will be active to give the players some interesting options for defending the castle.

**Fight, dont Build!**

There will be no blocks available to be placed into the level, except TNT. Blocks broken by rocket launchers and TNT will be broken forever. The Castle has a bedrock foundation, so eventually the monsters will be able to get in.

# Bringing people together (for better or for worse)

## How to bring people together in Minecraft

1.  Limit space and resources

2.  Highlight player activity

3.  Force cooperation through game rules

4.  Common server goal

5.  Personal glory through public stats

## How to achieve these points?

- Maps need to be small enough that a player can walk across it within 30 minutes.

- Players can see points of activity on the hud/use an in game item.

- Open world pvp. Only safe zone is near to the world origin.

- Hunger is more powerful

- Animals attack in herds and are more efficient at running away. Players must work together to hunt. In fact, all animals should fight back, even farmed ones. This will also increase the usefulness of ranged weapons and complex farming systems.

- Plants die if not harvested in time.

- No unlimited water supplies

- Each biome has one primary resource and one that doesn’t spawn. Primary resource is in large veins to promote pvp and player density.

- Trees take much longer to grow. Deforestation without replanting should be a serious concern unless the world is centered on a forest biome, in which case something else will be unavailable.

- Inventory reduction. Refer: Dont Starve. Being forced to pick items you NEED over ones you want (or the versa, which is much more exciting). Most likely players can only have their toolbar to hold items, and one extra row when wearing no armor. This also makes tools take up more space. Stack sizes should be reduced to ~10.

- Keep the resources on the server. Chests spawn when you log off with your resource

## How to keep resources on the server

No food consumption when offline

chests spawn on the ground when you log off with your resources in them.

Chests in totem areas can only be opened by the totem owners.

Making a server with Economy that WORKS.

Things that reduce value of items in minecraft

- retooling costs are zero. You can have all the tools handy, or make them out of basically free materials if you need one or two uses.

- No localization of markets. Everything is available everywhere

- transport/logistics of items is safe and easy

- Items have very little “weight”. Anyone can carry many many items.

- Crafting is instant and 100% safe

- The “fuel” for doing work is food but it is very abundant and not required very often

- There are no commodities with significantly higher/lower “wealth density”

Some fix ideas

**The cost of “work”**

higher tool costs. Iron/gold/Diamond tools require iron rods instead of wood sticks

Lower tier tools have less uses and have a chance to not drop the item you are breaking.

Passive Wear and tear on tools (damage over time just from keeping them in the inventory)

No punching resources, or it never yields a block

Food needs to be eaten more regularly when online

Food goes bad to prevent players stockpiling. Perhaps the more food carried the faster it goes bad.

preserved food.

**Logistics of minecraft**

Less inventory space: possibly only the 9 toolbar slots!

Less stack sizes: most blocks only up to 8

A logistical utility cost to wearing armor: fills 2/10 inventory slots while armor plate is worn.

Chests have substantially less capacity and cant be placed very close to eachother.

**At the speed of a crafting bench**

Crafting always takes time like a furnace. Players must return later to collect items.

Opportunity for items to be stolen as they finish

Smelting takes much longer, but the same resources.

Ingredients must be added in stages: with the waiting time this also promotes players thinking ahead to make each part.

**Free resources and food**

Animals fight back, even passive ones. Encourages use of range weapons for hunting or machines.

Dirt and cobble have low drop rates.

Uncooked/tier 1 foods give very low amount of hunger back, higher chance for sickness (delayed)..

**Localization of resources**

Large biomes (very large).

Most land has no resources under it.

Small “islands” that are slightly different have resources underneath.

There are also fake biomes with no resources or very little under them

Rent paid to the server for the area of claim/towns.

Each resource only spawns in a few biomes

Players cannot dig/place blocks in the wild

Players must use Towny to “make a claim on an area”. This requires 3 players to work together.

Gives others a chance to come and attack the party for their goods.

Ores only stack to 3. Ingots stack to 8.

As it is difficult to move large amounts of resources, so minecarts will be useful

**Player interaction and anti-NPC activities**

Trades can be direct P to P or using IC2 tradeomats.

Long distances and high prices for items not available locally may cause players to seek out NPC villages and possibly even to develop them.

Some players may benifit from killing off villagers who offer particular trades.

NPC villages should help keep price ceilings for items as if a player monopolizes a product too heavily, others will seek out villagers for trading.
