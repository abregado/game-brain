Bylong Valley Brainstorming

- Space Game Local Markets!

- different contaners for different cargos

- container module slots

- Multiple erosion events

- calc rivers, erode: cycles

- river addition at junctions

- regions grouped by catchment

- dwarves as guardians between two conflicting races

- giants building with rolled masisve stones

- Rolling rocks over surfaces to change their size and surface

- building objects only by pushing rocks

- pressure mechanics in a dam building game

- runoff required as well tokeep water level up

- rolling resistance and weight used when moving items

- material strengths, crumbling bad materials

- ferry materials across water

- log trains

- build a pyramid/huge stone structure/dam game

Digging game with water seepage. Pump water out somewhere else to keep decneding

Player councils organsing content for other players

"city builder" in a town. Zombie survival. structures are already in place and you can just build walls between them to create a safe zone. dismantle some buildingas to build barracades. Search outside the safezone for supplies. Info only when survivors return to your safezone. Send people out and they might not return.

Build a town around a factory to house the workers, provide the services, ect. Population fluctuates as the factory does better or worse. Layoffs, boom employment, ect all possible. Different worker types have different expectations.

Quarry manager. Cut, move and shape stone. Discard useless stone, but remember everything must be put somewhere.Having something removed either means selling it or paying someone to take it away!

retail shop with one of each size of clothing in each style. Customers pay at the shop by order it from an internet store to be delivered to their home. Store has less stock value. Customers just pay to come on and try on samples. In the lobby customers can look through a catalogue on a touch screen to see if there is anything they like, and even order items without trying them on.

Ice Tribals stages. First stage is all about meat and hutning. Ice structures are already set up with all the meat industries and enoiugh space for your tribals to sleep. The player is shown how to send tribals out on a hunt. Creatures encountered should be yeild much meat and a small amount of bone and furs. Player is introduced to meat industries. There are not many tribals to use so the creatures must also be very easy to fight. Perhaps some sort of land seal. The carnivorous enemy should be small and fight in packs. Easily scared. Kill one or two of the pack and the rest will flee, their corpses yeilding very little usable materials and no meat. The players goals should be to collect enough meat to last the next two stages (quite easy with so few tribals).

After the first segment the player is shown how to load cargo onto his tribals to be transported to the next stage. Everything expect the meat is pre-packed and locked. The player only need load the meats then end the stage. His destination is also pre-selected.

The second stage should be about fur and the fur industries, as well as building your ice buildings. First up the player should be shown how to build enough space for the tribals to sleep, explaining the warmth mechanic. The creatures should be interesting and yeild both meat and fur (mostly the latter). Both primary and secondary industries for fur are explained during this section. Clothing, shelters and bags are sewn and equipped onto tribals. The warmth mechanics is reexplained showing the effect of clothing.

At the end of this segment the player loads this tribals completly. Sets must be taken to ensure all of the tools and shelters are loaded before additional furs and meats.

The third stage is all about bone, tools and large carnivores
