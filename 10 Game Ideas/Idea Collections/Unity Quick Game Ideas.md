# 16.10.26

4x round based coop

~20 minutes per round

Events and reading between rounds

Real time during round

Players actively try to stay at equal power level

Players cannot be knocked out

Asynchronous enemies

- Horde, destruction team

- Ancients team

Ancients act as terrain and event sources

Combat replay at end of round

Each empire wants different things

Screens and flow

- Empire select

- Last turn battle recap

- Events presented to each player

  - Players make choices about which events to engage with

  - These provide short term goals

  - Some events are generated by requests sent in previous turns

- Players send requests to each other “secretly”

- Round starts

  - Realtime gameplay

  - Combats get queued up but not resolved

- Battle recap. Everyone sees everyones battle outcomes

- Turn starts new

What do people actually do?

Allocating budget and effort

Named characters are good for story telling

One-two players always fighting want to always be fighting for the XP

Grouping planets together for best combos

No best grouping for all situations

Rearranging eco for current situation is the main eco

Tension between keeping enemy at bay and discouraging the populace from becoming warlike

Need an exploration mechanic.

- During enemy downtime, explore beyond where they attack

Enemies come in waves with random length between them. Possibility for wasted effort and preparation

Assume the basic level of competency is reached and have the players decide the rest. What did the empire neglect and what did they focus on.

# 22.01.24

**Follow the leader… into the dark**

Inspired by 12 orbits

Many players on one screen.

Multiplayer top down walking (dodging) simulator

Camera keeps everyone in view

Press any button on any controller to join

That button moves you forward

“Forward” means, towards the player in front

When the player in front presses the button, they advance along the path

Along the path are obstacles that require timing to get past

Sometimes a button will be on the path, the leader will step to the side to stand on it

Then the next player overtakes and becomes the leader

There is a “health clock” that ticks down.

The leader collects nibbles that lie on the path to extend the clock

Once the nibbles spawn, they disappear after a short time, driving the leader to go faster

If anyone is damaged, the health clock is reduced by a small amount.

Challenges include:

- Moving platforms

- Doors closing and opening

- Spikes

- Monsters that walk along a path

More players means a larger health clock maximum

Theme Ideas:

- Minecarts on a track

- Climbers connected by rope

- Robots with an extension lead connecting them

- Caterpillar protecting its vital organs

- Adventurers in the woods/dungeon (marching order)

BLOAT:

Sometimes there are two or more buttons

Sometimes the group gets split into multiple groups, enabling 2/3/4 way split screen.

Places where two or more players can get attached, so one of them controls movement and the other has an ability like:

- Moving backward and forwards

- Creating bridges to the side for other players

- Jumping over gaps (which swaps the roles on the other side)

- Illuminating areas

- Shooting

- Freezing bullets

- Shield

- Dash

Everyone has their own health, but only the players in the middle can heal

# 20.06.22

## Regiment movement

Drag line to place regiments of units. Units move from current formation to new formation intelligently. Regiments can have dynamic or fixed width. As troops die, the others push to the front to keep the regiment solid. Replacement troops are routed from the rear map edge to the regiment and then arrive behind the regiment to fill it up.

How to control regiment facing when only drawing the frontline?

- Idea 1: Drag a line for the frontline then click again to select facing.

- Idea 2: the direction of the dragged line dictates the facing. Show the backlines as well to indicate this.

- Idea 3: Units don’t have facing, but only the front units facing the enemy can fire. Player needs to deal with the problem.

# 17.12.21

## State blocks system. 

Each buildable object is made from state components. Each state component can exit to a different state. Objects can have as many states as you like. Use a state registry component to reference exit points.

## Topdown engine idea

Context sensitive NButton supported system. Each object at any given state has one action per button, and a linked verb to make that possible. When approaching, calculate which buttons are possible and show the player.

The players inventory is searched to see which verbs are available. Only show buttons the player has the correct verb for.

## Item selection for topdown. 

Each bumper cycles what is held in either the left or right hand. To draw two handed objects, hold one bumper and press the other to cycle.

Aim/attack/use with the corresponding trigger.

Items in inventory get flagged with "ready" and the hand you want to use it in.

## Camera follows carryable item

Local coop camera where the camera follows an item, not a player. The item can be carried by a player.

Players can leave the circle of light but their speed is reduced as in the Emerge beacon system.

# 24.03.14

## Single screen, Single unit RTS control wit pathfinding.

Touch the screen to give a waypoint, the single unit moves there avoiding the obstacles in the way. Use either a tile grid for collision or movement nodes. Its a lot harder than it sounds :P Good for RPGs, City Builders or squad based action games!

BONUS POINTS: multiple units that move together but avoid one another

## Drag and Drop Inventory

5 "slots" at the bottom of the screen. An object to gain a new random item into your "inventory". slots around the rest of the play area. Using touch and drag to place objects into slots, and single tap the "deck" to draw a new item.

BONUS POINTS: Inventory slots can be limited in which items they can hold or free to take any item. Your main inventory should be typeless, and the slots in the play area should be strongly typed. Imagine this as an inventory screen with "backpack", "weapon", "armor" ect slots like in Diablo. Or even better theme it as an engine block with bolt on mods, each item could add more slots of different types, so you build it from the block out (like a puzzle, or like that car game from the 80's with the goddamn aqueduct races)

## Portalesque Shooter - or make it 2d for mobiles

Single Room, FPS (Easy to do in Unity). three types of object exist in the room. Connection Points, Blockers, and You. Left click Connection points to start a link, then click another connection point to link them together (straight beam). Connection points cant link if there are blockers in the way. Link all points together to win each level.

BONUS POINTS: randomly generate levels that are always winnable. AKA have the game generate the solution, then add in blockers randomly that do not block the solution, then have all the links turn off and place the player in the game.

## Tilt Racer

2d Top down style racer (GTA1 style). Tilt the device to move the car along the bottom of the screen. Obstacles "fall" from the top of the screen at ever increasing speed. If you turn the car too quickly then it spins out and costs you points/time.

BONUS POINTS: if your touching the screen when tilting, you could drift

## Tilt Racer 2

The car is in the centre of the screen and will continue to accelerate over time but only in a straight line. Instead of moving the car by tilt, use the compass to rotate the level underneath the car. EG - car always drives north in the real world and you turn the device to move the world. Could be super trippy and fun. and really brainfucky :D No car physics, just the world moves perfectly and the car doesn't move from the centre of the screen.

BONUS POINTS: interesting physics (actually not doable in one weekend I assure you), lap counter, lap times, scoreboard.

## Upgrade unlock game

Socket system: your character/base starts off with limited slots for items.

Each item has a number of customization slots (a sword might have blade hilt and pommel)

Each modification has a number of upgrades that can be performed

Each slot has a type

Objects have a number of types they can go in

Each mod slot has a type

Each mod can go in a number of mod slots

Handling of prereq other mod quantities.

Timers do an action when they are clickked and counted down. Timers are shown on the sode of the screen.

Actions give/take/modify commodities, or replenish events

Commodities have no properties, only a quantity and description. They are represnted as an icon in an inventory area.

Events use/give/take items and reward the player with commodities. These have a larger picture and several optikns. Often they will be unavailable until a timer is avtivated. Events are represented on the centre "map" as buildigns or quest markers.

Items have modifications which fit into slots. There are a number of properties which are modified by the mods. Each item has a type which effects when it can be clicked. Items also have a cooldown and a commodity cost. They may have more than one actitivit associated with them. Items are represented with a graphic on one side of the screen
