Sacrilege in games

Hiding encounters so that it requires the player to play a level once just to know what to expect.

Offering quality of life upgrades as end game content.

Giving the player an easy too do but boring opportunity, which if taken will allow that player to ruin thier own fun.

Offering/ producing dlc while in early access

Pixel hunting gameplay

Need to Get 100% to progress.

Always online single player game

Notifications that you should play

Offering in game rewards for watching ads

Offering ads as an ultimatum (watch or we will throw this huge reward away)

Killing the player with something they cannot see or interact with without warning (cutscenes included)

Taking control away from the player unexpectedly

Making the character do something while control is taken away from the player.

important items being undroppable

Quest givers would give you no option but to take this quest

New game+ as Paid DLC
