Trading Mechanics in their raw form are there to provide:

- A motivation for players to traverse the space

- A way for players in increase wealth

Problems with common trading mechanics include

- Predictability

- Repetition of simple easy tasks

- Promoting monotonous play

- Require no skill to master

- Based on Luck

The great thing about Trading Mechanics

- Players feel like they are part of a larger system

- Interaction between players (in multiplayer examples)

Abstract version of Trading:

Players reach one location and are shown several other locations with a “profit” rating if those locations are reached within a time limit. The profit is based on the current player “cargo size”. Once a route is completed, the profit for that route drops to zero and slowly builds up again.

Medium-Abstract Version:

At each location, players see a cost to move to some other locations. They also see the profit from moving between those locations, the time-limit and the risk. Player equipment affects all of the numbers. Profits for a move are reset to zero when the route is completed and slowly build up again.
