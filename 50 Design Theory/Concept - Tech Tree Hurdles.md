Concept – Tech Tree Hurdles

Aim: To design a tech tree mechanic that simulates real life uncertainties with researching new theories, while still allowing metagaming. Players should be encouraged to continue research into an area, but the results and time frame should never be certain. Rewards should still be proportianal to resource expendature to assist with game balance.

Example of Problem: Current 4x games with technology trees are always "sure things". Players know what they will be getting either becasue the game shows them before resources are committed, or becasue another player (or manual) has made this information available.

Solution 1: Hurdles. Each technology has a hurdle tech (Theories) which must be researched before any practical applications can be chosen. Only a subset of the total number of practical applications become available to the player when a Theory is completed.

Pros:

- Players can still move through the technology tree along pre-planned paths

- uncertainty about the actual effect on gameplay will make players weigh up the risk more carefully

- High cost of Theories means that players will not often be event in various disciplines of technology.

Cons:

- Players might feel cheated by the RNG if they dont get the Practical Applications they were hoping for

Solution 2: Burst research. Players pump research resources into the technology tree categoies. These accumulate in a "research bank" which as it grows has a higher chance of paying out. Players can never know when the points will pay out, and my chose to start on a different category before one category has paid out. Any categories with points in them hold their points indefinatly. When a bank pays out the player can spend all of the accumulate points in the relevant technology tree category. The longer a bank stays closed, the more points a player will get as a bonus at the end.

Pros:

- Players can still choose exactly the results they want from the tech tree

- Sometimes research will not pay out for a long time and the player may choose to switch and hope for a faster payout on the other category.

- Uncertainty about WHEN you can spend, instead of about WHAT you can spend it on

- Players will spend points on the best tech for them AT A RANDOM TIME. Good strategy will allow the player to buy techs that are needed before the next payout.

Cons:

- Balance may be an issue

- When only a few categories exist players might end up with many many points stored and no payout

Solution 3: Tech distribution. As Solution 1 players must spend technology on Theories to unlock more choices. However before the game starts one technology per tier per Category is completly unavailable to all players in the game. Then when a player finishes a Theory he may choose a number of the Practical Applications. The next player to complete this Theory can choose from the remainder and so on. Each technology can only be gained by a single player. Players wont know which technologies have been picked until they finish the Theories.

Pros:

- No RNG shaftage

- Tech race mentality should encourage interesting gameplay (fastest player gets what they want)

- Players can still pre-plan their target technology path

Cons:

- Balance becomes paramount, if one tech is better it will always be chosen first.

- Effective Counters are important. Scissor paper rock effect may be very strong

Solution 4: Category Investment and Linkage. As players invest in a category they recieve a discount on future technology in the same category. The more resources invested in one category, the more discount is given in that category. As players research up(down) the tree, links to other categories become known, transferring some of the highly investest categorys discount across to a different category. These links are not part of the technology tree and are randomly generated. Effectivly random opportunities for a player to start divesting.

Pros:

- players early game will follow more focused research methods, creating unique entities

- pre-game choices still very important

- late game players will have choices between researching counters to their enemies, or following high payback paths not of their own chosing

- Different gameplay every game. Replayability

Cons:

- Rare circumstances may see one player highly (dis)advantaged

Solution 5: Player chosen pre-determination. Players must select their path through the techtree before the game starts. They may research these in any order during the game however sometime they will hit a Block. These Blocks are placed randomly and can only be cleared by researching other technologies. Each player will hit several Minor Blocks, clearable by researching a few other techs, and one Major Block which means the player has to research EVERYTHING else he selected that is available before the block is cleared.

Pros:

- Major Blocks are self balancing. Low tech one will be more annoying but will also be easier to clear (less techs underneath)

- Promotes players to speculate where the blocks will be. No point researching a powerful combo of techs if the block might come before the vital tech.

- Gives intellegence power. If you know where your opponents blocks are you can work towards effective counters.

Cons:

- Some combinations of blocks may be completly unclearable

- player annoyance when blocks are at the top of a long chain of techs (hard to clear and limited immediate benefits)
