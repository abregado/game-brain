Changes and Expansion on ideas

Losses must be acceptable. Either troops can be revived after battle, or a few losses is expected during each mission.

Perhaps remove save options during Battlescape

Randomise level if you retry

Makes for a very IRONMAN style gameplay

Hiring Personnel

The ability to select a prerequisite value of skill for new personnel. Hiring higher grade staff takes longer (more failed applicants). This would negate the Hire/Fire approach to getting the best troops at the start.

Dogfight complexity

Simply make the aerial combat more interactive and tactical. Posisiton, range, speed, altitude ect. Multiple aircraft in one battle (could be done in Xcom with careful positioning). Ability to choose battlefield by holding fire until UFO not over cities.

Manufacturing Complexity

Retooling of Workshops. Bonuses to long runs. Setup Costs separate from Material Costs. Ability to synocronize several runs to manufacture components.

Research

Reverse engineering alien tech into human designs. Alien equipment must be modified to be usable by humans. A reason to be researching multiple techs at once. Random breakthroughs. Perhaps a maximum number of scientists working on a single project OR retooling time for labs.

Basebuilding Complexity

Add strategic elements to base design. Corridors, multilevel bases, Bulkheads, forcefields, security rooms.

Transfers/Logisitcs Complexity

Can display links between major cities. Deliveries can be tracked as they move through the system and can be ambushed by aliens. Deliveries come by Air or Truck. Can pay for military escort or provide your own. Cargo planes/trucks purchasable items. Can see where items are being built and shipped from. If large orders are made, stock comes from several locations.

Visual Changes

Aliens employ technology to render downed craft invisible to view from above. Ships do a flyby of the crashsite or entrance before landing. Satellite Imagrey provided if cloaking fields damaged in crash.

Mission Types:

Send Interceptors to escort civilian airlines

Aliens caught in the middle of diabolical acts:

Abducting humans

SOS missions: Attacking military bases and convoys

New Content Ideas

Cybernetic IMPLANTS!

Stat Boosts, maybe basic equipment can be implanted as well (medkit, psi-gear, ect). Not all soldiers willing to undergo surgery.
