So far 5 that I can think of

- **Timers/Countdowns:** As seen in Farming games. Certain offers or actions take a certain amount of time to become active or refresh after use

- **Tick System:** Each action “costs” some ticks. Ticks regenerate over time. The players can only make so many actions during each day. Many older browser games used this system. A newer example would be Farmvilles Water

- **New Level Seeds:** Each day there are new levels for the player to try. These levels are only available on that day as the RNG seeds are based on todays date. As with Spelunky Daily Challenge

- **Highscore Rollover:** Scores are shown for any 24 hour period. If players wish to stay on the scoreboard they need to play regularly. This also encourages players to play heavily in bursts.

- **Dailies:** Quests or Rewards that can only be completed/gained once per day. These include login rewards and “Free spins”. A lot of free-to-play games have these.
