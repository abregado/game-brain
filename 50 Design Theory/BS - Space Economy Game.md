Problems

- Fun progression is at odds with inflation

- Games tend to have boring base level gameplay

- Automation invalidates player action

- Automation is not primary gameplay

Questions

- What is limited?

  - Player time

  - good positions

  - Tight clusters of the correct things

- Which parts cannot be automated?

Nice to have

- Always let my implementation have an effect on the outcome, even in small ways.

Concrete Ideas

Temporary Deployment and return

Exact deployment pattern makes some difference, even if not much

Goldilocks zone numbers

Stabilizers that keep locations where they are instead of despawning them
