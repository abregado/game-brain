Concept – Feature – Technology Tree Base fields (Building Blocks)

This is an idea relating to the very basic things that al technology are built from. For humans they would be something like: Plastics, Electronics, Petrochemicals, Robotics

More advanced ones might be: Nanostructures, Quantum Assemblers, Frictionless Surfaces, ect

Biotech ones might be: Carbon-steel Invertebrates, Myomer Fibres, Super-optic Strands

Combinations of these are used as the foundation for each “Tech Field”.

Nuclear Weaponry might have: Radioactives and Neutrontubes

Possible areas to think about:

Transport

Energy Generation

Material Sciences

Tools

Communications

Medical

Data manipulation

Observation technologies

Manipulation technologies
