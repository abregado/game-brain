Done let your sinks become a Faucet.

- In 7 Days to Die, Zombies are used as a resource drain and pressure, but towards the end they seem very easy and give a lot of random resources. In our test game Zombies would give much more resources than it takes to kill them.

Warn players of new threat development

- 7 Days to Die uses sounds to alert the player that new types of Zombies are in play.
