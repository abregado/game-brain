### Goals

- Teach mechanics for later content

- Get players to be active and visible

- get players to group up

- Have players generate more content for others

- Fun!

- Content for all player ages

- A more living world where the NPCs are not just lumps of HP and resources

### Considerations

Players need stimulation and the feeling that their choices affected the outcome.

content not just to be involved in, but to make the world feel alive

The worst outcome should never be going backwards

Multiple levels of success are nessesary

pacing should be high but short

If multistage then rewards between stages

Options to take more risks on every mission

only very new players need to be protected.

Equipment loadout suggestions

Missions for all load outs

Detect load out/ layout and offer mission based on that

If travel time, then allow multiple tasks at the business end

Each mission/task builds in difficulty

secondary effects on other areas of gameplay, all players should want/not want the content completed even if they dont want to get directly involved.

Completed missions from one faction effect mission difficulty/rewards/availablity from another faction?

Completed missions in one area make another area more dangerous, but the missioned area less dangerous (overall increase in danger)

### bring a friend

there should be good reason to bring a friend every time you play. even if the content is easy to solo, bringing a friend should help and you sould be rewarded for it.

if extra rewards are given then the content must be designed in a way that all players need to be active participants.

examples include:

- all players need to collect the mission items. these are instanced so only one drops per player.

- reward are given instages to nearby players

- 

### Mission content ideas

repeat task: player is told to visit a location. Pressing a button causes a wave to spawn. Defeating the wave allows the player to complete the mission, but also press for another wave. Waves build in difficulty until the player has to return to home base.
