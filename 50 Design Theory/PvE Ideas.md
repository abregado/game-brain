Pve rat ideas

Multiple groups of hitpoints. Hits are allocated randomly, or the ai can protect partially damaged sections. This creates enemies that hilevel players find hard to kill, or at least as equally hard as younger players find them.

Population

Labor, work potential, income, tax

Infrastructure

lubrication, bandwidth, access to material, coverage, technology

Experience: an ever increasing number which unlocks new player tools

Data, Strategy value, Learning, reputation, technology, research

Health: a value that if zero, the player dies

Integrity, reputation, armor, thickness,

Food: a consumable that staves off penalties

Battery, charge, fuel, energy, momentum, speed, invisibility, reputation, oxygen, burnables, heat, sanity, steam, letters
