### Aims of these mechanics:

- Create intense links between the game and the player

- Give the player a sense of persistence

- Immersion through attachment and ownership

### Problems with the mechanics:

- Repetitive tasks

- Traditional XP based levelling

- Grind

- No real customization

- Linear progression

- No real failure state

### Best points about these mechanics:

- Collector mentality is fuelled

- Exploring evolution trees

- Feeling of owning one’s successes

- No feedback that a player is playing suboptimally

- Players intrinsically explore the virtual space very well

- Plenty of mechanical reasons to revisit old locations

### Super Abstract Version: 

- The player moves through a virtual space, and as they do so, they collect orbs of different shapes and colors. There are visual and audio hints as to where in the virtual space specific orbs will appear.

- Here orbs are simply stickers being placed in a sticker book. Once an orb is found then it won’t be found again.

- The player can see collections of similar orbs on a “Collection page”. Hints as to associations can also be found here.

### Medium-Abstract Version:

- Same as above but the player must also cause certain orbs to change by completing tasks. Combinations of orb must be brought to specific places on the map in order to make them change. Clues are given based on sound and graphics of both the orbs themselves and the landmarks.

- This is a good candidate for finite random level generation, as only one of each combination needs to be generated. Each player can have a unique solution for finding all orb combinations.

- Here orbs are not only collection items, but also tools for activating landmarks and other changes.

### Non-Abstract Version with different Theme:

- Huebot v2.0

- Player traverses a maze, collecting crystals of different colors

- The player brings these crystals to altars, each which needs several crystals

- The Crystals become combined

- Three tiers of crystal exist, each requiring multiple of the earlier tier crystals

- Altars become like factories for later tier crystals, players must revisit the locations often.

- Tier 2 crystals allow landmarks to be wired together, automating the process of collection.
