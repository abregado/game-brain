# PvE Content Delivery Systems

This document contains ideas for delivering content to player where the primary opponents are NPCs and the rewards are not provided by other players. The goal is to design systems which allow the player to feel as they have choice over their progression, while keeping the difficulty as close to their skill level as possible.

## One Liner Summaries

- Dynamic Scaling: Offer increasingly difficult missions to players to push them to take greater risks. Step difficulty down after each failure.

- Tiered: Offer a range of difficulty levels regardless of known player capabilities

- Exam: Infrequently offer hurdle content that is much harder than previous missions, these must be overcome to progress to more difficult missions but they may be turned down at no cost.

- Basket: Have all missions on the table, but players cannot complete the same mission again until they have completed all missions.
