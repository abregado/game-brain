### Aims of these mechanics:

- Keep the player playing constantly

- Overload player with feedback

### Problems with the mechanics:

- Repetitive tasks

- Traditional XP based levelling

- Grind

- No real failure state

- Players will burnout instead of finishing on a high

- No reuse of areas

- Little to no player skill

### Best points about these mechanics:

- Short action/reward loop

- Feeling of progression is very strong

- Popular genre

### Super Abstract Version: 

- Clicker games.

- Player clicks, gets more numbers, and can then click faster

### Medium-Abstract Version:

- Player completes quicktime events or timing puzzles to make their numbers go up

- The player spends numbers on specializing in making certain puzzles easier

- Puzzles get harder as game progresses

### Non-Abstract Version with different Theme:

- Shooter game, similar to Raptor (idGames).

- Player completes levels and unlocks new choices for their ship as they go

- Each level briefing gives information to help the player choose what the load onto their ship

- Ship upgrades are unlocked so the choices are persistent.

- The quantity and quality of upgrades increases over the course of the game.

- Old upgrades should not become obsolete.
