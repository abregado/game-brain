Concept

Feature

Technology Tree

Research and Manufacturing interdependencies

Ways that manfacturing and research can be interdependant:

- Prototypes need building

- Concept (labour) → Testing (resources) → Improvement (labour) → Testing (resources)

- Mfged items generate “tech points” when BUILT

- Mfged items generate “tech points” only when USED

- Items statistics and characteristics unknown until used in the game

- researching any field CONSUMES mfged items from the same field

Wikipedia lists the process of research as

- Observations and Formation of the topic

- Hypothesis

- Conceptual definitions

- Operational definition

- Gathering of data

- Analysis of data

- Test, revising of hypothesis

- Conclusion, reiteration if necessary
