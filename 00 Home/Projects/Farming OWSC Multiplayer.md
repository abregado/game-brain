
Volume builder for structures
Daily cycle, return to bed to progress
Each day has one type of weather, and it holds for the whole day.
In the midgame the player gets a weather forecast
Machines get loaded up during the day, but only increase their progress when a new day starts

When leaving the base players have to select a subset of tools for the day.
Tools are added to Suits, and each player selects one suit to take.
After being used or modified, suits take one day to recharge.

Collectables are per biome and weather and each one needs a particular tool to collect
An NPC comes every few days to collect collectables.
Until the NPC arrives, the player needs to keep the collectables stored in a special machine (Terrariums? Stasis Chambers?).
One machine is needed per collectable kept safe

Machines make progress at the end of the day if they have all their fluids satisfied and items that they need.
Crafting cycles take 1-5 days
Almost all machines need a fluid.

Some machines produce fluids, but these require an item.
Fluid provider machines generally give 3-5 fluids with 1-2 types.
Each machine requires either wall or roof space, always External sections.
Pipe connections are on the outside of the building, item inputs are on the inside.

Resource deposits in the wild have upgrade slots, which take consumable items from the suits config to upgrade.

Players drop off a series of Harvester machines, leave them for a few days, then return to either restart or collect the machines.
The harvesters collect resources each day, but the player needs to come to manually trigger a launch which flys those items back to the base. 
The base has a Collection pad, and the resource wait there to be collected by the player when they return home.
