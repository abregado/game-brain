BS - 4x problems

**General 4x problems**

Complexity hidden several UI layers deep

Military action requires reflexes or save scumming

When at war, no attention left for eco management

Consequences for a military slipup are very high

Game is actually several competing games

Expanding from a single point into an empty map automatically creates at least two games

Expansion gameplay is always transient

Early game micro overload turns into easy midgame

Many small choices each that isn't very effecting of the outcome

Expansion is always better

Progress is always up, forward, more. Always looks the same.

No opportunities for comebacks.

Soft endings (except Rebellion)

Peace and war too binary (peace with bigger, war with smaller)

**Problems that Stellaris specifically demonstrates**

Most racial choices are tiny and meaningless

At the scale the game is supposed to be played, the interface is terrible

The universe is too rich, expansion choices do not matter

Minimal opportunity cost

Pandering to two audiences
