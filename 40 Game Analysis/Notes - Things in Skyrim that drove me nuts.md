Things that drove me nuts in Skyrim

First 6 hours of gameplay. Reached High Hrothgar in this time. Killed the tutorial guards, some undead nords, LOADS of wolves, a single chicken and a dragon. Got killed by villagers, trolls and giants repeatadly.

- 

- Wolves were almost the only thing I killed for 5-6 hours

- Lack of warning if something is REALLY dangerous (trolls and giants early on)

- Cannot accept or refuse quests, dialouge simply ends with an affirmative assumption

- Disparity between character vocal colume and distance to character. Far away voices seem to override some close by voices.

- Melee combat feels too disjointed for first person, ranged combat feels inaccurate in 3^(rd) person.

- Dragons are pathetically weak (due to level scaling?)

- Level up system was so transparent that I didnt notice it was there. This is fine except you must provide some input else you get no Magika/Health/Stamina upgrades or perks. Transparent or Visable, not halfway between!

- Characters repeat themselves very often

- Model/Animation polish (quivers were noticablly terrible)

- NPC reactions/relations to you seem unrelated to anything (You save the world and nobody notices, characters still ask you for their lvl 1 slippers after you have saved the world, or the opposite, they seem to be your best friend and trust you fully when they meet you for the first time!)

- MENUS! Oh god the menus are terrible!

- Quick select. Could be under menus but I raged as often about this as the menus

- No decernable feelings of being epic or powerful...

- Killcams were more annoying than interesting

- Restoration magic seems infinitly moer powerful than potions and other healing methods (are there any more?)

- Forced entry to some dialogue

- Inventory is so unrealistic. Weights, number of items, weapons, ect.

- Lack of guidance in crafting (no lists of "used in")

- Wilderness areas do not feel so. You can travel over the longest distance without fear of running out of supplies

Fixes:

- 3^(rd) person cinematic camera for important conversation/dialogue. No more jumping on the head of the Jarl while he is explaining your epic mission.

- Killcam viewpoint further away or using fixed 3^(rd) person cameras mounted in the levels

- Killcam focus is behind the arrow/projectile in ranged/magic killcams

- Triggerable killcam moves. Sometimes you dont want them triggering in the middle of a bunch kills

- More variety of "easily replaceable foes" in early game. Even a different colored wolf would be nice.

- NPC's say their lines once then never again (or very rarely)

- Speach volumes are more modified by distance from the character but also by importance (quest link conversations should be louder than general rabble)

- NPC's should not offer your menial tasks once the story line is complete.

- Holding down a quick select should pause the game and show you the combination you are about to switch to.

- Quick select combinations of abilities or just one

- Inventory should include number of items, item size as well as weight

- Gold should be heavy. Gems used for large wealth. You can only have as much wealth as you can carry (until you own property)

- Early dragons should not be killed, instead either yeild to you or run away.

- To kill a dragon you must trap it. Nothing that is 1000 years old would have a disregard for its own life!

- Every quest should have an "accept now/later" option. One category in the quest log could have locations of quests awaiting acception.

- Option to start with fixed (high) level

- a visual mechanic to denote the avatars fear of a creature. Even a gui icon next to their name will do.

- Automatic level-up of health/magicika/stam based on a profile

- forced level ups as soon as they happen

- Perks must be chosen during level ups. NO SAVING THEM UP!

- Dragons should begin to tag team you. They are supposed to be very intellegent beings.

- More temporary bonuses as reasons to come back to town and to help push through tougher areas (Preparation for difficult areas)

- Some indication that the next area will be more difficult than previously (strange mood/feelings when entering difficult areas)
