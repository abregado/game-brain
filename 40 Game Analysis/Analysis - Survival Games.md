## Survival Elements

### Temperature

Does not seem to be a big deal at all in the Starter Biome

### Food

This is the strongest starting demand. The food clock ticks down very slowly unless the player uses Stamina. Farm plots are not too difficult to get in the early game and crops do not need to be tended. This allows the player to feed themselves easily without any effort from passive farming very quickly.

Until farms are running, the player needs to search the surrounding area for food plants. Since food drops quickly when using Stamina, this leads to the player walking slowly around looking for plants.

### Sickness

To fend off sickness, players need to craft medicines from combinations of plants. As long as the player keeps one of each medicine handy, they will not have issues with sickness. The good thing is that sickness seems to only be the result of player action, not bad luck.

### Oxygen

I have not played enough on an airless planet to know how this really works. Bae oxygen seems to just be having enough power and having sealed rooms.

### Fuel

Fuel can be made from a rarer ore type (Promethium) or from Wood. I never ran out.

### Defensive pressure

I have not been attacked yet.

### Primary Activities (first 5 hours)

- Travelling between 4 points (3 resource patches, base)

- Building the first Hover Vehicle

- Getting access to Promethium

- Fighting Spiders

### Crafting

- Some recipes are mobile craftable

- Long craft times

- Return to base to craft higher tech items

- Auto intermediate crafting

- No linked inventories, so players need to move items between machines manually

### Blueprints

- Player can build anything they already blueprinted without having a constructor, base or even the right components.

- The player only needs to have enough resources stored in the “Factory gui”

- The player can store unlimited resources in the Factory gui

- Factory resources are accessible from anywhere
