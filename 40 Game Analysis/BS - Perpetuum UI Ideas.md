### 

### Market and Trading UI

Show players which resources are missing from each location.

Display items being sold here that are being purchased for a higher price elsewhere

Set price to buy/sell directly from cargo/storages

Opt to buy resources directly from Factory/Reverse Engineering/Prototyping

Change limit of buy/sell orders to number of terminals/outposts you can buy/sell

Show active users per terminal on entering

Add “ghost item” to inventory to create buy order

### Map and Camera

mouse wheel to zoom out and gain a map screen

removal of the map window

zooming right in goes over the shoulder, with auto tracking primary target

terminals and teleports shown only on map when zoomed out (as list on the side)

landmarks/overview removed

camera centrered on your own bot but lower in the screen (wasted screen realestate under bot)

### Landmarks Replacement

local area objects listed down right or left side as icons

only name and icon shown

hover detailed information

NPCs show name as well as speciality (DPS, Tank, EW, Speed)

click interaction (see under Interaction)

### Target Lock Indicators

larger range and lock indicators on current targets

larger again for primary

rat information on the target reticle

max speed difference shown (once max speed is discovered, or current known max)

NPC squad linking shown with lines

directional arrows from your bot to your current targets

current line of sight (blocked/unblocked)

npc’s known max firing range

Targeting computer removed (it doesnt show anything that isnt elsewhere on the screen) or move it to the right as a floating windowless element.

Targets that are attacking have floatin info about dps. (Calculated localled from logs)

### 

Terminal windows

By default load up your default windows, saved per terminal

Terminal ui schemes instead of serices buttons

Filtered inventory windows (show all CTs in this terminal)

Currently fitted bots shown as a list, summary for each, right mouse drag to scroll through. Sorted by popularity/size/name and favorites

### Geometry Overlays

locking range, weapon optimal and falloff shown overlayed on terrain

intergration of the artifact and directional scanner interfaces onto the terrain.

option to configure geometry viewmodes (cycle with a keypress)

these are like overview settings but effect the game world

shaders to see which view your in

include options for terrain slope

squadmate repair ranges option

### Interaction

selecting left and right click interactions for each type of thing

then removal of the Landmark information

shift-right click on a thing to access the other options. shift-left click to select that thing, normal left/right click to assign it to that button.

auto loot option for containers

starts with the smallest item

greys out container after first grab

container is yellow if it contains module loot

container is white if it contains only plasma/kernals

show how much cargo is actually in a loot container

### Own Bot Status

your bot status shown layed over your own bot or surrounding it

keep some distance, the more zoomed in, the more distance to keep

use both sides of the bot

ammunition information shown above weapons, of if zoomed out a lot, near other info

include current max ammo for this type held in cargo

Active modules shown near your bot

hover info for keybinding

right click to set keybindings for interactions (fire/reload)

keybinding saved per bot

Cargo shown as current:max ratio

Accumulator shows current, max and expected in 1 second (taking into account recharge and module use)

### Minor gameplay tweaks ideas.

More varied groups on alpha islands

Larger groups, easier npcs, most have no loot (less containers to click), perhaps loot drops from last to die in group.

Npcs aggress when thier group is attacked

More roamers on alpha islands, agreesive but easy ones.

afking should not be possible

The red icons are good.

### Plants/harvest profession

plants have seasons, this could create boom and bust cycles

plants could grow during one period, spreading out, hybernating in another

during the harvest season they would fruit/bloom and can be harvested.

Plants could spread more when players leave some fruit on them

cultivation means defending the area from scavengers.

Plants die after several cycles so it is important not to over harvest

New plants don't fruit for the first x cycles.

Each plant is available at different times so harvesters always have something to do

Searching for and recording plots during the off season becomes a important task

### Mission variety

wow, 4 level zero combat missions! And you need to do 30+ to get to level 1.

Actually coming under attack while doing missions would be nice.

Intercept haulers that are guarded

mixed groups!

Remove standings gates. Allow groups of new players to complete lvl 4 content.

### player interaction in the field

Flagging field containers for transport, destination, reward.

Flagging loot claims from a structure, paying per kill instead. Loot builds up outside the structure instead. Basically claimin loot from one area, but paying out instead. Anyone can come and offer more (paying for a number of kills at that time) kills always award the highest offer first.

market system used to purchase goods

Auto sell if a buy order is in place

Remove modules from loot
