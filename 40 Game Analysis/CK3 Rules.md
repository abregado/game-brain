Giving Land:

- If you give land to someone with the same realm tier as you, they will become independent

- Dont give away your highest titles

- Dont give titles that are the same tier as your highest title

- Give to high stewardship

- Give to same religion and culture

- GIve one duchy to each vassal to reduce inflighting

- Duchy title owner should hold the dutch capital, so they have access to the duchy building slot
