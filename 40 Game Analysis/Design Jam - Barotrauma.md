Rounds and Questions

What is the Talents system job in the design?

Roman:

- encourage vital positions are covered, by making a player good at one thing.

- Preventing players fighting over particular tasks

- Feeling of choice during progression

- **Customization of your locked in class choice**

- Limit the regret of being locked in a class system

- Lower skill/exp players can still be useful

- Grant powerful playstyles to individuals that would be too powerful if everyone had them.

- **Reinforce the roleplay of the class you chose**

- **Thematic reinforcements**

Rafael

- One player cannot do everything

- Enforcing communication

Peter

- Limiting what you can do

- Progression though recipes

- Research tree replacement

- Feeling of team progress

Tom

- **Personal progress distinct from team progress**

- Adding Replayability to individual classes

- **Enable having more than 6 players (one of each class)**

Ben

- Promoting behaviours that are counter to coop gameplay

- Increasing “good” choices outside the optimal

- Dampening of the committee feeling

Dave

- Diversify the players and characters

- Give the feeling of having a unique place in the team.

  - Personal responsibility

  - Feeling of participation “what do I bring?”

- Add Choices that the team makes together
