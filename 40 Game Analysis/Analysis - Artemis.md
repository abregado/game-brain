What works?

- Communication is necessary between ships and stations

- Starship Feeling

- Jump Drive was awesome

- Jump Drive player skill

- Engineering has a lot of things to do

- Helmsman was interesting

What doesn’t work?

- Energy gameplay is not interesting, causes dead time

- Mission loop is not interesting

- Gamemaster tools are very poor

- Too much submarine feeling

- Warp has no powerup or cooldown, just like “Going faster ++”

- Lasers are not intuitive

- Science and Comms only needed one per team of ships

- Game fluff is generic and boring

- Nuke was overpowered

What would you change (apart from the bad stuff) ?

- More survival elements

- Salvage of wrecks

- 2d plane feeling

- Bigger map: after energy fixes

- More mission types

- Area of Effect weapons destroy other projeciles

- Warp should disable weapons and shields
