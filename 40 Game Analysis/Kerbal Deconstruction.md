What went right

- Progress at own speed

- Challenging

- Deep Gameplay

- Non-violent

- Humor!

- Realistic yet fun simulation

- Tech and cash progression

- Always another puzzle to solve

- Goals to lead player

- Player can ignore built in goals and make their own

- Freedom of Expression

- Feeling of Exploration

What went wrong

- Some missions seemingly very difficult at progression level when given

- Lack of essential UI and calculations

- Forces player to hand calculate a lot of things

- Limited flight tutorials (changing plane, radial burns, hofmann transfers)

- Contracts give a lot of cash in Advance

- Contracts have VERY long timeframes

- Waypoints in Flight mode

- Badly placed Maneuver Node controls

- lack of Maneuver fine tuning

- Rover Physics (ground vehicles acting like aircraft)

What could have been done better

- Better use of Time Pressure

- Financial pressure in career mode: Once you have enough money, you have really a lot of money.

- Gameplay more focused on running simultaneous missions

- Construction help (weight/delta-v/total lift at speed)

- Missions to visit hidden places

- More uses for engineer and multi-kerbal crews

- More uses for planetary and orbital bases

- Explanation or warning about best transfer windows

- better use of Negotiations

Top Three Features

Exploration

The feeling of discovery given by exploring each place in the solar system is amazing. Each planet has a lot of different elements (terrain roughness, atmosphere type, gravity). When searching for science each biome can be exploited for points and it is fun locating them.

Modding Support

The developers opened up almost everything to modders and this was a massive driver for new content. Many of the problems above can be solved with simple community made plugins.

SPAACE!

The simulation is challenging and rewarding. It is also realistic enough to use as a demonstration of real life Orbital mechanics.

Suggested Mods

Kerbal Alarm Clock, Waypoint Viewer, Colored EVA suits, Kerbal Engineer

Future Mod Ideas

Maintenance and Running Costs

Each structure and vehicle has a daily cost associated with it. Players need to keep completing contracts to pay the bills and cannot simply take 2 years to complete a single mission to Duna.

Contract Hard Mode

Move 80% of the Advance Payment to the reward section of all contracts. Reduce completion times to be much shorter. Players should need to launch a mission very quickly to achieve the goal. This will also promote having infrastructure ready to be deployed in space to complete new contracts.

Rover Control

A set of controls for controlling Rovers, with throttle, max speed and other ground vehicle options.

Science Checklist

Show every biome seen and show how much of the total science has been extracted from that biome. Hint to the player how many biomes are on each planet. Possibly contracts to get Science from those biomes (difficult landing sites).
