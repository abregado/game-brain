Criteria

Violence: Either none at all, eliminating enemies is not the primary gameplay or the player does not fire projectiles or “hit things”.

Controls: 1 or 2 buttons and movement only. Button combinations or quicktime events non-existant. Not touch controls.

Text: Extremely limited text, or none at all. If text then it shouldnt be necessary to continue (eg: contain clues to solve riddles). Many localizations if any text.

Theming: No touchy topics like sexuality, violence, abuse, death, suffering, religion.
