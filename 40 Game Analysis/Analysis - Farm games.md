### Mels farm suggestions

Make short timer gameplay more like a minigame.

Multiple ui elements stacking up was a problem

### Top Farm points

World felt better, more things to unlock in the future

World seemed busy, with the lorries coming in and out

Lorries made you feel important

Choices were important earlier, when deciding who to sell to

Feed requests often got in the way

Spend premium currency often got in the way

animals moving made the farm feel alive, and distinguishes them from buildings

### Farmville 2 was

Felt trapped in a small space

Farm selling board felt boring

Keys currency was not intuitive

Animals were cuter

Ran much worse on low end device

More precise job creation/object ordering

Much steeper job time scaling

Animals arriving in boxes doesnt make much sense

### Ben's ideas

Slowly changing season to effect what you can grow

Slowly changing demand, visualized somehow

Auto destinations for products. Tap once and it loads the goods into the next machine/process

Might become more like a rhythm game once its set up

put down baby animals and after the timer they grow up and start producing
