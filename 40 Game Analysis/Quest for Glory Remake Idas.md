**Analysis**

Biggest Issues with the game:

- Walking through many screens

- Important UI is hidden

- Act \<-\> Rest Loop is unclear

- No Autosave

- No map

- No interaction highlighting

- Very small interaction areas

- Very limited clues (one per)

**Idea 1: Modern interface and loops**

QoL upgrade ala Broken Age.

- Double click to skip to exit

- Onscreen inventory

- Larger interaction points with highlighting

- More passing statements as clues

Background day night indication with fixed number of screen changes per day.

Fixed resting locations (where the sky is visible)

Enemies appear in an area and you choose to fight them or run. Only the Panther and Rex cannot be run from.

Defeat in combat has you wake up in the nearest resting location (Druid, Hermit, Meadow) on the next morning.

Stamina like in Stardew. It does not increase, just a fixed resource so you need to rest twice per day.

**Idea 2: More strategic Pawn movement on map**

Player is in the Adventurers Guild and plans their day on a map of the area.

- Map expands as the player explores

- Locations appear on the map as ? as player hears about them from people.

Only important locations are explorable in a point and click format

Conversations take place in the taverns. When you go to one, then you meet a new character to get clues from. This could be a character you already met, to get information you missed.

Combat occurs randomly when choosing to travel from one important location to another.

The Panther and Saurus Rex are shown on the map, blocking off some locations each day.

Stamina becomes how much you can do in a day, ala Stardew.
