What works?

- Slow movement encourages crew team play

- Pre-game Loadout gameplay

- Maps have different strategies

- The ship steering was very enjoyable

- Default controls for up/down forward/back are bad

What doesnt work?

- Steep learning curve

- Menu and Account system

- Lacking quick play (not really the case)

- rewards and achievements counter to the teamplay heavy mechanics

- Hwacha seems Over Powered

- Matchmaking depends on single players looking for games, but the gameplay needs

- No feedback on what you did wrong

- Engineer running and clicking

- respawn time for ships?

- Seeing the opponent's ship loadout

What would you change (apart from the bad stuff) ?

- More team community decisions for the Loadout component

- More maps with horizontal obstacles to fly under

- reduce fps control when not in a gun

- change between loadout preconfigs between deaths
