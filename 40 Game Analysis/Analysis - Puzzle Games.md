Lens 1:

- The player doesn't need to figure out WHAT to do, only how to do it

- What is the Logical Contradiction or Catch?

Lens 2:

- A game with a dominant strategy

Lens 3:

- Game with one solution

Problems to look for:

- Can the player “snooker” themselves?
