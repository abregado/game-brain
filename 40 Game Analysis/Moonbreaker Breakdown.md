Moonbreakers Breakdown

A free to play space flight shooter game. More reminiscent of deathmatch FPS shooters than a flight sim. Perhaps best compared to Arcade flight games but without any of the gimmicks. Really nothing there except its free-to-play which in itself doesnt add anything.

The “customization” is a joke. Experience is spent on a ship to make it better in arbitrary (and tiny) steps. One cannot customise a ship at all, only make it better in 6 areas. Graphical customizations are not available. New ship classes are purchasable through a RMT. These are simply better than the free versions (as is standard).

The GUI is functional but every player (enemy and friend) gets their name printed next to their ship in space. This happens at both short and very long range, meaning while flying into a crowded battle you cant see who is where and what team they are on.

Several of the supported game modes play out very similar to one another (team deathmatch style). The objectives are either uninteresting, punishing, or seemingly nonexistant. Most involve lowering your opponents “score variable” and keeping yours up while the time runs out.

The flight model is servicable, but there is no way to pull off smart maneuvers. Flying around eachother in circles, head to head clashes and tailing someone with matched speed are the only tactics. Asteroids can be used to create some cool getaways but since the player cannot see very much, they often cause suicides.

Simple Fixes to create a more interesting game

weapons have interesting cooldown, reload and fire delay combinations

semi-automatic triggers and burst fire

secondary weapons have a charge mechanic

afterburner is not on/off (it takes time to build up speed)

Most actions have a “dug too deep” effect

speed bleeds off much slower

afterburner can be charged and released to give a burst of speed forward

mouse wheel moves power from engines to maneuvering thrusters

rate of change between thrusting and turning is slow

Missiles could work as follows:

Pilot holds down right mouse button to acheive minimum lock

holding longer tightens the lock

holding too long disables the missile for a while unlaunched

Release to deploy the missile

Tap right button to engage missile forward thrust

When the missile has reached the speed you want, tap the right button to engage the missiles maneuvering thrusters, deactivating the forward thrust.

Missile forward thrust consumes more fuel from the missile.

Right tap again to deploy a special warhead (MIRV, Cluster, Premburst, ECM, Chaff)
