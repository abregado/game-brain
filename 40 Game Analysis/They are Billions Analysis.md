**Cardinal Sins**

- Requiring game information that the player can only find out by playing through once: Attack directions, ect

- Requiring the player to scan the screen for pixels: Finding collectables

**Fundamental Concerns**

- There is no ability to recover, if you mess up, the game is over but you will have to wait until its over to restart

- Enemy numbers snowball to make this even stronger.

- Best opening move to build three tents?

- Mission score malus for retries!?!

- Save backup option in Mission Select screen seems like it moves save scumming to a different game layer.

- Due to the score malus for retries, now I will tech up as much as possible and complete every easy mission before moving on.

- Learning by failing is fine when the iteration cycle is small, but waiting until day 55 to find out you failed is too long.

- Being able to come back and try on a harder difficulty is nice, but pointless due to Retry malus.

**Hidden Valley Issues**

- Early infected wander in from the north but not from across the bridge, meaning the player has no idea they will attack from both directions. The Message “They are attacking from the north” further causes confusion.

- Second Time around the infected did not attack from over the bridge

**Hunters Meadow**

- Since one infected attacking a house can spell doom, I was incentivized to build a lot of towers.

- SOOO many tents needed for 600 colonists at this stage.

- If I want to ace this mission I need to come back with better technology

**Cape of Storms**

- Arbitrary “in the future” events that are primary objectives don't seem to work very well. The player either waits a long time to find out they failed, or they wait a long time knowing they will win.

**Hero Missions**

- Is it possible to miss some of the pickups? Yes, it is. Lame.

**Swarm Missions**

- It does not tell you the directions at the start of the map, so you cannot plan a strategy before you start..

**Hero Level ups**

- Some upgrades are just not as good, why do I have two options if one is better?

**Interface Issues**

- I have to hover every square to see where the good spots are

- No indication why I cant build another structure while building consecutively

- Autosaves happen but I cant load them? Is this just so I cant save scum?

- Hotkeys to select a building category but none to go backwards or select a specific building
