## What Worked

- UFOs attacking in waves forcing the player to pick their air battles.

- Tactical terrain was interesting.

- Graphic style is really nice.

- Alien types were interesting and each added different strategy elements.

- Morale System kept my soldiers in groups and gave value to having a good commanding officer.

- The game was fun to play!

## What Didn’t Work

- Requiring different loadouts on aircraft against different UFO types. It was frustrating to check and change your craft before every launch.

- Furies are never worth researching.

- Complete black fog of war at the start of every mission. Didn't we get a view of the UFO from the air? (Solution 7)

- Displaying remaining combat fuel and its effect on combat outcomes.

- Needing to hire soldiers just to fire them again, so as to cycle the recruits list. This list was implemented to stop such behavior (as it was in X-COM) but fails to deliver this goal. (solution 6)

- Massive cost of losing a base to alien attack. (Solution 1)

- No way to have soldiers gain experience without fighting a tactical battle.

- Massive advantage for moving within 6 tiles of a target, but no way to spend extra AP on aiming. (solution 1)

- Not knowing who is the commanding officer in the mission (they need to be protected due to the morale system)

- Given the large tradeoff of using grenades (less salvage), they are prohibitively expensive to use in combat (AP wise).

## Choices which mattered but which were not easy to implement

- Aircraft Loadouts. Checking which UFO type you have to intercept, then opening the base hanger menu and checking if you are correctly equipped, then back to geoscape to launch interceptors. (solution 8)

- When to launch landing options. The player wants to try and land his missions during the daytime, but it is hard to tell. No option to set an ETA or to schedule launches.

- Knowing if to launch a dropship or if to wait for a manufacturing job to finish. (solution 4)

## Choices which did not matter

- Newly researched weapons were a straight up upgrade. There is never any value to choosing the previous tier of weapon.

- Maximum damage output in Tactical battle is point blank with Burst, Safest Damage is one aimed shot and one normal shot. (solution 1)

- How many things to simultaneously Research/Manufacture. It seems that simply doing one thing at a time is always best. (solution 5).

- Running away or half completing a mission was never a real choice.

- Choice of which soldiers to send on a mission. Just send the best, most well armed soldiers to the hardest missions, and send a newbie squad to the easier missions.

- Who is the commanding officer in a mission.

## Suggestions

1.  Aliens should locate and attack your bases much earlier on, with smaller groups. Both to inform the player that this can happen, and to give base defenders valuable experience.

2.  Aimed shot should use up all available AP, fire once and give a bonus to the next round if firing with aimed shot.

3.  Soldiers which were shot by aliens with aimed shot should be marked, so they can move to a safe location, or you can focus on aliens which are aiming.

4.  Geoscape notifications which countdown things which will finish soon, such as manufacturing jobs. Science could even be visible all the time, since mostly you are only researching one thing at time.

5.  Maximum Scientists/Engineers working on a project at 100% efficency. Extras at added as reduced efficency. OR Each lab or workshop can add a efficency bonus to a single project (say 20%) and each project can only recieve a single lab/workshop bonus. This means that two labs each with a project and 5 scientists is better than a single lab with 10 scientist, but it is still worth putting 10 scientists in one lab if you need to rush the job.

6.  Mark minimum soldier stats to join the Xenonauts project. Aiming too high means new recruits are not available very often. Also ability to remove recruits from the recruit queue. Each slot should refill in an amount of time based on your relations with nearby funding blocks. This could add a new pressure to your players.

7.  The entire map is revealed and the player can select the orientation of the landing craft, and possibly have multiple landing options.

8.  When selecting to intercept an UFO, show options for load out and show the chance of successful encounter at the current range.
