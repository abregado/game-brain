Open Transport Tycoon Deconstruction

What Works

- Always something to do

- Logic circuits (signals)

- Maximizing a network

- Designing interchanges and stations

- Watching the cities grow

- Competing against yourself

- Exploring the tech tree

What doesn’t work

- No goals

- Few real choices regarding tech tree and upgrade path

- Upper limit of industry production

- Cities too easy to grow

- No real obstacles to construction limit the need for creativity

- No immediate demands or interruptions driving the gameplay

- No real difficulty

- Money Creep eventually sets in no matter what

- not enough information given to the player before he makes a choice

- Road Vehicles too granular

- Simple choices are too time consuming to make

- Set up and wait. Once something is set up there is very little tweaking to be done afterwards.

- Waiting Pedestrians build up to crazy levels with no penalty

UI Upgrades

- Train purchasing screen or detailed vehicle information to show:

  - “tiles until max speed” - unloaded

  - “tiles until max speed” - loaded

  - time to travel 20/40/60 tiles full cargo

  - value of cargo at these ranges

- Ghost building: Design a whole network section and pay for it all at once

- Important information shown in Main Vehicle Display:

  - speed (better display)

  - profit

  - cargo types (icons)

  - reliability

  - value of cargo at current age

- Template Building (copy paste areas)

- Town Authority Rating shown next to Town Name as smily face (as in the town listing)

Ideas from Tower Defence

- Continuously increase the pressure on the player

- Reward streak gameplay

- Streak gameplay is where player earns their profit

- Breaking a streak goes back

Ideas from City Builders

- Construction takes time to complete (years)

- Player designs his networks for trains that are not yet available

Ideas from EVE

- Industries pay different amounts for cargos

- If an industry is not supplied it will slowly increase its offer

- Each industry has a “happy zone” in which it will level off its price

- Towns increase their offers as well

- The player should be encouraged to modify his timetable frequently to maximize profits

- Profit stagnation occurs if the player never modifies his network (current gameplay)

- Cities build their own train stations, airports and bus stops to promote you selling to them (lower infrastructure and setup costs)

- If not supplied, perhaps Towns will set up their own Competitors, which will just deal with local needs.

Ideas from Farmville

- Always a quest to complete

- short and long term goals

- always more quests than can be completed

- Quests have a timeout

- Enhancing the subsidy system might help with this

- Docks which regularly change their acceptances and wanted amounts. Prices are always several times higher. This might encourage players to suddenly re-route trains and vehicles from other jobs.
