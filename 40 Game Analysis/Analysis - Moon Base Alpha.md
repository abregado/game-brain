What works?

- Slow movement speed

- Time cost for your actions

- Unobtrusive and Diegetic indicators

- Level design

- Tech, assets and visuals all nicely in narrative

- Minigame worked

What doesnt work?

- Camera auto correct

- Massive penalty for pressing the wrong button

- A lot of time with no controls

- Minigames not varied enough

- Bugs with bridge couplers

- Hitboxes too big, it is more annoying than things clipping.

- Command centre mechanics wasted

- Rover recharge mechanic wasted

- Dropping should be instant

- Too many robots

- Rover in reverse

- Area denial

What would you change (apart from the bad stuff) ?

- Replayability

- Jetpacks/Flying Vehicles

- Level Editor

- Building challenge, not just repair

- Physics for throwing

- Secondary mechanics for robots to be repaired

- Vehicles should be able to turn on the spot or be more drivable

- Damage inflicted to base

- Two or more players to coordinate

Is a rebuild possible

- Very easily, even with more scope.
