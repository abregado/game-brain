## What works?

- Research tree visualization is great.

- Deep Processes cause players always to have something to tweak or add. “Your work is never complete” feeling really sweeps the player up.

- Autocrafting means that complex recipes and factory design dont ruin the onboarding process for new players.

- One recipe per item. Means that recipe chains are much easier to understand.

- You can fish while in a tank.. It even seems to be faster. Fish were very popular once discovered.

- Blueprints are amazing, for new and old players.

## What doesn’t Work?

- Research dependency structure is confusing. Redundant tech dependencies.

- Recipe visualization: Players need to visualize too many steps in their heads.

- On replay, auto-build (robots) for blueprints takes too long to reach.

- Biter AI does not create meaningful gameplay. They do not affect the design decisions you make.

- Enemy range indication for Worms and Biters is inadequate. Worms have a “ready-state” but it is not enough. Knowing where the aggression radius for Biters is.

- Building range, most players felt it was not enough, but we did not experience construction robots.

- Belts move you by default. WHY!?

- Terrain generation is meaningless both for combat and factory design. You can remove all obstacles in your way, and where/on what you build also makes no difference.

- Solar not powerful enough. Not possible to go completely green. We discussed that overpowering solar has massive detrimental effects of the rest of the game.

- Uranium enrichment is weird. We discussed moving “unenriched” Nuclear tech to far earlier in the tech tree.

- Oil Tech really hits hard. There is no simple fluid based recipes before this, and very little pipe use.

- It is not obvious how Equipment and higher levels of armor are used together

## Suggestions

- Disasters. Random stuff to shake the status-quo. We discussed that these need to be avoidable through automation, one example was how Disasters are managed in the game Pharaoh.

- Pipes getting clogged with pollution and other detrimental effects on the factory based on pollution (See disasters).

- Placing trees, or at least regrowing them.

- Moving pollution. Pipe it somewhere else to dump it out. Then the base amount of pollution for most things could be increased to make biters more difficult. Pollution becomes a design challenge, instead of something you can’t control.

- Alien Domestication. Not only training them to fight (the obvious choice), but feeding them to keep them peaceful.

- Respawn in a random place when you die, to increase the consequences of death.

- Tooltips for hotkeys associated with the item you are holding. Examples include, Grenades, Capsules, Blueprints.

- Early recipe with fluids in assembler-2, even if it is only water. This is to combat the “oil tech learning cliff”

- Early autoplace solution for blueprints. Most felt that a restart would frustrate them due to the amount of hand placing that would be needed. Even with blueprints to take the designs forward, the thought of placing everything was troubling.

- Remove armor and just have equipment slots on a player. Some of those slots can be filled with Armor Plating, which would replace Iron and Steel Armor. Unlock a bigger grid through research. Players would be introduced to equipment grids much earlier.
