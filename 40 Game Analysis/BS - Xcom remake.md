Geoscape only remake idea brainstorming

Starting base structure, has all the functionality of the starting xcom base. Extra facilities are added onto this.

Troopers have a base survival rate. Tactical combat is worked out as a series of choices. Pre-battle choice, 2 infight choices. Should only take 5-6 clicks total to resolve a landing party.

Expansion of the tech tree to include biotech upgrades and cybernetics. Soldiers should have ethics about use of each technology.

World politics should be more complex. Each country has its own anti-alien forces but are not as powerful as XCOM. They may get to a ufo first, call for help with a tough group of aliens or lay claim to a ufo. They can also eventually be controlled by the aliens and attack xcom facilities. Recovered ufos and alien tech as well as bodies can be sold to any nation, each time they get more powerful technology. Once researched the actual technology can be sold as well.

XCOM production facilities should be used to build prototype technology. These are used by troops very early. Further research will enable cheaper construction and eventually you can sell the schematics to nations and purchase the products direct from them (national production can build more items much faster for cheaper). Better production technoques come about from producing more units. Small runs are much more expensive than large ones, earlier more expensive than later.

Research should have a few expensive theories that act as hurdles. each of these unlocks a larger tree made up of main nodes and many smaller nodes. Researchin an alien artifact first requires it to be dismantled, then a choice about what field you would like the research to go in (weaponry, defences, medical, environmental, ect)

Logistics should be more interesting. Multi-base gameplay should begin after just a few fights. Dedicated facilities can be researched (secure storage faciltieis, industrial bases, airbases, training yards). Constant transfers can be set up using transport hangers.

Alien crews should be a pushover, but as time goes on more and more soldiers are brought on each ufo flight.

Alien tech should have the ability to self destruct, depriving the player of much to research. This can be circumvented after a short time with research and particular tactics.

Shooting down a UFO should be more difficult at the start, the early game tactic should involve waiting for the UFOs to land. Air support can be called in from allied nations, and your own aircraft can be sent to follow UFOs instead of just engaging them. Also patrols can be set up on a rostered system. Flight numbers and squadrons, including formations can also be set up.

Options to bomb a crash site first to make it easier should be available. This can sometimes set of an explosion in the alien reactor causing damage if it is in an urban zone. Nations should fin this very unapealing.

Post battle should show experience/ranks gained, ammunition expended, injuried and casulties. Clean up crews should be brought in afterwards to collect artifacts and ufo components.

One use aerial/orbit depployed "abilities" can be used for a cost or penality to make a tactical mission easier. After shooting down a ufo, the interceptor or squardon can make a pass over the area to assess the damage. This lets the player know how much risk he is taking going into battle.

Early game the player should have many weak troops, late game squad sizes should be much lower but singl troopers being much more powerful. Support crews and reserve units should increase in number.

Current research and production runs should be shown on the geoscape layer. Common options should be only 2 clicks away from the geoscape with the buttons being as close as possible to eachother to move through the menus.

Hangers have multiple uses. Interception and cargo craft can be stored there, as can recovered ufos. Landing zones can be built to accomodate transfer/logistical aircraft. Aircraft can be stored in smaller storage bays but take longer to prepare for launch. Example:// A UFO is detected. A single interceptor is ready can be launched immediatly. Once a fighter launches another can be prepared in 5 minutes. Should the player decide two fighters are needed, the first fighter must fly a holding pattern waiting for the second. THen the effective flight range in decreased as the first has less fuel, and the UFO will have flown much farther. Larger flights take longer to launch. The player must decide how many of each to build: launch bays and storage bays.

Since this remake is more about the geoscape mode, most of the players time should be spent there. New challenges are required to make the gameplay interesting and strategic. The players game time should be spent deploying the correct aircraft to intercept UFOS or alien ground missions. EVERY OPPORTUNITY FOR A MISSION SHOULD BE A REAL CHOICE! To commit or to let the aliens do their thing. For this to occur, the player must be given a rough idea on what the outcome will be, as well as the penalities for choosing not to help.

Captured artifacts can never be used. They should be disassembled and the parts used to build human tech weaponry, or to upgrade existing tech.

Any choice that is automatically the best should be avoided (Every trooper having a medical kit). Choosing some powerful equipment should always have its drawnbacks and close other equipment options (cant wear bulky armor when a trooper has boosted muscle tech)

Different deployment methods for troopers and aliens. Aerial drop pods, parachutes, teleportation, ground vehicles. Aliens can start extended ground wars and deploy AA to protect their troops.

Differnt waiting times before acting have different effects. Attacking a landed ufo immediatly means all the aliens are still inside

Being able to manufactore alien technology doesnt come from simply replicating a collected artifact. Alien bases and manufacturing centres must be captured and researched before you can build new components.

Aliens should show some sence of self preservation. This should be more prevalent in the smart castes. Soldiers should often sacrifice themselves while the weaker leader aliens flee the battle.

Squad fatigue and work cycles should be modelled. Not all troops are available instantly at any moment. Squads can be kept on alert but then do not train.

When you have a base in a country and relations are very good you can request to have that countries military intel hardware wired into your defence grid. Detection and Sams from this country that are not tied up in other conflicts are available for you to command.
