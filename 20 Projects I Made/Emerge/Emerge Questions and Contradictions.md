## What was the cataclysm?

Something that can detect exactly the biological signature of this one species.

Destroys everything around those signatures.

Was coming closer from outside the solar system.

Since the planet only had one animal type (the sentient species) it was clear the planet would be destroyed.

Going to space was not an option, since the effect detects them trying to leave.

A very old satellite that was already at the edge of the solar system with biological samples was destroyed.

A manned mission to the outer solar system in which the crew were in status was destroyed.

A manned colony of seven, two planets further out was destroyed. 30% of the mass of that planet was removed in the destruction.

Two missions were set up. A new manned mission and an unmanned mission that only had plants. Only the manned mission was destroyed.

The effect was localised in a lab on the homeworlds moon. Several samples and their surroundings were destroyed. This created a certainty that the effect was real.

The rate of closure was calculated so a "Zero day" for the homeworlds destruction could be calculated.

Automated probes were not destroyed.
