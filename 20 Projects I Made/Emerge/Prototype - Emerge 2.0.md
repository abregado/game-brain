Fuck this. What if I made Emerge the way I wanted to originally?

No barriers. Entire world is open from first round.

Puzzles are locked by how many special batteries you can create at the same time. These activate lasers.

4x4 elevator shaft

Player starts with 1x1 elevator.

Player fills a slot.

2 round resources

Time

Group Stamina

4 types of day weather

- Normal

- Dust Storm

- Lightning Storm

- Eclipse

4 types of night weather

- Normal

- Cold night

- Dust Storm

- Eerie night

Machines

Terrain flatterner (sweeper/roller)

Turns a 3x3 or 5x1 area in front of the machine into a flat area where machines can be placed. Takes 1 day to clear 10 tiles. Needs a new battery every day. Reveals hidden items in the soil.

Thermoreg

Every day the Hot side fills and every night the Hotside discharges to create a battery. Must be placed in special locations.

Condenser

Fills an adjacent pallet with items over the day when activated. Must be placed in special locations.

Plater

Replaces flat terrain with steel floor. Replaces a 3x1 in front of the machine. Each plate must be loaded separately. Needs a battery to function all day. Can place 10 plates per day.

Filler

Fills adjacent Platers with items from an adjacent pallet. Requires a battery to function.

Sail Tower Mainmast

Creates a sunshade. Requires a battery to function for 1 day. Connect to other masts nearby to create areas of shade
