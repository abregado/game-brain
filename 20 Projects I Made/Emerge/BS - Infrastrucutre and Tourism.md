Concept - Infrastructure as part of the game process

In many current games map infrastructure is designed and fixed by the developers or mappers. In most real life situations infrastructure comes about due to a short term need, and then later is used due to its relative cheaperness (next to new infrastrucutre). Cities and roads are placed in locations that they were needed hundreds of years ago and today either fall into disuse or get overhauled even tho there are better ways to make new ones.

Examples: Master of orion starlanes, racing tracks, RTS roads/resources, city builder terrain,

The process of building infrastucture could be built into the game itself (or proceedurally generated before each game session) to simulate such a period of growth. This could be a part of the early game, or as an entirely separate section of the game.

Example: the first part of a racing game could see the players all influencing the development of a small section of a city. The city then crumbles to dust and the players race inside the new post-apocalyptic setting.

Example: players take turns developing (very abstractly) an advanced race in a 4x game. As this develops the starlane structure builds up. The civilization dies and then the players begin the game.

Tourism for MMOs

Simple idea. Free trials that allow unlimited freedom for players to view the game world but disallow any interaction with it.

Example: Players can dowbload the EVE client for free, fly around in their n00bship and see how everything works but cannot use the market/fire any weapons/ect

Example: In an exploration FPS MMO players can explore but not aquire any goods.

Gold RUsh for industrial space game. Infrastructure for Industry Space game could come from players chosing the first sites to build the starting space stations and facilities. By using a Gold Rush method whereby Windfalls of resources are high to start with and diminish quite quickly back to their natural norms. At the same time make new infrastructure avaiable to be constructed. Fueling stations, nav beacons, starship docking, storage locations ect all are nessesary for players to take advantage of the windfall and those who stand to make the most from these windfalls will consrtuct them. Since they are still publicly usable, the game world benefits forever. Some of these strucutres will fall into disrepair, while others will become staple stopping points for newer players. Each time a new phase of exploartion is unlocked, the gold ruish will start again and new structures will become available for all players.

AFTL obstacles. Only having direct point to point flight is a recipe for monotony. Different areas should block aftl use, while others should simply force a speed limit. Other ways still might just increase fuel use. This should encourage more development diversity as there is no "best way" to move things around.

Ideas: Planets/Moons leave behind a gratitational shadow which efects AFTL operation. Skimming the atmosphere of a planet saves time but uses more fuel. Dust clouds cannot be flown throufgh directly. Some ores/rocks generate navigational jamming effects meansing ships have to make consecutive jumps to get close to them. Delta-V of the destination must be matched.

Tourism FPS MMO: Planet Calypso tries to be real game, but fails in almost every aspect. I would propose to instead make a world that is soley for tourism purposes. All players can come visit for free and can interact with a everything. However transport on these worlds is a monopoly controlled by the system. Vehicles and Teleports are purchasable items and should be nessesary to get the most out of the experience. of courese players can walk, or hitchhike, or charge money for sightseeing tours. The game world would have to be highly detailed and interesting to stand up on tourism alone.
