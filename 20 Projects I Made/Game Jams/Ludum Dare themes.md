Ludum Dare

The last 6 themes were:

## Discovery

memory game, discover what is under something else. Hunting for something you don’t know what is. Discover Combinations of objects? Trial and error mechanic? Click locations in the right order like a code lock to progress.

Machines. Put together in diffrent ways will generate different results. use these combinations to effect changes inthe game world and overcome challenges.

Prospecting. Search for clues and drop a probe. Come back later to see success.

Learning. Players can move obstacles in their way using a few different methods. A rockfall and a tribe of trolls are both impassable but when together they cancel eachother out.

Decoys. Present players with choices that may or may not be true. Things and events should not always be as they seem. Players should not only think “what will i get if i choose A” but also “What will i lose from B if A is a lie”. Decoys should always be visible. A trap for instance could be easily detectable, but sometimes there are “friendly” traps.

Pushing out from a central location. WHat you hope to find could be in any direction.

## Its Dangerous to go alone, TAKE THIS!

A videophone/telephone, but you are the one who is giving the phone. Your friend calls you up and explains the situation and you have to decide what he does. a dialogue tree game.

Perhaps you are given something that makes it MORE dangerous.

## Escape

Procedurally generate a cavern that is ongoing for a very long time. The exit comes eventually. It is dark and you can only leave 4 markers behind. Every 5 minutes you are teleported to a random part of the cavern and the rotation is randomised. You can only see where you hover the mouse.

Something stopping you from getting far:

Walls (obvious)

A need that you can’t take with you: water, food, air, power

Gravity pulling you down. Lander clone?

enemys attack, returning you to center/start location

## Alone

Either you are alone, or there is only one entity in the world. Solid objects dont count. Cant really be a combat/Action game and still feel thematic. Survivial/time attack? Game of avoidance. Your character or the protagonist wants to stay alone. Avoid other people. Stealth game. You are shadowing another creature in the game.

No other entities are aware of you.

Solo character travelling between locations on a space ship/ocean vessel/walking a road

Party combat where you attempt to keep yourself alive but have all your allies killed by the end of the fight (Renegato)

Desolate World.

## Tiny World

Platformer with world wrap?? Im sure thats not original. Removal of one sense. Removal of one axis. 1d platformer... cant be done. Frame of reference. You are very small and large things are moving all around you all the time. Perhaps the level itself is alive.

Exploration of a small tile world. Worlds inside worlds? World starts tiny and the player grows to fill it

## Evolution

Devolution? Give the player a puzzle and all the tools to complete it, then remove one tool and ask them to complete it again. Continue until only one tool remains.

## Likely game types

Top down Tile based movement game. Rougelike combat. Turn or realtime doesnt matter.

Action shooter movement. 360 degrees of movement, no discrete units is a possibility with cameras and world viewpoint scrolling.

Fully maths driven strategy or management game. Action keypress controls, mouse or GUI elements a possibility.
