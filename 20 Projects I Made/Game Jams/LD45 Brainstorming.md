Discrete interactions

Puzzle elements

Single player

5 minutes gameplay

Themes liked:

Unconventional Movement

Isolation

Take one, leave the rest

Making Connections

One speed, or stop. No lowering your one speed

**Cardinal Jumper Tile Game**

Move only in cardinal directions

Move a fixed number of squares

Step on special tiles to increase/decrease your jump distance

Pits and Spikes.

**Tetris Double ended Brainfuck**

Tetriminos come from top and bottom.

They are made from 4 colors

Your “paddle” is coated in a color

Blocks of the same color touching your paddle are destroyed

A hole in your paddle allows blocks to go through

If the wrong color touches your paddle it gets stuck on
