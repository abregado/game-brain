Kua ideas

Pillars

**Success based on Engineering**

If the player has the right design, they should be able to succeed. Piloting skill should be secondary.

**Player agency.**

Players can get help without giving up total control.

EG:// i can give player two control over my lift engine

**Self paced progression.**

The player chooses when they "remove the training wheels".

Hard and more complex missions can be played by removing them.

Bonuses given for attempting without them.

No profiles, players can at any time choose their level of skill.

One player can be in a higher tier and the other can still come along.

**Break time options**

There should be multiple ways for ships to park so the player can take a break.

**Graphics**

Connect related parts with physics pipes and cables, or armor plates.

Orthographic view, but slightly tilted to show the ground. Might be too annoying…
