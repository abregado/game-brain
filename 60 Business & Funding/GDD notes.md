## “**Manu makes mistakes” talk**

Leute Ausbeuten oder selbst Ausbeuten oder von andere Leute ausgebeutet

Reiterated Kickstarter doesn't work unless you do pre marketing.

Check the shipping rules for box sizes to different counties.

Send backs will be a thing.

Keep “bus people” problem im Auge. What happens if someone is not available for a long time?

Always have a plan B.

Expect it not to go as planned

Be ready to jump, the good opportunities come without warning.

Write your pitch document for your jury not for yourself.

## From Pitch to Player - Tips for Successful Feature Design

What is best for the game, not what is cool, or what you made.

Anticipate questions and have answer ready

Don't answer it in a presentation, let the person ask and then answer confidently

Ritualistic work. Weekly the same things. Give regular updates.

Correct course often and early

Every prototype should only show what is necessary to prove

Clear goals for the prototype

Look behind the feedback. What is the cause of this response to the game?

A good designer can recognize a good idea, even if it is not theirs.

A good designer does not necessarily need to create good features, but must be able to recognize them

## Self-Publishing: How to Properly Finance Your Project on Kickstarter

26% success rate 2024

But it is going down

2015 only 17% success

USA dominates the Kickstarter market

75% game backers will back more than once

Prelaunch phase is the most important

Twitter still works

Socials are No longer community driven but content driven

Cross promo with other ks important

Make your KS early and generate followers

Newsletter doesn't bring many but it brings very invested people

Prelaunch landing page can be made early

Steam banner in festivals!!!

Meta Ads?

Backer kit has a 2x ROI

Der Mann spricht zu schnell für mich…

Unglaublich schnell…

Key Art is so important.

Keep it simple

A/B test because it is so important

So make two and pick one

Don't be cheap with your KS pledge levels.

Pledgers want to give you money

Good customers here!

Digital only!!!!

Discord roles and badges work well

Add-ons so people can pledge for others

Alpha access!!!

Fake stretch goals. Don't set your campaign goal at what you want, but have your goal at the second stretch goal.

Do stretch goal reveals
