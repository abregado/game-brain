### Session 3: Feature Creation for MP Notepad

#### Feature: Angel personifcation

This was discussed to be somewhere between a regular Avatar based representation of the player and a God game. Instead you are an “angel” and the character you see on the screen is not you, but your ability to stay in the game world is linked to him/her.

This may cause the player to have less attachment to the game characters. This feature is compatible with more genres than the regular idea of Avatar personification.

The player would suffer a slight indirect control penalty to the character. Perhaps similar to some 3rd person or overhead cam RPG games with only a single protagonist.

#### Feature: Drawing lines in the sand or building walls

Players can individually, or as a group, surround an area with an unbroken line. Once complete this line disappears and the players gain control of the space inside. Anyone who helped draw the line gains access to building inside. Players will need to work together to encapsulate large areas before the “ink” starts to fade. The larger the groups of players become, the more unwieldy the drawing will become, and the more risk there is of a player missing ONE tiny space causing the whole things to fade away.

#### Feature: The Cthulhu Supremacy

This was an good attempt to come up with a team (or Realm) based PvP objective. We felt the players should have a choice if they should participate, even if they could just find a different server with their particular needs.

Take a basic land control tog-of-war mechanic. Add the ability for the player to effect the environment in his controlled area. Throw in a no-mans land for good measure. Then have one teams border push forwards continuously.

The teams embody the virtues of good and evil. The defenders are trying to hold back the evil forces. The attackers are literally devouring the game world by pushing their border accross the entire map.

We would like to see the teams have assymitrical bonuses. Things that the defenders build last much longer but are harder to build. The attackers can create structures quickly but they decay very rapidly (also closer to the world ending encroachment).

While we want players have a choice if they participate, the objective should affect them regardless. Cthulhu will eventually devour the game world and win, regardless of which team you are on. Player who wish to have some short game sessions could do as they wish without upsetting the balance too much.

There was discussion as to game length. Persistence is great, but if the game is set to last indefinitely then the objectives don't really matter anymore. The mechanics could scale to create scenarios where the inevitable devouring comes after a week, day or month. There is no reason why the “good” team couldn’t push back the evil and then not complete the victory condition, prolonging the server reset.
