# Working Title

One liner catch phrase

Author\[s\] names

### Introduction

Three sentences which build upon the oneliner on the front page

Include two or three words about each section below.

Write this last.

### Player Story

Tell a story about a play session in the game from the point of view of the character/avatar/player.

If the game has a central character, mention them here.

One paragraph is perfect.

Two paragraphs is bordering on too much.

Leave on the reader thinking “What would I do next?”

### Background

Why is this game important now?

What drove you to want to make this game?

List an inspiration for this game?

If you are chasing a market gap, explain the current market forces?

- Most important feature name: A sentence about the most important feature

### Genre and Theme

What art style/mood/feeling will the game have?

Is the game real time/turn based/action/strategy?

Fit the game into one of the mainstream genres and add modifiers.

### Technology

Which libraries, engines, rendering techniques will be used to create the game?

Are there any pre-established gameplay techniques that will be used (monetization, ect)?

### Platform

On which devices can this game be played?

Why was this platform chosen (location of audience, technological constraints)?

### Market

What other games do the players of this game play?

What is it about this game that those players would like?

Describe some attributes of your target customer.

### Development

Which skill sets will the project need? Do you have them all?

How long do you have for development? Is that enough?

Are there any development constraints which are not listed above?

### Extras

- Mood board/Color Palette

- Concept artwork/music at end of pitch

- Design pillars (3x one word “axioms” of the design)

End of High Level Concept / Pitch Document

### Do

- Keep each feature short. A title and one sentence is enough. If there is some cool interaction between two features, it should be in the Player Story.

- Setting and Story (if a selling point) should be mentioned throughout the document.

- Include number of players somewhere, but not as a feature unless it is a selling point.

- Hint at what the player will feel when interacting with feature X.

- Intro and Tagline should hint at Design pillars.

- Be honest if you have not considered one of the points, rather than just removing the section.

- Be honest with your development constraints, an interested publisher will want to help.

### Don't

- Pretend that your game is completely unique

- Explain how features will work (instead explain why they are important)

- Write the Intro and Tagline first, they are the most important things!

- Include interface/control information (unless it is different, then it is a design feature)

- Include music/sound information (unless it is a selling point)

- Include anything you would describe just for the sake of pointing out the thing will be good. EG:// “The game will have good sound/controls/graphics”.
